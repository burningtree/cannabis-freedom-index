Beyond Europe, the widest measure of enforcement is how many people the police bring into formal contact, as suspects, arrests or cautions, for using or possessing drugs, as governments report it to [UNODC](https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/data-and-analysis/world-drug-report-2026-annex.html).[@1] It covers 104 countries. Per 100,000 people, [Sweden](/country/se/) reports the most, about 741 in 2024, then [Spain](/country/es/) and [Sri Lanka](/country/lk/); several countries report none at all.

All drugs are counted together, because cannabis is not reported separately.[@1] Some include administrative fines and some do not, so the comparison is rough. For cannabis alone in Europe see [offences for cannabis use](/datasets/cannabis-offences/); for what a contact with the police leads to, see each profile's [arrests and prisoners](/context/arrests/) and [beyond the penalty](/context/consequences/) sections.
