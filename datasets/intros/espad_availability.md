Whether a teenager could get cannabis if they wanted to says more about the market than about the law. The [ESPAD](https://www.espad.org/espad-report-2024) school survey asked students aged 15 to 16 in 37 European countries the same question in 2024, and the median is 28% who say it would be fairly or very easy.[@1] [Denmark](/country/dk/) and [Germany](/country/de/) are highest, at 41%, with [Slovenia](/country/si/) close behind; [Moldova](/country/md/) and [Ukraine](/country/ua/) lowest, under 10%.

Germany's survey was taken in 2024, the year of its [Cannabis Act](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/). The [chart of how easy it is to get](/charts/#easy-to-get) sets the answers beside each score; compare with [students who have tried cannabis](/datasets/espad-lifetime/) and each profile's [black market](/context/black-market/) section.
