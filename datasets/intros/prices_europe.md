What a gram costs on the street says how available cannabis is despite the law. The [European Union Drugs Agency](https://www.euda.europa.eu/data/stats2026/ppp_en) collects the mean retail price of herb and resin from police purchases, seizures and surveys in each country.[@1] Among 29 countries the median is about €10 a gram; the highest recent prices are in [Norway](/country/no/), [Malta](/country/mt/) and [Cyprus](/country/cy/), the lowest in [Croatia](/country/hr/), at about €3.

Dutch prices are for cannabis bought in [coffeeshops](/culture/traditions/the-coffeeshop/), not on the street, and prices are not adjusted for inflation.[@1] The [price chart](/charts/#price) follows each country since 2002, and the chart of [THC per euro](/charts/#thc-per-euro) combines price with [potency](/datasets/potency-europe/). For what a gram means for a worker, see the [minimum wage](/datasets/minimum-wage/) and the chart of [work for a gram](/charts/#work-for-a-gram).
