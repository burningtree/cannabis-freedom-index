The [European School Survey Project on Alcohol and Other Drugs](https://www.espad.org/espad-report-2024) has asked 15- and 16-year-olds the same questions every four or five years since 1995, which makes it the longest comparable series of cannabis use in Europe.[@1] In 2024, across 37 countries, the median is 11% who have ever tried cannabis. [Czechia](/country/cz/) is highest, at 24%, with [Liechtenstein](/country/li/) and [Slovakia](/country/sk/) next; [Moldova](/country/md/), [Georgia](/country/ge/) and [Romania](/country/ro/) are lowest, under 5%.

Germany's figures are for Bavaria only.[@1] The [chart of teenage trends](/charts/#teen-trends) follows each country since 1995, wave by wave. Compare with [how easy students say it is to get](/datasets/espad-availability/) and with [use at 15 and 16](/datasets/youth-use/) outside Europe.
