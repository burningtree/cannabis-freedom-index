Some countries punish people who use cannabis; others send them to treatment, and some do both. The [European Union Drugs Agency](https://www.euda.europa.eu/data/stats2026/tdi_en) reports how many people entered drug treatment in a year and how many came mainly for cannabis.[@1] In 2024 cannabis was the main reason for 66% of those entering treatment in [Hungary](/country/hu/), 52% in [Germany](/country/de/) and 45% in [France](/country/fr/) in 2023, and for about a tenth in [Estonia](/country/ee/), [Bulgaria](/country/bg/) and [Slovenia](/country/si/).

Some people come of their own accord, others are sent by a court or the police, and countries count treatment differently.[@1] A high share can reflect courts sending people to treatment as much as harm from cannabis. The chart of [punish or treat](/charts/#punish-or-treat) sets treatment beside [offences](/datasets/cannabis-offences/); each profile's [beyond the penalty](/context/consequences/) section explains what diversion means there.
