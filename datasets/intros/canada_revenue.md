A legal market pays the state. [Statistics Canada](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=1010016501) reports what Canada's governments take from the legal cannabis trade each fiscal year: the profit of the provincial cannabis authorities, federal and provincial excise taxes, sales taxes and other revenue.[@1] The series runs from 2021/22 to 2024/25, the years after the [Cannabis Act of 2018](/history/canada-cannabis-act-2018/) and the [legal edibles of 2019](/history/canada-edibles-2019/).

The [chart of the state as merchant](/charts/#state-merchant) breaks the revenue down by source. Other countries that sell cannabis legally appear in each profile's [cannabis industry](/context/production/) section; for how far legal sales have pushed out illegal ones in Canada, see [where people get their cannabis](/datasets/canada-sourcing/).
