Faith shapes drug laws, and sometimes cannabis is part of the faith. The [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/religion/feature/religious-composition-by-country-2010-2020/) estimates each country's religious make-up from more than 2,700 censuses and surveys; this site takes the largest group.[@1] Of 191 countries, Christians are the largest group in 117, Muslims in 51, the religiously unaffiliated in 11, Buddhists in 8, Hindus in 3 and Jews in 1.

The [religion chart](/charts/#religion) compares the scores of countries by their largest faith. The atlas follows cannabis inside religions: the [Rastafari sacrament](/culture/traditions/rastafari-sacrament/), [Shivaratri at Pashupatinath](/culture/traditions/shivaratri-at-pashupatinath/), [Santa Maria in Santo Daime](/culture/traditions/santa-maria-in-santo-daime/) and the Sikh [shaheedi degh](/culture/traditions/shaheedi-degh/). Each profile's [culture and traditions](/context/culture/) section tells how cannabis sits in the country's life.
