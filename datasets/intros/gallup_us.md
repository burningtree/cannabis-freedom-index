No country has asked the question for longer. Since 1969 [Gallup](https://news.gallup.com/poll/514007/grassroots-support-legalizing-marijuana-hits-record.aspx) has asked Americans whether the use of marijuana should be made legal, and the yes answers rose from 12% that year to a record 70% in 2023.[@1] The series spans the years of [NORML's founding in 1970](/history/us-norml-founded-1970/), the decriminalisation wave of the 1970s, the [first legal shops in Colorado in 2014](/history/us-colorado-first-shops-2014/) and the federal move toward [Schedule III](/history/us-hhs-schedule-iii-2023/).

The [opinion gap chart](/charts/#opinion-gap) sets public opinion against what the law allowed in each year. For moral views in 25 countries at once, see Pew's [is using marijuana morally wrong?](/datasets/pew-marijuana/); the [United States profile](/country/us/) has the history state by state.
