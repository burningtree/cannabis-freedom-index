The index on this site scores one freedom; the [Human Freedom Index](https://www.cato.org/human-freedom-index/2025) of the Cato and Fraser Institutes scores freedom in general, from 0 to 10, from 87 indicators of the rule of law, movement, religion, expression, association, trade and regulation.[@1] For 2023 it ranks [Switzerland](/country/ch/), [Denmark](/country/dk/) and [New Zealand](/country/nz/) highest and [Syria](/country/sy/), [Iran](/country/ir/) and [Yemen](/country/ye/) lowest, across 165 jurisdictions; the median is about 6.9.

The two measure different things, and a country can rank high for freedom in general while punishing cannabis hard. The [freedom chart](/charts/#freedom) sets the Human Freedom Index against this site's score country by country. None of it enters the score, which is built only from [six parts of cannabis law](/map/possession/). For a measure built by other experts, see [civil liberties](/datasets/civil-liberties/).
