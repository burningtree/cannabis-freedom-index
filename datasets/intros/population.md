Population is the background to every other figure: it turns counts into rates and shares into numbers of people. The site takes it from [Our World in Data](https://ourworldindata.org/population-sources) and the World Bank, a figure every ten years from 1900 and the latest one, for all 220 places.[@1] [India](/country/in/) and [China](/country/cn/) each have over 1.4 billion people; [Tuvalu](/country/tv/) and [Nauru](/country/nr/) about ten thousand.

It weights the population-weighted world average, shown beside the plain average of countries, and sorts the rankings by size.[@1] It also turns survey shares into the [number of cannabis users](/datasets/cannabis-use/), drawn in the [treemap of users](/charts/#users-treemap), and gives rates per person for [offences](/datasets/cannabis-offences/), [shops](/datasets/osm-shops/) and [research](/datasets/research-papers/).
