A street price means little without a wage beside it. [Eurostat](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/earn_mw_cur/default/table) publishes the national gross minimum wage for a month of full-time work, in euros, as it stood each January.[@1] In 2026 it is highest in [Luxembourg](/country/lu/), at about €2,700, [Ireland](/country/ie/) and [Germany](/country/de/), and lowest in [Ukraine](/country/ua/), [Moldova](/country/md/) and [Albania](/country/al/); 31 countries have a figure.

Countries without a national minimum wage, such as Austria, Denmark, Finland, Italy and Sweden, are left out.[@1] The chart of [work for a gram](/charts/#work-for-a-gram) divides the [street price of cannabis](/datasets/prices-europe/) by the wage, to show how many minutes of work a gram costs; a fine for possession, given in each profile's penalties, can be read the same way.
