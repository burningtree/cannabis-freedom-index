News coverage shows where cannabis is argued over. [The GDELT Project](https://www.gdeltproject.org/) monitors news worldwide; this site counts the cannabis and marijuana articles from each country's outlets as a share of all the news GDELT follows.[@1] The [United States](/country/us/) accounts for by far the most, followed by the [United Kingdom](/country/gb/) and [Mexico](/country/mx/); 147 countries have a figure.

The share is of all news worldwide, not of the country's own news, so large media markets dominate, and outlet coverage is uneven.[@1] The chart of [cannabis in the news](/charts/#in-the-news) follows the series since 2017; compare with what readers look up in [views of the cannabis article on Wikipedia](/datasets/wikipedia-views/).
