Five measures from the [World Bank's development indicators](https://databank.worldbank.org/source/world-development-indicators) describe how a country lives: the share of people using the internet, living in towns and cities and aged 65 or over; life expectancy at birth; and enrolment in higher education.[@1] The map shows the internet: nearly everyone is online in [Bahrain](/country/bh/), [Kuwait](/country/kw/) and [Saudi Arabia](/country/sa/), and under one in ten in [South Sudan](/country/ss/) and [Burundi](/country/bi/); the median country is at about 81%.

Higher-education enrolment can pass 100% where many older students enrol.[@1] The chart of [what goes with it](/charts/#what-goes-with-it) sets these, along with [income per person](/datasets/gdp/), beside each country's score; how each law came about is in the [history](/history/).
