Surveys depend on what people admit; sewage does not. The [SCORE group and the European Union Drugs Agency](https://www.euda.europa.eu/data/repository/drugs-municipal-wastewater-europe-source-data-2025_en) measure THC-COOH, which the body makes from cannabis, in the wastewater of cities over one week a year, in milligrams a day per 1,000 people.[@1] The highest recent loads are in the [United States](/country/us/) and [Canada](/country/ca/), around 600, followed by the [Netherlands](/country/nl/) at about 200; the median of 33 countries is about 72.

The load depends on how much and how often people use, so it is not a count of users, and one city may stand for a whole country.[@1] It is still the one measure that cannot be shaped by what people are willing to say about an illegal drug. The [wastewater chart](/charts/#wastewater) follows cities since 2011; compare with the survey figures in [cannabis use](/datasets/cannabis-use/).
