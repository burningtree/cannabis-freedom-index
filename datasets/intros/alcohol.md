Alcohol is the legal drug every cannabis law is measured against. The [World Health Organization](https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/total-alcohol-consumption-per-capita-litres-of-pure-alcohol) estimates how many litres of pure alcohol each person aged 15 or over drinks in a year, sold legally or not.[@1] In 2020 [Romania](/country/ro/), [Georgia](/country/ge/) and [Latvia](/country/lv/) drank the most, 13 to 17 litres; in [Saudi Arabia](/country/sa/), [Somalia](/country/so/) and [Mauritania](/country/mr/) almost none. The median country drinks about 4 litres.

The [alcohol chart](/charts/#alcohol) sets drinking beside each country's cannabis score, and shows where one drug is part of daily life while the other is a crime, or the other way round. Where cannabis is drunk at a festival, the atlas tells of it, as in [bhang at Holi](/culture/traditions/bhang-at-holi/); for the most common cannabis figure, see [cannabis use](/datasets/cannabis-use/).
