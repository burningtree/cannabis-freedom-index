Trials are how cannabis medicines are tested on patients. [ClinicalTrials.gov](https://clinicaltrials.gov/), run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine, registers studies around the world; this site counts those with a cannabis or cannabinoid treatment that have actually started, once for each country with a site.[@1] The [United States](/country/us/) has the most, about 680, then [Canada](/country/ca/) and [France](/country/fr/); 52 countries have at least one.

The register does not cover every trial in the world, and the current year is incomplete.[@1] The [trials chart](/charts/#trials) follows the count since 2000. How patients get cannabis today is in each profile's [medical cannabis](/context/medical/) section; for research beyond patients, see [research on cannabis](/datasets/research-papers/) and the [research](/context/research/) sections.
