What people look up shows what they are curious about. The [Wikimedia Foundation](https://doc.wikimedia.org/generated-data-platform/aqs/analytics-api/) publishes how often each article is loaded; this site follows the article on cannabis as a drug in 26 language editions, every month since July 2015.[@1] Each language is linked to the country where most of its readers live; the Japanese, German and Italian editions rank highest.

A language is not a country: German Wikipedia is read in Austria and Switzerland too, and page loads are not unique readers.[@1] The [chart of who looked it up](/charts/#looked-it-up) follows each edition month by month, to be read beside changes in the law such as [Germany's Cannabis Act in 2024](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/). For coverage in the news rather than readers' curiosity, see [cannabis in the news](/datasets/news/); for the words themselves, the atlas entry on [cannabis](/culture/language/cannabis/).
