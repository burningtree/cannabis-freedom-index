Where cannabis is sold openly, shops appear on the map. In [OpenStreetMap](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Tag:shop%3Dcannabis), the open map of the world, volunteers tag dispensaries, licensed shops, coffeeshops and CBD shops alike as cannabis shops.[@1] In October 2026 the [United States](/country/us/) had about 4,700 tagged, [Canada](/country/ca/) about 1,600 and [France](/country/fr/) about 700; 63 countries have at least one.

The count says as much about mappers as about shops: [Thailand](/country/th/), which had thousands of shops after [its dispensaries opened in 2022](/history/thailand-dispensaries-open-2022/), has only a few hundred tagged.[@1] Each count is a dated edition, so the trend builds up as it is repeated. What may be sold, and by whom, is in each profile's [cannabis industry](/context/production/) and [recreational use](/context/recreational/) sections; the atlas explains the [dispensary](/culture/language/dispensary/) and [the coffeeshop](/culture/traditions/the-coffeeshop/).
