Where cannabis may be studied, papers follow. [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/), an open catalogue of scholarly works, lets this site count the papers whose title or abstract mentions cannabis, marijuana or a cannabinoid, by the countries their authors work in, every year since 1950.[@1] Per million people, small places with a single active group can rank highest, so the map is best read for large countries and for change over time.

Modern cannabis science began when [Raphael Mechoulam](/culture/people/raphael-mechoulam/) and Yehiel Gaoni [isolated THC in 1964](/history/israel-mechoulam-thc-1964/), and [Lumír Hanuš](/culture/people/lumir-hanus/) later helped find the body's own cannabinoids. A paper with authors in several countries counts for each.[@1] Each profile's [research](/context/research/) section says who may study cannabis there; for medicines tested on patients, see [clinical trials](/datasets/clinical-trials/).
