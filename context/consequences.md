A cannabis conviction often costs more than its sentence. A criminal record can close jobs, licences, travel and housing; some countries keep separate drug registers, take away driving licences or deport foreigners. These sections describe what follows a person beyond the punishment itself, in each country.

Some governments have begun to undo it. In 2022 the US president [pardoned everyone convicted of simple possession](/history/us-biden-pardons-2022/) under federal law, and [South Africa's](/country/za/) [Cannabis for Private Purposes Act](/history/south-africa-private-purposes-act-2024/) provides for wiping old convictions for use and possession once it takes effect. Clearing records is known in many places as [expungement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expungement).[@1]
