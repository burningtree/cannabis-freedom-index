Countries convict drivers who have used cannabis in very different ways. Some must prove the driver was impaired; some set a fixed limit of THC in the blood; some punish any detectable trace. [Germany](/country/de/), for example, [set a limit of 3.5 nanograms per millilitre](/history/germany-driving-limit-2024/) of blood serum in August 2024, with no cannabis at all for novice drivers. An overview is in [drug-impaired driving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drug-impaired_driving).[@1]

These sections say what the law needs to convict, and the map sorts countries from an impairment test to a zero-tolerance rule. The difference matters most where cannabis is legal or prescribed, because a driver can be within the drug law and still break the driving rule.
