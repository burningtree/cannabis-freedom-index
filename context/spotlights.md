Some things about cannabis in a country fit no other heading: a famous market street, an unusual institution, a place where the law and the custom part ways. Spotlights collect them, one short story each. They are the places and practices the scores cannot show, such as Copenhagen's [Pusher Street](/culture/traditions/pusher-street/) or India's [licensed bhang shops](/culture/traditions/licensed-bhang-shops/).

Few countries have spotlights yet, and the map shows which. For the legal picture behind them, see each country's profile, or the overview of [the legality of cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legality_of_cannabis) around the world.[@1]
