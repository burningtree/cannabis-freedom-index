Wherever cannabis was grown for generations, farmers kept local plants adapted to their land, known as [landraces](/culture/language/landrace/). Prohibition, eradication and the spread of imported hybrids have threatened many of them. In [Morocco's](/country/ma/) Rif the old kif plant was largely replaced by [high-yielding hybrids](/culture/traditions/rif-hashish-craft/), until the regulator [authorised the local beldiya variety](/history/morocco-beldiya-authorised-2024/) for the legal programme in 2024. A general account of the idea is in [landrace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landrace).[@1]

These sections describe each country's local cannabis, the people who keep it, and what bans and new laws did to it. They are written only where sources say something specific about the country's plants.
