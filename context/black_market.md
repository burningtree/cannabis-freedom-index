In most of the world cannabis is bought illegally, and the illegal market is what most users actually meet. These sections describe its size where estimates exist, where the cannabis comes from, what it costs and how openly it is sold. The UN drugs office's [World Drug Report](https://www.unodc.org/unodc/en/data-and-analysis/world-drug-report-2026-annex.html) publishes the country figures on use and seizures that much of this rests on.[@1]

Some supply routes are old and famous. [Morocco's](/country/ma/) Rif has supplied hashish to Europe since the 1960s, and a survey [in 2003](/history/morocco-cultivation-survey-2003/) found 134,000 hectares under cannabis; the [craft and its hybrids](/culture/traditions/rif-hashish-craft/) have their own entry. For the wider picture see [the illegal drug trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illegal_drug_trade).
