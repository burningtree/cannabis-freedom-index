Adult use is legal in only a handful of countries, and each did it differently. [Uruguay](/country/uy/) was [first, in 2013](/history/uruguay-legalisation-2013/), putting the whole chain under the state; [Canada](/country/ca/) [followed in 2018](/history/canada-cannabis-act-2018/) with licensed shops; [Malta](/country/mt/) chose [non-profit associations in 2021](/history/malta-legalisation-2021/); [Germany](/country/de/) [legalised possession and home growing in 2024](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/) without shops. An overview is in [the legality of cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legality_of_cannabis).

Elsewhere use is tolerated or decriminalised rather than legal. In the [Netherlands](/country/nl/) selling small amounts in a [coffeeshop](/culture/traditions/the-coffeeshop/) is still an offence that prosecutors do not pursue, within rules on nuisance, minors, advertising and a limit of 5 grams per sale.[@1] These sections describe how adults actually get cannabis in each country, legally or not.
