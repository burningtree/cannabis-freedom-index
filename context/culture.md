Cannabis laws were written over older customs: [bhang at Holi](/culture/traditions/bhang-at-holi/), the [Rastafari sacrament](/culture/traditions/rastafari-sacrament/), Morocco's [kif pipe](/culture/traditions/kif-and-the-sebsi/) and [maâjoun](/culture/traditions/maajoun/), the words people have for the plant from [ganja](/culture/language/ganja/) to [rongony](/culture/language/rongony/). These sections tell how cannabis sits in each country's life: its customs, faiths, words, music and festivals, and what prohibition did to them. See also [cannabis culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_culture).[@1]

The [cultural atlas](/culture/) holds the entries themselves, and each section links to those that belong to the country. Where the law made an exception for a custom, as India did for [bhang in 1961](/history/india-bhang-exemption-1961/), the section says so.
