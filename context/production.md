A legal cannabis industry exists where the state licenses people to grow, process and sell for profit. [Canada](/country/ca/) built a national market of licensed producers and shops [in 2018](/history/canada-cannabis-act-2018/); [Uruguay](/country/uy/) kept production under state licence, and its [pharmacies began selling in 2017](/history/uruguay-pharmacy-sales-2017/). [Morocco](/country/ma/) [licensed medical and industrial cannabis in 2021](/history/morocco-law-13-21-2021/), with growers in cooperatives in the [Rif](/culture/traditions/rif-hashish-craft/). See also [the cannabis industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_industry).[@1]

Most countries allow less: licensed production for medicine only, or for hemp only, or nothing at all. These sections say who may produce, on what terms and for whom, and the map shades countries from an open adult market to no legal production.
