Legal cannabis is often for residents only. [Uruguay](/country/uy/) [opened its three routes](/history/uruguay-legalisation-2013/) only to registered residents, and [Germany](/country/de/) requires [six months' residence](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/) to grow at home or join an association. In the [Netherlands](/country/nl/), the country best known for [cannabis tourism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_tourism), only residents have been permitted in coffee shops since 1 January 2013, though municipalities decide how strictly to enforce it.[@1] The rule was [first enforced in the south in 2012](/history/netherlands-residents-only-2012/).

These sections say whether someone who does not live in a country can get cannabis legally, and what happens to a foreigner who is caught. Taking cannabis across a border is a separate, heavier offence almost everywhere, including between two places where it is legal.
