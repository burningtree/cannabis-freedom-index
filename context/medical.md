Medical cannabis came back into law through the side door of state and national exceptions. California's [Proposition 215](/history/us-california-medical-1996/) let patients with a doctor's recommendation possess and grow it in 1996; [Germany](/country/de/) let doctors [prescribe flower and extracts in 2017](/history/germany-medical-2017/), paid for by health insurers after approval. In 2020 the UN [took cannabis out of its strictest schedule](/history/un-cannabis-rescheduled-2020/) for drugs with little medical value, without changing the duty to prohibit other use. See also [medical cannabis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_cannabis).[@1]

A medical law on paper is not the same as access. These sections say who qualifies, who may prescribe, what forms are sold and what they cost, and the map counts a programme as established only where patients are actually treated, not where research or export licences alone exist.
