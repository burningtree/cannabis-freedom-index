A cannabis club is a non-profit association whose members grow cannabis together and share the harvest, with no shop and no profit. The model grew up in [Spain](/country/es/), where the [social clubs](/culture/traditions/cannabis-social-clubs/) operate in a grey zone: the Supreme Court [convicted organisers in 2015](/history/spain-supreme-court-clubs-2015/), leaving the clubs tolerated but unprotected. An overview is in [cannabis social club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cannabis_social_club).[@1]

Some countries have since written clubs into law. [Uruguay](/country/uy/) made them one of the [three legal routes in 2013](/history/uruguay-legalisation-2013/), [Malta](/country/mt/) made [licensed associations](/history/malta-legalisation-2021/) the only supply besides home growing, and [German associations](/history/germany-cultivation-associations-2024/) of up to 500 members became legal in July 2024.
