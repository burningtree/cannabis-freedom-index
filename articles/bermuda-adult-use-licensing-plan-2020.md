Bermuda's government announced a broader licensing proposal covering adult
use, personal and commercial cultivation, and retail sales.[@1]

Attorney-General Kathy Lynn Simmons said feedback on the earlier
medical-only proposal had questioned its complexity and asked for wider
reform.[@1]

The resulting licensing bill reached the governor in 2022, when the UK
instructed her not to assent to it as drafted.[@2]
