From 7 March 2026, Slovenian organisations seeking to produce, trade or hold cannabis for science had detailed rules for their permit applications.[@1] Applicants had to explain the research, identify their equipment and show how they would trace material, secure premises and destroy waste.[@1] The rules implemented the scientific route in the [2025 cannabis Act](/history/slovenia-medical-law-2025/).[@2]

## Research required an institutional permit

The Act requires permission even when an organisation only holds cannabis for chemical or biological testing, rather than growing or manufacturing it.[@2] The Health Ministry issues the scientific permission to eligible legal entities, and the law allows work with any cannabis variety under that permission.[@2]

Eligible applicants must meet registration and professional requirements, including the relevant medicine-manufacturing status or registration as a research and development provider.[@2] The application must identify the quantities, premises and duration of the research project or teaching process.[@2] Permission lasts for that project or process, up to five years, with scientifically justified extensions possible.[@2]

## The application had to explain the science

The implementing rules require a scientific account of the purpose, methods and goals, together with the responsible researchers’ scientific references.[@1] Applicants must list the necessary equipment, show that they possess it and declare compliance with good control laboratory practice.[@1]

Traceability must start with seed or planting material and continue through production, trade and study.[@1] Where growing is involved, applicants must also demonstrate compliance with good agricultural and collection practice for medicinal plants and supply information identifying the variety.[@1]

## Security and disposal were part of the project

A security service must prepare a risk assessment and protection plan, including records of employees’ access to the premises.[@1] Applicants also need an agreement with a business registered to destroy hazardous substances and must specify incineration or another suitable destruction method.[@1]

The Act separately requires disposal of remaining scientific cannabis after permission expires or is withdrawn, with documentation sent to the ministry.[@2] This makes the end of a project part of the controlled process, alongside acquiring and studying its material.[@2]

The [Slovenia profile](/country/si/) records this licensed scientific route separately from patient prescribing and the still-proposed [personal-use bill](/history/slovenia-personal-use-bill-2025/).[@1,2]
