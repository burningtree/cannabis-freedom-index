Native Nations SXM presented its proposed cannabis framework to a
parliamentary committee, including an independent oversight commission,
licensing and product-safety controls.[@1,2]

Its promoters argued that regulation would support local economic
development and protect health and young people.[@2]

Parliament later announced a continuation of the meeting after the March
hearing was adjourned.[@3]
