The Misuse of Drugs Act took effect five years after its enactment: the
Supreme Court later identified 1 July 1995 as the commencement date.[@1]

That date mattered in R v Francois, a 2000 trial over 166.4 g of resin in
individually wrapped portions, where the court applied the replacement law's
cannabis possession and trafficking rules to conduct charged under it.[@1]

Commencement of a replacement prohibition — the complete original sentencing
schedules and intervening amendments are needed before assigning a historical
score change.[@1]
