In May 2023 Romanian deputies proposed replacing criminal penalties for possessing up to three grams of cannabis for personal use with fines of 1,000–3,000 lei.[@1] The REPER-sponsored bill would have spent the fine revenue on prevention and support services, and exempted medically prescribed cannabis from the personal-use penalties.[@1]

## Why three grams

The sponsors' argument was practical: prosecution damaged users' records and obstructed treatment.[@1] A fine keeps the offence on the books without the criminal record that follows a user into job applications and background checks.

The bill went further than a tariff change. Earmarking fine revenue for prevention and support tied enforcement money to the services the sponsors said users needed.[@1] And the medical exemption acknowledged that prescribed patients should never have sat inside a personal-use penalty at all.[@1]

## What the Senate did

The proposal drew negative evaluations from all reviewing committees and the Senate rejected it.[@2] The 1,000–3,000 lei fines never entered the statute book, and possession of any amount stayed criminal under Law 143/2000.[@2]

The idea did not die with the vote. The "3grame" initiative became the reference point for every later decriminalisation argument in Romania.[@1] The 2023 proposal is traced in [Romania's](/country/ro/) history as the first parliamentary attempt to draw a misdemeanour line under cannabis possession.
