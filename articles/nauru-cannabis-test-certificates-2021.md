Appointed officers could identify seized cannabis with specified
colour-change tests and submit certificates that courts had to accept
unless materially disproved.[@1]

The regulations took effect on 27 August 2021 and replaced the earlier 2016
drug-analysis rules, while leaving existing proceedings under those
rules.[@1]

A 2024 one-gram cannabis prosecution later relied on a police test using
the KN reagent system.[@2]
