San Marino’s June 2021 medical cannabis law created an authorisation route for greenhouse cultivation, processing and trade for medicinal or therapeutic purposes. The Health Authority became the national cannabis control agency, responsible for permissions, production estimates and oversight.[@1]

## Prescribed treatment had a separate legal footing

The [1997 drug law](/history/san-marino-drugs-law-1997/) already exempted personal use conforming to a prescription issued or recognised by the Social Security Institute. It otherwise punished unauthorised possession and use, including personal possession without a supply purpose.[@2]

The 2021 law preserved the existing criminal rules while protecting activities carried out within its authorisation system and the recognised-prescription exception.[@1] That distinction left two routes in law: regulated medicinal activity and criminal liability for unauthorised conduct.[@1,2]

## Businesses needed approval and production controls

Companies wishing to cultivate, process or trade had to obtain approval through the cannabis agency and Congress of State. The law required security planning and allowed specific conditions to protect health and public order.[@1]

The agency also took responsibility for production estimates under the international drug-control system. Its role connected individual business permissions with national reporting and supply controls.[@1]

An [amendment in February 2023](/history/san-marino-medical-definition-revised-2023/) revised the business authorisation process and the definition of cannabis. The replacement definition covered seeds, flowers, resin and other plant parts without retaining the original wording about THC content above 0.2%.[@3]

## The licensing rules specified how crops could be grown

The [March 2023 licensing decree](/history/san-marino-cannabis-licensing-decree-2023/) required detailed applications identifying premises, activities, staff and security arrangements.[@4] Cultivation had to use indoor technological greenhouses and the specified controlled growing methods.[@4]

The rules also required certified seeds or cuttings from certified seeds, with records showing origin and traceability. Processing applications needed pharmaceutical manufacturing arrangements and compliance documentation.[@4] A business permission therefore depended on meeting requirements for premises, staff, security and product handling.[@4]

## Patients reached the system through state pharmacies

The February amendment reserved medical cannabis retail to state pharmacies against a prescription, under rules drawn up by the Social Security Institute.[@3] The Health Authority’s FAQ likewise identifies state pharmacies as the exclusive retail route for medicines and pharmaceutical-grade cannabis raw material.[@5]

The same FAQ explains that businesses submit their intended annual production quantities to the agency, which provides the national estimate to the International Narcotics Control Board.[@5] The [San Marino profile](/country/sm/) follows these connected permissions for growers, processors, pharmacies and patients, alongside the penalties for unauthorised cannabis.[@1,2,5]
