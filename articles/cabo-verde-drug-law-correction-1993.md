The official gazette republished Law 78/IV/93 because the July version had
been incomplete and inaccurate.[@1]

The corrected article 9 uses seven days for the relevant user-trafficker
quantity test, where the July printing used five; this is separate from
article 20's small personal-consumption offence.[@1,2]

The correction is not treated as the first cannabis restriction.[@1]
