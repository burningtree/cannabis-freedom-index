Cabo Verde's health regulator ERIS issued a three-page clarification on cannabinoid cosmetics
on 6 May 2024. It reiterated the prohibition described in its regulatory framework and warned
that an ingredient inventory should not be read as a list of permitted ingredients.

The circular identifies cannabis and resin as controlled under Table II-A of Decree-Law 92/92.
It addresses cannabis-related preparations and CBD or other cannabinoid ingredients in cosmetics,
and places responsibility on operators to comply with the applicable restrictions.

Its scope matters: this is cosmetics guidance. It does not independently establish the legal
position of every food, seed or medicine containing a cannabis-related ingredient, nor does it
announce a general cannabis patient programme. The history entry records a regulatory clarification
rather than a newly established blanket ban on every use of CBD.
