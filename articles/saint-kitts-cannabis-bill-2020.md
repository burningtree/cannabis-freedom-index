The Cannabis Bill 2020 provided for a Medicinal Cannabis Authority to
regulate and license the cultivation, supply, possession, production and
use of medicinal cannabis.[@1]

It became the Cannabis Act, No. 8 of 2020, which a 2023 bill proposed to
amend.[@1]
