Kosovo’s replacement criminal code took effect on 1 January 2013 with a lower penalty for first-time offenders possessing less than three grams of cannabis for personal use: a fine or up to one year in prison. Ordinary unauthorised possession carried both a fine and one to three years.[@1]

## A separate rule for possession

The distinction put the person’s previous offending and the amount held at the centre of the penalty. A first offence below three grams qualified for the lower tier. The ordinary possession offence used a different combination of punishments: a fine together with a prison term.[@1]

Both tiers remained criminal offences, and the code required confiscation of the drugs. A person holding a small amount could therefore still face a criminal case, even where the court could choose a fine under the special first-offence rule.[@1]

## Earlier laws targeted the drug trade

The [provisional code in force from 2004](/history/kosovo-provisional-drug-code-2004/) punished buying or holding drugs with intent to sell or distribute, and separately punished supplying them. Its production offence covered cultivation and preparation intended for sale, with a fine and one to ten years in prison.[@2]

Kosovo then adopted its [2008 drug law](/history/kosovo-narcotics-law-2008/), placing cannabis and its resin, extracts and tinctures in a table restricted to scientific research, education and police purposes. The ordinary medical-treatment permissions applied to other tables.[@3]

These rules gave the replacement code a wider background: cannabis was already controlled, and suppliers and producers already faced substantial prison exposure.[@2,3]

## Growing and supplying faced longer terms

The new code separately named cultivation of cannabis plants for producing drugs, punishable by one to ten years in prison. Plants, equipment and cultivation materials were to be confiscated.[@1]

Buying or possessing drugs with intent to sell or distribute carried a fine and two to eight years. Distribution and sale carried a fine and two to twelve years. Those offences turned on the supply activity, while the lower possession tier addressed a first-time offender holding a small amount.[@1]

## The three-gram distinction survived

The [code that took effect in April 2019](/history/kosovo-criminal-code-2019/) retained the same ordinary possession range and the lower tier for a first offence below three grams of cannabis or hashish for personal use. It also retained the separate cannabis-growing offence.[@4]

That continuity matters to anyone tracing the rule: the special small-possession penalty was already part of the earlier code. The [Kosovo profile](/country/xk/) explains the conditions and the continuing prison exposure.
