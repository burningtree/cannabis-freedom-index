One deleted paragraph turned fewer than two thousand recorded possession offences a
year into more than thirty thousand.

## The paragraph

The [1997 drug act](/history/poland-drug-law-1997/) made possession an offence with up
to three years in prison, but its article 48(4) said that a person holding a small
amount for their own use was not punished. A little over six months after the act took
effect, a bill appeared to delete that paragraph.

## The argument

The police said the exemption protected dealers: a seller carrying one dose could
claim it was his own, lose it and walk away, sometimes a dozen times over. The
minister Barbara Labuda, who called herself a supporter of liberal solutions,
backed the change with regret, saying dealers had used the rule "practically with
impunity" in schools, housing estates and discos.

Doctors and therapists from detox wards warned that the change would fall mainly on
their patients, who are easier to catch than dealers. A senior police officer answered
that the law was not meant to punish addicts.

The Sejm passed the amendment on 26 October 2000. It took effect on 12 December.

## What followed

| Year | Recorded possession offences |
|---|---|
| 1999 | 1,896 |
| 2007 | 31,260 |
| 2012 | 18,441 |

A 2010 study by the Institute of Public Affairs found that in 2008 possession made up
53 per cent of charges under the drug act and trafficking 24 per cent; 65 per cent of
charges involved marijuana. Most cases ended quickly, almost half without a trial.
Police could rarely link a possession arrest to a major trafficker, and enforcing the
article cost the state at least 80 million zloty a year. A study of Kraków court files
found that 79 per cent of marijuana cases involved less than three grams.

## Attempts to undo it

In 2005 the health minister, Marek Balicki, proposed that small amounts again go
unpunished. The provision was dropped, and the [new act](/history/poland-drug-act-2005/)
passed without it. Since [December 2011](/history/poland-prosecutor-discretion-2011/)
a prosecutor may drop a small case; in 2012 that ended 2,307 of them. In
[2014](/history/poland-constitutional-tribunal-2014/) the Constitutional Tribunal held
the ban constitutional. A [bill](/history/poland-decriminalisation-bill-2026/) to
exempt 15 grams has waited in the Sejm since March 2026.

In the index the amendment cut possession from 3 to 1, enforcement from 4 to 1 and use
from 2 to 1 for [Poland](/country/pl/).
