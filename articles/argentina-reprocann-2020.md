On 12 November 2020 Argentina published Decree 883/2020, a new regulation of its medical cannabis law, Law 27.350. Its central change was to let patients grow their own.

## The registry

The decree creates REPROCANN, a national register of patients who obtain cannabis for medicinal, therapeutic or palliative use through "controlled cultivation".

- A patient may register directly, or through a family member, a third person or an authorised civil organisation.
- Registration needs a medical indication and signed informed consent.
- What it gives is an authorisation to cultivate.

The decree sets no limit on the number of plants. It allows the provinces that adhere to the law to issue their own authorisations and report them to the national health ministry.

## Other routes to a product

| Route | Rule |
|---|---|
| Pharmacy | a patient with a medical indication may buy registered products or preparations made up by an authorised pharmacy |
| Import | registered products may be imported |
| Public supply | the state programme provides cannabis derivatives free to patients who have only public health cover; insurers must cover the rest |
| Public production | the agricultural and science institutes INTA and CONICET may grow for research and production |

The decree repeals the 2017 regulation.

## What it did not change

Law 23.737 still makes growing cannabis without authorisation a crime. The registry is the authorisation: a person inside it grows lawfully, a person outside it does not. Nothing in the decree touches possession or use by people who are not patients, which the courts had already stopped punishing in private since [2009](/history/argentina-arriola-2009/).

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Argentina](/country/ar/) from 20 to 26 with the [2020 decree](/history/argentina-reprocann-2020/). Cultivation rises from 1 to 3 and access from 0 to 1, because home growing became lawful for anyone a doctor will sign for, and a pharmacy route opened beside it.
