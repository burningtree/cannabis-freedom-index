An independent review prepared for Greenland compared prohibition,
decriminalisation, limited legal access and commercial cannabis
sales.[@1]

It examined health, criminal-justice and economic trade-offs, with a
strong focus on young people, prevention and treatment.[@1]

The health department commissioned the work after a parliamentary proposal
seeking a balanced assessment.[@1]
