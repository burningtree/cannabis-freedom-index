Law 168 regulated the import, manufacture and sale of narcotic drugs. It is
the earliest Dominican drug law recorded here.[@1]

Thirteen years later the preamble of Law 50-88 called it ineffective and
said it did not clearly classify drug offences or their penalties, and the
new law repealed and replaced it.[@1]

What Law 168 said about cannabis itself was not found.[@1]
