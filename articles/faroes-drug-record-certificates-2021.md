Criminal-register regulations took effect on 1 April 2021.[@1]

They include drug judgments in the private-certificate rules, with two
years for fines included in the decisions section and longer limits for
other decisions and imprisonment.[@1]

Certificate disclosure limits are separate from the retention of the
underlying record.[@1]
