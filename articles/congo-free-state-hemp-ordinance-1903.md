On 22 January 1903 the Congo Free State, the private colony of King Leopold II of Belgium, issued an ordinance-law headed "measures against the use of smoking hemp". A decree approved it on 1 March. It is seven articles long and it bans everything at once.

## The seven articles

| Article | What it says |
|---|---|
| 1 | Growing, selling, transporting and holding hemp for smoking are forbidden, and so is using it, by smoking or in any other way |
| 2 | For fifteen days, people may declare their hemp fields and hand in their hemp at the nearest post, and will be paid its value once it is destroyed |
| 3 | Offences bring a fine of 100 to 1,000 francs and penal servitude of fifteen days to one year, or one of the two; crops are destroyed, hemp is confiscated, and so is any instrument used for smoking it |
| 4 | Village chiefs are jointly liable for the fines and costs, unless they warned the authorities of the offences in their village |
| 5 | The authorities destroy illegal plantations at the offender's expense |
| 6 | Confiscated hemp is destroyed as laid down in an order of 8 May 1899 |
| 7 | The ordinance takes effect immediately |

## What stands out

Two features of the text stand out. The first is the buy-out: for two weeks the state offered to pay growers and holders for what they surrendered. The second is the collective liability of chiefs, which made a village chief answerable for offences he did not report.

The ordinance also shows that 1903 was not the first measure. Article 6 relies on an order of 8 May 1899 that already dealt with destroying confiscated hemp, so some form of seizure existed four years earlier. That order has not been read for this index.

Cannabis was by then well established in the Congo basin. Explorers had described the Bena Riamba, a movement among the Bashilenge of Kasai built around smoking it, in 1881.

## Why the index moved

The [1903 ordinance](/history/congo-free-state-hemp-ordinance-1903/) is the only scored step in the [Democratic Republic of the Congo's](/country/cd/) history, from the unregulated state to prohibition. It covered possession, cultivation, sale and use in a single text, which is why one step carries the whole fall. A 2021 measure authorised cannabis for medical and scientific purposes under licence without changing the position of people who use it. The country's present total is 9.
