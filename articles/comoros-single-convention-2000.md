The Comoros acceded to the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961 as
amended by the 1972 Protocol on 1 March 2000.[@1]

The convention lists cannabis and cannabis resin among its controlled
drugs and obliges its parties to limit their production, trade, possession
and use to medical and scientific purposes.[@3]

On the same day it acceded to the United Nations Convention against
Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances of 1988.[@2]
