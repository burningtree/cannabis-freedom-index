In 2022 Pakistan rewrote the penalty table for cannabis. The amendment replaced a shared drug-quantity scale with separate prison ranges for bhang, charas and hashish oil, and removed death as a possible sentence for cannabis trafficking. It did not make the law milder in every respect: small amounts received new minimum prison terms.[@1]

## One table becomes three

The Control of Narcotic Substances Act 1997 had graded cannabis by weight on a single scale. The 2022 amendment split it. Bhang, the herbal form, now runs from six months to three years for up to 999 g, and reaches a minimum of fourteen years and possible life imprisonment from 20 kg. Charas starts at ten months to five years for up to 499 g and reaches a minimum of twenty years and possible life from 10 kg. Hashish oil starts at fourteen months to seven years and reaches the same twenty-year floor at 5 kg.[@1]

The table also sets fines: up to 10,000 rupees for the smallest bhang band, up to 40,000 for the smallest charas and hashish-oil bands, and minimum fines rising to 800,000 rupees at the top of the charas scale.[@1]

## What changed for users and sellers

The old law allowed death for more than one kilogram of cannabis. The 2022 table replaced that with prison ranges and a possible life sentence, but it also made prison mandatory at the bottom of each scale. A person caught with a small amount of charas now faces at least ten months, where the earlier lowest band allowed up to two years and did not set a minimum.[@1]

The amendment therefore did two things at once: it removed the death penalty from cannabis cases and it narrowed the courts' discretion over small quantities. The [1997 Act entry](/history/pakistan-narcotics-act-1997/) records the earlier scale, and the [2022 entry](/history/pakistan-cannabis-penalty-tables-2022/) records the replacement.[@1]

## The rest of the death penalty

Cannabis had already left the federal death-penalty rows when the 2022 amendment passed. In August 2023 parliament removed death from the remaining federal drug offences, leaving life imprisonment as the maximum.[@2] The [2023 entry](/history/pakistan-drug-death-penalty-abolished-2023/) records that later step.
