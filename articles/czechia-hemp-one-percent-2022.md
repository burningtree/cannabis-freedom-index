Czechia redrew the legal boundary for technical hemp on 1 January 2022. The permitted THC threshold rose from 0.3% to 1%, and growers received a simpler regulatory route. The change concerned low-THC hemp, not ordinary adult-use cannabis.

## A wider margin for farmers

Under the previous rule, technical hemp could contain no more than 0.3% THC. The legal analysis published by epravo.cz explains that favourable growing conditions could push a crop above that level even when a farmer was trying to remain compliant. Raising the limit to 1% gave growers more room for natural variation.

The amendment also changed how compliance was demonstrated. Instead of having to prove the THC content of the plants, a grower could show that the seed came from a variety in the European Union's Common Catalogue of agricultural plant species.

## Cultivation without a permit

Technical-hemp cultivation no longer required official permission. That did not remove every obligation: anyone cultivating more than 100 square metres still had to report information specified by law to the local customs office, including the cultivated area and harvested quantity.

The relaxed rules applied to hemp used for industrial, food, cosmetic, technical or horticultural purposes. The legal source says no licence or controlled-substance handling permit was required for those activities, including cultivation, import and export of technical hemp.

## The line above 1%

Cannabis above the new threshold did not become generally lawful. Cannabis intended for therapeutic use remained subject to a separate licensed system, while high-THC personal cultivation still fell under the criminal and administrative framework then in force. A parallel part of the same amendment [opened medical cultivation](/history/czechia-medical-cultivation-opened-2022/) to qualified licence applicants.

That distinction is why the event should not be read as recreational legalisation. It changed which low-THC plants qualified for the technical-hemp regime; it did not create shops, social clubs or a right to supply psychoactive cannabis.

## What moved in the index

Nothing moved in the Cannabis Freedom Index. Its six segments track psychoactive cannabis possession, cultivation, enforcement, sharing, consumption and access. The hemp threshold is recorded as national legal context, while the later [2026 reform](/history/czechia-home-grow-2026/) is the event that changed personal-use scores for [Czechia](/country/cz/).
