Mongolia amended its Criminal Code on 10 January 2020, changing several parts of Article 20.7 on illicit narcotic and psychotropic substances. The annotations in the Mongolian consolidated code identify that date for the additions and revisions.

One consequence is easy to miss: an English translation still hosted by the legal-information service preserves an older treatment of organised drug offences. The two versions consequently describe different sentencing frameworks.

## Acquisition without a selling purpose

The amended first paragraph expressly includes unlawful acquisition of prohibited substances or their raw materials without a purpose of selling. It also lists preparation, storage and giving to another person.

The ordinary range remains one to five years of travel restriction or imprisonment. Adding an act to the offence and increasing the sentence are different changes; this annotation does not show a new higher ordinary possession range.

For cannabis, the provision makes clear that acquisition can fall within the criminal framework even without an intention to sell. It supplies no gram threshold below which that listed conduct becomes lawful.

## Organised criminal groups

The older English text places organised-group activity within the five-to-twelve-year aggravated range. The [current Mongolian provision](https://legalinfo.mn/mn/detail/11634) instead has a separate paragraph 4, added in January 2020, providing twelve to twenty years or life imprisonment for an organised criminal group.

This is a substantial change to the aggravated framework. Life imprisonment should nevertheless not be presented as the ordinary sentence for possessing cannabis for oneself: the organised-group classification is an additional legal circumstance.

The amendment also revised paragraph 3.3 to address cross-border conduct. Persistent activity and crossing the border appear in the five-to-twelve-year tier, separate from the organised-group provision.

## What did not become the same offence

Article 20.7(2) retains a two-to-eight-year range for listed conduct undertaken for sale. Its distinction from the non-sale paragraph remains important, including when describing giving a substance to another person.

Other activities retain their own provisions. Article 20.8 concerns premises supplied for drug use; Article 20.10 concerns cultivation. The organised-group sentence in Article 20.7 cannot simply be copied across to every cannabis-related offence.

## Reading amendment dates accurately

The [2015 adoption](/history/mongolia-criminal-code-2015/) established the replacement code, but a current consolidation incorporates amendments made afterwards. The separate organised-group paragraph dates from 2020, rather than from the original adoption.

The [Mongolia profile](/country/mn/) now follows the Mongolian text for these penalties. The higher organised-group range coexists with the ordinary non-sale provision; the amendment established no lawful personal possession, cultivation or supply route.
