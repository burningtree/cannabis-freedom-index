The commander of the National Police Forces, General Serge Hervé Ngoma,
reported that cannabis was the drug most often intercepted by the
police.[@1]

Seizures rose from 433 kg in 2024 to 975 kg in 2025, and the anti-drug
office OCLAD took part in operations accounting for nearly a tonne.[@1]

He presented the rise as a sign of growing use and trafficking.[@1]
