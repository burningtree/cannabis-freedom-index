On 7 April 2025 Congo's senators approved a drug-control proposal intended to
combat trafficking and strengthen public security.[@1]

Its sponsor, Yves Fortuné Moundele-Ngollo Ehourossia, linked illicit drugs to
organised crime, money laundering, corruption and violence.[@1] The debate came
more than twenty years after Congo approved accession to the UN drug
conventions, and the country had lacked a matching legal framework ever
since.[@1]

President Denis Sassou-N'Guesso signed the new drug law in August.[@1]
