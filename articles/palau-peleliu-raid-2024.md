Police seized 182 cannabis plants, $31,600 in cash, seeds, joints, scales
and a ledger in Peleliu; Anna Gotlob was charged on 4 July 2024.[@1]

Peleliu had been raided repeatedly since 2019, showing that local
cultivation persisted despite the crackdowns.[@1]
