In October 2005 Venezuela cut the prison term for criminal drug possession from four
to six years down to one to two.[@1] The twenty-gram cannabis ceiling stayed where it
was, while the consumer procedure kept assessing an individual dose from tolerance,
dependence and personal characteristics.[@1]

## A decade of cuts

The cut was the second in twelve years. The 1993 reform had already reduced possession
from six to ten years to four to six — explicitly so the sentence would fit inside the
parole system — and renamed "tenencia" as "posesión" to match the 1988 Vienna
Convention.[@2] The reform memorandum complains that courts had produced no consistent
doctrine on what counts as possession, and recounts a judge who refused detention in a
marijuana-seed case on the grounds that the seeds contained no THC — the reason the
reformed text covered plants that "reproduce" a controlled substance as well as ones
that "contain" it.[@2]

## What the 2005 law set

Article 34 punished unlawful possession — for purposes other than the law's
authorised activities and personal consumption — with one to two years.[@1] It kept a
catalogue of ceilings: two grams for cocaine and derivatives, twenty grams for
cannabis sativa found on or under the holder's control, with the judge deciding what
an average person's personal dose would be, ignoring purity and rejecting stockpiles
held "as provision".[@1]

Growing sat in article 33: six to ten years for sowing, cultivating, harvesting or
trafficking seeds, resins and plants — three to five for hired labourers.[@1]
Trafficking bands in article 31 ran eight to ten years, six to eight under a kilogram
of marijuana, and four to six for small distributors and body-pack carriers, with no
procedural benefits for any of them.[@1]

The [2010 drug law](/history/venezuela-drug-law-2010/) kept the one-to-two-year
possession term and the twenty-gram band, and later courts added flexibility around
the edges — [2014 alternatives](/history/venezuela-small-drug-cases-suspended-sentences-2014/)
for minor cases and a [2017 ruling](/history/venezuela-drug-suspension-bar-struck-2017/)
on suspended sentences. [Venezuela's profile](/country/ve/) reflects the scheme the
2005 reform shaped.
