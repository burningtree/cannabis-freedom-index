On 2 March 1982 the Philippine parliament of the Marcos years, the Batasang Pambansa, amended the Dangerous Drugs Act. Batas Pambansa 179 raised the sentences for possessing or using drugs, and it removed most of the distance the 1972 Act had kept between marijuana and the rest.

## The new sentences

| Possession or use of | 1972 Act | After 1982 |
|---|---|---|
| Marijuana | 6 months and 1 day to 6 years | 6 years and 1 day to 12 years, and a fine of 6,000 to 12,000 pesos |
| Other prohibited drugs | 6 years and 1 day to 12 years | 12 years and 1 day to 20 years |
| Regulated drugs without prescription | 6 months and 1 day to 4 years | 6 years and 1 day to 12 years |

Marijuana kept a lower range than heroin or cocaine, but its floor was now six years for any amount. Cultivation stayed at 14 years and 1 day to life, with the land or greenhouse confiscated, and the maximum applied where the plants stood on public land.

## Other changes

- **Definition.** Indian hemp now covers all forms and parts of the plant, including hashish, bhang and resin. Seeds had been added by decree in 1980.
- **Testing.** A person arrested under the Act must be tested at once if the officer has reason to think they are under the influence. A positive result is prima facie evidence of use.
- **Seized property.** Proceeds and instruments of an offence are forfeited to the state, and an officer who fails to account for seized drugs faces eight to twelve years.
- **Officials.** Police, soldiers and public employees found guilty receive the maximum penalty.
- **Aggravation.** Committing any crime under the influence of a dangerous drug becomes a qualifying aggravating circumstance.

The treatment route survived. A dependent who submits voluntarily is still exempt from liability for possession or use, and a first offender under 18 may be given probation.

## Why the index moved

The index moved the [Philippines](/country/ph/) from 2 to 0 with [Batas Pambansa 179](/history/philippines-penalties-raised-1982/). Enforcement fell from 1 to 0, because the one lower tier left in the law, for a person caught with a little marijuana, now began at six years.
