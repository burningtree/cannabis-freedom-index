In 2004 the scientific research department ran an epidemiological survey of
drug users in Brazzaville, Dolisie and Ouesso, reaching 239 people through
police, customs, prisons and psychiatric services.[@1]

Most were adults — 2.87% under 18, 27.69% aged 19–25, 64.92% aged 26–35 — with
first drug contact clustering around age 8.[@1] The government later cited the
survey while describing cannabis, cocaine and heroin as the main drugs
accessible in the country.[@1]

It used the evidence to discuss gaps in prevention, treatment and support for
vulnerable children and former combatants.[@1]
