The Lords' medical-cannabis report belongs in 1998. The existing page address retains
1997 for continuity, but the dated event has been corrected from official records.

The parliamentary catalogue says the report was ordered printed on 4 November 1998;
the publication announcement is dated 11 November. It says the inquiry began in April.
That chronology supersedes the 1997 reference in Suzanne Taylor's chapter.

The committee recommended urgent trials and transferring cannabis and resin to
Schedule 2 for named-patient prescribing. It also supported retaining recreational
prohibition. These were recommendations, not an enacted permission for patients.

Taylor's account remains useful for the differences between patient organisations:
the Alliance for Cannabis Therapeutics wanted interim herbal access, while the MS
Society prioritised trials. The [1997 conference](/history/uk-cannabis-clinical-trials-conference-1997/)
is now recorded separately. The [2001 follow-up](/history/uk-lords-medical-cannabis-followup-2001/)
continued the pressure for change.
