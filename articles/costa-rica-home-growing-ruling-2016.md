The Criminal Chamber declared inadmissible the prosecution's appeal against
the acquittal of a man who had repeatedly grown cannabis on his roof.[@1]

That left intact the lower courts' finding that, without proof the crop was
meant for trafficking or distribution, cultivation for personal use was not
enough for a conviction.[@1]

Home growing has been tolerated case by case ever since — no plant limit, no
affirmative right.[@1]
