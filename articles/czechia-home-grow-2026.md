Czechia did not pass a cannabis law. It passed a reform of its criminal code meant to
empty prisons, and cannabis rode in on it. That explains both how far the change went
and what it left out.

## Moving the line

Czech law has redrawn the line between tolerated and criminal several times.
Possession for one's own use stopped being an offence in
[1990](/history/czechoslovakia-decriminalisation-1990/); an amount "greater than
small" became a crime again in [1999](/history/czechia-recriminalisation-1999/); and
the courts settled on [10 g](/history/czechia-ten-grams-2014/) in 2014. Below the
line, possession brought a fine.

## The reform

The government's draft allowed 50 g at home. An amendment in the Chamber doubled the
figures, and on 30 May 2025 the bill passed by 142 votes of 159. In the same sitting
deputies rejected a regulated market. The Senate and President Pavel approved it in
July, and it took effect on 1 January 2026.

For adults aged 21 or over the law now runs in three bands.

| | Legal | Fine | Crime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plants | up to 3 | 4 or 5 | more than 5 |
| Dried cannabis at home | up to 100 g | 100 to 200 g | more than 200 g |
| Dried cannabis outside | up to 25 g | 25 to 50 g | more than 50 g |

Sentences handed down earlier for acts that are no longer crimes are not carried out.

## What it left out

There are no shops and no clubs. Growing or supplying for anyone else is a crime, and
people who grow together and divide the harvest are treated as co-perpetrators. The
driving limit did not move. The former drug coordinator Jindřich Vobořil called the
result "a compromise, not a final step".

Compared with its neighbours, Czechia allows more at home than
[Germany](/country/de/) or [Malta](/country/mt/) (50 g each) and asks for a higher
age, 21 against 18. Unlike them, and like [Luxembourg](/country/lu/), it has no
clubs.

## Already under pressure

In 2024 about 35% of people detained for drug crimes were held over cannabis, and
there were 10,369 administrative offences, almost all for small amounts. That was the
load the reform set out to lift.

On 8 June 2026 the new prime minister, Andrej Babiš, announced
[18 measures](/history/czechia-tightening-plan-2026/) in the other direction,
including a lower THC limit for hemp and a ban on CBD products. None had been adopted
when this was written.

The reform gives [Czechia](/country/cz/) the highest score in Europe on this index,
with 1 out of 10 for sharing and for access, because everything involving a second
person stayed a crime.
