From 5 December 2023 Rwandan judges gained room the old tariff tables never gave
them: where mitigating circumstances were accepted, life imprisonment could fall to
at least fifteen years, down from twenty-five.[@1]

Fixed prison terms of six months or more could fall to half the offence's minimum
sentence, fines to a quarter of the statutory minimum, and community-service
sentences could be suspended.[@1]

For cannabis the change softened the edges without moving the ban: use and
possession kept their one-to-two-year band with community service as the express
alternative, and Category I supply kept its life sentence on paper — now with a
judge able to cut it to fifteen years.[@1]
