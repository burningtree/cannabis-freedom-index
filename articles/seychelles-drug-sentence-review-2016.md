Prisoners sentenced under the old drug law could apply to a new tribunal to
review the time they still had to serve.[@1]

The judiciary's public notice set an August deadline for people already
convicted when the replacement law began, and a three-month application period
for people convicted later on old charges.[@1]

The tribunal could change a sentence while leaving the conviction intact,
considering rehabilitation, the new sentencing range and the risk of
reoffending — implementation of the sentence-review route in the 2016
replacement law.[@1]
