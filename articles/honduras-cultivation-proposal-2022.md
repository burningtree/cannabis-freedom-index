Salvador Nasralla proposed legalising cannabis cultivation, entirely for
export, to create jobs; he said it would not legalise medical or other use
at home.[@1]

No bill was filed.[@1]

Two deputies backed a debate, and a former deputy noted that a bill for
terminally ill patients filed in October 2020 had not advanced.[@1]
