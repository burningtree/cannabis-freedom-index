Tuvalu's report to the UN child-rights committee cited a 2006 Funafuti
survey in which 5% of respondents aged 15–24 reported having used
marijuana.[@1]

It also reported marijuana imports from Fiji and concerns about border
controls, while describing youth programmes addressing alcohol and
drugs.[@1]

Updating drug laws and coordinating prevention work were among its stated
priorities.[@1]
