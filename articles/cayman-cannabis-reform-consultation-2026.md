Cayman's Law Reform Commission opened consultation on replacing criminal
penalties for personal cannabis possession and use, asking about limits of
30 grams of dried cannabis, 150 grams of wet cannabis and four plants per
household.[@1,2]

It also asked whether old minor convictions should be cleared, arguing that
criminal records can limit people's economic opportunities.[@2]

The review followed the 2025 referendum and invited comments by 1 September
2026; in October the premier expected the final report and supporting
legislation in November or December.[@1,3]
