Peru's Penal Code of 1991 contains a two-sentence article on which the country's treatment of drug users still rests. Article 299, in its original wording, reads: whoever possesses a drug "in a personal dose for his own and immediate consumption is exempt from punishment".

## The original rule

The 1991 text named no quantity. It left the matter to the court: "To determine the personal dose, the judge shall take into account the weight-dose correlation, the purity and the seizure of the drug." A person found with cannabis therefore depended on a judge accepting that the amount was a personal dose.

## How the article changed

The consolidated code published by Congress records each version.

| Law | Date published | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Legislative Decree 635 | April 1991 | personal dose for immediate consumption exempt from punishment; the judge decides what a dose is |
| Law 28002 | 17 June 2003 | fixed amounts: 8 g of marijuana or 2 g of its derivatives, 5 g of cocaine base paste, 2 g of cocaine hydrochloride, 1 g of opium latex. Possession of two or more types of drug is excluded |
| Legislative Decree 982 | 22 July 2007 | adds 250 mg of ecstasy |
| Law 30681 | 17 November 2017 | adds that possessing cannabis for medicinal purposes is not punishable, in the amount needed by a registered patient |

From 2003 the heading of the article is "Posesión no punible", non-punishable possession, and the exemption no longer turns on a judge's assessment of dose and purity. The two-drug exclusion cuts the other way: someone carrying small amounts of two different substances loses the protection.

## What the article does not cover

Article 299 is about possession for one's own immediate use. It says nothing of growing. Supply stays under articles 296 and 298, the second of which deals with small-scale dealing and production. The [2017 amendment](/history/peru-medical-2017/) also changed article 300, so that a doctor who prescribes cannabis to a registered patient commits no offence.

## Why the index moved

Possession rises from 1 to 3 and consumption from 1 to 3, because holding a personal dose for one's own use stopped being punishable. Enforcement rises from 2 to 3, and no further, since under the 1991 wording the outcome depended on a court's view of each case. Cultivation and sharing stay at 1. The total moves from 11 to 20. The [2003 amounts](/history/peru-eight-grams-2003/) are recorded in the timeline as a separate entry without a score change.
