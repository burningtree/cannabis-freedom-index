Green Lava Labs Pharmacy opened at Bentick Square in Kingstown, opposite the
main hospital, to serve local and visiting patients.[@1]

Saint Vincent's first cannabis pharmacy put a counter — and a pharmacist —
between the licensed hillside growers and the patients the 2018 industry law
was written for.[@1]
