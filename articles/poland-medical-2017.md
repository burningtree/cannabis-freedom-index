A parliament that would not decriminalise three grams voted 440 to 2 for medical
cannabis. Then, for more than a year, there was nothing to buy.

## A judge and a doctor

In November 2014 the Constitutional Tribunal
[upheld](/history/poland-constitutional-tribunal-2014/) the ban on possessing and
growing cannabis. But the judge rapporteur said there was essentially no justification
for banning possession and use on medical grounds, for instance in terminal illness,
and the Tribunal decided to signal the matter to the legislator.

The following year a case gave the question a face. Marek Bachański, a neurologist at
the Children's Memorial Health Institute in Warsaw, had been treating children with
drug-resistant epilepsy with cannabis preparations. In July 2015 the hospital
suspended the therapy, saying its bioethics committee had not approved it; the health
ministry called it illegal. In October [he was
dismissed](/history/poland-bachanski-dismissed-2015/). Parents protested outside the
hospital, and an activist brought cannabis from the Netherlands to fill his
prescriptions.

## The bill

The bill came from Kukiz'15, a right-wing opposition club, and its best-known backer
was the rapper and MP Piotr Liroy-Marzec. Its first draft would have let patients or
their guardians grow their own. That was removed, and the governing party did not
agree to growing in Poland at all.

What passed the Sejm on 22 June 2017, by 440 votes to 2 with one abstention, was
narrower:

- cannabis herb, resin and extracts may be registered as a pharmaceutical raw material;
- pharmacies prepare medicines from it on a doctor's prescription;
- vets may not prescribe it, and the raw material is imported.

The law took effect on 1 November 2017.

## An empty shelf

No producer applied until June 2018, when Spectrum Cannabis, part of Canada's Canopy
Growth, filed for one product. It [went on
sale](/history/poland-first-pharmacy-cannabis-2018/) in December 2018 in three
pharmacies, in Poznań, Konin and Wrocław, at about 60 to 65 zloty a gram. The first
delivery was seven kilograms. It served about 300 patients and was gone within days.

## What it became

Online clinics then made prescriptions easy to get, until the ministry
[required an in-person examination](/history/poland-telemedicine-restricted-2024/) by
specialist clinics in November 2024. Prescriptions fell by more than half and grew
again in 2025. Since [2022](/history/poland-hemp-act-2022/) state research institutes
may grow the raw material in Poland.

In the index, access for [Poland](/country/pl/) rose from 0 to 1 with the law and to 3
once pharmacies had something to sell.
