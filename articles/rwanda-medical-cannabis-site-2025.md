Rwanda's development board reported that off-site infrastructure for the
Musanze cannabis project was 83% complete, with double fencing still
outstanding, according to reporting on 15 May 2025.[@1]

The public infrastructure project had spent over one billion of its 2.2
billion francs allocated for 2024/2025.[@1]

The licensed producer's planned launch had already been delayed, as the
country worked to develop cannabis-oil production aimed mainly at
export.[@1]
