Andorra treats possession of cannabis as a crime and has no medical programme. In
September 2026 its government proposed, for the first time, to let
the plant be grown there.

## The law as it stands

Personal possession is a criminal minor offence in Andorra, punishable with arrest
and a €500–1,200 fine under article 499 of the current Criminal Code. There is no legal route for patients.

How strictly the law is read was shown in
[July 2025](/history/andorra-cbd-ruling-2025/). The Constitutional Court dismissed
the appeal of a resident who had spent more than a year in prison after being
stopped at the border with just over half a kilo of cannabis flower sold as CBD,
and who was then expelled for five years. Under the Penal Code, the court held, any
cannabis containing THC, however little, is a toxic drug.

## The proposal

On 1 September 2026 the government presented a reform of the Agriculture and
Livestock Farming Act. It described it as the most important step so far towards
allowing cannabis to be grown for medicinal purposes.

The reform creates the legal structure for a single cultivation licence.

## What it does not do

The proposal is about growing for medicine. As recorded here it concerns a
cultivation licence only: nothing in it changes the offence of possession, and the
index has no record of a prescription route for patients in Andorra.

The reform still has to pass.
