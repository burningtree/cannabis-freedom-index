Germany set out to build Europe's first licensed cannabis market. What it passed
instead was a law with no shops in it.

## The promise

Since a [1994 ruling](/history/germany-constitutional-court-1994/) of the
Constitutional Court, small amounts for personal use were generally not prosecuted,
with each state setting its own threshold. Medical cannabis became available on
prescription in [2017](/history/germany-medical-2017/).

After the 2021 election the Social Democrats, Greens and Free Democrats agreed to go
much further: cannabis sold to adults in licensed shops. One study put the market at
4.7 billion euros a year.

## The retreat

It did not survive contact with European law. After talks with the European
Commission the government announced a
[narrower plan](/history/germany-two-pillar-plan-2023/) in April 2023: home growing
and non-profit associations first, shops only as regional trials later.

The [Bundestag passed](/history/germany-bundestag-vote-2024/) the Cannabis Act on
23 February 2024 by 407 votes to 226. The Bundesrat let it through on 22 March, and
it took effect on 1 April.

## What the law allows

| | Limit |
|---|---|
| In public | 25 g |
| At home | 50 g |
| Plants | Three per adult |
| Association | Up to 500 members; 25 g a day and 50 g a month each |
| Members aged 18 to 20 | 30 g a month, at most 10% THC |

Going slightly over is an administrative offence; more than 30 g in public or 60 g
at home is a crime. Adults must have lived in Germany for six months to grow or join
an [association](/history/germany-cultivation-associations-2024/), which keeps
visitors out.

Use is banned within sight of schools, playgrounds and sports grounds, in pedestrian
zones between 7 a.m. and 8 p.m., and near anyone under 18. Associations may not
allow consumption on their premises.

Old convictions for conduct that is now legal can be deleted from the register on
application. Medical cannabis moved to a law of its own and no longer needs a
narcotics prescription. A [driving limit](/history/germany-driving-limit-2024/) of
3.5 ng followed in August.

## What it leaves out

Nobody may sell cannabis, and nobody may give it away. Passing home-grown cannabis
to another adult is a crime, and so is a member sharing what the association
supplied. The only lawful sources are one's own plants, an association, or a
prescription.

That is why, in the index, possession rose from 2 to 5, growing from 1 to 4 and
consumption from 3 to 5, while sharing stayed at 1. Access for
[Germany](/country/de/) moved only when the associations opened, and stands at 3.
