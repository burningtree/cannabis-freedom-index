Czechia completed the legislative work for its biggest personal-use cannabis reform in decades during 2025. Parliament approved the criminal-code amendment, the Senate backed it and President Petr Pavel signed it. The new rights, however, would not begin until 1 January 2026.

## Through Parliament

The Chamber of Deputies approved the draft at the end of May. *Business of Cannabis* reported that the Senate then supported it in early July, with 47 of the 62 senators present voting in favour. The reform was part of a wider criminal-law package intended to reduce imprisonment and adjust penalties, rather than a stand-alone cannabis act.

Earlier ambitions had been broader. Proposals for cannabis clubs and licensed retail did not survive into the enacted text. What remained was a model based on an adult's own plants and possession, without a lawful route for recreational buying or sharing.

## The limits Parliament chose

The signed law set three headline allowances for people aged 21 or over: three plants, 100 grams of dried cannabis at home and 25 grams outside the home. CNN Prima reported that four or five plants would be an administrative offence and more than five a crime. Possession likewise moved through administrative and criminal bands above the legal amounts.

Cannabis offences received their own treatment within the amended criminal code, allowing penalties to reflect their assessed social harm separately from other controlled drugs. The legislation did not create shops, clubs or a licensed supply chain.

## Signature, then a wait

President Pavel signed the amendment on 17 July. That completed the approval process but did not make the possession and cultivation allowances immediately available. The effective date remained 1 January 2026.

The distinction matters in a legal timeline. During the second half of 2025, people were still governed by the old rules: small possession and cultivation remained administrative offences rather than newly lawful conduct. The operative change is therefore recorded separately at [the start of 2026](/history/czechia-home-grow-2026/).

## What moved in the index

Nothing moved when the law was passed and signed because its substantive provisions had not yet taken effect. This entry records the completed legislation; the later entry records the change to possession, cultivation, enforcement and consumption in the [Czechia index](/country/cz/).
