Cuba's replacement Penal Code punished simple drug possession without
authorisation or prescription with six months to three years in
prison.[@1]

Cannabis growing and supplying drugs carried three to eight years, rising to
seven to fifteen for relatively large amounts; growers could also lose their
land or land-use rights.[@1]

The code replaced the older Social Defence Code and took effect on 1
November 1979.[@1]
