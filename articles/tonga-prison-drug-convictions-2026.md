ABC Pacific reported that nearly 40 per cent of Tonga's prison inmates were
serving drug-related sentences, and that the prison population had risen
from about 250 to 300 in three years.[@1]

The figure covers all drugs, not cannabis alone.[@1]

The police commissioner separately said in February 2026 that the drug
problem was smaller than some feared and proposed wastewater testing.[@1]
