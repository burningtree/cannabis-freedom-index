Botswana's Cannabis Act received assent on 17 October 2025 and took effect that December. It allows cannabis to be
grown, processed, stored, traded, imported and exported, but only for medicinal, scientific,
research and industrial purposes, and only under licence from a new National Cannabis Control
Authority. It does not let anyone else use or keep the plant.

## From exemption to statute

Botswana's drug law since [1992](/history/botswana-drugs-act-1992/) had prohibited cannabis. The
first attempt to grow it legally went through a side door: in
[2018](/history/botswana-fresh-standard-exemption-2018/) the agriculture ministry gave a company an
exemption under the plant protection law, then police removed the crop and the ministry withdrew
it. The company won in the High Court and lost in the
[Court of Appeal](/history/botswana-court-of-appeal-fresh-standard-2022/) in 2022. The 2025 act
replaced exemptions with a licensing system written into law, and its long title says it is meant
to "domesticate" the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961.

## What the act sets up

The minister in charge is the minister for land and agriculture. The Authority issues
licences for cultivation, processing, storage, distribution, import and export; keeps a register
of every cultivation site; sets limits for products; and decides where licensed activity may take
place, weighing the risk of diversion and contamination.

| The act | What it says |
|---|---|
| Purpose | Medicinal, scientific, research and industrial use only |
| Who may possess cannabis | Licensees, and health professionals, vets, nurses, laboratory heads and authorised officers while on duty |
| Young people | No sale to under-18s, with up to two years for a licensee who sells to them; a minor with a prescription may get cannabis medicines through a pharmacist, with a parent's consent |
| Wild plants | A landowner must report cannabis growing wild within seven days, or face up to five years |
| Premises | Letting a place be used for unlawful use of cannabis is an offence |

The definition of cannabis is wide: the flowering tops, the resin, any extract or preparation, and
cannabinoids. "Industrial purposes" means uses that do not produce psychoactive effects.

## Assent, then commencement

Assent did not make anything legal yet: the act takes effect on dates the minister sets by notice
in the *Gazette*, and it came in two steps. Parts I to V, which set up the Authority and its board,
commenced on 12 December 2025 (S.I. 157/2025). Parts VI to XIII commenced on 22 December 2025
(S.I. 162/2025): among them Part VIII, which holds the licensing provisions (sections 34 to 48)
and lets licensed health professionals supply cannabis medicines to patients, and Part XI, which
says who may lawfully possess cannabis. Only from that day was there a lawful route. In
[January 2026](/history/botswana-industrial-cannabis-regulations-2026/) the government published
rules for industrial cannabis with a THC limit of 0.7 per cent. For everyone without a licence,
in [Botswana](/country/bw/) cannabis remains as illegal as it was.
