The Senate took up a bill by Senator Edward Espiritusanto of La Romana to
amend Article 75 of Law 50-88.[@1]

Under the law in force, simple possession carries six months to two years
in prison and a fine of 1,500 to 2,500 pesos.[@1]

The bill would remove prison for possession or consumption and have the
authorities refer the person to evaluation, treatment, rehabilitation or
harm-reduction programmes under health ministry protocols.[@1]

Penalties for distributors (3 to 10 years), traffickers (5 to 20 years) and
financiers (30 years) would stay as they are.[@1]
