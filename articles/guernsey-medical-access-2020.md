On 28 September 2020, Guernsey widened the route to prescribed cannabis. Islanders could seek private care from off-island clinics and import the prescribed products under licence, while local prescribing permission extended to GPs as well as specialists.[@1]

## The earlier permission had practical barriers

The [2019 changes](/history/guernsey-medical-rescheduling-2019/) had allowed authorised local specialists to prescribe pharmaceutical-standard cannabis in defined circumstances. But the health committee found practical barriers to patients obtaining treatment through that route.[@1]

Medical director Peter Rabey explained that the new changes would let people use UK prescriptions through an import-licensing process and broaden local prescribing to GPs. The reform addressed both finding a willing prescriber and obtaining the medicine lawfully.[@1]

## A prescription and an import licence have separate jobs

The official patient guidance describes private prescriptions and a licensing process for each individual import shipment. A patient’s clinic sends the prescription and transcript directly to the licensing team; the patient completes the matching online application.[@2]

The prescription must carry the correct Guernsey residential address because it is used to label the shipment. Once a licence is issued, the patient forwards it to the dispensing pharmacy so that the medicine can be sent.[@2]

The guidance also states a £30 fee for each individual import licence. Patients remain responsible for communicating with their clinic and pharmacy, making the paperwork part of the practical route to treatment.[@2]

## Patients also began meeting in shared spaces

By January 2025 the Green Retreat had completed a successful first year as a social space for medical cannabis patients. ITV reported that entrants had to show their prescription and identification proving they were at least eighteen.[@3]

There was no entry charge. Founder Bruce Caruso described providing somewhere that patients could feel safe using their medication responsibly before returning to their day.[@3] The [patient lounge](/history/guernsey-patient-lounge-operating-2025/) developed alongside the prescribing system created by the earlier reforms.[@1,3]

## Prescribing raised new oversight questions

A 2025 parliamentary response described official concerns about diversion of prescribed medicines and a joint review of cannabis prescribing. It identified gaps in data collection and governance, and said most medicinal cannabis prescriptions came from private clinics.[@4]

Those questions became part of the next stage of policy discussion. For patients in [Guernsey](/country/gg/), the 2020 reform had established the combination of private prescribing and licensed imports that the official guidance continues to describe.[@1,2,4]
