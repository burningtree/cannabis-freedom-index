In February 2026 Juba's authorities went after the selling spots. Security forces of
Central Equatoria State, led by the deputy governor with the Juba City Council, raided
the Suk Sita and Hai Amarat areas and seized marijuana — known locally as bangi — with
tramadol and codeine syrups.[@1]

The council warned that anyone trafficking in illegal substances would face the full
force of the law.[@1] The raid fits the enforcement pattern the profile records: a 2022
Bor bhang case ended with prison terms and SSP 50,000 fines, and the Penal Code's five-
and fourteen-year tiers stand behind every seizure.

A century after the 1924 hashish law first banned the plant across the south, the
market it prohibited is still policed raid by raid in the capital's markets.
