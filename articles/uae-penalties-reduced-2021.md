In November 2021 the United Arab Emirates announced a new drug law. Federal Decree-Law No. 30 of 2021 on combating narcotics and psychotropic substances took effect on 2 January 2022. For a person caught using or holding a drug, it replaced years in prison with months.

## Where the law had been

The earlier federal law, No. 14, set a minimum of four years in prison for personal use or possession. That was the sentence given in 2010 to a British traveller in transit at Dubai airport, on whose shoe customs found 0.003 grams of cannabis. A 2016 law cut the minimum to two years.

## The new scale

| Offence | Penalty for use or possession for personal use |
|---|---|
| First | three months in prison, or a fine of 20,000 to 100,000 dirhams |
| Second, within three years | six months in prison, or a fine of 30,000 to 100,000 dirhams |
| Third | two years in prison and a fine of at least 100,000 dirhams |

A court may send a first-time offender to a specialised rehabilitation centre in place of prison, for no more than a year, on the strength of a medical report. The option is closed to repeat offenders and to anyone treated under a court order before.

Two changes concern foreigners. Deportation of an expatriate convicted of use or personal possession is no longer mandatory; the court decides. And a visitor entering the country for the first time with food or other products containing a narcotic faces no legal action: under Article 96 the items are confiscated at the port of entry and destroyed.

## What became harsher

The same decree-law raises penalties for those who draw others in. Inducing or facilitating drug use carries at least five years, rising to seven or ten where the person was harmed, and to life imprisonment or death where they died. Running a place for drug use carries at least seven years.

## What followed

The softer rule on deportation did not last. Amendments in December 2025 again require courts to deport foreigners convicted of narcotics offences after their sentence, with exceptions for close relatives of citizens.

## Why the index moved

The index moved the [United Arab Emirates](/country/ae/) from 0 to 2 with the [2021 decree-law](/history/uae-penalties-reduced-2021/). Only enforcement changed, from 0 to 1: a first offence can now end in a fine or treatment. Possession and consumption stay at 0 because prison remains a realistic outcome, and the other segments were at 0 before and after.
