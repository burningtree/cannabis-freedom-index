A new rule required courts to order forfeiture of a conveyance connected
with drug trafficking when the convicted person possessed or controlled it
and the court found it was used, or intended for use, in the offence.[@1]

The rule also incorporated procedures for owners to seek restoration.[@1]

It extended the drug law's existing property-forfeiture rules.[@1]
