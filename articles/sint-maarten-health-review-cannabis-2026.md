Health minister Claret Connor said he had sought help from the Pan American
Health Organization to assess cannabis policy and comparable Caribbean
experiences.[@1]

He stressed mental health, treatment capacity and protection of vulnerable
residents alongside possible economic benefits.[@1]

He called for a comprehensive review of advantages and disadvantages before
decisions on legislation.[@1]
