On 3 February 2000 President Ismaïl Omar Guelleh put the prime minister in
charge of the drug file.[@1]

A national committee chaired by the prime minister was tasked with coordinating
drug policy, prevention, public information and monitoring supply and
demand.[@1] It followed recommendations from a 1999 youth conference.[@1]

Health, education, justice, police and gendarmerie representatives were brought
together, with a secretariat charged with collecting and publishing
drug-policy information.[@1]
