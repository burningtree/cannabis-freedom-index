The government withdrew its Permitted Use of Cannabis for Religious Purposes
Bill before the debate on the medical cannabis laws.[@1]

Rastafari representatives had told the select committee that the state cannot
grant a right to their sacrament, and that a law would expose their worship
to scrutiny by international drug control bodies.[@1]

It is a rare drug-policy moment: the believers asking the state not to
legalise their rite — because the right, they said, was already
theirs.[@1]
