The first two medical-cannabis dispensaries opened after the 2015 executive
order, reclassification and subsequent licensing work.[@1]

Governor Alejandro García Padilla visited one of the businesses and reported
1,953 registered patients and 23 licensed companies across the industry.[@1]
A later court judgment independently records The Family Dispensary beginning
sales to authorised patients on that date, before a dispute over its local use
permit.[@1]
