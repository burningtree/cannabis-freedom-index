The Dominican Republic acceded to the United Nations Convention against
Illicit Traffic in Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances of 1988 on
21 September 1993.[@1]

Article 3 of the convention requires each party, subject to its
constitutional principles, to make the possession, purchase and
cultivation of drugs for personal consumption a criminal offence.[@2]
