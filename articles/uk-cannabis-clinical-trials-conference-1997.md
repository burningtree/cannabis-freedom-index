A 1997 conference helped connect patient advocacy with professional interest in
cannabis trials. Suzanne Taylor identifies its organisers as the MS Society, Royal
Pharmaceutical Society and British Medical Association.

The meeting brought scientists, clinicians, patients and funders together around
research protocols for multiple sclerosis and pain. It also attracted pharmaceutical
industry interest. Taylor's account emphasises the MS Society's preference for trials
and its concern about being associated with general legalisation campaigns.

This conference and the later
[House of Lords report](/history/uk-lords-therapeutic-cannabis-inquiry-1997/) are
separate events. The official parliamentary record dates that inquiry and report to
1998. The conference was an organising step toward research, not a legal authorisation
for self-medication.
