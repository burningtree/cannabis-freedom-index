Germany's 2024 law was meant to create cannabis clubs. What it created instead was
a pharmacy market, and at the end of July 2026 the government made its first cut
into it.

## How the pharmacy became the shop

Doctors have been able to prescribe cannabis in Germany since
[2017](/history/germany-medical-2017/). The
[Cannabis Act](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/) changed one detail with large
effects: medical cannabis stopped being a narcotic, so it no longer needed the
special narcotics prescription.

Telemedicine platforms did the rest. Prescribing volumes grew by an estimated 3,000
per cent between March 2024 and December 2025. The market is now put at 1.15
billion euros, about three quarters of it private prescriptions paid for by the
patient.

The clubs, meanwhile, barely count. By August 2026 there were 455 approved
[associations](/history/germany-cultivation-associations-2024/) and 896
applications waiting, and club cannabis was under 0.1 per cent of consumption.
Fully built out, the clubs would cover an estimated 3.5 to 4.3 per cent of demand.

## The change

From the end of July 2026, cannabis flower is no longer reimbursed by the statutory
health insurance funds. The health ministry's own guidance dates the change from
29 July.

| | Statutory insurance |
|---|---|
| Cannabis flower | No longer reimbursed |
| Standardised extracts | Still reimbursed |
| Dronabinol and nabilone | Still reimbursed |
| Flower on a private prescription | Still allowed, at the patient's cost |

## Who it affects

Not most buyers. Statutory insurance already covered less than a quarter of the
market, so the patients who lose are the ones the 2017 law was written for: people
with a serious illness whose insurer had approved flower. They can switch to an
extract or pay themselves.

## The larger measure is stuck

The cut that would reach the private market is a separate bill. In
[October 2025](/history/germany-medical-restrictions-bill-2025/) the cabinet adopted
an amendment requiring an in-person consultation for a new prescription and banning
mail-order delivery of flower. It passed first reading in December 2025 and had a
committee hearing in January 2026. It has not moved since.

## In the index

Reimbursement decides who pays, not what is lawful, so
[Germany's](/country/de/) score does not move. Access stays at 3, and a private prescription
remains the main way Germans buy cannabis legally.
