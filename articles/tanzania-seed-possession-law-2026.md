Mainland Tanzania closed the last gap in its cannabis definition in 2026. Act 6 of 2026
brought seeds — along with mature stalk and fibre — into the meaning of cannabis, and
removed the drug-production-purpose condition from the offence of possessing or supplying
prohibited-plant seeds.[@1]

The change matters because of where it lands: the seed offence operates within section 11
of the Drug Control and Enforcement Act, whose existing minimum sentence is thirty
years.[@2] Unauthorised seed possession and supply therefore carry the same floor as
cultivation itself.

The Attorney General's registry records the Act's commencement on 2 October 2026.[@3]
Seeds, once the one part of the plant the definition left alone, are now fully inside the
prohibition — a tightening that touches growers first, since every plant starts as a
seed.
