Voters on Caye Caulker were asked whether the government should legislate
for a regulated cannabis industry, with part of the revenue kept for the
island.[@1]

A majority said no.[@1]

Supporters had pointed to money for schools, health care and tourism jobs;
the 'Hope Not Dope' campaign opposed it.[@1]
