Mali withdrew the industrial-hemp authorisation granted in 2017 for a
supervised production project.[@1]

Central drug office director Adama Tounkara said the producers had sought
to change the agreed terms, leading to a new decision cancelling
permission.[@1]

He confirmed the withdrawal at a May press conference as claims that the
project was still operating circulated on social media.[@1]
