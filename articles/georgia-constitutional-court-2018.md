On 30 July 2018, Georgia's Constitutional Court removed the remaining general penalty for consuming marijuana. The ruling protected private use as an aspect of personal autonomy, while leaving the country without a legal way to produce or buy the cannabis being consumed.

## From criminal to administrative punishment

The court had already ruled in [2017](/history/georgia-consumption-2017/) that criminal punishment for marijuana use was unconstitutional. Consumption nevertheless remained an administrative offence, exposing a user to financial or other non-custodial sanctions.

Girchi party members Zurab Japaridze and Vakhtang Megrelishvili challenged that remaining punishment under Article 16 of the constitution, which protected the right to free development of personality.

## The autonomy ruling

The court accepted that protecting health and public order were legitimate aims. It nevertheless found a blanket penalty disproportionate. As reported by *Civil Georgia*, the court treated marijuana use as conduct protected by personal self-development and considered the danger arising from individual use very small.

It therefore declared sanctions for non-prescribed consumption unconstitutional. The decision concerned use itself. It did not invalidate offences for cultivation, possession or supply.

## Limits around other people

The court also recognised that restrictions could remain constitutional where consumption threatened third parties. Parliament responded in November with detailed rules covering public places, educational settings, public transport, the presence of minors and people performing official duties.

The Administrative Offences Code consequently distinguishes private adult consumption from use in specified settings. It also retains a GEL 500 fine for small-quantity purchase, storage, carriage or transfer. Georgia therefore reached legal private consumption without establishing legal access.

The later code applies separate fines to use by people under 21, use in public space, use around minors and consumption while performing official duties. A privately owned residence is expressly treated differently from most other buildings.

## What moved in the index

The ruling raised [Georgia's](/country/ge/) enforcement score from 3 to 4 and consumption from 2 to 3. Possession stayed at 3, cultivation at 1, sharing at 1 and access at 0. The narrow movement reflects the decision's narrow subject: punishment for use ended, but the acts needed to obtain cannabis remained unlawful and later legislation defined places and circumstances where consumption could still be fined.
