On 9 June 2024, alongside the European elections, Slovenians answered two advisory cannabis questions: 66.71% backed growing and processing cannabis for medical purposes, and 51.57% backed growing and possessing it for limited personal use.[@1] Neither result changed the law by itself, but together they set the programme for everything that followed.

## Two questions, two majorities

Both questions asked whether Slovenia should allow the activity on its territory — cultivation and processing for medical purposes, cultivation and possession for limited personal use.[@1] The medical majority was decisive; the personal-use majority was narrow but clear.[@1]

## The medical answer became law first

The 2024 vote for medical cannabis led to the Medical and Scientific Cannabis Act of 2025, under which any physician may prescribe cannabis on professional judgment.[@3] The bill that followed the personal-use vote took longer: the Cannabis for Limited Personal Use Act was notified to the European Commission as a draft on 30 July 2025.[@2]

## What the vote did not do

A consultative referendum advises; it does not enact. Personal possession stayed a non-criminal misdemeanour with a fine, growing stayed prosecutable as production, and the draft four-plants-per-adult rule stayed a draft.[@1,2] Even Ljubljana's [Metelkova](/culture/traditions/metelkova/) autonomous zone, whose politics the 2024 vote belongs to, gained no separate drug law from the result.[@1] The referendum remains the base layer under [Slovenia's](/country/si/) current scores: a public instruction its parliament is still carrying out.
