On 5 December 2017 Paraguay's Senate gave final approval to a law creating a national
programme for the medical and scientific study of cannabis — PROINCUMEC — and took
cannabis, its resin, extracts and tinctures off the dangerous-drugs list.[@1] Law
6007 entered into force on 27 December 2017: medical cannabis was legal.[@1]

## What it replaced

Until then the only framework was Law 1340 of 1988, which represses trafficking and
leaves personal growing illegal — there was no medical route at all.[@1] The first
crack came in May 2017, when the health ministry authorised importing cannabis oil
under its control.[@2]

## The families who forced it

The law followed the oil. Paraguay had authorised importing cannabis oil under health
ministry control that May, and parents' and patients' associations pressed for a
domestic supply.[@2] Roberto Cabañas, vice-president of the medicinal cannabis
organisation, celebrated the vote: his daughter has Dravet syndrome and the family was
paying $300 a month for imported oil, while the new law would let seeds come in for
oil production at home.[@2] Peru, Chile, Argentina and Colombia had already legalised
marijuana for medical purposes; Paraguay joined them, short of Uruguay's full
legalisation.[@2]

## A state programme, not home growing

The law obliges the state to supply oil free to patients on a national register — the
register the whole programme depends on — and regulates production, industrialisation
and research for medical use.[@1] Growing at home was not included.[@1] Implementation
took another year: the programme was only regulated in late 2018, and the later tweaks
to the decrees concerned how many permits could be requested.[@1] The separate hemp
track opened with Decree 2725 in 2019.[@1]

## What moved in the index

The [2017 law](/history/paraguay-medical-2017/) moved [Paraguay's](/country/py/) medical
category from none to limited, the first lawful medical use in a country that still
punishes personal possession. The programme it created is the ancestor of the 2025
exceptional pilot for medicinal combinations.
