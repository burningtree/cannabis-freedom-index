Curaçao's Advisory Council asked the government to explain why its
replacement drug bill would let it exempt substances, activities or
circumstances from existing prohibitions.[@1]

The council wanted a reasoned explanation with examples, alongside clearer
procedures for individual permissions.[@1]

The final July law kept an exemption power covering cannabis but required the
government to seek Parliament's approval before making such a decree.[@2]
