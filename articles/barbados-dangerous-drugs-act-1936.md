Barbados passed its Dangerous Drugs Act in 1936, its first drug
law.[@1]

What the filing preserves is the application machinery rather than the Act's
own wording: the Application Order of 6 December 1951, made under section
10(2), applied Part IV — which bars importing or exporting covered drugs
except under Part V and empowers regulation against improper use — to the
scheduled drugs, including any non-external-use preparation made from extract
or tincture of Indian hemp.[@1]

Whether the 1936 text already named cannabis is unconfirmed here; the order,
not the Act, is what the file reproduces.[@1] The 1973 Narcotic Drugs Act
would later expressly repeal the 1936 law.[@1]
