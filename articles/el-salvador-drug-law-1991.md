Decree 728, in force from 4 March 1991, regulated growing, production, use,
possession and trafficking.[@1]

Possession brought three to six years in prison, growing five to ten.[@1]

The 2003 law replaced it: the term for less than two grams fell to one to
three years, while the one for growing rose to five to fifteen.[@1]
