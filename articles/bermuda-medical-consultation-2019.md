Attorney-General Kathy Lynn Simmons opened consultation on a scheme for
prescribed cannabis to be smoked or vaped, with a new authority licensing
local cultivation and manufacturing.[@1]

The proposal followed the 2014 pharmaceutical law and 2019 hemp
exemption.[@1]

After public feedback questioned the narrow scope and regulatory cost, the
government shifted towards a wider adult-use licensing plan in 2020.[@1]
