President Emmerson Mnangagwa commissioned a US$27 million medical cannabis
farm and processing plant built by Swiss Bioceuticals at Mount
Hampden.[@1]

Contemporary reporting said only fifteen of fifty-seven cannabis permits
had been activated and described concern that some were being held for
speculation.[@1]

Later in 2022 the medicines regulator began accepting applications for
hemp-based CBD products as complementary medicines.[@2]
