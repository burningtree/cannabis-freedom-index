Congressional Bill 23-205 would rewrite Title 11 chapter 11 to drop Trust
Territory, Director and federal-law references and add substances for the
Behavioral Health and Wellness Program.[@1]

It was reintroduced as C.B. 24-41 on 11 August 2025.[@2]

It was a modernization bill, not legalisation, and did not appear in the
public-laws tables by October 2026.[@1,2]
