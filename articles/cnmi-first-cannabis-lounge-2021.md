Adults aged 21 and over could use cannabis on site at Common Wealth
Cannabis in Garapan, reported open on 16 August after receiving a lounge
licence in July.[@1,2]

Customers could bring their own cannabis or buy it at the adjoining shop,
while staff checked identification at the entrance.[@1]

The venue extended the 2018 legalisation beyond home use after the first
retail shop opened that summer.[@1]
