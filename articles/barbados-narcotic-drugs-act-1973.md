The Narcotic Drugs Act 1973, assented on 30 July, amended and consolidated
narcotics law and expressly repealed the Dangerous Drugs Act of
1936.[@1]

Cannabis and cannabis resin offences covered smoking or dealing on managed
premises, knowingly cultivating the plant, and dealing generally; penalties
ran to a $5,000 fine or five years on indictment and $2,500 or twelve months
summarily, with forfeiture, and the burden of proving any licence or
exception lay on the accused.[@1]

Commencement awaited a proclamation.[@1]
