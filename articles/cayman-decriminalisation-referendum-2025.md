Cayman voters backed decriminalising the consumption and possession of small
amounts of cannabis in the referendum held on 30 April 2025.[@1]

Premier André Ebanks later told Parliament that 55.64% supported the
proposal and promised a Law Reform Commission review of possession limits,
penalties and safeguards.[@1]

The commission opened public consultation in July 2026, with possession and
consumption still criminal offences under the existing law.[@2]
