Ethiopia replaced its 1999 drug law with a wider health law requiring
special permits for importing, manufacturing, distributing, storing or
possessing narcotic drugs.[@1]

It kept special prescription and secure-storage rules, and retained seven
to fifteen years in prison plus a fine for advertising that encouraged drug
abuse.[@1]

These controls were later rewritten in the 2019 food-and-medicine law.[@2]
