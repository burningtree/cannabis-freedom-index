Tanzania moved the boundary between lower-quantity cannabis trafficking and the life-imprisonment tier from fifty to one hundred kilograms in October 2021.[@1] It also changed the lower tier's thirty-year sentence into a minimum of thirty years.[@1]

## The earlier boundary came from the 2017 law

The [2017 amendment](/history/tanzania-trafficking-thresholds-2017/) had divided cannabis trafficking into two groups: thirty years for fifty kilograms or less, and life imprisonment above that amount.[@2] It also added possession and production to a trafficking definition already covering giving, supplying and storing drugs.[@2]

That meant the quantity rules operated within a broad set of prohibited supply activities.[@2] The lower tier still involved decades in custody.[@2]

The 2021 changes came through a law amending several statutes, with separate clauses changing the drug-law thresholds.[@1]

## The boundary doubled while the sentence became a minimum

One clause changed the upper trafficking provision from fifty to one hundred kilograms.[@1] Another made the same change in the lower-tier provision, keeping the two sides of the boundary aligned.[@1]

For cannabis amounts above fifty kilograms and no more than one hundred, the amended text placed the case within the lower-quantity tier.[@1,2] Amounts above the new boundary remained within the life-imprisonment tier.[@1]

The amendment also inserted words making thirty years a minimum in the lower tier, replacing the earlier fixed-term wording.[@1,2] The change therefore combined a wider quantity range with stricter wording about the sentence available within that range.[@1]

## Personal-use weights stayed a separate question

The [2016 regulations](/history/tanzania-small-quantity-regulations-2016/) had defined small quantities in grams, including fifty grams of cannabis and five grams of resin or oil.[@3] The Act's personal-use tier required proof that the amount was for consumption rather than sale or distribution.[@4]

The 2021 trafficking clauses changed kilogram boundaries, not those listed gram amounts.[@1,3] Eligibility for the personal-use rule continued to depend on its own statutory conditions.[@4]

## Both trafficking tiers remained severe

The earlier [2015 law](/history/tanzania-drug-control-act-2015/) had set life imprisonment for trafficking before the weight-based split was introduced.[@4] The 2017 and 2021 amendments developed that split while retaining long imprisonment on both sides.[@1,2]

For a person prosecuted for cannabis trafficking in [Tanzania](/country/tz/), crossing the new boundary could determine whether the applicable statutory tier began at thirty years or imposed life imprisonment.[@1,2]
