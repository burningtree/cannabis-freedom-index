On 21 July 2023 growing and using cannabis at home became lawful in Luxembourg. The law had been published in the official journal at the start of that week.

## What the law allows

- A household may grow up to four cannabis plants. They must be kept out of sight of minors.
- Cannabis may be consumed at home, on the premises where it is grown or kept.

The limit is per household, not per adult.

## What it does not

Everything outside the home stays prohibited, but the consequence of the smallest offence changed.

| Conduct | Before | From July 2023 |
|---|---|---|
| Carrying up to 3 g in public | a fine of up to €2,500 | a fine of €145, with no proceedings and no criminal record |
| Carrying more | criminal offence | criminal offence, with a higher fine or prison possible |
| Selling | criminal offence | unchanged |

There is no shop, club or dispensary. A resident who does not grow has no lawful way to obtain cannabis.

## The vote

The bill was supported by 38 members of the Chamber of Deputies: all the governing parties, Déi Lénk, the Pirate Party and three members of the ADR. The Christian Social People's Party voted against.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Luxembourg](/country/lu/) from 20 to 37 with the [2023 law](/history/luxembourg-home-grow-2023/).

| Segment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Possession | 2 | 4 |

| Enforcement | 4 | 6 |

Sharing stays at 1 and access at 1: the law is built around the plant on the windowsill, and it gives nothing to the person who would rather buy.
