France's June 2013 decree opened a route for authorised cannabis-containing medicines.
Its exception depended on the pharmaceutical product holding a qualifying marketing authorisation.

Signed on 5 June and published on 7 June, Decree 2013-473 took effect on **8 June 2013**.
It added a third part to Public Health Code article R.5132-86, exempting specified operations
involving pharmaceutical specialties authorised in France or through the European Union.

The listed operations included manufacture, transport, import, export, possession, supply,
transfer, acquisition and use. Their scope was tied to those authorised medicines; the list
was not a general permission to grow cannabis or obtain any cannabis product for treatment.
Nor did the decree itself show that a medicine was stocked or available to patients.

Parrella's contemporaneous overview describes the change broadly as approval of therapeutic
cannabis. The original wording supports the narrower description used here. The history date
records commencement, separately from signature and publication.
