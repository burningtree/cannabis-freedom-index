The United States Coast Guard stopped a vessel about eight nautical miles
off Môle-Saint-Nicolas carrying about 3,200 pounds of marijuana, valued at
3.8 million dollars, and detained one person.[@1]

The cargo and the suspect were handed to the Haitian authorities for
prosecution.[@1]
