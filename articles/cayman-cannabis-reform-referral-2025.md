Premier André Ebanks told Parliament that small-scale cannabis reform would
be referred to the Law Reform Commission after voters backed
decriminalisation.[@1]

He asked for recommendations on possession limits, administrative penalties,
enforcement, international obligations and the protection of vulnerable
people.[@1]

The commission's later discussion paper dates Cabinet's referral to the same
day and opened those questions to public consultation in July 2026.[@2]
