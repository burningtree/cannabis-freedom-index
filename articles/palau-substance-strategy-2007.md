The Substance Abuse Prevention Strategic Plan 2007–2011 said marijuana
possession laws were "not stringent" and recommended increasing
penalties.[@1]

The plan was a policy document, not a change in the criminal law; the
citation tier for two ounces or less remains.[@1]
