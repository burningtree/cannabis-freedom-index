Belize decriminalised small amounts of cannabis in November 2017. The Misuse of Drugs (Amendment) Act, No. 47 of 2017, received the Governor-General's assent on 2 November and was gazetted two days later.

## Ten grams

The act adds a new subsection to the possession offence. Where a person holds not more than ten grams of cannabis or cannabis resin, the possession "is not unlawful and an offence is not committed".

One place is treated differently: the premises of a school or other educational institution, including its playing fields. Possession of up to ten grams there is a contravention but not an offence.

| Occasion | Consequence on school premises |
|---|---|
| First | a warning from the police |
| Second or later | a violation ticket with a fine of 100 Belize dollars |

The person cannot be arrested or detained for it, and it forms no part of a criminal record. Anyone under 18 found in possession must take part in an approved drug treatment or counselling programme.

## Where it may be smoked

Smoking is allowed only on private premises, and only where the person holds no more than ten grams: at their own home, at another person's home with that person's consent, or in private guest accommodation such as a hotel room with the consent of the proprietor. The act also lifts the liability of occupiers who permit such smoking, and the ban on owning pipes in those circumstances.

## Old convictions

A person convicted of possessing cannabis, before or after the act, and sentenced to a fine of not more than 1,000 dollars may apply to have the conviction expunged from police records.

## What stayed illegal

The act does not touch cultivation or supply. It redefines cannabis to exclude licensed medicinal preparations and industrial hemp, at a THC level to be prescribed. A later bill to create a regulated market was shelved in 2022.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Belize](/country/bz/) from 11 to 25 with the [2017 act](/history/belize-decriminalisation-2017/): possession from 1 to 4, enforcement from 2 to 4 and consumption from 1 to 3. Cultivation and sharing stay at 1 and access at 0, because a person may hold and smoke ten grams but has no lawful way to obtain them.
