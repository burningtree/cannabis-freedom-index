On 10 January 1962 Bolivia gave cannabis its first named place in domestic law. The
Narcotic Drugs Act listed cannabis and its galenical preparations among the narcotic drugs,
was published in the Gaceta Oficial on 7 February, and was communicated to the United
Nations under the 1931 convention.[@1]

## The ladder

Unauthorised preparation, trade or supply drew three to twelve years plus a fine, and
possession without proof of lawful origin drew three to five years.[@1] The law scaled up
for officials, gangs, supply to minors, and cases ending in illness or death.[@1]

## Replaced, not forgotten

The ladder itself was swept away in 1988, when Law 1008 replaced prison for small users
with compulsory treatment and set trafficking at ten to twenty-five years.[@2]
