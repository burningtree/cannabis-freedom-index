Canada's modern commercial industrial-hemp programme began on **12 March 1998**. Health Canada
places the first commercial growing licence in May that year, after research conducted in
1994–1998.

That sequence matters when reading older cannabis histories. Parrella's 2014 overview associates
1994 with commercial permission. The government's account distinguishes earlier scientific
research from the later licensing programme.

The programme covered controlled industrial cultivation and associated licensed activities.
It did not legalise recreational home-growing or create a general right to supply cannabis.

The cited FAQ describes the earlier regulatory system. It supports this historical event and
should not be used as a complete guide to present Canadian licensing rules.
