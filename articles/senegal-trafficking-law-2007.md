Law 2007-31 rewrote articles 95 to 103 of the Drug Code.[@1]

Growing, producing and international trafficking of high-risk drugs carry
ten to twenty years of forced labour and a fine of three times the value
of the drugs seized; giving or offering drugs to a person for personal use
carries five to ten years.[@1,2]
