The fishing boat *Lammie* became a visible example of how maritime networks supplied
the expanding Dutch cannabis market.

In Stephen Snelders's account, the boat carried 10,000 kg of Lebanese hashish in 1974.
A naval vessel chased and fired at it. Facing boarding, the captain sank the boat.
The transport had been organised through contacts between an Amsterdam criminal
entrepreneur, fishermen and a fish trader.

The chapter places this episode in a broader movement from small supplies shared
among acquaintances toward larger import operations. It predates the
[1976 toleration reform](/history/netherlands-toleration-1976/) and documents the
illegal supply side rather than a legal permission to buy, possess or grow.
