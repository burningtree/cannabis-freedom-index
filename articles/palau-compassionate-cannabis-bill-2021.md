The Compassionate Cannabis Use Act would have let licensed doctors prescribe
cannabis and set up a Belau Cannabis Authority to license imports,
manufacture and research.[@1]

It passed first reading in the House of Delegates after a campaign by the
Belau Cannabis Society; President Surangel Whipps Jr. voiced
reservations.[@1]
