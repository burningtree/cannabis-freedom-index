Slovakia's first restriction on cannabis was a Czechoslovak law. On 27 January 1938 the republic of which Slovakia was then part enacted its Opium Act, Law No. 29/1938, and the same text that brought Indian hemp under control in Prague did so in Bratislava.

## What the Act was for

The Act carried three international agreements into domestic law: the opium conventions of 1912 and 1925 and the 1931 convention limiting the manufacture of narcotic drugs. Until then Czechoslovakia had relied on rules inherited from Austria-Hungary, which dealt with poisons and medicines and not with drugs as a class.

It sorted narcotic substances into four groups and set up a system of special authorisations, inspections in pharmacies and registers.

## The offence

Anyone who without authorisation intentionally manufactured, prepared, processed, possessed, held for sale, put into circulation, offered, imported, exported or carried in transit a controlled substance committed an offence.

| Case | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Ordinary | three months to three years in custody, and a fine of 5,000 to 50,000 crowns |
| Large-scale or commercial, or causing serious injury, death or wider danger | one to five years, and a fine of 10,000 to 100,000 crowns |

Making or keeping equipment clearly meant for unauthorised manufacture was punished too.

## Prohibition with a door left open

The Act did not forbid every use. Authorised handling for medicine stayed possible through pharmacies under the permit system, which is why the index leaves one point of access after 1938.

## A shared history that divides later

Slovakia and the Czech lands kept the same drug law for more than half a century, through the [criminal code of 1962](/history/slovakia-criminal-code-1962/) and the [decriminalisation of 1990](/history/slovakia-decriminalisation-1990/). The two timelines part only after the federation was dissolved, when Slovakia made possession a crime again in 1994, five years before the Czech Republic did. The same law is described for Czechia [here](/history/czechoslovakia-opium-act-1938/).

## Why the index moved

The [1938 Act](/history/slovakia-opium-act-1938/) is the first step in [Slovakia's](/country/sk/) score, from the unregulated state to 20. Possession and cultivation fall from 10 to 2, enforcement to 3, sharing to 1, consumption to 2 and access from 8 to 1.
