At midnight on 1 April 2019 a law the Knesset had [passed the previous July](/history/israel-knesset-decriminalisation-2018/) came into force. Until then the statute had threatened up to three years in prison for using cannabis. From that day a first or second offence ended in a fine.

## The ladder

| Offence | Outcome |
|---|---|
| First | fine of 1,000 shekels (about $275), no criminal record |
| Second, within five years | fine of 2,000 shekels, no criminal record |
| Third | Al-Monitor: a conditional arrangement and referral to rehabilitation. Jewish News: a criminal investigation, or loss of a driving or gun licence, if within seven years |

The two reports describe the third step differently, and both are given here as printed. The law names no weight. Israel's Anti-Drug Authority treats about 15 grams as personal use, the figure the newspapers used.

## Home and street

Jewish News reported the change in these words: "Use and possession of marijuana in small amounts in personal homes is not an offense." The fines apply to having or using a personal amount in public. People licensed to use medical cannabis must show the licence if the police stop them.

## Who is left out

The reform does not apply to soldiers, to minors or to people with a criminal record. Minors are sent to rehabilitation programmes. The Jewish Chronicle noted when the law passed that it also does not cover "parallel offences", such as trying to get rid of the evidence.

## Temporary

The law was passed as a temporary measure for three years, after which the Knesset could decide whether to keep it. It grew out of the recommendations of a panel set up by the public security minister, Gilad Erdan; the [cabinet decision of 2017](/history/israel-cabinet-decriminalisation-2017/) has its own entry. In the Knesset only one member voted against. Oren Lebovitch of the Ale Yarok party gave it a guarded welcome: "at least some Israelis will be able to avoid criminal records".

## Why the index moved

Possession rises from 2 to 3: a small amount in public now brings a fine without a record, and at home it is no offence. Enforcement rises from 3 to 4, because the police hand out fines where they used to open criminal files. Consumption rises from 2 to 3. Cultivation and sharing stay at 1; none of the reports describes any change for growing or supplying. Access was already 3 because of the [medical programme](/history/israel-medical-programme-2007/). The total moves from 20 to 25.
