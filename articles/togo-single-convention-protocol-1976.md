Togo ratified the Protocol amending the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs on 10 November 1976.[@1]

The Protocol was adopted in Geneva on 25 March 1972 and entered into force on 8 August 1975.[@1] It strengthened the international control board and, among other changes, lets parties provide treatment, education, after-care and rehabilitation for people who use drugs, as an alternative to conviction or punishment or in addition to it.
