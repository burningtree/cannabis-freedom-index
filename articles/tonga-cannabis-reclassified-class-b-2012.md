The Illicit Drugs Control (Amendment) Act 2012 created Class B, moved
cannabis from Class A to Class B, and cut the maximum from T$750,000 or 25
years to T$50,000 or seven years; under 28 g carried T$5,000 or one
year.[@1]

The change took effect on 27 November 2012.[@1]
