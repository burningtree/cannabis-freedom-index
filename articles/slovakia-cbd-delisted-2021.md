Slovakia removed cannabidiol, or [CBD](/culture/language/cbd/), from its psychotropic-substance list on 1 May 2021.[@1] Parliament’s amendment deleted the entry naming CBD, changing the substance’s position under the controlled-drug law.[@1]

## Parliament deleted the named substance

The National Council approved the amendment on 18 March 2021, and it was published on 9 April before taking effect in May.[@1] The operative provision removed the cannabidiol entry from the second group of psychotropic substances.[@1]

The amendment worked through the existing controlled-substances statute, which separately regulates authorisations, cultivation, production and handling.[@1,2] Cannabis and THC remained covered by their own entries and rules.[@2] For growers, the relevant permissions therefore continued to depend on the plant, variety and purpose involved.[@2]

## Hemp cultivation retained its own rules

The current statute permits industrial growing for obtaining fibre and seed, alongside a separate research route.[@2] Varieties listed in the EU common catalogue, including varieties listed when they were sown, are exempt from the ordinary Health Ministry cultivation permit.[@2]

Other cultivation and processing need permission, with safeguards against diversion.[@2] A cultivation permit ordinarily lasts one year, and permit holders must hand the harvest to a processing organisation or destroy the remaining material under supervision.[@2] These conditions govern how plants and harvests move through authorised production.[@2]

## Food and medicines followed different assessments

The Agriculture Ministry’s [February 2026 notice](/history/slovakia-cbd-food-guidance-2026/) explains that CBD is treated as a novel food under EU rules.[@3] It reports EFSA’s temporary intake benchmark while emphasising gaps in the available safety evidence.[@3] Food classification and safety assessment therefore remained relevant after the controlled-substance deletion.[@3]

A separate [2023 NIHO assessment](/history/slovakia-cbd-epilepsy-assessment-2023/) evaluated the purified CBD medicine Epidyolex with clobazam for Dravet and Lennox-Gastaut syndromes from age two.[@4] That report considered clinical and economic evidence for a particular treatment and patient group.[@4]

## Later controls covered other cannabinoids

Slovakia [added HHC and related cannabinoids to its drug schedules in November 2024](/history/slovakia-hhc-controlled-2024/).[@5] The amendment named substances individually, including HHC, HHC-O and HHC-P.[@5] It also revised authorisation and hemp-cultivation provisions within the controlled-substances law.[@5]

The [Slovakia profile](/country/sk/) follows these separate rules for controlled cannabis, industrial hemp, CBD and licensed research.[@2,5]
