Act 26 of 1962 amended section 2 of the Dangerous Drugs Act, replacing the
definition of Indian hemp with one covering every part of the Cannabis sativa
plant, its resin and every preparation of them.[@2]

The current consolidation carries that definition forward: Indian hemp
includes all parts of any plant of the genus Cannabis whether growing or not,
the resin, and every compound, manufacture, salt derivative, mixture or
preparation of plant or resin.[@1]

The amendment also added a conspiracy offence: agreeing to commit or abet any
offence under the Act carried the same punishment and forfeiture as the
offence itself, even where the other party was outside the colony's
courts.[@2]
