On 20 February 2020 Cabo Verde's president vetoed parliament's authorisation
to rewrite the 1993 drug law.[@1]

Parliament had authorised the government to draft a new drug law that included
suspended sentences with compulsory treatment.[@1] President Jorge Carlos Fonseca
vetoed it after the Constitutional Court found the delegation too broad and
indeterminate.[@1]

The veto kept the 1993 architecture in place: up to three months or a day-fine
for small personal-use amounts, four to twelve years for other cultivation and
supply, and voluntary-treatment suspension rather than compulsion.[@1]
