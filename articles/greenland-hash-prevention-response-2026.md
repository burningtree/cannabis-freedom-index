Greenland’s government backed stronger customs controls and prevention in
its response to a parliamentary debate on reducing hash use.[@1]

It pointed to funding for three to five additional customs officers in the
2025 budget and Allorfik’s expanding cannabis treatment and counselling
since 2016.[@1]

Its response linked action to children and families and the national
public-health strategy.[@1]
