Ecuador's [2008 constitution](/history/ecuador-constitution-2008/) says in article 364 that addictions are a public health problem and that users may in no case be criminalised. For five years nothing said how a judge should tell a user from a dealer. On 21 May 2013 the governing board of CONSEP, the national drug-control council, filled the gap with Resolution 001-CONSEP-CD-2013, published in the Registro Oficial on 20 June.

## What the resolution did

The resolution has three articles. The first adopts a Ministry of Public Health study on toxicity, with psychological and biological analyses, and the health minister's proposal of "maximum admissible quantities" a person may hold for personal use. The second sends the study to the courts, "to provide elements of analysis and guide their conduct" in applying the constitutional rule. The third orders the results to be built into the national drug prevention plan for 2013–2017.

## The table

| Substance | Maximum for personal use |
|---|---|
| Marijuana | 10 g |
| Cocaine base paste | 2 g |
| Cocaine hydrochloride | 1 g |
| Heroin | 0.1 g |
| Ecstasy | 0.015 g |

## A guide, not a law

Infobae reported at the time that a CONSEP resolution does not have the force of law, so judges were not obliged to follow it, and that the amounts were therefore to be written into the criminal code the government was then drafting. CONSEP's head, Diego García, said the table changed nothing in principle: "the non-criminalisation of consumption already exists in the Constitution". The [criminal code of 2014](/history/ecuador-criminal-code-2014/) went on to say that holding drugs for personal use, in the amounts set by the relevant regulation, is not punishable.

## Why the index moved

Three scores rise by one point each. Possession goes from 2 to 3, because a person with up to 10 g now had a published amount to point to. Enforcement goes from 3 to 4, because the table was addressed to the courts as the measure for separating users from traffickers. Consumption goes from 2 to 3 for the same reason. Growing, sharing and access are untouched: the table speaks only of holding a drug for one's own use. The total moves from 17 to 22. The table lasted ten years, until it was [repealed in 2023](/history/ecuador-threshold-table-repealed-2023/).
