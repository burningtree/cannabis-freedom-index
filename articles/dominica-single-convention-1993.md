Dominica acceded to the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961 on 24
September 1993.[@1]

The convention lists cannabis and cannabis resin among its controlled
drugs and obliges its parties to limit their production, trade, possession
and use to medical and scientific purposes.[@2]
