Denmark issued a separate order applying its euphoriant-substances law to
the Faroe Islands, providing the basis for the islands' later drug
controls.[@1]

The law carried forward controls from an older opium statute; later
amendments in 1969, 1988 and 2014 changed the scheme.[@1]
