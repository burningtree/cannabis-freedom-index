Koreans wove hemp for five thousand years and could smoke it without breaking any law
until 1976.[@1] Since then South Korea has followed its citizens' cannabis use across
borders.

## Hemp without a drug law

Samples of hempen cloth in Korea date back to about 3000 BC. In the 1930s hemp was
grown in every province, and in the late 1950s about 9,000 hectares were still under
cultivation.[@1]

The [Narcotics Act of 1957](/history/south-korea-narcotics-act-1957/) listed "Indian
marijuana" as a forbidden narcotic. It reached only the imported drug; Korean hemp
went on being grown.[@1]

## The crackdown

In the winter of 1975 the government of Park Chung-hee turned on the music scene. More
than 50 entertainers were arrested, among them the rock guitarist Shin Jung-hyeon,
who served four months.[@1] Shin's case shows the campaign's edge: arrested for
marijuana possession in 1975, he was thrown into a psychiatric hospital, served prison
time under torture, and stayed blacklisted from concerts and radio until Park's
assassination in 1979.[@3]

The Cannabis Control Act followed in 1976 and banned cannabis in all forms. It was
merged into the Narcotics Control Act in 2000 without softening.[@2] Recreational use
carries up to five years in prison or a fine of up to 50 million won.[@1]

## The law travels

Korean criminal law applies to citizens abroad. On 16 October 2018, the day before
Canada legalised cannabis, the Korean embassy there
[posted a warning](/history/south-korea-abroad-warning-2018/): citizens who use, buy,
possess or carry cannabis overseas can be punished when they come home, even where it
is legal.[@1]

Celebrities remain the visible cases. Sentences have run from 25 days to 10 months;
the rapper T.O.P received a suspended ten-month sentence in 2017.[@1]

## Two small openings

On 23 November 2018 the National Assembly
[allowed a few cannabis-derived medicines](/history/south-korea-medical-2018/) to be
imported for patients, and imports began in early 2019.[@2] In 2020 the Andong area of
North Gyeongsang was made a special zone where licensed industrial hemp may be grown.[@1]

In the index the 1976 Act took [South Korea](/country/kr/) to the bottom of almost
every segment in one step.
