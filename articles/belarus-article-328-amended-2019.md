Belarus cut two minimum prison terms for drug supply by two years on 27 October 2019. Basic supply could now receive three to eight years, while several aggravated supply offences carried six to fifteen years.

## Prisoners’ families had pressed for relief

The change followed campaigning by relatives of people convicted under the country’s drug laws. The [Mothers 328 movement](/history/belarus-mothers-328-hunger-strike-2018/) had staged hunger strikes and sought shorter sentences and reviews of disputed cases.

The lower house approved the amendments in June 2019. President Alexander Lukashenko signed the law in July, and the drug-sentencing changes took effect three months after publication.

Responding to the parliamentary vote, Mothers 328 leader Larisa Zhigar welcomed the lower minimums but said they were insufficient. She wanted more help for minors serving long terms and hoped courts would actually use the new lower limits.

## Basic supply received a three-year minimum

The amendment changed the minimum for the basic supply offence from five years to three, retaining its eight-year maximum. The offence covered unlawful manufacture, acquisition, possession and transport with intent to supply, as well as supply itself.

For the next aggravated tier, the minimum fell from eight years to six. Its fifteen-year maximum remained.

That tier covered circumstances including group involvement, repeat offending, large quantities, specified dangerous drugs and supply to minors or at certain institutions and public events. The classification of an offence therefore continued to matter as much as the reduction in its minimum.

## Other drug offences kept their separate ranges

The organised-group tier retained ten to twenty years. Supply causing death through drug consumption retained twelve to twenty-five years.

Personal possession remained punishable with restriction of liberty for up to five years or prison for two to five years. The code offered a separate set of penalties for growing drug-containing plants, including a fine for the basic offence.

For someone convicted of supply, the reform gave the judge more room at the lower end of the prison range. It left the structure of personal-possession, cultivation and aggravated-supply offences in place.

## Courts gained more alternatives six years later

In [March 2025](/history/belarus-drug-sentencing-options-2025/), Belarus added restriction of liberty for three to five years as an alternative for basic supply. Judges also gained a short custodial sentence for personal offences, while the organised-group minimum fell to eight years.

These later changes expanded the kinds of sentence available. [Belarus’s profile](/country/by/) reflects both the 2019 reductions and the wider choices introduced in 2025.
