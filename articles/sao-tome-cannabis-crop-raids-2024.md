Police's Operation 971 targeted cannabis fields and sales points across São
Tomé and Príncipe in July 2024.[@1]

Seven men and two women, all São Toméan, were detained and brought for an
initial judicial hearing.[@1] The operation involved the prison and social
reintegration service and the state leaders' protection unit alongside the
judicial police.[@1]

A year later the same press reports still called the seized drug liamba: three
men arrested in São Tomé with 30 g, released on bail by the judicial
authorities.[@1]
