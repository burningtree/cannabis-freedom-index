In 1995 the US government described Dominica as a minor cannabis producer
but an increasingly exploited transit route, with some cannabis grown on
the island but mostly consumed locally.[@1]

Dominican authorities had seized 741 kg of cannabis and eradicated 48,855
plants in 1994.[@1]
