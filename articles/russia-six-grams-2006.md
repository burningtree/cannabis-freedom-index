On 7 February 2006 the Russian government replaced a table of doses with a table of weights. Resolution No. 76 repealed the 2004 rule under which twenty grams of marijuana was the point where possession became a crime, and set the point at six.

## From doses back to grams

The rule it replaced had worked by multiplication. A government resolution of May 2004 fixed an "average single dose" of each drug, and the Criminal Code treated more than ten doses as a large amount. One dose of marijuana was two grams, of hashish half a gram.

Resolution 76 dropped the dose. It lists, drug by drug, what counts as a "large" and an "especially large" amount for the possession and supply articles of the Criminal Code.

| | 2004: crime above | 2006: large | 2006: especially large |
|---|---|---|---|
| Marijuana | 20 g | 6 g | 100 g |
| Hashish | 5 g | 2 g | 25 g |
| Hashish oil | — | 0.4 g | 5 g |
| Heroin | 1 g | 0.5 g | 2.5 g |
| Cocaine | 1.5 g | 0.5 g | 5 g |

For cannabis the weight is taken after drying. For the other drugs the figure applies to the whole mixture that contains the substance.

## Why it was done

The change had been announced the previous May. The justice minister, Yuri Chaika, said a dose was hard to define by medical, social and legal criteria at once, and that the idea had created the impression that drugs were being legalised.

The Federal Drug Control Service had criticised the dose rule. Its head of legal affairs said small dealers carried exactly the permitted amount, and the service calculated that a tonne of heroin could be held across the country without punishment.

Lev Levinson, of the group New Drug Policy, called the repeal a step backward. By his count about 30,000 people had been kept out of prison for small amounts of marijuana during the year the rule was in force.

## What was kept

The idea that a small amount is not a crime survived. Below six grams of marijuana or two of hashish, possession without intent to sell is an administrative offence, punished with a fine or up to fifteen days' detention. Those two figures are still the line in Russian law.

## Why the index moved

The [resolution](/history/russia-six-grams-2006/) lowered [Russia's](/country/ru/) total from 15 to 11. Possession fell from 3 to 2 and enforcement from 2 to 1: a threshold of six grams still spares the smallest amounts, but what lies above it is a criminal charge. The [2004 rule](/history/russia-decriminalisation-2004/) it replaced is described separately.
