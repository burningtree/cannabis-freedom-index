Germany's Cannabis Act came into force in two stages. From [1 April 2024](/history/germany-cannabis-act-2024/) adults could possess cannabis and grow three plants at home. From 1 July 2024 the second stage applied: the provisions on Anbauvereinigungen, non-profit cultivation associations, which are the only way to obtain cannabis legally without growing it oneself or holding a prescription.

## What an association is

The law defines it as a registered non-profit association or cooperative whose sole purpose is the joint, non-commercial growing of cannabis and its distribution to members for their own use.

| Rule | Content |
|---|---|
| Permit (§ 11) | required from the state authority; valid seven years |
| Members (§ 16) | adults only, at most 500, resident in Germany; a person may belong to one association |
| Participation (§ 17) | only members may grow, and they must take an active part |
| Location (§ 12) | not within 200 m of a school, youth facility or playground |
| Advertising (§ 6) | prohibited, as is sponsorship |

## How much a member may receive

| Member | Per day | Per month | THC |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aged 21 or older | 25 g | 50 g | no cap |
| Aged 18 to 20 | 25 g | 30 g | at most 10% |

The cannabis may not be passed on to anyone else, and nothing may be consumed on the association's premises or within sight of them.

## What it is not

An association is not a shop. It cannot sell to non-members or visitors, it cannot advertise, and membership requires residence in Germany. Associations registered as clubs must set a minimum membership of three months, which closes the door to short-term joiners. Commercial sale, which the government had first proposed, is not part of the Act.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Germany](/country/de/) from 42 to 44 with the [second stage](/history/germany-cultivation-associations-2024/). Only access changed, from 1 to 3: a resident adult who does not grow now has a lawful source, but one that requires joining a members' association, with no retail sale and nothing for visitors.
