The Medicinal Cannabis Authority issued sacramental authorizations to
Nyabinghi Theocracy Church Inc., Rasfreeman Foundation for Unification of
Rastafari and Wan Love Chant, allowing religious cultivation and use under
the Cannabis Act framework.[@1]

The authorizations were for sacramental purposes, not commercial
business.[@1]
