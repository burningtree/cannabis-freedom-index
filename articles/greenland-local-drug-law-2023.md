Greenland introduced its own drug law on 1 January 2023, together with regulations allowing an exception for cannabis products containing no more than 0.2% THC through authorised importers.[@1,2] The law retained pharmacy and prescription permissions for controlled medicines while prohibiting possession and supply outside its authorised routes.[@1]

## Parliament wanted access to low-THC oils

The reform followed [parliamentary decisions in 2019](/history/greenland-low-thc-parliament-2019/) calling for access to low-THC cannabis products and oils comparable with Denmark's earlier change.[@3] Greenland had [taken responsibility for drug legislation in 1992](/history/greenland-drug-law-competence-1992/), alongside health services, so Denmark's 2018 rule did not automatically apply there.[@3,4]

The government explained in 2019 that introducing such an exception required Greenlandic legislation.[@4] The resulting local law replaced the inherited Danish statute when it took effect in January 2023.[@1]

## The exception depended on an authorised importer

The accompanying regulation excluded qualifying cannabis products from its controlled-drug definition when they contained no more than 0.2% THC and had been imported by an authorised legal entity, such as a company.[@2] Permission came from the Chief Medical Officer's office, which could issue general or individual import permissions and attach conditions.[@2]

That importer was responsible for ensuring the products met the THC ceiling.[@2] The regulation also allowed permission to be withdrawn if its conditions were breached.[@2] The legal route therefore connected the product's THC content to the organisation responsible for bringing it into Greenland.[@2]

## Prescription permissions continued alongside the exception

The new law allowed the national pharmacy to import and handle controlled medicines and protected receipt and possession under valid prescription rules.[@1] Its explanatory notes said doctors' ability to prescribe controlled medicines on a medical assessment would continue.[@3]

Outside those routes, the law prohibited importing, selling, buying, delivering, receiving and possessing the controlled substances.[@1] Breaches could lead to measures under Greenland's Criminal Code, with specified supply in settings frequented by young people treated more seriously.[@1]

## Local distribution conditions followed

A [replacement regulation in April 2024](/history/greenland-low-thc-distribution-2024/) added a requirement that low-THC imports arrive in batches for distribution after receipt at local storage controlled by the importer.[@5] It also required information identifying the product and documenting its THC content.[@5]

The current regulation retains those conditions.[@6] They define the low-THC route alongside the medical permissions and wider cannabis prohibitions in [Greenland](/country/gl/).[@1,6]
