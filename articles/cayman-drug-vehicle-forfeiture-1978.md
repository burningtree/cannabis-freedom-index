Cayman required courts to confiscate a vehicle, boat or aircraft under an
offender's control when it was used, or intended to be used, to commit or
facilitate a drug offence.[@1]

The earlier law made forfeiture discretionary for a first conviction and
allowed recovery through a fixed CI$6,000 redemption payment; the amendment
introduced an ownership-restoration process with conditions including an
innocent-owner route or a payment linked to the vessel's value.[@1]

The official legislation history dates the change to 4 July 1978.[@2]
