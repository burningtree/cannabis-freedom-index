Saint Vincent passed its Dangerous Drugs Ordinance in 1937 (No. 21 of
1937), its first drug law.[@1]

What the filing preserves is the application machinery rather than the
Ordinance's own wording: the Application Order of 1947 (No. 24, made 25 April
and gazetted 6 May), made under section 13(2), applied Part V to the
scheduled drugs, including any non-external-use preparation made from extract
or tincture of Indian hemp.[@1]

Whether the 1937 text already named cannabis is unconfirmed here; the order,
not the Ordinance, is what the file reproduces.[@1]
