Agriculture Director General Timothy Tumukon told the public that cannabis
and hemp activity required strict licensing, secure management and export,
with no local sales.[@1]

His comments followed public confusion in late 2025; he said the government
had changed legislation to help approved firms obtain bank accounts.[@1]

The export industry followed the licensing law and February 2023 rules.[@1]
