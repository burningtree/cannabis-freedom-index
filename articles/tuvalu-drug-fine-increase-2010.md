Tuvalu doubled the general drug-offence fine in the High Court penalty
clause from $2,000 to $4,000 as part of wider changes to fines and monetary
amounts.[@1]

The later consolidated drug law pairs that fine with ten years'
imprisonment where no other penalty is specified.[@2]

Cannabis-specific minimum prison terms followed in 2025.[@3]
