Medicinal drug importers could obtain a one-year licence from Cabinet on
the minister's recommendation, with renewal and record-keeping conditions,
under the 2008 revision.[@1]

Travellers could carry lawfully prescribed drugs for up to one month of
treatment.[@1]

This continued the earlier medicinal import-licensing approach, which had
expressly covered Indian-hemp preparations in 1952.[@2]
