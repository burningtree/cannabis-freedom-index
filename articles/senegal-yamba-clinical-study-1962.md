French clinicians Collomb, Diop and Ayats published a study of cannabis
intoxication in Senegal in 1962.[@2]

The period evidence they drew on reported that by the 1950s the herb,
called yamba, was grown and smoked pretty much everywhere in Senegal.[@1]

The study documents clinical cases of cannabis intoxication in the
country.[@2]
