The Constitutional Chamber ruled that the government's bill to regulate
recreational cannabis, file 23.383, was unconstitutional because it
conflicts with the three UN drug conventions, which rank above domestic
law.[@1]

The electoral tribunal had asked after a citizen sought to put the bill to a
referendum.[@1]

The bill could go forward only if Costa Rica left the conventions.[@1]
