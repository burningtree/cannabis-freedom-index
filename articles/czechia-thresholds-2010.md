Czechia began 2010 with a new criminal code and explicit quantity tables. The change did not legalise cannabis. It made the boundary between an administrative offence and a crime more predictable, and it treated cannabis less severely than larger-scale drug offending.

## The problem inherited from 1999

Since [1999](/history/czechia-recriminalisation-1999/), possessing an amount “greater than small” for personal use had been a crime. Smaller quantities remained administrative offences. The statute did not say exactly how much was greater than small, leaving police, prosecutors and courts to work from non-binding guidance and case law.

The new criminal code retained that basic division. During its passage, the justice minister described the quantity rules as a clarification of an existing principle rather than a new decision to punish possession.

## The cannabis figures

Government Regulation No. 467/2009 supplied a table effective with the code on 1 January 2010. For cannabis, up to 15 grams of dried material or five plants fell below the criminal threshold. Conduct inside those limits could still lead to an administrative fine; it was not lawful possession or cultivation.

The new code also distinguished cannabis-related possession from possession of other controlled drugs in its penalty structure. The practical result was a clearer and less punitive framework for personal cannabis conduct than the uncertainty that preceded it.

## Clarity that did not last

The table settled the everyday numerical question, but it rested on the government's power to define a feature of a criminal offence by regulation. A district court later challenged that delegation.

In [2013](/history/czechia-constitutional-court-2013/), the Constitutional Court held that only legislation could define what conduct was criminal and annulled the relevant authority and table. The offence survived. The Supreme Court then supplied judicial guidance in [2014](/history/czechia-ten-grams-2014/), using a lower indicative figure for dried cannabis.

## What moved in the index

The 2010 framework raised [Czechia's](/country/cz/) possession score from 2 to 3, cultivation from 1 to 2, enforcement from 4 to 5 and consumption from 3 to 4. Sharing stayed at 1 and access at 0. The gains reflect defined non-criminal bands for personal possession and growing, even though both remained administrative offences rather than legal rights.
