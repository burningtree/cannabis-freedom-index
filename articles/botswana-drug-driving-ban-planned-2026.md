On 25 February 2026 the assistant minister of transport, Keoagile Atamelang,
announced proposed Road Traffic Act amendments at a consultative meeting in
Kgomokasitwa, describing existing provisions as targeting drunk drivers while
leaving drug impairment largely unaddressed.[@1]

That is the announcement's characterisation, not an accurate statement that
drug driving was lawful: published section 46(2) already prohibits driving
while unfit through drugs, with penalties under section 46(4).[@1] No
commencement date or enacted replacement was established.[@1]
