By September 2019, a bill sent to Curaçao's Parliament proposed a Medicinal
Cannabis Curaçao Office to work with growers and sellers.[@1]

The Advisory Council's earlier review described a system in which licensed
growers would sell their cannabis plants exclusively to the proposed
bureau.[@2]

In April 2023, opposition lawmakers were still pressing for approval after
two parliamentary discussions.[@3]
