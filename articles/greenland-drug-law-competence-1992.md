Greenland took over legislative responsibility for euphoriant substances
when it assumed responsibility for health services in 1992.[@1,2]

Existing health legislation continued until replaced locally, so later
Danish cannabis reforms needed Greenlandic decisions to apply there.[@1,2]

This became central to the later debate about low-THC oils.[@2]
