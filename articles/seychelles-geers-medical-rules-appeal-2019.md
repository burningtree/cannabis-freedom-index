The appeal court set aside an order requiring ministers to write medical
and scientific drug regulations in Alexander Geers's separate cannabis
case.[@1]

The majority held that the Constitutional Court could not grant that remedy
after finding no breach of his constitutional rights; Geers's own appeal
also failed.[@1]

Two judges dissented on lifting the order, while agreeing that his appeal
should be dismissed.[@1]
