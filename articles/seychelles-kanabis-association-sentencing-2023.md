After two men were given custodial sentences for cannabis offences, the
Seychelles Kanabis Association said the sentences were excessive and out of
line with reality.[@1]

Its chairman, Nelson Esparon, said the 2020 medical cannabis rules were not
fit for purpose, so that people seeking cannabis for treatment had to turn
to illicit sources and risk prison, and urged the government and parliament
to review the law.[@1]
