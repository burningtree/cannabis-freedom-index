Decree 153 of 2003 set the two-gram line: one to three years and a fine for
possessing less than 2 g, three to six years and a fine for 2 g or
more.[@2]

Sowing or growing without authorisation drew five to fifteen years.[@2]

Nine years later the Constitutional Chamber would read the possession bands a
new way: possession for one's own use sits outside criminal punishment, and
the weight is one factor among others.[@1]
