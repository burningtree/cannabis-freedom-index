In FSM v. Ehsa, 24 FSM R. 151, the Supreme Court excludes marijuana found
in a bag outside the justified firearms search, but finds probable cause
concerning approximately seven plants.[@1]

It requests briefing on national subject-matter jurisdiction over locally
grown cannabis.[@1]

The order leaves that question open; it is neither a general cannabis
acquittal nor a final nationwide sentencing rule.[@1]
