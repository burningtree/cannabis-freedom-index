On 2 October 2012 a separate law covering drug trafficking, consumption and
possession was promulgated, almost a year after parliament approved it.[@1]

In the meantime parliament had passed a new Penal Code covering many of the
same offences with partly different rules.[@1] A later judicial training manual
argued that criminal penalties should follow the code, while the separate law
continued to govern matters such as special procedures.[@1]

The overlap endures in practice: courts apply the code's three-day-quantity
tiers and trafficking bands, with the 2012 law behind the procedures.[@1]
