The Réunion historian R. P. Meerlsman, in an unpublished parish history of
Saint-Gilles-les-Hauts written around 1895, records a crime committed on the
island in 1830 by a Malay: the assassin "had smoked Amale to get his head
high" before committing it.[@1]

The anecdote, preserved through Benoist's chapter, is the earliest dated
individual cannabis-use report located for the island.[@1]

Amale belongs with jamala and zamal in the island's Malagasy-rooted word
family — one of the coast's oldest named smokes, attached to its oldest dated
police blotter.[@1]
