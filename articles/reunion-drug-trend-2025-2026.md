The French drug observatory's September 2026 report on 2025 found
home-grown zamal at about €10 a roll of roughly 10 g with quality still
declining, while imported skunk at €10 a gram was supplanting it and
imported resin sold at about €20 a gram.[@1]

Customs and police tightened air and sea controls under the prefect's
impulse: three operations searching all baggage at Roland-Garros airport
seized 20 kg of cannabis resin, and 58 air mules were intercepted in
2025.[@1]

The prefecture campaigned against mules at the airport while the
gendarmerie command named consumer repression a priority.[@1]
