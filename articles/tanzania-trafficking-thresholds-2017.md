Tanzania introduced a thirty-year prison term for cannabis trafficking involving fifty kilograms or less, while keeping quantities above that in the life-sentence tier.[@1] The same amendment broadened the trafficking definition and rewrote the conditions under which a person convicted of drug use could receive treatment.[@1]

## The earlier law already carried life for trafficking

The [2015 drug law](/history/tanzania-drug-control-act-2015/) had set life imprisonment for trafficking and at least thirty years for unauthorised growing of prohibited plants.[@2] It also contained a separate provision for small amounts intended for personal consumption, with a fine, three years in prison, or both for drugs outside its specially listed group.[@2]

The government [published an amendment bill](/history/tanzania-amendment-bill-2017/) in October 2017.[@1,4] President John Magufuli assented to the resulting law in November, and it was published on 1 December.[@1,4]

## Fifty kilograms divided two trafficking penalties

Cannabis and khat received a specific weight boundary.[@1] Trafficking more than fifty kilograms remained within the life-imprisonment rule.[@1] At fifty kilograms or below, the new trafficking offence carried thirty years.[@1]

Cases in that lower-quantity tier were assigned to subordinate courts, below the High Court.[@1] The amendment added possession and production to the definition of trafficking, which already included giving and storing drugs alongside buying, selling, transporting and distributing them.[@1]

That broader wording made how a person held or supplied drugs important to the offence as well as the weight involved.[@1,2] The separate personal-consumption provision remained in the law, with the accused responsible for proving that small quantities were intended for their own use.[@1,2]

## Treatment required consent and a medical assessment

For a person convicted of a drug-use offence, a court could direct treatment instead of immediately imposing prison or another sentence.[@1] It had to be satisfied that the person was dependent, that the dependence had motivated the offence, that treatment was possible within a reasonable time, and that this course served the interests of justice.[@1]

The person had to consent.[@1] A written medical report had to address the dependence, available treatment and prospects of success.[@1] Violence, weapons, organised crime, quantities beyond personal use and a role in directing others were among the circumstances that ruled against this route.[@1]

## The amendment combined different changes

The law separated lower-weight trafficking from the life-sentence tier while expressly bringing more conduct within trafficking.[@1] It also made medical evidence central to the treatment decision.[@1]

The [2021 amendment](/history/tanzania-trafficking-threshold-expanded-2021/) later moved the trafficking boundary to one hundred kilograms and made thirty years the minimum in the lower tier.[@3] Together, these changes shaped [Tanzania's cannabis controls](/country/tz/) through successive definitions, penalties and exceptions.[@1,3]
