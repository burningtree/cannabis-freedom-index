The draft handed to President Porfirio Lobo included a provision for
shooting down aircraft carrying drugs.[@1]

Under the 1989 law a first offence of possession for use brings up to thirty
days in a rehabilitation centre and a fine of 500 to 1,000 lempiras, a
second thirty to ninety days, and a third internment until the person is
judged reintegrated; no state centre exists, so the rule is not applied.[@1]

The law sets no quantity: the judge decides what is personal use.[@1]
