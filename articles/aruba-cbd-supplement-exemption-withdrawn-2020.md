A separate exception had covered specified supplements, foods and external-use
products — and in January 2020 it was withdrawn.[@1]

The withdrawal left the medicinal import route as the only CBD channel:
commercial import, storage and distribution of CBD medicines with no more than
0.2% THC require ministerial permission following a health-inspectorate
assessment.[@2]

What had briefly been a supplement shelf became a pharmacy permission
slip.[@1,2]
