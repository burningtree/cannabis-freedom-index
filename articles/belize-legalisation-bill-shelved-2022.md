The National Assembly had passed the Cannabis and Industrial Hemp Control
and Licensing Bill, which awaited the Governor General's signature, when
the Council of Churches gathered the 20,000 signatures needed to force a
referendum.[@1]

Prime Minister John Briceño deferred the vote until further notice, citing
its cost of 5 million dollars and banks' fears for their links with US
banks.[@1]

The bill was to be amended so that it could become law only after a
referendum.[@1]
