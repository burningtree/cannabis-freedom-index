The Drugs and Poisons Act 1930 commenced on 12 August 1930.[@1]

Part III, sections 33–39, prohibited importing Indian hemp, growing it and
possessing it, with section 36 creating the offence and section 43(2)
setting the penalty.[@1]

It was Tonga's first cannabis ban and remained the framework until the
Illicit Drugs Control Act 1988.[@1]
