For seven years Georgia had the most unusual cannabis law in its region, and it had
been written by judges. In July 2025 parliament took most of it back.

## What the court built

From [2006](/history/georgia-zero-tolerance-2006/) Georgia ran a zero-tolerance
policy of mass street drug testing. The Constitutional Court dismantled it in four
rulings: no prison for possessing up to 70 g
([2015](/history/georgia-no-prison-2015/)), none for buying small amounts or growing
for oneself ([2016](/history/georgia-no-prison-for-growing-2016/)), no criminal
penalty for use ([2017](/history/georgia-consumption-2017/)) and no fine either
([2018](/history/georgia-constitutional-court-2018/)).

Parliament [confined lawful use](/history/georgia-consumption-restrictions-2018/) to
adults of 21 at home. Buying, selling and growing stayed illegal, so the cannabis a
Georgian could lawfully smoke could not be lawfully obtained.

## What parliament changed

The amendments, proposed by the ruling Georgian Dream party and adopted on 2 July
2025, delete the separate cannabis article the court had worked on and put marijuana
under the general narcotics offence.

| Dried cannabis | Buying or possessing |
|---|---|
| Up to 5 g | Fine; a repeat offence, up to 1 year in prison |
| 5 to 70 g | Up to 6 years |
| 70 to 250 g | 5 to 8 years |
| Over 250 g | Up to 20 years or life |

Growing has a new article with two to six years in prison at the top. Archil
Gorduladze, who presented the bill, said openly that it was written to get around the
court, whose rulings the party called "completely incompatible with the reality of
2025". The rules on consumption were left alone.

## What came with it

The same package lets police hide the identity of cooperating witnesses at any stage
of a case. Saba Brachveli, a lawyer with the Open Society Foundation, said it was
"preparing the system for the mass planting of drugs"; months earlier Transparency
International had reported drugs planted on three detained protesters. In April
parliament had already [raised sentences](/history/georgia-drug-laws-tightened-2025/)
and made refusing a drug test a crime.

In the index, possession fell from 3 to 2 and enforcement from 4 to 2.
[Georgia](/country/ge/) still allows an adult to use cannabis at home, and still
offers no lawful way to come by it.
