In 1971 South Africa's Abuse of Dependence-producing Substances Act applied in
the territory it then ruled as South West Africa.[@1]

Namibia kept the Act as its drug law at independence in 1990 — which is why the
country's cannabis prohibition still runs through a Pretoria statute that calls
it "Cannabis (Dagga)".[@1]

The 1971 Act is the prohibition behind today's floor scores: possession or use
draws up to 10 years or R20,000 on first conviction, and over 115 g is presumed
dealing.[@1]
