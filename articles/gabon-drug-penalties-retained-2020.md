On 30 June 2020 Gabon's replacement Penal Code kept the previous year's drug
architecture and renumbered it.[@1]

The distinction survived intact: illicit use kept up to a year, general
possession kept its ten-year term, and giving drugs directly for another person's
consumption kept its own supply tier.[@1] Courts kept the option of ordering
treatment instead of the use penalty — and of punishing evasion of that
treatment.[@1]

The law replaced the previous code outright, so the tiers moved articles but not
substance: use stayed short, possession stayed long, and the treatment door
stayed open.[@1]
