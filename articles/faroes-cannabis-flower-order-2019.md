The Faroese Pharmacy Service placed an order for cannabis buds from a
Dutch supplier after a doctor requested them.[@1]

Existing cannabis drops were already prescribed to 61 patients, but this
order concerned flower.[@1]

The order followed the April 2019 opening of the medical trial scheme,
which the Chief Medical Officer later confirmed.[@2]
