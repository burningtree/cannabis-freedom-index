On 14 July 2021 King Mohammed VI signed, at Fès, the dahir promulgating Law 13-21 "on the licit uses of cannabis". It appeared in the French edition of the Bulletin officiel on 5 August 2021. Morocco did not repeal its prohibition: the law opens with the word "notwithstanding" and then names the three texts it carves an exception from — the 1922 dahir on poisonous substances, the dahir of 24 April 1954 "prohibiting kif hemp", and the 1974 law on drug addiction.

## What can be authorised

Article 1 lets the state authorise growing, producing, processing, manufacturing, selling, transporting and exporting cannabis and its products, and importing and exporting seeds and plants. Article 3 lists nine activities and says nobody may carry on any of them without an authorisation from a new body, the National Agency for the Regulation of Activities relating to Cannabis, a public establishment seated in Rabat (Article 31).

The purposes are limited. Authorisations to grow are given only for the quantities needed to make products for medical, pharmaceutical and industrial ends (Article 5). Varieties above a THC level set by regulation may be grown only for the medical and pharmaceutical industries (Article 6), and no product other than a medicine may exceed that level (Article 17).

## Who may grow

| Condition (Article 7) | Rule |
|---|---|
| Nationality | Moroccan |
| Age | Legal majority |
| Residence | A douar in a province listed by decree |
| Organisation | Member of a cooperative created for the purpose |
| Land | Owner, owner's consent, or a certificate from the local authority |

Growers must use seeds and plants certified by the agency and deliver the whole harvest to their cooperative, which sells it under contract to authorised companies (Articles 8 and 10). Each authorisation is valid for ten years (Article 27). Decree 2.22.159 named the first provinces: Al Hoceima, Chefchaouen and Taounate.

## Penalties

Anyone who grows, processes, transports or sells cannabis without a valid authorisation stays under the penalties of the 1974 law (Article 50). The new law adds its own offence for licensed growers: three months to two years in prison and a fine of 5,000 to 100,000 dirhams for growing outside the listed areas, exceeding the authorised surface or failing to hand over the whole crop (Article 51).

## Why the index moved

Before the law, every cannabis plant in Morocco was illegal and no lawful product existed. Law 13-21 created a licensed route to grow cannabis and to make medicines from it, so access rose from zero to one. The cultivation score did not move, because the licences go only to cooperative farmers supplying industry, not to people growing for themselves. Nothing changed for people who use kif: possession, use and unlicensed growing remain offences under the texts the law leaves in force.
