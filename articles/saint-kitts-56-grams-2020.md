Parliament raised the amount treated as a ticketable infraction from 15 g
to 56 g and passed a Cannabis Act for medical and religious use.[@1]

The medicinal licensing parts were brought fully into force in 2023.[@1]

The later amendment carries the ticket bands still in force: up to 56 g of
cannabis or 15 g of resin draws an EC$50 ticket, with the holder keeping
the cannabis.[@2]
