Bulgaria’s hemp permit ordinance moved to a threshold below 0.3% THC on 5 May 2023.[@1] The permission concerned registered farmers growing for fibre, food or feed seed, or sowing seed.[@1] It continued a controlled agricultural route, with applications, approved varieties and crop checks.[@1]

## Farmers needed permission before growing

The ordinance required applicants to be registered agricultural producers and to meet conditions concerning specified customs and drug convictions.[@1] The [2018 permit framework](/history/bulgaria-hemp-permit-ordinance-2018/) also covered trade in the permitted plants and their products.[@1] The 2023 amendment expressly required varieties from the Bulgarian official list, the EU common catalogue or the specified variety-admission route.[@1]

The Agriculture Ministry’s current service asks for an application and a declaration about the relevant criminal history, including members of a company’s management.[@2] Where a representative applies, the supporting documents include a notarised authorisation.[@2] The ministry offers several submission routes, including its administrative office, post and signed electronic channels.[@2]

## The threshold applied to specified plant material

The ordinance measures THC in the leaf mass and the flowering and fruiting tops.[@1] Its permitted uses are listed crop purposes, so the percentage must be read together with those purposes and the permit requirements.[@1] The ministry describes the permission as free of charge and valid for three years.[@2]

Growers must supply the information and declarations required by the ordinance, including the undertaking that leaves and flowering tops will not be taken, used or processed.[@1] Crop controls include destruction where the measured THC content exceeds 0.3%.[@1] A permit therefore requires attention to what is grown and how the harvest is handled, alongside the original application.[@1]

## The statute and permit text need careful reading

The reproduced narcotics statute still prints a lower threshold, below 0.2%, in its agricultural exception.[@3] The amended ordinance and the ministry’s operating service both state below 0.3%.[@1,2] Those are the respective texts a grower encounters; the sources read here do not resolve criminal liability for a crop falling between their thresholds.[@1,3]

The [Bulgaria profile](/country/bg/) retains that difference explicitly while describing the permitted fibre and seed activities.[@1,2,3] For farmers, the established route remains an Agriculture Ministry permit tied to the specified purposes, varieties and crop-control duties.[@1,2]
