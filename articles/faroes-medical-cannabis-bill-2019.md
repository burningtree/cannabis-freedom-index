MP Jenis á Rana resubmitted a proposal to let Faroese patients join
Denmark's medical-cannabis trial after parliament rejected it in 2018.[@1]

Many MPs now supported wider access but argued that the health minister
could add products to the medicines list without a new law.[@1]

Minister Sirið Stenberg offered to discuss that route with the social
affairs committee; official medical guidance dates the trial scheme's
start to April 2019.[@1,2]
