The Netherlands Antilles' ordinance of 30 July 1929 on opium and other
narcotics is the earliest Antillean drug law traced here.[@1]

As the 1960 Opium Ordinance's own notes record, it replaced the 1929 text
(P.B. 1940, no. 138) — Aruba, Curaçao and Sint Maarten each carry the same
1929 starting point in their histories.[@1]

The 1960 replacement was signed on 28 March 1960 and took effect on 1 June
1964 under a decree of 29 April 1964.[@2]
