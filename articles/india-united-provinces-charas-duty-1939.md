In 1939 the United Provinces raised the duty on charas by 20 rupees a seer and reduced the number of opium and hemp-drug shops by about a fifth.[@1] The province also took the shops into state management in selected areas of eleven districts.[@1] The Government of India included these steps in its report to the League of Nations, dated at New Delhi on 12 December 1940.[@1]

## The duty and the shops

The duty increase is stated on its own: 20 rupees more on each seer of charas.[@1] The shop cut is stated for opium shops and hemp-drug shops together, at about 20 per cent. The return gives one figure for both and does not split the share. A seer is given in the same provincial paragraph as about 935 grams, on the opium issue price, which rose from 90 to 110 rupees a seer on 1 April 1939.[@1] That April date belongs to the opium price. The charas duty is listed among the year's changes without a day of its own.

[The 1896 excise act](/history/india-act-xii-1896/) already applied in the United Provinces, then the North-Western Provinces and Oudh, and treated charas as an intoxicating drug. The 1939 increase is a further duty on charas, on top of that licensed trade. [The 1930 Act](/history/india-dangerous-drugs-act-1930/) left the shops and the duty to the province.

## Districts taken into state management

State management, already used for some opium shops, was extended to the opium shops of Benares city and to all opium and hemp-drug shops in selected areas of Garhwal, Saharanpur, Muzaffarnagar, Bulandshahr, Cawnpore, Moradabad, Aligarh, Agra, Jhansi, Gorakhpur and Basti.[@1] Benares is named for its opium shops. The hemp-drug shops in the list are the ones in those selected areas. The cut of about a fifth is a separate change from this takeover: one reduces the number of shops, the other moves the remaining selected shops into the hands of the province. The League circulated the return from Geneva on 9 May 1942.[@1]

## The same year's return

[The 1930 Act](/history/india-dangerous-drugs-act-1930/) had left hemp possession and sale to the provinces. [Bombay prohibited charas in the city and in Ahmedabad from 1 August](/history/india-bombay-charas-prohibited-1939/). [Bihar prohibited ganja and bhang](/history/india-bihar-ganja-prohibition-areas-1939/) where it extended prohibition, and raised the duty on both. [A central notification of 28 October](/history/india-hemp-extract-preparations-1939/) covered preparations of Indian hemp extract. The current account of the law is on [India's profile](/country/in/).
