Parliament adopted an amendment to the 1972 narcotics law setting two to
ten years' imprisonment and fines of one to ten million CFA francs for the
offences covered by revised Article 3.[@1]

Imprisonment was mandatory and suspended sentences were generally
excluded, with an exception for voluntary cooperation with the
investigation.[@1]

The explanatory memorandum identified traffickers, excluding simple
users, as the target.[@1]

This date is parliamentary adoption; the government catalogue identifies
the resulting Law 87-12 as dated 24 February 1987.[@2]
