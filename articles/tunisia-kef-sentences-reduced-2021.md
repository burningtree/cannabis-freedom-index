On 21 January 2021 a judge in El Kef handed 30-year jail sentences to three
young men for cannabis consumption in a public place — a sports stadium, a
circumstance letting the judge add 20 years to the five for consumption and
five for possession under Law 52.[@1]

The verdict drew protests: citizens, civil society and human rights
organisations called to amend Law 52, which sets a minimum of one year in
prison and a fine from 1,000 dinars for use.[@1,2]

After the appeal hearing was postponed from February to 9 March, the court
reduced the three men's sentences to two years in prison.[@2]
