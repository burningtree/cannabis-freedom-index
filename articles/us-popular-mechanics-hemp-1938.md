Herer reproduces *New Billion-Dollar Crop* as a central exhibit in his case for
industrial hemp. Popular Mechanics' own retrospective confirms that the article
appeared in its February 1938 issue.

The reproduced text promoted mechanised processing and replacing imported materials.
Its closing paragraphs also identified practical obstacles: farmers needed accessible
processing machinery, processors needed enough nearby acreage, and federal rules
required grower registration.

Those qualifications matter when reading Herer's account of a lost industrial future.
The article documents optimism and expected barriers. Its product counts and market
forecasts are contemporary claims, not independently verified outcomes in this index.

The source available here is a later reproduction, checked alongside the publisher's
retrospective, rather than an inspected original magazine issue. It establishes no
personal permission to grow cannabis and does not prove an industrial conspiracy
behind the [1937 tax law](/history/us-marihuana-tax-act-1937/).
