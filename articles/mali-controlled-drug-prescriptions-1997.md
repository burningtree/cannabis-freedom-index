Mali required special prescription forms for narcotics on its controlled
list, including cannabis and resin, and limited each prescription to seven
days of treatment, with a fresh prescription needed to continue.[@1,2]

Pharmacists had to keep stocks locked up and report theft or diversion,
while imports needed a special ministerial certificate.[@1]

These handling rules operated under the earlier criminal drug law and its
scheduled list of narcotics.[@1,2]
