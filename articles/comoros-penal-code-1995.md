Article 328 of the Comoros Penal Code, adopted in 1995, punished growing,
buying, importing, processing, transporting and distributing — even for free —
or selling narcotics "particularly the hashish or Indian hemp".[@1]

The tariff ran one to ten years in prison with a fine of 1,000,000 to
50,000,000 francs.[@1] Helping others use, by providing a place or any other
means, drew the same penalties, and courts ordered confiscation and public
destruction of seized substances on top.[@1]

The naming lasted: where the 2020 code speaks only of narcotics, the 1995 text
still shows what the law meant — hashish and Indian hemp, by name.[@1]
