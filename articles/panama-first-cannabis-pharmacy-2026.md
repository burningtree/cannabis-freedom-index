On 22 January 2026 Panama opened its first specialist medical cannabis pharmacy —
five years after passing Central America's first medical cannabis law. The ministry
reported seven commercial licences alongside the opening.[@1]

## CBD on the shelves, THC still pending

What the new channel actually sells is narrower than the law's promise. The drug
directorate's register lists Cannapharma CBD products for that date — but nothing that
establishes an available THC medicine route at retail.[@2] The July 2023 prohibition on
cannabis-product sales was the starting point; the pharmacy is the first step away
from it.[@3]

## Patients, then product

The sequencing is deliberate: in June 2026 the ministry approved patient and companion
forms, with a qualified doctor certifying treatment and the ministry verifying
registration before issuing a card for authorised pharmacies.[@1] Cards first, THC
medicines after — Panama is building the medical system in the order the law allows.
