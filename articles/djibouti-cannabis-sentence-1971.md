Mohamed Aden Darar received six months in prison and a three-year
residence ban for possessing and selling cannabis.[@1]

A later administrative order barred him from the Djibouti district and the
Obock and Tadjourah circles after his release in January 1972.[@1]

The case followed the local publication of France's revised drug law in
February 1971.[@1]
