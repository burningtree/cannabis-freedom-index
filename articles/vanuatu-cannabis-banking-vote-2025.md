Vanuatu's national broadcaster reported parliamentary support for changes
aimed at overcoming the bank-account and international-transfer barriers
facing licensed cannabis and hemp businesses.[@1]

The published bill proposed financial-intelligence checks on applicants'
money and key people, a coordinator's office and a system of penalty
notices.[@2]

Agriculture Minister Ian Wilson said licences issued in 2023 had been held
up by banking problems; opposition MPs supported the changes while
stressing security.[@1]
