Romania's Senate rejected a proposal to replace criminal penalties for possessing up to three grams of cannabis for personal use with an administrative fine.[@1,3] On 18 September 2023, its electronic roll call recorded 103 votes for the rejection report, one against and four abstentions.[@1,3]

## Three grams could still bring a fine

The [proposal introduced in May](/history/romania-three-gram-proposal-2023/) would have made this possession an administrative offence carrying a fine of 1,000–3,000 lei.[@1,2] It directed the revenue towards prevention and medical, psychological and social support for people who use drugs.[@1,2]

The distinction mattered because the existing personal-use offence was criminal.[@5,1] Under the [2014 reform](/history/romania-penal-code-2014/), cannabis possession and personal growing carried three months to two years in prison or a fine.[@5,1] The proposal would have changed the legal treatment of possession within its stated three-gram ceiling.[@5,1]

It also addressed patients: medically prescribed cannabis possession would be exempt from the personal-use penalties, with a special medical-cannabis law to follow.[@1] The bill therefore combined a small-possession reform with a proposed protection for people using cannabis as treatment.[@1]

## Senators argue against the change

Opposition crossed party lines.[@4] A contemporary report described PNL committee chair Cristian-Augustin Niculescu-Țâgârlaș warning that the measure would encourage drug use and urging education instead.[@4] PSD senator Florian Bodog invoked the prospect of children holding cannabis, while USR senator Narcis Mircescu also opposed the proposal and drew attention to trafficking.[@4]

The Senate's own record identifies the vote as approval of the rejection report.[@3] Its roll call gives the party breakdown and individual votes, allowing the outcome to be checked directly.[@3]

## The lower house still had a role

The Senate was the first chamber to consider the proposal.[@2] After its rejection, the file was registered in the Chamber of Deputies on 20 September.[@2] That chamber's health, legal and defence committees later submitted a joint rejection report, recorded on 12 December.[@2]

The parliamentary file consequently distinguishes the Senate decision from the later committee stage.[@2] It records the proposal as sent to the Chamber for debate.[@2]

For people carrying cannabis in [Romania](/country/ro/), the Senate vote delivered no three-gram exemption.[@1,2,5] The proposed administrative fine and prescription protection remained proposals, while the existing personal-use offence continued to apply.[@1,2,5]
