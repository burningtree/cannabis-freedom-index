The Dangerous Drugs (Amendment) Act 1985, assented on 22 August and commenced
on 26 August, raised the general fine from K500 to K2,000 and the maximum
imprisonment from three to fifteen years, and strengthened trafficking
penalties.[@1]

It did not change the prohibition or create a medical route — a penalty
increase within the existing ban.[@1]
