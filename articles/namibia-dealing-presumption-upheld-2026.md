Juvani Swartbooi, sentenced to 12 months for dealing in 2021, asked the
Windhoek High Court to strike down the rule that holding more than 115 g of
dagga presumes dealing unless the contrary is proved.[@1]

Deputy Judge President Hannelie Prinsloo dismissed the application: dealing
draws stiffer penalties than possession, a similar South African rule fell only
in 1995, and the prosecution must still prove possession of at least 115 g
beyond reasonable doubt — as Swartbooi's own admissions had established.[@1]

The dismissal keeps the presumption on the books: over 115 g, the holder must
prove it was not for dealing.[@1]
