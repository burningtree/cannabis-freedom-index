Vietnam replaced its drug-prevention law on 1 July 2026. The new text reorganises
the country's rules on prevention, monitoring and rehabilitation, while leaving no
opening for cannabis cultivation.

## A new law replaces the 2021 text

The National Assembly passed Law No. 120/2025/QH15 on 10 December 2025. Article 55
made it effective on 1 July 2026 and ended the effect of the 2021 Drug Prevention
and Control Law on the same day.

The law covers illegal drug use, rehabilitation, the duties of public bodies and
families, state management of controlled activities, and international
cooperation. It defines an illegal drug user as someone whose unauthorised use is
confirmed by a positive test.

## Cannabis is named

The definition of a "narcotic plant" expressly includes the cannabis plant,
alongside opium poppy and coca. Article 5 then prohibits cultivating a narcotic
plant or guiding another person to cultivate one.

The same article prohibits illegal production, storage, transport, trade,
distribution, import and export of narcotic substances. It also prohibits illegal
use, organising use, forcing or encouraging another person to use, and sheltering
or supporting illegal use.

## What may be licensed

The law recognises a tightly controlled category of lawful drug-related activity.
Competent agencies may permit research, testing and production of narcotic
substances, as well as transport, storage, trade, use and cross-border movement.

But the provision on lawful activities expressly excludes cultivating narcotic
plants from permitted research and production. That makes the structure important:
the Act does not merely ban unlicensed cannabis growing while offering a licence
route. It leaves cultivation outside the listed lawful activities.

The text does allow controlled addictive and psychotropic medicines under pharmacy
law, and it regulates medicines carried for treatment by travellers. Neither
provision creates a cannabis-growing route.

## Management as well as prohibition

The Act places strong emphasis on monitoring people who use drugs and on voluntary,
compulsory and medication-assisted rehabilitation. It also permits electronic
monitoring in specified rehabilitation and post-rehabilitation settings.

Those rules sit beside the [2025 offence for repeat drug use](/history/vietnam-repeat-use-crime-2025/),
which had already tightened the consequences of use before this replacement law
took effect.

## In the index

The new law restated and reorganised an already prohibitive system. It did not
create a new freedom or remove an existing one, so [Vietnam's](/country/vn/) score
does not move.
