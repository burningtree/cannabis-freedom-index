Premier André Ebanks told Parliament that the final cannabis-reform report
and supporting legislation were expected to reach the Attorney General for
government consideration in November or December 2026.[@1]

The July consultation had asked about possession limits, home growing,
administrative penalties and clearing old minor convictions, following the
2025 referendum.[@2]

Adults remained subject to the existing criminal law while the review and
preparation of legislation continued.[@3]
