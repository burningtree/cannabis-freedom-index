On 25 July 2022 Liberia's senators clashed over whether accused traffickers
should get bail at all.[@1]

A Senate committee objected to a proposed ban on bail for drug trafficking and
recommended an immediately payable bond worth twice the value of the seized
drugs.[@1] Another senator argued that wealthy dealers could pay and escape
prosecution.[@1] The West Africa Drug Policy Network, which worked on the
amendment with Representative Thomas Goshua and the drug agency, urged a
compromise protecting people who use drugs.[@1]

The argument previewed the 2023 overhaul: trafficking became non-bailable, while
other possession kept cash bail worth twice the bond.[@1]
