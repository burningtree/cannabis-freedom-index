Law 88-686 on the repression of illicit trafficking in and use of
narcotics governed drug offences for more than thirty years with a purely
punitive approach.[@1]

Article 8 punished possession or use with one to five years in prison and
a fine of 200,000 to 5 million CFA francs; supply and production carried
ten to twenty years under Articles 1 and 2.[@1]

A regional review of 2017 noted that the sentences were mandatory and
that the law offered no alternative to prison, though a judge could order
a convicted user to undergo detoxification.[@2]
