Czechia's medical-cannabis law created a legal pharmacy route in 2013, but legal status did not by itself make treatment affordable. From 1 January 2020, public health insurance began paying 90% of the retail price for a defined monthly quantity.

## Ninety per cent covered

The Ministry of Health listed the measure among the principal health-system changes taking effect in 2020. In outpatient care, insurance would cover 90% of the final consumer price of individually prepared medicines containing medical cannabis, up to 30 grams of cannabis a month.

The ordinary reimbursement limit was not an absolute treatment limit. The ministry said an insurer could approve reimbursement above 30 grams when the prescribing doctor justified the need, up to the programme's maximum of 180 grams a month. SÚKL's current programme information preserves the same basic structure: 30 grams ordinarily reimbursed and a higher amount possible with insurer approval.

## Why payment mattered

Before this reform, the state had built the legal machinery in stages. The [2013 law](/history/czechia-medical-2013/) authorised prescription and pharmacy dispensing, and the [first domestically grown supply](/history/czechia-first-domestic-medical-cannabis-2016/) entered distribution three years later. Patients nevertheless remained responsible for the price before the insurance change.

The ministry presented reimbursement as a way to improve economic accessibility. It identified patients with multiple sclerosis, AIDS, cancer and chronic non-cancer pain among those for whom medical cannabis could form part of treatment. Coverage concerned cannabis supplied through the regulated health system, not cannabis grown at home or obtained outside a pharmacy.

## The controlled route remained

Insurance funding did not loosen prescribing or dispensing controls. Medical cannabis remained an individually prepared medicine issued through pharmacies on a qualifying prescription. The monthly amount was monitored, and treatment continued to sit within the controlled-substances and medicines framework.

Nor did reimbursement affect recreational law. Personal possession and cultivation were still governed by the administrative and criminal thresholds that preceded the [2026 adult-use reform](/history/czechia-home-grow-2026/).

## What moved in the index

Nothing moved in the Cannabis Freedom Index. The medical-access score had already increased when prescription cannabis became lawful in 2013. Paying most of the cost made that route substantially more usable, but it did not move [Czechia](/country/cz/) into a different scored access category.
