Japan's 1930 report to the League of Nations records the 1922 regulations
for opium, morphine and cocaine: manufacture, import, export and possession
were banned except for licensed physicians, dentists and pharmacists, with
up to a year's penal servitude or a 200-yen fine.[@1]

The rules named opium, morphine and cocaine, not cannabis.[@1]

They are the state of the law in 1930, not proof that no local rule touched
cannabis.[@1]
