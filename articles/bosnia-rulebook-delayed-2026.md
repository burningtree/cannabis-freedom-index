Bosnia and Herzegovina's decision to permit cannabis-based medicines did not
produce patient access in 2026. A September follow-up confirmed that the rules
needed to prescribe and dispense them had slipped into the following year.

## Approval was only the first step

On [29 December 2025](/history/bosnia-medical-use-approved-2025/), the Council of
Ministers moved cannabis, resin, extracts and tinctures from the prohibited list
to the list of substances under strict control. The decision removed the basic
state-level legal barrier to medical use.

It did not create a working prescription system. Entity health authorities still
had to define matters including eligible conditions, which medical specialists
could prescribe, patient and doctor registers, doses, pharmaceutical forms and
dispensing procedures.

## The August deadline

After months without concrete progress, a July follow-up gave the Federation
health ministry until 1 August to prepare amendments to its prescribing and
dispensing rulebook. That deadline is recorded in the [earlier timeline
entry](/history/bosnia-rulebook-deadline-2026/).

At the second follow-up on 21 September, the ministry reported that it had held
two meetings with professional associations. The participants supported regulating
the subject, and a draft had been prepared and delivered to the federal health
minister's cabinet.

## Adoption moves to 2027

The same update made clear that the draft would not become an amendment during
2026. The Federation instead planned a new rulebook, aligned with relevant
European Union rules, for the first quarter of 2027.

The work was also incomplete elsewhere in the country. Officials said amendments
to the corresponding rulebook in Republika Srpska were in their final stage. The
pharmacists' chamber explained that an adopted rulebook was a prerequisite for
agreeing standardised pharmacy preparations based on cannabis.

One technical step had been completed: Bosnia and Herzegovina's pharmacopoeia
commission adopted monographs for cannabis flower and cannabidiol in May 2026.
That did not itself let a doctor prescribe or a pharmacy dispense either product.

## In the index

This was a delay, not a new legal entitlement. [Bosnia and
Herzegovina's](/country/ba/) medical access score therefore remains 0: the law
recognises a future controlled route, but patients still cannot use it.
