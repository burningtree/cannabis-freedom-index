The colonial government reported fourteen convictions for possessing opium
or ganja: twelve fines of ten to five hundred guilders and two six-month
prison terms.[@1]

It reported no detected cannabis-cultivation offences that year.[@1]

The figures combine the two drugs and do not give a cannabis-only
sentencing rate.[@1]
