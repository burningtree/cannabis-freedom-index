On 26 October 2020 Dominica's House of Assembly passed a four-page amendment to its drug law. It did not create a cannabis market. It removed the offence for a person holding a small amount and for an adult growing a few plants at home.

## What the act says

The Drugs (Prevention of Misuse) (Amendment) Act, No. 16 of 2020, was assented to and gazetted on 30 October. It inserts two sections into the 1988 Act and rewrites one.

| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| 7A | A person with 28 g or less of cannabis or cannabis resin is not guilty of an offence; more than 28 g remains unlawful |
| 7A(3) | Earlier convictions for 28 g or less are treated as spent, except for people under 18 |
| 7B | Smoking or using cannabis in a public place is an offence with a fine of 1,500 East Caribbean dollars, and the conviction does not go on the criminal record |
| 8(1A) | A person aged 18 or older may grow up to three plants at the place where they permanently live |

A "public place" covers buildings, public transport and facilities open to the public, and the roads and paths beside them. It does not cover a private home that is not used for business, or a usual place of worship of the Rastafarian or another faith. Vaping counts as smoking.

## What it left alone

The 1988 Act had prohibited the production, supply and possession of controlled drugs and, separately, the growing of any cannabis plant. The amendment touches only possession, home growing and public use. Supplying cannabis to another person is still an offence, and nothing in the act licenses sale, so there is no lawful place to buy.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Dominica](/country/dm/) from 11 to 39 with the [2020 act](/history/dominica-decriminalisation-2020/).

| Segment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Possession | 1 | 5 |
| Cultivation | 1 | 4 |
| Enforcement | 2 | 6 |
| Consumption | 1 | 3 |

Sharing stays at 1 and access at 0: the law makes room for the person who grows and smokes their own, and for no one else.
