"The most important step has been taken, but the devil is in the details," the MP
Saša Magazinović wrote on the day the decision passed. Half a year later the details
were still missing, and no patient had been prescribed cannabis.

## The obstacle

Bosnia and Herzegovina's [2005 drug law](/history/bosnia-narcotics-law-2005/) carries
a list that sorts substances into tables, and cannabis sat in the table of prohibited
ones. As long as it stayed there no doctor could prescribe it. Patients used cannabis
oil anyway, bought illegally; at a
[2023 conference](/history/bosnia-medical-cannabis-conference-2023/) Magazinović said
they should be able to buy it "in pharmacies rather than in dark parks".

A state task force had existed since 2016. What held things up was who should act:
the medicines agency said amending the list was not its job, and in the end the
Ministry of Civil Affairs did it.

## The decision

On 29 December 2025 the Council of Ministers unanimously moved cannabis, its resin,
extracts and tinctures to the table of substances under strict control. They can now
be medicines, on prescription, like other strictly controlled drugs. Growing cannabis
for medicine remains prohibited, so everything will be imported.

## Why nothing has reached a pharmacy

Prescribing rules are made by the entities, not the state, and those rulebooks did
not change. On [6 July 2026](/history/bosnia-rulebook-deadline-2026/) a meeting found
that "no concrete activities" had been undertaken, and gave the Federation's health
ministry until 1 August to draft rules on indications, prescribers, registers and
doses. Until then the medicines agency cannot send the UN control board the estimate
that must precede any import. No report found says whether the deadline was met.

The model is the narrowest in the region. North Macedonia licensed growers and later
revoked 26 licences, with 18.2 tonnes seized from 2020 to 2024;
Albania passed a law in 2023 and issued no licences; Serbia, Montenegro and Kosovo
prohibit medical use.

Because the route does not yet work, the entry carries no score change, and
[Bosnia and Herzegovina's](/country/ba/) access score stays at 0 until a pharmacy can
fill the first prescription.
