The Drugs (Prevention of Misuse) Act 20 of 1988 prohibited unauthorised
production, supply and possession of controlled drugs and separately
prohibited cultivation of any cannabis plant.[@1]

Its definition of cannabis covered the plant and every part of
it.[@1]

School-premises possession carried a rebuttable trafficking presumption,
with the burden on the accused.[@1]
