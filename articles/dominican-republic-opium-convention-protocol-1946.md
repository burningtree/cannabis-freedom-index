The Dominican Republic became a party on 11 December 1946 to the Protocol
of 11 December 1946, which transferred to the United Nations the League
of Nations' functions under the earlier drug treaties.[@1]

It thereby remained bound by the International Opium Convention signed at
Geneva in 1925, the first treaty to put Indian hemp under international
control.[@1]
