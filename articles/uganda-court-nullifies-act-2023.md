On 5 May 2023 Uganda's Constitutional Court struck down the entire Narcotic Drugs Act —
not over drugs, but over procedure: parliament had passed the 2015 law without a
quorum.[@1] The judiciary stressed that the ruling did not legalise cannabis.[@1]

Parliament moved fast. By August 2023 it had passed a replacement, a stricter law
carrying life imprisonment for trafficking and property forfeiture.[@2] That Act
commenced on 14 April 2025, restoring the ban on a harsher footing.[@3]

The episode is a procedural accident with substantive consequences: a quorum challenge
voided the drug law, and the replacement answered with the heaviest penalties in the
country's history — up to twenty years for possession, ten for use, life for
trafficking.[@3]
