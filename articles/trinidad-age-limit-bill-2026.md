Seven years after Trinidad and Tobago stopped punishing adults for cannabis, its
Senate voted to decide again who counts as an adult. For cannabis the answer would
be 25.

## The law it changes

Trinidad [banned ganja](/history/trinidad-ganja-ordinance-1925/) in 1925, ten years
after putting it under licence. The ban lasted until
[December 2019](/history/trinidad-decriminalisation-2019/), when adults were allowed
to hold up to 30 g and grow four plants each, with a ticket instead of prosecution
for up to 60 g. No shops were created.

## The bill

The Miscellaneous Provisions (Alcohol, Cannabis, Dangerous Drugs, Gambling and
Tobacco) Bill, 2026 resets four age limits at once.

| Activity | New minimum age |
|---|---|
| Drinking alcohol | 21 |
| Smoking tobacco | 21 |
| Gambling | 25 |
| Using cannabis | 25 |

Breaches carry a fine of 50,000 dollars.

## A vote on party lines

The Senate passed it early on 7 October 2026 by 15 votes to 12, with three
abstentions. All fifteen government senators voted for it. No opposition senator
did, and of the independents three abstained and the rest voted against.

The attorney general, John Jeremie, called the limits regulatory measures backed by
medical evidence and pointed to similar rules in the United States, Canada, Sri
Lanka, Lithuania and Singapore. The penalties, he said, were "proportionate" beside
the 25 years to life already in the Dangerous Drugs Act. "No piece of legislation is
perfect."

His critics asked why someone who can vote, marry, sign a contract and join the
protective services at 18 should be barred from buying a drink, and asked for local
figures on road deaths and harm among young people instead of foreign studies.

## What happens next

The bill goes to President Christine Kangaloo and becomes law when she signs it.

If it does, [Trinidad and Tobago](/country/tt/) will set the bar higher than the
two countries that legalised possession this year:
[Grenada](/history/grenada-decriminalisation-2026/) and
[Czechia](/history/czechia-home-grow-2026/) both chose 21.

The index has not moved the score. It records what the law is, and until assent the
law is still the 2019 one.
