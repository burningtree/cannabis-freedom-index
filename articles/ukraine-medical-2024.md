On 16 August 2024 Ukraine's medical cannabis law took effect. It let licensed companies grow
cannabis for medicine under police-monitored conditions, and patients receive cannabis medicines on
an electronic prescription. Months later, no patient had yet been treated with them.

## Five years of pressure

The campaign began with a petition. In January 2019 the Ukrainian Association of Medical Cannabis,
backed by sixteen organisations, put one on parliament's website, saying that more than two million
Ukrainians could benefit, among them people with epilepsy, cancer and post-traumatic stress. It
[passed the 25,000 signatures needed](/history/ukraine-medical-petition-2019/) in March, and in
April Volodymyr Zelenskyy said in an interview that he supported medical use. In a
[non-binding poll](/history/ukraine-national-poll-2020/) he called in October 2020, nearly
65 per cent of participants were in favour.

The bill, number 7457, won the health ministry's support in July 2023 and
[passed its first reading](/history/ukraine-medical-first-reading-2023/) days later with 268 votes.
Parliament [adopted it](/history/ukraine-medical-bill-adopted-2023/) on 21 December 2023 as Law
3528-IX, and Zelenskyy signed it on 13 February 2024.

## What the law allows

| | |
|---|---|
| Who may grow | Only licensed companies with a GMP certificate, under 24-hour video surveillance open to the National Police |
| Seed | Varieties whose dried straw holds no more than 0.2 per cent THC; 0.3 per cent for industrial hemp from February 2027 |
| Who decides the conditions | The health ministry, which approves the list of diseases |
| How patients get it | On an electronic prescription, carrying no more than one prescription's worth |
| Recreational supply | Remains a crime |

The ministry's list includes chronic and neuropathic pain, spasticity, nausea from chemotherapy,
Parkinson's disease, Tourette's syndrome, Lennox-Gastaut and Dravet syndromes, tuberous sclerosis, and
weight loss linked to HIV and AIDS.

## Waiting for medicine

The law came first; the medicine did not. In
[January 2025](/history/ukraine-first-products-registered-2025/) the first three products, oils made
by Curaleaf, were added to the national register of medicines, and on
[2 June 2025](/history/ukraine-first-import-permit-2025/) the medicines regulator issued the first
permit to import a cannabis substance. The government eased import rules for companies already
licensed to handle narcotics, with each import approved by the medicines service and the security
service. But clinical protocols, pharmacy registration and distribution were still being built,
and in early 2025 no patient had been treated.

For everyone else in [Ukraine](/country/ua/) nothing changed: recreational cannabis stayed illegal.
