The Act to Control Illicit Drugs, Controlled Chemicals and Equipment was
signed into law.[@1]

Possession, cultivation, use or supply of illicit drugs, including
marijuana, could bring a fine of up to 750,000 pa'anga or 25 years in
prison, and import or export up to 1 million pa'anga or 30 years.[@1]
