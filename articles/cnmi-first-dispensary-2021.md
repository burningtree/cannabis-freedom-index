Adults gained a licensed place to buy cannabis in the Northern Mariana Islands when Saipan Select opened in Tanapag in July 2021. It held a soft opening on 14 July and a grand opening two days later, nearly three years after legalisation.

## A legal home supply came before licensed shops

The [2018 law](/history/cnmi-cannabis-act-2018/) allowed adults to grow at home and share limited amounts without payment. Homegrown cannabis could not be sold, so that route could not supply a retail market.

The [2019 amendment](/history/cnmi-cannabis-registry-tax-amendment-2019/) filled gaps in fees, taxes and the homegrown registry. The commission then adopted business regulations and began processing applications.

Its licensing process included administrative and compliance reviews, approval in principle, an inspection and a board decision. Applicants therefore had several stages to complete before receiving permission to operate.

## Saipan Select grew cannabis beside its shop

The first store offered flower from an adjoining indoor farm with five thousand square feet of growing space. Customers could buy a gram, a joint or larger amounts up to one ounce at a time.

During a visit, production assistants showed the local newspaper the propagation and growing areas. The retail operation brought the cultivated crop into a shop where customers could view the finished buds and choose from a menu.

## Other growers and retailers were joining the market

The commission's report for the year recorded the first Class I production licence for T-Marianas in March. Saipan Select followed with production and retail licensing in May, and CanaMariana's received production and retail licences in July.

By the report's September snapshot, the commission had issued three retail licences, three producer licences and one lounge licence. It also recorded fifteen active homegrown-registry cards.

Those figures show the two routes developing alongside each other: registered cultivation for personal use and a licensed chain supplying commercial customers.

## A lounge added a place to consume cannabis

A [first cannabis lounge in Garapan](/history/cnmi-first-cannabis-lounge-2021/) was reported open in August. Adults could bring their own cannabis or buy from its adjoining shop, then consume it on site after an identification check.

Retail and lounge licences served different needs. One supplied cannabis to take away; the other offered a regulated place to use it. Together, they gave the [Northern Mariana Islands](/country/mp/) practical access beyond the home-growing route created by the legalisation law.
