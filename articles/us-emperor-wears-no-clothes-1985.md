Jack Herer describes completing the first *Emperor* in 1985, after gathering cannabis
information since 1973 with encouragement from Ed Adair. His project joined the right
to use cannabis to a campaign for industrial hemp.

## One plant, several arguments

The book ranges across fibre, paper, food, fuel and medicine. Herer argues that a plant
associated with intoxicating use had been denied an industrial and medical role, and
that restoring those uses should accompany ending prohibition. His introduction openly
presents the work as a campaign for reform.

This makes the book a source for the movement's arguments as well as a guide to earlier
documents. It does not make every claim in those arguments an established historical or
scientific fact.
