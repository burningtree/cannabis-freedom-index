Until 2017 a person found with a small amount for their own use could be dealt with under administrative law. From 2017 onwards, the civil liberties network Liberties reported, administrative penalties for possession were abolished, and what remained was the criminal code.

## The rule after 2017

| Conduct | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Possession for personal use | a fine, arrest, or up to two years in prison |
| Distributing a small amount | two to eight years |
| Distributing a large amount | eight to ten years |
| Bringing a small amount across the border | may be charged as smuggling: three to ten years |

## Sharing is distribution

The law draws no line between selling for profit and passing a joint to a friend. Both are distribution, and distributing even a small amount is classed as a serious crime.

That classification mattered more after a second change. Amendments to the penal code, presented as a measure against corruption, removed the automatic possibility of a suspended sentence for serious crimes. Courts had usually given non-custodial sentences in small sharing cases. After the amendments a suspension was still possible but depended on the judge.

## The objections

Judge A. Cininas opposed the amendments in public: "Such laws are destroying people's lives." His complaint was that punishment did not distinguish between dealing and handing something on.

A Liberal member of parliament, A. Armonaitė, tabled amendments to grade the penalties and to decriminalise possession and sharing among friends, replacing them with warnings and administrative fines. The health minister supported that approach. R. Karbauskis, chairman of the Farmers and Greens Union, did not: politicians, he said, should hold to zero tolerance. Several hundred people demonstrated outside parliament in 2016 and 2017 for legalisation.

## Why the index moved

Three scores fall by one point each. Possession goes from 2 to 1 and consumption from 2 to 1, because the administrative route was closed and any amount now leads into criminal proceedings. Enforcement goes from 3 to 2 for the same reason. Cultivation and sharing were already at 1. The total moves from 17 to 11.
