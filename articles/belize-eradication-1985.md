Until the 1980s Belize was the fourth-largest supplier of cannabis to the
United States, after Colombia, Mexico and Jamaica.[@1,2]

From the middle of the decade eradication campaigns backed by the United
States cut production sharply: aerial spraying began in 1982 with Mexican
and American help, and discovered marijuana was destroyed immediately by
air or by hand.[@1,2]

By 1994 production was negligible.[@1]
