At the national World Drug Day ceremony on Grande Comore, the interior
minister, Mohamed Ahmed Assoumani, said the police, gendarmerie, coast
guard and customs had seized 585 kg of cannabis between 2023 and 2025,
along with 15.5 kg of heroin and other substances, and that 27 overdose
deaths had been recorded in the same period.[@1]

The government described its response as combining security, health and
social action; the World Health Organization called for prevention and
better access to care.[@1]
