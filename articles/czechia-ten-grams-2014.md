When Czechia's Constitutional Court removed the government's drug-quantity table in 2013, it did not remove the criminal offence for possessing an amount “greater than small.” Judges still needed a way to decide where that line fell. In March 2014, the Supreme Court supplied guidance.

## From a decree to judicial guidance

The [2010 rules](/history/czechia-thresholds-2010/) had attached a clear figure to cannabis: up to 15 grams of dried material fell below the criminal threshold. The [Constitutional Court](/history/czechia-constitutional-court-2013/) later held that the executive could not define an element separating crime from non-crime in a government regulation.

That ruling restored the question to the judiciary rather than legalising every quantity. The underlying criminal-code language remained in force, so courts had to interpret what “greater than small” meant in individual cases.

## Ten grams and active substance

The Supreme Court's 2014 position gave police and courts an indicative standard. As later summarised by ČTK and *Seznam Zprávy*, an amount greater than small was generally one exceeding the usual dose of an ordinary consumer. For marijuana, the guidance used more than 10 grams of dried material containing at least one gram of active substance.

That made the practical possession boundary stricter than the invalidated 15-gram table. The assessment also depended on laboratory analysis of the material, not weight alone. The source describes how police dry seized cannabis and experts determine its active content before the legal classification is made.

## Cultivation remained separate

The judicial guidance concerned interpretation of criminal quantities after the regulatory table disappeared. It did not rewrite the separate treatment of growing. Five plants remained the established line below which cultivation was handled as an administrative rather than criminal matter.

Small possession also remained unlawful. Falling below the criminal threshold meant an administrative offence, not legal possession. Full legality within defined limits arrived only with the [2026 reform](/history/czechia-home-grow-2026/).

## What moved in the index

Nothing moved in the Cannabis Freedom Index. Although the indicative dried-cannabis threshold fell from 15 to 10 grams, possession below it was still an administrative offence and the same scoring band continued to apply. This entry records a real tightening inside that band for the [Czechia timeline](/country/cz/).
