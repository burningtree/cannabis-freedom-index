Three patients in Vinnytsia became the first people to receive prescribed cannabis medicines in Ukraine on 11 June 2026. A woman with multiple sclerosis and two wounded veterans received hard capsules containing delta-9 THC, beginning with a 5 mg dose to be adjusted by their doctors.[@1]

## A prescription became usable

Ukraine’s [medical cannabis law](/history/ukraine-medical-2024/) had taken effect on 16 August 2024, but patients needed medicines, licensed pharmacies and working prescribing systems before they could use it.[@1] The June dispensing turned that legal permission into treatment for patients with multiple sclerosis, neuropathic pain and phantom pain after amputation.[@1]

The Health Ministry’s patient guidance confines treatment to approved conditions and cases in which usual medicines fail or cause unwanted reactions.[@2] It lists contraindications, including pregnancy for THC-containing medicines and particular psychiatric risks, and provides for stopping treatment when it does not help.[@2]

## Six pharmacies could dispense

At the launch, six pharmacies belonging to one licensed operator could supply the medicines, in Vinnytsia, Dnipro, Lviv, Ivano-Frankivsk, Ternopil and Khmelnytskyi.[@1] That was a much smaller network than the 180 practice locations held by entities with relevant licences; the ministry expected around seventeen more pharmacies to join.[@1]

A pharmacy needs medicines and controlled-drug authorisations, and a quota to compound cannabis medicines.[@1] Registered or individually prepared medicines therefore reach patients through a controlled pharmacy channel.[@1]

## What a patient needs

A primary-care doctor or specialist issues the electronic prescription, which is valid for ten days.[@1] The patient provides the pharmacy with its number and redemption code; where preparation is necessary, the pharmacist arranges a return visit.[@1]

The patient guidance says the prescription identifies the medicine’s form, dose and active ingredients; paper prescription forms are outside this route.[@2] It also allows travellers to carry prescribed cannabis medicines for their own use within the amount covered by a doctor’s prescription.[@2]

## Preparing to reach more patients

On [21 July 2026](/history/ukraine-medical-digital-tools-2026/), the ministry asked doctors and pharmacies to prepare their digital systems for wider individual compounding.[@3] Prescriptions and dispensing run through the electronic health system, alongside cannabis stock tracking.[@3] Ukraine’s [country profile](/country/ua/) follows how that narrow supply route develops into practical access.[@3]
