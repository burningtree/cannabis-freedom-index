In territory under rebel administration, Fidel Castro's order made
harvesting, trafficking, carrying, keeping or smoking marihuana
punishable.[@1]

Cuba's later national prevention programme dates the order's entry into
force to 7 October 1958 and presents it as the start of the revolutionary
movement's campaign against drugs.[@1]

The order concerned rebel-administered territory; it does not establish the
first nationwide restriction.[@1]
