A beylical decree of 3 October 1884 laid down prohibitions and regulations on
Indian hemp and takrouri, or kif.[@1]

The 1953 decree that ended legal sales cites it, together with a decree of 1
August 1939 on toxic substances, as the earlier law: the prohibition and
regulations in the 1884 and 1939 decrees applied without distinction to all
types of hemp except hemp for textiles.[@1]

It is Tunisia's first cannabis decree on record — the taking of takrouri from
market to statute book.[@1]
