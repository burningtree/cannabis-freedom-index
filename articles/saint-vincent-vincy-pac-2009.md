Local police and soldiers from Regional Security System countries, Trinidad
and Tobago among them, burned cannabis fields and shanties in an anti-crime
operation.[@1]

Three men, one a Barbadian, were shot dead at Vermont on 21 May.[@1] Prime
Minister Ralph Gonsalves contrasted it with the US-led operation of December
1998, which he said was done for public relations.[@1]
