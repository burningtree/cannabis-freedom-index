On 8 February 2023 Vanuatu signed the rules for its licensed cannabis industry: the
regulations for importing, cultivating, harvesting, producing and exporting medical
cannabis and industrial hemp, setting the standards a licence holder must meet at
every stage.[@1] Licences could be applied for once the regulations took effect
through the government gazette.[@1]

## Export only, from the start

The rules implemented the Industrial Hemp and Medical Cannabis Act of late 2021,
which had created the licensing system and confined it to production for
export.[@2] That orientation never loosened: in January 2026 officials reaffirmed
that licences required secure management and banking, and that licensed products
could not be sold locally.[@3]

The drug law stayed severe alongside the industry. Unlicensed possession, supply and
growing draw up to twenty years or VT100 million, with courts grading small personal
cases below commercial ones — a prohibition the export licences do not touch.

[Vanuatu's profile](/country/vu/) reflects the split the [2021
Act](/history/vanuatu-hemp-cannabis-act-2021/) created and the [2023
rules](/history/vanuatu-export-cannabis-rules-signed-2023/) switched on: licensed
export hemp and medicine on one side, criminal recreational growing on the other.
