National Security minister Ryan Robinson Perinchief said drafting
instructions had been issued for hemp and medical cannabis reforms, with
initial implementation targeted for the final quarter of 2026.[@1]

The hemp changes were intended to clarify compliance for products already
being sold, while the medical work aimed to improve patient access.[@1]

He said a wider regulated adult-use regime would take longer because of
Bermuda's constitutional relationship with the UK and the need for further
coordination.[@1]
