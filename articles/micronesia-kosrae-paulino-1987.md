In Kosrae State v. Winfield Paulino, the State Court denies suppression of
two marijuana plants after an officer saw one from a road and police
obtained a warrant.[@1]

The court applies possession section 13.531 and explains that weight
determines the penalty category rather than whether possession is an
offence.[@1]

This suppression ruling does not establish a usual sentence or an exemption
for a small home grow.[@1]
