For less than two years Russia measured drug possession in doses. In May 2004 a government resolution fixed what counted as a single dose of each drug, and the Criminal Code made everything up to ten doses a matter for a fine. For marijuana a dose was two grams. Twenty grams in a pocket stopped being a crime.

## The dose and the multiplier

The Criminal Code had been amended to use a new measure, the "average single dose". Government Resolution No. 231 then set the doses.

| Drug | One dose | Criminal above (10 doses) |
|---|---|---|
| Marijuana | 2 g | 20 g |
| Hashish | 0.5 g | 5 g |
| Heroin | 0.1 g | 1 g |
| Cocaine | 0.15 g | 1.5 g |

More than ten doses was a "large" amount and more than fifty "especially large"; keeping a large amount could bring up to ten years. Below ten doses the penalty was administrative: a fine of 2,000 to 40,000 roubles or up to fifteen days of corrective work.

The change was steepest for heroin, where criminal liability had begun at 0.005 grams.

## The objections

The Federal Drug Control Service criticised the resolution, as did doctors and some members of the State Duma. The head of its legal department, Dmitry Kostennikov, said the rule emboldened small dealers, who carried exactly the permitted amount. The service calculated that the norms allowed a tonne of heroin to be held across Russia at any moment without punishment.

## A year later

On 20 May 2005 the speaker of the Duma, Boris Gryzlov, announced that the government had submitted amendments removing the "average single dose" from the Criminal Code. Only "large" and "especially large" amounts would remain, with the figures to be set by a new resolution that the Ministry of Justice was drafting.

The justice minister, Yuri Chaika, gave two reasons. A dose was hard to define by medical, social and legal criteria at once. And the concept had created the impression that drugs were being legalised.

Lev Levinson, of the group New Drug Policy, called the repeal a step backward. The liberal year, he said, had been a major achievement: by his count about 30,000 people who would have gone to prison for small amounts of marijuana had not.

The new thresholds arrived in February 2006. For cannabis the line fell at six grams, where it still is.

## Why the index moved

The [resolution](/history/russia-decriminalisation-2004/) raised [Russia's](/country/ru/) possession score from 1 to 3 and its enforcement score from 1 to 2, taking the total from 8 to 15. Level 3 on the possession scale is decriminalisation, with at most an administrative fine. The [cut to six grams](/history/russia-six-grams-2006/) took part of that back, and the total fell to 11.
