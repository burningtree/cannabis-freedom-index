Ten months after it [stopped punishing 15 grams and four plants](/history/antigua-decriminalisation-2018/), Antigua and Barbuda passed a second law. The Cannabis Act, 2018 went through the House of Representatives on 11 December 2018 and the Senate on 18 December. It does two things the first law had not: it sets up a licensed medical market, and it gives religious bodies rights beyond the ordinary limits. It does not legalise sale for general adult use.

## Medicine by recommendation

The Act creates the Antigua and Barbuda Medicinal Cannabis Authority. An authorised medical professional may give a "qualifying patient", someone with a serious illness or condition that significantly reduces quality of life, a numbered recommendation. With it the patient can apply for an identification card, valid for up to a year, and may then "obtain, possess, store and use medicinal cannabis".

A visitor holding a valid recommendation from another jurisdiction may use a licensed dispensary and is given a visitor patient card, but may not take the cannabis out of the country.

## Licences

The Authority licenses cultivation, dispensaries, the individual dispensers who work in them, lounges where patients may medicate, testing laboratories, processing and extraction, infused products, transport, and research.

## Sacrament

| Subject | Rule for registered adherents of a religious body |
|---|---|
| Who | adherents "including, but not limited to, the Rastafarian faith", registered with the Authority |
| Growing | more than four plants, up to an amount set by order, on land designated by the minister |
| Holding and carrying | more than 15 g, as authorised |
| Dispensing | at a sacramental dispensary, no more than 15 g to any one adherent |
| Where it may be used | a private dwelling, a place of worship or an event declared exempt; not in public spaces |

Importing or exporting cannabis for religious purposes without the minister's written authority, or letting a minor use it, brings a fine of up to $50,000 or up to twelve months in prison.

## Why the index moved

Only access changes, from 0 to 1. For the first time there is a lawful place to obtain cannabis, but it is open to patients with a recommendation and a card, and to registered adherents at their own dispensaries, not to adults in general. Possession and cultivation were already at 4 from the earlier Act, which covers everyone; the larger religious allowances apply to registered bodies only and do not change the general rule. The total moves from 36 to 37.
