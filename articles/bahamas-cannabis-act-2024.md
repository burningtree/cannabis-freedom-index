The Cannabis Act received assent on 26 July 2024. It lays out medical and
religious access, licences and a $250 fixed penalty for possession below
30 g.[@1]

But it allows different provisions to commence on dates appointed by the
minister — and the licensing, medical and fixed-penalty Parts were not among
the sections brought into force.[@1]

Enactment alone therefore did not change the operative rules: the Dangerous
Drugs Act still governs, and no licence applications had opened by July
2026.[@1]
