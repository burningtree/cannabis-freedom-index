Bermuda enacted powers to stop and search people reasonably suspected of
possessing illicit drugs, and vehicles, vessels or aircraft suspected of
carrying them, without a warrant.[@1]

Officers had to justify the reasonableness of their conduct.[@1]

A later inquiry into the 1968 disturbances said the law was being
interpreted too widely in marijuana policing.[@2]
