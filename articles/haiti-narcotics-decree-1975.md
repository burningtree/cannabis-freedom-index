The decree of 18 December 1975 governed the trade in and use of narcotics
and the offence of illicit trafficking.[@1]

It was amended by laws of 1979 and 1980, partly repealed by a law of 7 June
1982, and amended again by decree in 1989, before the 2001 law took its
place.[@1]

Its text was not read; it is known from the 2001 law's preamble.[@1]
