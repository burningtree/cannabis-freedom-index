The Act on counteracting drug addiction of 24 April 1997 made possessing drugs a crime in Poland, and in the same article said that a person holding a small amount for their own use would not be punished. The exemption lasted three years.

## Article 48

| Paragraph | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | Unauthorised possession: up to 3 years in prison |
| 2 | A lesser case: up to 1 year, restriction of liberty or a fine |
| 3 | A large quantity: up to 5 years and a fine |
| 4 | A person who possesses a small amount for their own use is not punished |

The Act did not define a small amount.

## The rest of the scale

| Conduct | Penalty |
|---|---|
| Putting drugs into circulation (Article 43) | 6 months to 8 years; 1 to 10 for a large quantity |
| Import, export or transit (Article 42) | up to 5 years; at least 3 for a large quantity |
| Giving a drug to another person (Article 45) | up to 2 years; up to 5 if to a minor |
| Supplying for gain (Article 46) | 1 to 10 years |
| Growing hemp other than fibre hemp (Article 49) | up to 2 years, restriction of liberty or a fine |

## Treatment

The Act paired punishment with care. A prosecutor could suspend proceedings against a dependent person, charged with an offence carrying up to five years, while they underwent treatment, and the case could then be conditionally discontinued. A court could attach treatment to a suspended sentence. Substitution treatment was allowed at public facilities licensed by the health minister.

The Act took effect three months after publication, on 15 October 1997.

## What happened to paragraph 4

In [2000](/history/poland-criminalisation-2000/) parliament deleted the exemption, and any amount became punishable. Possession cases rose from 1,896 in 1999 to 31,260 in 2007. A study of cases before the Kraków courts in 2008 found that about half concerned cannabis and that 79% of the marijuana cases involved less than three grams.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Poland](/country/pl/) from 27 to 21 with the [1997 Act](/history/poland-drug-law-1997/). Under the 1985 law unlawfully held drugs could only be confiscated; now possession was an offence with up to three years. Possession falls from 4 to 3, enforcement from 5 to 4 and consumption from 3 to 2. The exemption for small amounts is why the fall is no larger.
