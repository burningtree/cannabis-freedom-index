Police seized 3,894 cannabis plants from six bush allotments at Matahau
and Fou'i on 4 May 2017 and arrested eight men.[@1]

Four were sentenced on 7 June 2018.[@2]

It appears to have been larger than the Vava'u bust that media later called
Tonga's biggest.[@1]
