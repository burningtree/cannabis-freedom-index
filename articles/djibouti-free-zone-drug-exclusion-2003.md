Cannabis, hashish, cannabis resin and khat leaves were expressly barred
from entering Djibouti's free zones.[@1]

The decree otherwise opened those zones to goods of any origin, broad
commercial activity and extensive tax and customs exemptions.[@1]

The drug exclusion kept these substances outside the commercial privileges
offered to zone businesses.[@1]
