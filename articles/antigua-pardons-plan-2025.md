After its weekly meeting the Cabinet said it would bring a bill to the next
sitting of Parliament to pardon people convicted of possessing less than 15
grams of marijuana and to expunge their records.[@1]

The attorney general was asked to draft it.[@1]

Whether the bill has passed was not confirmed.[@1]
