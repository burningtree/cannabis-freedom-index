Law 122 of 1989 amended the narcotics law of 1960 and gave it the
penalties still in force.[@1]

Article 33 sets death and a fine of 100,000 to 500,000 pounds for
importing or exporting a narcotic without a licence, for producing one
for trade, and for growing or trading in the listed plants or their seeds
for trade.[@1]

Article 34 sets death or life at hard labour for holding, buying or
selling a drug with intent to trade, and death where an aggravating
circumstance applies, such as supplying a person under 21 or acting near
a school or place of worship.[@1]

Offering a drug to someone without payment brings life at hard labour and
a fine of 50,000 to 200,000 pounds under Article 35.[@1]
