In August 2024 President Filipe Nyusi did what no sitting Mozambican leader had done:
he publicly called for reflection on legalising cannabis, asking for studies on what
regulation could mean for the country.[@1]

The call landed in a system built for prohibition — a 1997 drug act, a 2024 Supreme
Court appeal testing search and evidence rules, and a 2025 seizure tally over 4.4
tonnes.[@1] Nyusi asked not for a bill but for thinking: studies first, positions
later.

Whether the studies come will decide if Mozambique joins the continent's slow
reconsideration or keeps the arrangement White found at Delagoa Bay in 1798:
smoke first, law later.
