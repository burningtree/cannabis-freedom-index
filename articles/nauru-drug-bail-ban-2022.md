People charged under the drug-control law, including cannabis offences,
were added to the cases in which bail could not be granted.[@1]

The restriction took effect on certification on 8 October 2022 and also
covered bail while appealing a conviction.[@1,2]

In a later one-gram cannabis case, the court ordered the defendants
released from remand after imposing fines and community service.[@3]
