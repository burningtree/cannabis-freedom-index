On 19 December 2019 the united criminal sections of Italy's Court of Cassation, the form the court takes to settle a conflict in its own case law, decided the case known as Caruso. Judgment 12348 holds that growing a few cannabis plants at home for one's own use is not a crime.

## The conflict it settled

Italian law punishes cultivation under Article 73 of the drug code, and for decades the courts treated any plant as enough.

| Year | Decision | Line taken |
|---|---|---|
| 1994 | Constitutional Court 443/1994 | growing for personal use could be treated like possession for personal use |
| 1995 | Constitutional Court 360/1995 | any cultivation is criminal |
| 2008 | Cassation, united sections, Di Salvia | cultivation is prohibited whatever its purpose |
| 2016 | Constitutional Court 109/2016 | negligible cultivation may be an "impossible offence" |

By 2019 the ordinary sections of the Cassation were split between a formal view, that a plant of the prohibited type is sufficient, and a view that the activity must really endanger public health. The third section referred the question upward.

## The rule

The court answered in two parts. Cultivation remains an offence regardless of how much active ingredient the plant holds at the moment it is found; a plant of the right botanical type, able to mature and produce the drug, is enough.

But cultivation of minimal size carried out at home falls outside the criminal law when these signs are present:

- rudimentary techniques;
- a small number of plants;
- a very modest yield;
- no indication of a link to the drug market;
- a product apparently meant only for the grower's own use.

## What stays

The grower is not left alone. Home cultivation for personal use falls under the administrative sanctions of Article 75, the same that apply to possession for personal use, which the commentary read here calls far from light. Anything professional or commercial stays a crime under Article 73.

The ruling is case law and no statute followed it, so each grower still depends on a judge finding that the signs are met.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Italy](/country/it/) from 23 to 26 with the [2019 judgment](/history/italy-home-growing-2019/). Cultivation rises from 1 to 2: a couple of plants on a balcony no longer mean a criminal trial, though they are still unlawful and the line is drawn case by case.
