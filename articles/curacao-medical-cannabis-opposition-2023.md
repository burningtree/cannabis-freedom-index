MAN and KEM lawmakers called for approval of Curaçao's medical-cannabis
export bill, according to reporting published on 7 April.[@1]

KEM's Michelangelo Martines and MAN's Giselle McWilliam wanted answers after
the proposal had been discussed twice and then stalled following a change of
government.[@1]

The campaign renewed the argument for a cannabis industry first advanced by
former minister Suzanne Camelia-Römer in 2018.[@1]
