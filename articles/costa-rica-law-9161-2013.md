Law 9161 amended Article 77 of the drug law so that sentences take
proportionality and the situation of women into account; in March 2012 some
120 women were in prison for bringing drugs into jails.[@1]

The same month President Laura Chinchilla pardoned seven women imprisoned
for trafficking.[@1]
