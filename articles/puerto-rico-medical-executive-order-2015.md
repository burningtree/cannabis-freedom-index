On 3 May 2015 Governor Alejandro García Padilla directed the health secretary
to follow the drug law's procedures, reclassify cannabis components as
appropriate and authorise their medical use.[@1]

The order argued that patients' health required distinguishing medical from
nonmedical use, and required a progress report within three months.[@1]

Regulations and licensing followed before dispensaries began serving patients
in December 2016.[@1]
