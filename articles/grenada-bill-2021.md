Agriculture minister Peter David laid a bill before the House that would
have let anyone over 18 hold 28 g and each homeowner grow five plants, and
allowed Rastafarian use in places of worship.[@1]

It died when Parliament was dissolved before the June 2022 election.[@1]

Five years later the 2026 Act passed with lower plant counts, a higher age
and a higher possession limit.[@2]
