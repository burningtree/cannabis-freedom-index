Order No. 173 established a special procedure for criminal acts related to
drugs and for robbery.[@1]

Until then drugs in Mali fell under the French poisonous substances law of
1926, amended by decrees of 1948, 1949 and 1952, which regulated their
trade, possession and use.[@1]
