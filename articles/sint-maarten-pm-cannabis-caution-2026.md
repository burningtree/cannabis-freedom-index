Prime minister Luc Mercelina told parliament that cannabis legislation
should follow a settled policy choice and the capacity to supervise it,
rather than a deadline within his government's term.[@1]

He distinguished legalisation from a commercial market and called for health
services, youth safeguards, legal and treaty review, and coordination
across the island's French border.[@1]

He left options ranging from continued prohibition to medical or broader
regulated models open.[@1]
