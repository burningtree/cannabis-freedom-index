Greenland’s health department proposed a national hash action plan
combining action against supply with prevention and treatment.[@1]

A later independent review says the full plan remained a proposal, while
some activities proceeded through the public-health programme.[@1]

In 2026 the government again emphasised prevention and customs
controls.[@2]
