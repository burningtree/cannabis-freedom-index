Circular 02-2010 of the Public Prosecutor directed prosecutors to turn away
police cases of simple possession with no criminal purpose.[@1]

A second circular in 2011 dealt with how to handle such police reports and
destroy the confiscated drugs.[@1]

Users stopped being prosecuted two years before any statute said
so.[@1]
