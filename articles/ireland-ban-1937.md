Ireland's ban on cannabis rests on the first Act the Irish Free State passed in 1934, the Dangerous Drugs Act, Number 1 of that year. The Act did not take effect at once. It was to come into operation on a day fixed by order of the Executive Council, and that day was 1 April 1937.

## Where cannabis sits in the Act

The Act is organised by substance. Part II covers "raw opium, coca leaves and Indian hemp", and it defines Indian hemp as the dried flowering or fruiting tops of the female plant of *cannabis sativa* from which the resin has not been extracted. The resins and all preparations based on them are included. Extracts and tinctures of Indian hemp are placed in Part IV, beside morphine and cocaine.

## What Part II does

| Section | Rule |
|---|---|
| 12 | Importing is unlawful without a permit from the Minister naming the quantity, the country, the port and the period |
| 13 | Exporting is unlawful without a matching export permit |
| 14 | The Minister may make regulations controlling or restricting production, possession, sale and distribution, and in particular prohibiting them except by licensed or authorised persons |

So the Act itself bans the cross-border trade outright and hands the rest to the Minister. Possession, sale and production inside the country were to be controlled by regulations made under section 14, and breaking such a regulation is an offence under the Act.

## The penalties

Section 33 sets one scale for every offence under the Act.

| Mode of trial | Maximum |
|---|---|
| Summary conviction | a fine of £50, six months in prison with or without hard labour, or both |
| Conviction on indictment | a fine of £500, five years of penal servitude, or both |

Everything involved in the offence was forfeited. Attempting or inciting an offence carried the summary penalties.

## Forty years

The 1934 Act remained Ireland's drug law until the [Misuse of Drugs Act of 1977](/history/ireland-misuse-of-drugs-1977/), which for the first time gave cannabis possession a lighter scale of its own.

## Why the index moved

The [1937 commencement](/history/ireland-ban-1937/) is the first step in [Ireland's](/country/ie/) score, from the unregulated state to 9. Possession, cultivation, sharing and consumption each fall to 1 and access to 0, since every dealing with the drug outside a licence became an offence that could bring prison.
