Croatia raised the ordinary minimum prison term for drug trafficking from one year to three years on 13 November 2025. The upper limit stayed at twelve years. The change affected the criminal supply offence, which includes producing or holding drugs intended for unlawful sale or distribution.[@1,2]

## The amendment changed the sentencing range

Parliament amended the minimum in Article 190(2) of the Criminal Code. The law appeared in the official gazette on 5 November and took effect on the eighth day after publication. For offences governed by the new range, the ordinary statutory sentence therefore began two years higher.[@1]

The offence covers several stages of supply: production, processing, transport, import, export and acquisition or possession for sale or distribution, as well as offering, selling and brokering sales. Its scope reaches someone holding cannabis for that purpose before a completed sale.[@2]

## Purpose separates possession from trafficking

Croatia had [removed personal possession from the criminal code in 2013](/history/croatia-misdemeanour-2013/), leaving it punishable as a misdemeanour. The Justice Ministry explained that this reduced criminal-court workloads and avoided a criminal-record entry for personal possession.[@3]

There is no statutory gram allowance separating personal possession from resale. Authorities assess the circumstances of each case. The ministry specifically explained that even a small amount can be treated as a criminal offence when the evidence shows resale; quantity alone does not decide the classification.[@3]

## Growing and giving have separate offences

Unauthorised production carries six months to five years, and the code expressly includes growing a drug-producing plant within production. Production for supply instead comes within the three-to-twelve-year range. Giving someone drugs to consume, or providing a place to consume them, has its own offence carrying six months to five years.[@2]

Specified cases involving children or protected settings carry three to fifteen years. Organising a dealer network carries at least three years, while specified supply offences causing death or serious harm to many people carry at least five.[@2]

## Confiscation had already widened

The [2024 amendment](/history/croatia-drug-assets-confiscation-2024/) brought drug-production and trafficking offences within extended asset confiscation. Where those offences generated financial benefit, the existing regime could reach assets disproportionate to lawful income unless the offender made a lawful origin plausible.[@2,4]

The [Croatia profile](/country/hr/) keeps these consequences alongside the separate penalties for personal possession, home growing and supply.[@2,3]
