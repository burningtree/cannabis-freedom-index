Cannabis was the only drug grown locally in Saint Lucia and met about 20
percent of local demand; the rest came from other Caribbean islands,
predominantly Jamaica and Saint Vincent, and from the United States, while a
significant portion was shipped from South America, including Colombia and
Venezuela.[@1,2]
