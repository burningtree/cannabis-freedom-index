From 29 November 2019 Rwanda's drug offence left no room for argument about what
being caught meant: the amended clause expressly covered being caught with narcotics
or psychotropic substances, alongside consuming them.[@1]

The penalty stayed where the 2018 law had put it — one to two years in prison or
community service.[@1] Separate production and supply offences kept their much
heavier tariffs, including a statutory life sentence for the highest category.[@1]

The clause matters because it closed the gap users once stood in: consumption and
possession became the same written offence, and community service became its
express alternative to a cell.
