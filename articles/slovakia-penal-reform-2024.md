Since [1994](/history/slovakia-possession-crime-1994/) Slovak law has punished holding a drug "for oneself". Until 2024 that offence went under one long heading with production and trafficking: "unlawful production of narcotic and psychotropic substances, poisons or precursors, their possession and trafficking". Act No. 40/2024, a wide amendment of the Criminal Code, rewrote the drug sections. The Act gives 15 March 2024 as its date of effect; a prosecutor's resolution from Košice shows the drug provisions being applied as the law "in force from 6 August 2024", with the old text in force until the day before.

## Four named offences

The reform gives each kind of conduct its own section.

| Section | Offence | Lowest tier, for cannabis |
|---|---|---|
| 171 | possession | up to one year in prison |
| 172 | growing plants or fungi, for own use | up to six months |
| 173 | production and trafficking | one to five years |
| 173a | precursors and equipment | — |

Possession is now defined: holding a drug, in at most a "larger" amount, that is not meant for distribution or profit. Acquiring it for one's own use counts as possession.

## Amounts, not doses

The old measure, the "usual single dose", is gone. A new annex fixes a "negligible amount" for each substance, for cannabis up to 2 grams of material or 200 milligrams of THC, and for plants at most two cannabis plants. Everything else is a multiple of that.

| Tier | Substances | Plants | Possession, cannabis | Growing, cannabis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| negligible | the annex amount | 2 plants | up to 1 year | up to 6 months |
| small | up to 5 times | up to 2.5 times | 6 months to 3 years | up to 2 years |
| larger | up to 250 times | up to 25 times | 1 to 6 years | 6 months to 3 years |

For drugs other than cannabis the lowest possession tier carries up to two years. A second possession offence within 24 months moves the offender into the one-to-six-year range whatever the amount.

## Applied backwards

Because the new text is milder, it applies to acts committed before it took effect. In the Košice case a man had been charged in July 2024 over 140 milligrams of a methamphetamine mixture found in his car in May. On 21 August the prosecutor reclassified the charge under the new section 171, found that the amount did not reach the annex threshold, and passed the case to the district office to be dealt with as a possible administrative offence.

## What did not change

Holding any cannabis is still a crime with a prison term attached, and so is growing two plants. The amendment lowers the scale; it does not take the user out of the criminal code.

## Why the index moved

Enforcement rises from 1 to 2. The smallest amounts now have a tier of their own, with a ceiling of a year for possession and six months for growing, and the new law reaches back to open cases. Possession and cultivation stay at 1, since both remain offences punishable with prison. The total moves from 9 to 11.
