Eritrea told the UN children's-rights committee that its 2015 Penal Code
was not yet enforced and identified the Transitional Penal Code as the
working law.[@1]

The published replacement contained separate personal-use drug offences
and a fine alternative, but its entry-into-force rule required gazette
publication.[@2]

A UN Special Rapporteur's 2025 report also said that the new codes had not
been brought into force.[@1]
