The agriculture ministry had awarded 57 licences for hemp cultivation and
production of derivatives by October 2024, good for six years.[@1]

Hemp products may contain up to 1.0% THC.[@1]

The first medical marijuana licence had gone to Azul Wellness in May
2023.[@1]
