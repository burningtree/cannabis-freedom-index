The government held public consultations in March and June 2019 on a
National Illicit Drugs Policy, which was approved by 2020.[@1]

The policy sat alongside the Drug Enforcement Taskforce launched in April
2018 and the Illicit Drugs Steering Committee set up in July 2018.[@1]
