Greenland’s parliament instructed the government to take steps allowing
cannabis products and oils containing no more than 0.2% THC.[@1]

The decisions sought access comparable with Denmark’s 2018 change, which
did not automatically apply in Greenland.[@1,2]

A local law and an authorised-import exception followed in January
2023.[@3,4]
