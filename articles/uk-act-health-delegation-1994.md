The [Alliance for Cannabis Therapeutics](/history/uk-alliance-for-cannabis-therapeutics-1992/)
worked to turn patients' experience into demands addressed directly to government.

Suzanne Taylor records delegations to the Department of Health in 1994 and 1997.
They included MPs, scientists and clinicians and sought access to herbal-cannabis
preparations for research and prescribing on a named-patient basis.

The proposal was more specific than recreational legalisation: patients wanted
medicine through clinicians while research proceeded. It also differed from the MS
Society's reluctance to press for immediate herbal access before clinical trials.

The entry records the first delegation's year, with the repeat visit in its description.
Neither meeting is treated as an enacted medical programme or a score change for the
[United Kingdom](/country/gb/).
