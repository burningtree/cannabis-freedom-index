In March 2022 Kenya rewrote the penalties of its narcotics Act. The amendment is usually remembered for its severity: thirty years in prison for a single gram. For cannabis it also did the opposite. It kept a separate, lighter rule for a person who holds bhang only to use it, and made that rule lighter still.

## One drug gets its own rule

Section 3 of the Act deals with possession. Since the amendment it singles out cannabis. A person who satisfies the court that the cannabis was intended solely for their own consumption faces up to five years in prison or a fine of up to 100,000 shillings. Before 2022 the maximum was ten years, with no fine as an alternative.

The court may add something else: committal to a court-appointed treatment programme, or voluntary rehabilitation, for at least six months.

No other drug is treated this way. For anything else, less than one gram brings a fine of at least five million shillings, at least five years in prison, or both.

## What happens when the court is not satisfied

The burden lies on the accused. If the court does not accept that the cannabis was for personal use, the next section applies, and it covers anyone who "trafficks in, or has in his or her possession" a narcotic.

| Offence | Penalty since 2022 |
|---|---|
| Cannabis solely for own use | up to 5 years, or a fine up to 100,000 shillings |
| Possession or trafficking, 1 to 100 g | at least 30 million shillings, or 30 years |
| Possession or trafficking, over 100 g | at least 50 million shillings, or 50 years |
| Smoking or otherwise using | at least 250,000 shillings, or at least 5 years |
| Growing a prohibited plant | 250,000 shillings or three times the crop's value, or up to 20 years |

The distance between the first row and the second is the whole of Kenyan cannabis law. Everything turns on whether a magistrate believes the amount was for the person's own use.

## Use and growing

The amendment did not soften the offence of use. Smoking, inhaling or otherwise using a narcotic, or being found without excuse in a place where people do, carries a fine of not less than 250,000 shillings or at least five years.

Growing was left as it was: up to twenty years, and where the grower owns the land, the court must order it forfeited to the government.

## Why the index moved

The [amendment](/history/kenya-amendment-act-2022/) raised [Kenya's](/country/ke/) possession score from 0 to 1. The scale gives 1 to a country where possession is a crime that usually ends in a fine or diversion, and a personal-use tier with a fine and a treatment option fits that description; a ten-year maximum with no alternative did not. The scores for growing and for sharing stay at 0, because twenty years for a plant and thirty for a gram passed on to someone else leave no room above it.
