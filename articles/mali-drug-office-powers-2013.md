Mali put operational coordination of drug investigations in the hands of a
central office with criminal-investigation powers.[@1]

Its director or delegate could authorise controlled deliveries and
undercover requests to sell drugs, with immediate notice to the relevant
prosecutor or judge.[@1]

The amendment updated the 2001 law's enforcement machinery; international
trafficking cases could be transferred to the office at the specialised
prosecutor's request.[@1]
