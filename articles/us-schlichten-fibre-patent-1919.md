Herer places Schlichten's decorticator in the story of a hemp industry he believes
prohibition prevented from flourishing. The patent supplies a firmer date for one
piece of that story.

US patent 1,308,376, *Means for Treating Fiber-Bearing Plants*, names George William
Schlichten of San Diego. Its first specification page records a filing on 27 December
1916 and a grant on 1 July 1919. Hemp is among the plants the machinery was designed
to process.

The book's reference to a machine patented in 1917 should not be used as the grant date
for this patent. Nor does the specification alone demonstrate production costs,
commercial adoption or the causes of later prohibition. It records an invention and
its claims, not a completed industrial transformation.
