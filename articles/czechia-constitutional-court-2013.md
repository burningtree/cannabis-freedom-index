On 23 July 2013, Czechia's Constitutional Court struck down the legal machinery that let the government set criminal drug quantities by decree. The decision was about who may define a crime, not a declaration that possession or growing was lawful.

## The challenged rule

The criminal code punished possession for personal use when the quantity was “greater than small.” A provision in section 289 authorised the government to specify those quantities. Government Regulation No. 467/2009 then supplied a table for more than one hundred substances, including cannabis.

The District Court in Liberec referred the question while hearing a prosecution involving methamphetamine. It argued that the quantity was an element determining criminal liability and therefore could not be fixed by a legal instrument below an act of Parliament.

## Only law may define the crime

The Constitutional Court agreed. Article 39 of the Charter reserves to law the decision about which conduct constitutes a criminal offence and what punishment may follow. The executive may issue implementing regulations, but it cannot take over a matter constitutionally reserved to Parliament.

The court annulled the words authorising the government to define an amount greater than small. It simultaneously invalidated the relevant section and annex of the government regulation. The annulment took effect when the judgment was published in the Collection of Laws.

## What the ruling did not do

The offence itself stayed in the criminal code. Possessing more than a small amount for personal use could still be prosecuted; the government table simply could no longer supply the decisive boundary. Courts again had to interpret the statutory phrase through judicial practice and the facts of each case.

That distinction led directly to the next milestone. In [2014](/history/czechia-ten-grams-2014/), the Supreme Court issued guidance that placed marijuana above the small-amount line at more than 10 grams of dried material containing at least one gram of active substance.

## What moved in the index

Nothing moved in the Cannabis Freedom Index. The judgment reassigned responsibility for defining the threshold but did not remove the offence or change the administrative treatment below it. The scores for [Czechia](/country/cz/) therefore remained unchanged while the source of the practical boundary shifted from executive regulation to the courts.
