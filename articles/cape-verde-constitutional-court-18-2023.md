Ruling 18/2023 of the Constitutional Court, published in the official
bulletin on 29 March 2023, concerned a man found with 15 g of cannabis.[@1]

He had been convicted of lesser trafficking under Law 78/IV/93 and
sentenced to 1 year and 6 months in prison, which two appeal courts
upheld; he argued the cannabis was for personal use.[@1]
