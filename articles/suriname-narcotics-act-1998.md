President Jules Wijdenbosch signed a replacement Drugs Act, retaining the
cannabis-growing ban and setting thirteen-year and life-or-twenty-year
penalty tiers for different deliberate cannabis offences.[@1]

The Act left commencement to a later presidential decision; it took effect
on 20 January 1999.[@1]
