In December 2019 Ecuador's National Assembly broke cannabis in two. The Organic Law
amending the criminal code, published in the Official Register on 24 December, took
non-psychoactive cannabis — the plant and any part of it with under 1% THC by dry
weight — out of the controlled-substances list and handed it to the National
Agricultural Authority.[@1]

## Medicine with a prescription, hemp with a licence

The same reform rewrote article 220 of the criminal code: holding or possessing drugs
containing cannabis as the active ingredient for therapeutic, palliative or medicinal
use, or for alternative medicine, stopped being punishable — provided a disease is
proven through professional diagnosis.[@1] Production and consumption of hemp were thus
decriminalised "with obvious restrictions".[@1]

The changes took effect in mid-2020, with the legislature ordering the agriculture
authority to issue the implementing regulations by October 2020 — adding to an
existing authorisation for producing, marketing and using controlled-substance
products for therapeutic or research purposes.[@1] Licensing followed through the
ministry's hemp rules, including the seven-licence scheme for seed, sowing and
production.

[Ecuador's profile](/country/ec/) reflects the split the reform created: licensed
below-1% production and diagnosed medical holding on one side, and the
[2019 change](/history/ecuador-medical-2019/) sitting between the
[2008 constitution](/history/ecuador-constitution-2008/) that made use a health matter
and the [2023 repeal](/history/ecuador-threshold-table-repealed-2023/) that removed the
10 g safe amount.
