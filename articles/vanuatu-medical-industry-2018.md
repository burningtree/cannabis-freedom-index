Vanuatu's cabinet backed a policy direction proposing medical-cannabis and
hemp production for overseas markets, with legislation still to
follow.[@1]

Agriculture Director Antoine Ravo saw agricultural potential, while
farmer-network representative Lavinia Kaumaitotoya warned about diversion
into illicit use.[@1]

The proposal was to be reviewed by Agriculture Minister Hosea Nevu before
parliamentary debate; the eventual licensing law began in January 2022.[@1,2]
