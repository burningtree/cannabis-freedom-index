Dominica's 1948 and 1951 Dangerous Drugs Orders were made under the
Dangerous Drugs Act, 1937 (Leeward Islands No. 23 of 1937), passed while
the island belonged to that federal colony.[@1]

The filing does not reproduce the law itself, so its exact treatment of
cannabis is not confirmed here.[@1]
