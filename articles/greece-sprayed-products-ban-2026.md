Twice in two months in 2026 Greece closed a gap in its cannabis law. The second
closure, in July, was aimed at a product that looks like cannabis, is sold as hemp
and is neither.

## The product

The law describes it precisely: plant material, such as hemp flower, to which a
natural or synthetic cannabinoid has been added, for example by spraying, and which
is then sold to be smoked or vaped.

## Two bans

In [May 2026](/history/greece-hemp-flower-ban-2026/) Law 5302 raised the THC limit
for hemp from 0.2 to 0.3 per cent and, in the same article, banned the sale of dried
hemp flower to consumers.

Article 84 of Law 5322/2026, in [Greece](/country/gr/)'s statute book since
24 July, goes after the treated product directly. It adds a paragraph to the
definitions of the drug law, Law 4139/2013.

## What is banned

Producing, trading, buying, selling or otherwise distributing any product that
meets two conditions:

- it is intended to be vaped, inhaled, burned or smoked; and
- it consists of plants or herbs to which cannabinoids, substances that mimic
  them, or other psychotropic substances have been added, by spraying, fogging,
  mixing or any other process.

The ban applies whatever the product is said to be for. The law states that such
products are not "cannabis products" in the sense of the medical cannabis rules.

## The penalty

Offenders are punished under Article 20 of the drug law, the article on
trafficking. That is a large step: a shopkeeper selling sprayed flower is now
treated as a dealer.

Buying is included as well as selling. Personal use of cannabis itself has been a
[minor offence](/history/greece-law-4139-2013/) in Greece since 2013, with at most
five months, normally suspended.

## The exemption

Pharmaceutical cannabis prepared and dispensed under the medical rules is excluded.
Greece has allowed it since [2018](/history/greece-medical-2018/), and patients
could get prescriptions from [2024](/history/greece-prescriptions-2024/).

## In the index

The index scores cannabis itself, and nothing changed for it, so the score does not
move. But the two laws together mark a clear direction: retail hemp flower was
banned in May, and treated plant material in July.
