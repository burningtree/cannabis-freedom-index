The 1908 ordinance set 1 March 1918 as the end of ganja imports.[@1]

The contemporary encyclopedia also described a ban on cannabis cultivation
and possession from that date, with pharmacy rules preserved.[@1]

Earlier imports had continued: the same work records 689 kg of ganja
entering in 1913.[@1]
