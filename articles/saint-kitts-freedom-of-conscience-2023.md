Parliament approved the Rastafari Rights Recognition Bill, which lets
registered groups grow and use cannabis as a sacrament at their places of
assembly, and the prime minister apologised to the Rastafari
community.[@2]

A companion Freedom of Conscience (Cannabis) Act lets adults apply for
licences to grow up to five plants in a secured part of a private home, to
carry limited amounts in permitted public places and to smoke in designated
areas.[@2]

The government made the application forms available in April 2026 and
waived the fees until the end of that year.[@1]
