Later summaries place a cultivation prohibition in 1884, but the Indian
Hemp Drugs Commission's 1894 account already describes cultivation as
prohibited in March 1879.[@1]

It identifies March 1884 with a different measure: export sale of
confiscated hashish to fund enforcement rewards.[@1]

The original instrument behind the separate 1884 cultivation claim has not
been established; this retained record is unresolved, not a verified first
cultivation ban.[@1]
