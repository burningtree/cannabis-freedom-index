Police statistics record 29.58 kg of hashish seized during 2025, compared
with 69.69 kg in 2024.[@1]

Drug-law reports fell from 107 to 62; that count covers all controlled
drugs.[@1]

The police state that small personal-use cases are prosecuted when
discovered while organisers and larger middlemen are their strategic
priority.[@1]
