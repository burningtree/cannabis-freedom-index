Tanzania's 2016 regulations defined a small quantity as no more than fifty grams of cannabis, or five grams of cannabis resin or oil.[@1] These limits supplied the amounts needed for a personal-use penalty tier already created by the drug law.[@1,2]

## The statute had left the amounts undecided

The [2015 Act](/history/tanzania-drug-control-act-2015/) distinguished small quantities intended for personal consumption from other drug offences.[@2] It told the minister to define those quantities in regulations.[@2]

The resulting rules were published on 13 May 2016.[@1] They defined cannabis herb as dried leaves and flowering tops and listed limits for cannabis, resin, oil and other drugs.[@1]

For cannabis the wording was inclusive: an amount of exactly fifty grams was within the stated small-quantity limit.[@1] Resin and oil had a separate five-gram boundary.[@1]

## Quantity was only one part of the personal-use rule

The Act required the holder to prove that a small amount was intended for personal consumption rather than sale or distribution.[@2] Being within a listed weight therefore addressed the quantity requirement, while the purpose of possession remained a separate question.[@1,2]

For drugs outside a specially listed group, the personal-use tier provided a fine of at least 500,000 shillings, a three-year prison term or both.[@2] Its alternatives meant a small quantity could still result in imprisonment.[@2]

The rule operated within a law that continued to prohibit unauthorised possession, use and cultivation.[@2]

## Trafficking thresholds used kilograms instead

The [2017 amendment](/history/tanzania-trafficking-thresholds-2017/) created a different boundary for trafficking: thirty years for fifty kilograms or less of cannabis, with larger amounts facing life imprisonment.[@3]

In [2021](/history/tanzania-trafficking-threshold-expanded-2021/), that trafficking boundary became one hundred kilograms and thirty years became the minimum for the lower tier.[@4]

Those kilogram limits divided severe trafficking punishments.[@3,4] The gram limits in the 2016 regulations served the separate small-personal-use rule, whose application also depended on proof of personal purpose.[@1,2]

## A fine was an option within a criminal system

The original Act combined the personal-use alternatives with a minimum thirty-year sentence for unlawful cultivation and life imprisonment for trafficking.[@2] The regulations gave a numerical definition to one part of that system.[@1]

For people holding cannabis in [Tanzania](/country/tz/), the practical distinction involved both the weight and the purpose of the material, followed by the court's choice between the penalties the law allowed.[@1,2]
