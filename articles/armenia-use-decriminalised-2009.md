On 5 July 2008 Armenia stopped sending people to prison for using drugs or holding a small amount for themselves. Two laws adopted on 26 May 2008 repealed the criminal offence of use and created administrative offences for small possession and for use.[@1,2]

## Two laws

Law HO-76-N repealed Article 271 of the criminal code, which had punished non-medical drug use. Law HO-77-N created Article 44.1 for small possession without intent to sell and Article 44.2 for use without a prescription.[@1,2] The administrative code set a fine based on a multiple of the minimum salary unit; at the time, the small-possession fine was 200 to 400 units and the use fine 100 to 200.[@3]

The reform did not legalise supply. Selling or distributing cannabis remained a criminal offence, and larger holdings stayed in the criminal code.[@3] It was a decriminalisation of the user, not of the market.

## What the fines mean now

The 2008 laws did not set the current 2.5 g line; the threshold came from later government quantity tables. In May 2026 the fine was doubled to 400,000–800,000 drams, with 800,000–1,100,000 for a repeat within a year. The use fine rose to 200,000–400,000 drams, or 400,000–800,000 for a repeat. The amounts are now fixed in drams rather than in multiples of the old salary unit.[@4] A person who voluntarily seeks treatment is exempt from the use penalty. The administrative code also covers cultivation below the criminal threshold.[@3]

The [1974 entry](/history/armenia-soviet-possession-crime-1974/) records the Soviet offence that the 2008 laws dismantled, and the [2026 entry](/history/armenia-penalty-increase-2026/) records the fine increase.

## A user reform, not legalisation

Armenia still has no legal supply. Small possession and use are administrative offences, sale carries three to six years, and cultivation becomes criminal above 2.5 kg of plant material.[@3] The 2008 settlement is therefore a narrow one: it took the user out of prison while leaving the market and the larger grower in criminal law. The [Armenia profile](/country/am/) sets out the current bands.
