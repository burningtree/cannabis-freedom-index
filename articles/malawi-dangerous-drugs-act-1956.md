The Federation's Dangerous Drugs Act (Act 25 of 1956(F)) commenced on 1 April
1956, consolidating the prohibition that the 1932 ordinance and the 1936
Noxious Weeds Act had built.[@1,2]

Its section 19 made any contravention — including unlicensed possession and
cultivation under the regulations — punishable with a K500,000 fine and life
imprisonment, as amended in 1995; the provision as consolidated reads a fine of
K500,000 and imprisonment for life.[@1]

It remained the recreational prohibition behind the licensed 2020 system.[@1,2]
