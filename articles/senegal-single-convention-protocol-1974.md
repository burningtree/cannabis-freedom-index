Senegal ratified the Protocol amending the Single Convention on Narcotic
Drugs on 25 March 1974.[@1]

The Protocol, adopted in Geneva on 25 March 1972 and in force from 8
August 1975, strengthened the international control board and, among other
changes, lets parties provide treatment, education, after-care and
rehabilitation for people who use drugs, as an alternative to conviction
or punishment or in addition to it.[@1]
