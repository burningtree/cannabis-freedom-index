The French drug observatory's 2021 survey found cannabis production on
Réunion local and small-scale, built on acquaintance networks, with no
resin processing on the island — resin arrived imported by post or by air
mules.[@1]

Local zamal quality had declined for a decade while its price rose from
about €0.50 to €5 a gram, and imported skunk at €10 a gram was filling the
gap.[@1]

Many residents wrongly believed one or two plants for personal use were
legal.[@1]
