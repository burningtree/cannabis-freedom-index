Kyrgyzstan’s replacement codes took effect on 1 December 2021, bringing small drug possession and small unauthorised cultivation into the administrative offences system. The previous misdemeanour code had provided fines and probation-style restrictions on liberty. The change altered the penalties facing people with small personal quantities or small grows.[@1,2,3]

## The previous rules could restrict daily life

Under the [code used from 2019](/history/kyrgyzstan-new-codes-2019/), small possession without intent to supply and small prohibited cultivation were misdemeanours. An adult’s basic restriction-of-liberty category ran for three to six months and involved probation supervision.[@2]

People under that restriction had reporting duties and needed permission to leave the country. Although a misdemeanour finding created no criminal record, it still imposed a penal response that could affect everyday movement and decisions.[@2]

## Small growing became an administrative fine

The replacement offences code set an individual’s small unauthorised cultivation penalty at 125 calculated units. It also covered a landholder’s failure to destroy wild drug-containing plants. Small possession without intent to supply carried a 200-unit fine or 40 hours of community work.[@3]

The distinction mattered to growers as well as people holding harvested cannabis. Cultivation had its own small-quantity rule, while possession and transport without supply intent were covered separately. Larger conduct remained within the Criminal Code.[@3,4]

## Larger quantities could still mean imprisonment

The criminal rules provide a fine or up to five years in prison for large personal possession, rising to five to ten years for an especially large quantity. Large cultivation, or cultivation by a group acting by prior agreement, also carries a fine or up to five years, with a higher range for especially large cultivation.[@4]

The cabinet’s quantity tables determine which band applies. In the later table, dried marijuana has a small-quantity ceiling of 20 grams and an especially-large threshold above 600 grams. Dried resin has different limits: three grams and 90 grams respectively.[@5]

## The new system kept personal conduct and supply separate

Small possession’s administrative treatment depends on the absence of intent to supply. The Criminal Code’s ordinary supply offence carries five to eight years in prison and confiscation, with higher ranges for aggravated cases.[@3,4]

The Supreme Court’s [later guidance on drug cases](/history/kyrgyzstan-drug-court-guidance-2025/) addressed how courts distinguish personal possession from supply. The [country profile](/country/kg/) brings together the administrative rules, criminal penalties and current quantity bands.
