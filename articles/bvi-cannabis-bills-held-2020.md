Governor Augustus Jaspert said a BVI cannabis licensing authority required
preparatory work and an agreement with the UK Home Office before the
licensing bill could receive assent.[@1]

The UK Foreign Secretary also requested the separate record-clearing bill
for further consideration, citing trafficking and security concerns.[@1]

A July 2026 legal review still recorded both bills as unassented.[@2]
