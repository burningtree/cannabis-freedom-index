The drug law's exemption, revised in 2005, covered a personal-use
preparation in a reasonable amount, correctly labelled and accompanied by a
doctor's letter from the traveller's country of origin.[@1]

The same law separately provided medical or scientific import permits and
exemptions for medicines properly prescribed and supplied by qualified
practitioners or pharmacists.[@1]

Cannabis and resin remained listed drugs, with cultivation prohibited
outside the later licensed industry route.[@1,2]
