In May 2025, three and a half years after Malta legalised cannabis, parliament amended the law
unanimously. The changes kept every personal allowance: adults may still carry 7 grams, keep 50 at home
and grow four plants. They tightened the rules for the clubs and created a new offence: a smell that
bothers the neighbours.

## The mood before

Use had not risen. A Eurobarometer survey published in December 2024
[put past-year use](/history/malta-eurobarometer-2024/) at 4 per cent, down from 7 per cent in 2021. But
public patience was thinner. Support for a ban had grown by eight points to 46 per cent, and 49 per cent
saw cannabis smoked in public as a problem where they lived, up 14 points.

## What changed

| Measure | Rule from 2025 |
|---|---|
| Smell | A €235 fine when cannabis smoke bothers neighbours, even from inside a home |
| Minors at associations | Fines of up to €10,000 |
| Location of associations | At least 250 metres from schools, sports facilities and youth centres |
| Associations' offences | Heard by a Court of Magistrates |
| Definition of cannabis | Covers THC and CBD; non-psychoactive cannabinoids with under 0.2% THC excluded |

## Who smells it?

The odour fine was the hardest to apply. On 20 May the police commissioner, Angelo Gafà, told MPs that
"the police are not experts in odour" and that he could not tell whether a smell was cannabis, a cigar
or a vape. He urged them to pass laws that could actually be enforced.

The job went instead to the regulator, the Authority for the Responsible Use of Cannabis. From mid-June
it [took complaints](https://tracker.cannabisfreedom.fyi/report/malta-odour-enforcement-2025/) on a freephone line. Complaints could not be
anonymous and had to say where the smell came from; officers would first talk to those involved and
recommend remedies, and fine only if the nuisance went on. Its chair, Joey Reno Vella, reminded the
public that smoking in public was still illegal.

The parliamentary secretary responsible, Rebecca Buttigieg, said the 2021 law had met its harm-reduction goals without
raising use among people who did not already use cannabis. In law, what [Malta](/country/mt/) allows adults to hold and grow is unchanged since
[2021](/history/malta-legalisation-2021/).
