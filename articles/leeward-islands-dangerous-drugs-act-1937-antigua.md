The Dangerous Drugs Act, 1937 (No. 23 of 1937) of the Leeward Islands federal
colony, which included Antigua, was still the law its government applied to
new drugs in 1951.[@1]

The filing does not reproduce the law itself, so its exact treatment of
cannabis is not confirmed here.[@1]
