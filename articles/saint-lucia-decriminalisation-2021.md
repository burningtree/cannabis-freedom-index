Saint Lucia removed the offence for holding a small amount of cannabis in September 2021. The Drugs (Prevention of Misuse) (Amendment) Act, No. 13 of 2021, passed the House of Assembly on 14 September and the Senate on 16 September, received assent on 23 September and is dated 27 September.

## What the act says

The act is six sections long and adds three provisions to the drug law.

| New section | Rule |
|---|---|
| 8A | A person in possession of 30 g or less of cannabis or cannabis resin is not guilty of an offence, and the penalties of section 37 do not apply to that quantity |
| 14A | A child or young person found with cannabis must take part in an approved drug counselling programme; the family may be asked to contribute to its cost |
| 27A | Smoking cannabis in a public place, or knowingly permitting it on one's premises there, is an offence with a fine of up to 1,500 East Caribbean dollars, which does not form part of the criminal record |

It also raises from 15 g to 30 g a threshold in section 8(4) of the principal Act.

A public place means any structure, facility or space open to the public, and the act names bars, restaurants, clubs and government offices. An establishment may set aside an area where smoking is permitted. A privately occupied home not used for a commercial purpose is not a public place.

## Home growing

The act itself says nothing about plants. Regulations published in the Gazette in December 2021, the Drugs (Prevention of Misuse) (Cannabis Cultivation) Regulations, allow a household to grow four plants.

## What it left alone

Possession of more than 30 g is still an offence under the ordinary rules, and so is supplying cannabis to another person. Neither the act nor the regulations license anyone to sell, so there is no lawful place to buy.

## Why the index moved

The index moved [Saint Lucia](/country/lc/) from 11 to 39 with the [2021 act](/history/saint-lucia-decriminalisation-2021/).

| Segment | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Possession | 1 | 5 |
| Cultivation | 1 | 4 |
| Enforcement | 2 | 6 |
| Consumption | 1 | 3 |

Sharing stays at 1 and access at 0. The step is the same size as [Dominica's](/history/dominica-decriminalisation-2020/) a year earlier, which followed the same pattern of a possession limit, home plants and a non-criminal fine for public smoking.
