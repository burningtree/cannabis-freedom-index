The Anti-Drug Abuse Act of 1991 raised penalties for Schedule I and II
manufacture, delivery and possession, added school-zone and under-18
delivery terms, allowed inter-island customs searches and required a drug
treatment plan.[@1]

Cannabis was Schedule I, so its penalties rose; simple possession stayed
criminal.[@1]

The law took effect on 31 October 1991.[@2]
