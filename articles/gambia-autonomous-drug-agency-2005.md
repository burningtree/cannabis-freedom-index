Drug enforcement moved into an autonomous National Drug Enforcement Agency
through a 2005 amendment.[@1]

The agency took responsibility for enforcing, regulating and coordinating
action against illicit drug trafficking and use, following earlier policing
by a specialised unit of the police.[@1]

A further amendment renamed it the Drug Law Enforcement Agency, The Gambia,
in 2014.[@1]
