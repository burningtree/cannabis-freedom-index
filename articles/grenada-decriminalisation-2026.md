From February 2026 adults aged 21 and over may possess 56 g of cannabis,
and four plants at a household are outside the growing offence; specified
old convictions are cleared and sale remains an offence.[@2]

The agriculture ministry says growers register before planting, with
registration rules to follow.[@3]

Parliament passed the bill with support from both sides.[@1]

The same Act creates a separate offence for smoking on school premises, or
in public/open areas within 100 yards during school hours, carrying up to
seven years summarily or twenty years on indictment, with a private-dwelling
exception.[@2]
