Decree 46-2020 set 25 June 2020 as the day the criminal code commenced.[@2]

Article 632(6) repealed articles 16 to 30 and 41 of the 1989 drug law,
including the personal-consumption security measure.[@1]

Article 311 now punishes sowing, cultivation, trafficking and possession for
those purposes.[@1]

Article 321 treats cannabis as a drug that does not cause grave harm, so the
basic sentence is four to seven years, or one to three years when the
circumstances show lesser gravity.[@1]

The repeal does not declare that holding cannabis for oneself is lawful.[@1]
