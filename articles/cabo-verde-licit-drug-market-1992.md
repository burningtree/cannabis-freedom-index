Decree-Law 92/92 established controls over the licit controlled-drug
market.[@1]

Article 6 prohibited cannabis cultivation while allowing Health Ministry
licences for scientific or teaching purposes; other provisions regulated
controlled medicines and prescriptions.[@1]

The date records publication, not proof of an operating cannabis patient
service.[@1]
