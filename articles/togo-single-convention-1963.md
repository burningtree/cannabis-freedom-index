Togo acceded to the Single Convention on Narcotic Drugs of 1961 on 6 May 1963.[@1]

The convention lists cannabis and cannabis resin among its controlled drugs.[@2] It obliges its parties to limit the production, trade, possession and use of those drugs to medical and scientific purposes.[@2]
