Premier Natalio Wheatley said talks with the UK had identified a route
towards licensing local doctors to administer medical cannabis.[@1]

He proposed considering regulations under existing legislation while
reconsidering the unassented 2020 licensing bill.[@1]

Work on the approval arrangements was still to be completed.[@1]
