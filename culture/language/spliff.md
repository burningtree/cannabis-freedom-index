*Spliff* is a Jamaican word for a hand-rolled cannabis cigarette. It is first recorded in 1936 and nobody knows where it comes from. It travelled from the Caribbean to Britain and from there across Europe, and on the way its meaning shifted from "a ganja cigarette" to "a ganja cigarette with tobacco in it".

## In Jamaica

In the West Indies a spliff is simply a cigarette of [ganja](/culture/language/ganja/). It normally contains a very small amount of tobacco, called *grabba*, which is chopped on a board with a knife.

It is the usual form in which [Rastafari](/culture/traditions/rastafari-sacrament/) take their sacrament: a large hand-rolled cigarette, rolled while a prayer is said and lit only when the prayer is finished. The alternative is the chalice, a water pipe.

## In Britain and Europe

The word spread to Britain and through the rest of Europe. There it met a different product. For decades the cannabis available in Europe was almost entirely imported [hashish](/culture/language/hashish/), a dense resin with the consistency of modelling clay. It can be smoked in a pipe, but to be smoked in paper it has to be crumbled into tobacco; a hashish cigarette without tobacco will not stay alight.

So the European joint was a tobacco cigarette with hashish in it, and *spliff* became the word for it. Some speakers now use the two words to keep the kinds apart.

| Word | Where | What it usually means |
|---|---|---|
| Spliff | Jamaica | A ganja cigarette, with a pinch of grabba |
| Spliff | Britain, Europe | Cannabis rolled with tobacco |
| [Joint](/culture/language/joint/) | North America | Cannabis alone |
| Joint | Europe | The same as spliff |

In Europe and some Commonwealth countries, and more recently in North America, the cigarette is rolled round a mouthpiece of thin cardboard or a cigarette filter, which the British call the *roach*.

## Cannabis with tobacco

Mixing the two is older and wider than the word. A Moroccan smoking [kif](/culture/language/kif/) in a sebsi and an Indian sadhu with a [chillum](/culture/traditions/sadhus-and-the-chillum/) are also taking cannabis with tobacco. *Spliff* is the name the habit acquired in English.
