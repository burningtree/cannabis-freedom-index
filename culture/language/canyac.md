Panama's courts had a local name for the plant before they had a modern drug law. The
1935 Judicial Register calls cannabis "commonly known locally as Canyac or Kan Jac" —
the only recorded Panamanian Spanish name for it.
[Panama calls cannabis Canyac in 1935.](/history/panama-local-name-1935/)
