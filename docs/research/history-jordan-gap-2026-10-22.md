## Material corrections

- The existing `jordan-first-time-users-2015` event now dates the first-use treatment guarantee to **16 May 2013**, keeping its existing ID and URL. The original amendment requires referral within twenty-four hours without prosecution or a judicial precedent. It also requires a special administrative register: this is not erasure of every record.
- The 2016 replacement permits prosecution of first-time users and judicial substitution of inpatient or outpatient treatment. Its original first-offence record protection already includes personal growing and manufacture. The 2021 amendment therefore did not first protect those acts or remove criminal intent.
- The 2016 death provision retains an international-gang trafficking clause present in the earlier amended drug law. It is a tagged, explicitly unscored retention event, with a new article explaining ordinary trafficking, aggravated trafficking and personal use separately.
- The September 2021 Lower House vote is now **debate**, dated 1 September, distinct from the enacted amendment operative **23 October 2021**. The enacted change is **other**, reflecting its mixture of penalty tiers, record protection, wider distribution control and licensing authority.
- The regulator’s September 2026 schedules put **cannabis plant material in table 3**, with personal-use punishment of **one to six months OR JOD200–500 OR both** under article 9(a)(4). **Resin remains in table 1**, with **one to three years AND JOD1,000–3,000** under article 9(a)(1). The old profile’s blanket one-to-three-year statement was wrong for current plant material.
- The 2023 instructions supply a product approval/prescription route for CBD natural medicines/preparations at no more than 0.3% THC, alongside external-use cosmetics/devices and hemp seed foods. Two events and articles distinguish those product routes. Medical CBD is filed under medical by the category priority; its narrow product rules are explicitly unscored.

## CBD and food details

- CBD natural medicines/preparations at no more than **0.3% delta-9 THC** require classification/approval and a valid medical prescription. Medical import/export requires a controlled-substances-section stamped invoice. Manufacturer approval, quality evidence and stability testing are specified.
- Finished external-use cosmetics/devices in a non-abusable form have a particular exemption from separate controlled-substances import/export approval; this is not general permission for all CBD retail. Other product rules still apply. Cosmetic/device manufacture under these instructions is external-use only, with raw-material controls and annual quantity/buyer reports.
- Food ingredients are hulled, **nonviable** hemp seeds and specified derivatives, with a source-plant maximum of **0.1% THC in leaves and flowering tops**. That crop condition is not a general permission for personal growing.
- Annex food limits: hulled seeds/hemp hearts **5 mg THC/kg**; seed oil **10 mg/kg**; hemp seed milk/beverages **0.2 mg/kg**; flour/protein/seed cake **5 mg/kg**. Each listed category has a **75 mg CBD/kg** ceiling. Seed oil requires an unsuitable-for-cooking label. Plant imagery is prohibited except seed pictures, with restrictions on hemp promotion and psychoactive claims in packaging and advertising.
- No named registered medicine, actual pharmacy availability or visitor/foreign-prescription import route was established. No broad medical-flower programme is claimed.
