# History expansion and articles: Soviet controls and Denmark

Continued the article backlog, then expanded the pass to new history coverage
following the user's updated objective.

## Examined sources and resulting changes

Read the full Historical Materials transcriptions of the USSR decrees of
[27 October 1934](https://www.istmat.org/node/42491) and
[11 March 1934](https://www.istmat.org/node/39909).
The former expressly specifies operation from 1935; its adoption date must not
be treated as verified commencement. The latter distinguishes its issue date,
publication on 12 March and grain-rate application from 15 March.
The site identifies its source as a Moscow 1948 compilation. Original gazette
scans were not inspected; these are primary legal texts through transcriptions.

Read the complete operative text of the
[RSFSR decree of 1 December 1934](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/Постановление_ВЦИК_и_СНК_РСФСР_от_01.12.1934_о_дополнении_Уголовного_кодекса_РСФСР_статьей_179-а)
on Russian Wikisource. It attributes the reproduction to LibUSSR; neither the
underlying gazette nor an independent scan was examined. Criminal-code penalties
are attributed to this reproduction, not to observed sentencing practice.
Separate commencement of this republican amendment was not established.

Added two checked, unscored records:

- `soviet-hemp-production-incentives-1934`: industrial-hemp production incentives
  and their conditions, using the decree's issue date.
- `russia-indian-hemp-criminal-code-1934`: the RSFSR amendment's sowing offence,
  alternative penalties and mandatory crop confiscation, using its issue date.

Added `russia-soviet-ban-1934.md`, distinguishing adoption from operation,
Indian-hemp restrictions from industrial production, and statutory punishment
from actual enforcement. Both new events link to the existing union decree;
the article links to both. No modern THC threshold is inferred.

For Denmark, read the complete official parliamentary
[L 51 summary and legislative chronology](https://www.folketingstidende.dk/samling/19541/lovforslag/L51/index.htm).
Read the TNI report's title/authorship and printed pp. 2–3 on the legal framework
and enforcement history. Added `denmark-euphoriants-act-1955.md`, distinguishing
the new possession offence from later warning and fine policies. The summary
supports Law No. 169's date and the rise of the prison maximum from six months
to two years. It is not a substitute for independently examining the original
enactment and controlled-substance schedule.

The web reader could not decode the original Danish enacted-bill PDF; the
1969 Retsinformation circular opened as a JavaScript shell. Neither is claimed
as independently read in this pass. The historical warning discussion follows
the TNI report and links to the existing 1969 entry.
