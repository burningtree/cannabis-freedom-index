## Corrections and limits

The old 1969 description attributed eradication to USAID's Office of Public
Safety and claimed aerial spraying. Replaced it with the directly documented
method: military reconnaissance aircraft locating crops, then security forces
cutting and burning them. This does not establish whether spraying occurred
at another time; it removes an unsupported claim from this event. The month
comes from the command history, not an estimate.

Both existing events are classified `enforcement`: the verified sources
describe a suppression directive and its application, without establishing a
new first prohibition or statutory penalty. The 1968 event retains its stable
ID and all existing score vectors are preserved without revalidation. As noted
in the earlier research, the large 1968 score step requires separate historical
reconstruction; this article must not imply that cannabis was unrestricted
before it. The dog deployment has `military` and `police-powers`; the crop hunt
has `military` and `eradication`. Neither new context nor more sources invents
a numerical score change.
