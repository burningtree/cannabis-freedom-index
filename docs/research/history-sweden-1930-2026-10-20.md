# Sweden’s 1930 cannabis controls — 20 October 2026

Added an article on the 1930 rules, replaced Wikipedia-only evidence with
government histories, rewrote the existing 1876 description and added the
missing 1933 sentencing event. Applied the current writing guide and category
precedence. The 1930 event is `tightening`, since the timeline already documents
earlier cannabis sales restrictions. The new 1933 event is `tightening`, tagged
`sentencing`; the 1876 sales event gains `retail`.

## Evidence and limits

- Read SOU 1967:41, part II, pp. 25–26, from the digitised text at Lagen.nu.
  Followed its National Library URN and downloaded the actual government report
  from `weburn.kb.se`; extracted and visually checked printed p. 26, PDF p. 29.
  The first attempted Lagen.nu PDF URL returned HTML, which was not treated as
  a PDF or statutory evidence.
- The government committee explicitly identifies the decree dated 17 October
  1930, cannabis/coca additions, prohibition of hashish imports, exports,
  manufacture, possession and offering for sale under section 8, and the new
  unauthorised-possession offence with fines under sections 6 and 10. It also
  identifies the 16 September 1933 decree, imprisonment in aggravated cases
  under section 12, and penalties for prescribers exceeding their authority.
- Read the government’s SOU 2008:120, chapter 4, pp. 158–159, in the actual
  Riksdag HTML, downloaded and retained locally. It connects the 1930 cannabis
  controls to the Geneva convention and independently describes earlier poison
  rules and 1933 imprisonment. Its account relies on Hoflund, so those two
  sources are not represented as independent historical investigations.
- Downloaded Hoflund’s third edition and read pp. 13–16; visually checked pp.
  14–15 and the book’s publication details. Used the 1876 and treaty chronology,
  without importing the separate ether/alcohol court case’s penalties into
  cannabis law. The monograph is original legal scholarship, not the original
  nineteenth-century ordinance.
- Neither original unamended 1930 nor 1933 decree was recovered. Their dates
  are dates of instruments identified in the government report, not verified
  commencement days. Retained year-only event dating. No precise prison term,
  list of aggravating factors or full medical-exception scheme is inferred.
  The original 1876 law also remains uninspected, as earlier research records.
- The text says that unauthorised possession brought fines, without taking the
  1923 maximum fine and assuming it applied identically to every 1930 cannabis
  offence. The hashish prohibition is distinguished from the general rule
  requiring authority to possess narcotics.
- No claim of a particular cannabis campaign, ministerial motive, arrest count,
  enforcement intensity or drug prevalence is invented. Treaty context is in
  the sources. The article’s final link to 1968 refers to the existing sourced
  entry, without treating the 1967 bill as enacted proof of all later changes.
- All existing score vectors are retained without revalidation. The new 1933
  event has no vector and explicitly records that its separate effect has not
  been reconstructed. No current profile or score was changed.
