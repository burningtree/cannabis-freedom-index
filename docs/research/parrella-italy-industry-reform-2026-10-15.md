## Withheld claims and next pass

Do not import the book’s nutritional “vaccine” description, environmental ratios, profitability
figures or generalised medical claims. Its ESPAD figures mix the sample with population estimates;
500,000 out of 2.5 million is not 5%, and 21.43 minus 19.4 is not 2.29 percentage points. Those
passages need original statistical tables before use. Avoid claiming the 1973 congress was the
first Italian drug campaign or an international congress without the original programme.

The 1998 prescribing entry remains a lead for a primary-source audit, rather than something
verified by this pass. Next substantive reading: **Scenari per il nuovo millennio**.
