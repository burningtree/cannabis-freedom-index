## Carneiro: a useful source-genre warning

Chapter 9 cites Carlos Franqui, *Retrato de Família com Fidel* (Record, 1981),
p. 98, for a remembered Ginsberg/Valdés exchange. The original memoir was not
examined; no meeting date, cultivation exemption or new Cuban event inferred.

Its Padura citation is *O homem que amava os cachorros* (Boitempo, 2013), p. 93.
The [publisher identifies this as a novel with a fictional narrator](https://www.boitempoeditorial.com.br/produto/o-homem-que-amava-os-cachorros-152760).
Do not convert a first-person passage cited from that work into Padura’s own
biographical testimony or population evidence. The cited page and narrative
voice still need inspection before public use. No Cuban entry changed.
