## Ohio

Ohio Revised Code section 3796.221, effective 20 March 2026, was read, with the Legislative Service Commission final analysis of Senate Bill 56. A gift is lawful only without payment, only up to 2.5 ounces of plant material or 15 grams of extract to the same adult on the same day, and only on privately owned land used mainly as a home or a farm. Possession of that amount is lawful when the marijuana comes from a licensed Ohio dispensary or lawful home growing. A transfer outside the gift rule is trafficking. Sharing stays at 5, because a capped adult gift remains legal, and possession stays at 5.

## Singapore

The Central Narcotics Bureau cannabis page, updated 2 July 2026, and the Misuse of Drugs Act Second Schedule as at 1 June 2026 were read. Death applies to trafficking, import or export of more than 500 g of cannabis, more than 200 g of cannabis resin, and more than 1,000 g of cannabis mixture. Possession below 330 g of cannabis, 130 g of resin or 660 g of mixture is up to 10 years or a fine of S$20,000. The bands above that carry mandatory imprisonment and caning. Consumption is at least one year and up to ten years. Cultivation is at least three years or a fine of at least S$5,000. Scores stay at 0. The 15 g trafficking presumption was not on the penalty page that was read, so it was left out of the revised sentences.
