## Sources and boundaries

- **Peron:** contemporary first-hand visit by Rose Ann Fuhrman in Cannabis Culture (5 November 1995), biographical reference and original ballot texts. Treat the magazine as sympathetic contemporary reporting, not independent validation of its therapeutic claims. Describe the club's emergence as early 1990s because accounts vary over its founding year. Attribute the position that all cannabis use was medicinal to Peron. Include his opposition to later adult-use initiatives rather than depicting reform advocates as a single bloc. Use the biographical reference's 1945 birth year; some older obituaries report a different age. Do not use the Wikipedia claim that a 2013 award was posthumous: he died in 2018.
- **Rathbun:** Gastro Obscura's 2018 retrospective, the contemporary Chronicle obituary and biographical reference, plus ballot texts. Keep reported patient benefit separate from clinical evidence. First arrest recorded by year only; accounts include inconsistent ages and quantities, omitted here. Avoid assertions that the city could exempt her supply from state or federal law. Portrait shows baked goods, but the caption does not claim the pictured goods contain cannabis. No speculative account of family tragedy causing activism.
- **Grinspoon:** his own 1994 retrospective introduction and Boston Municipal Court affidavit, with NORML's participant memorial and biographical reference. The affidavit describes an argument submitted for defendants, not a ruling striking down a statute. Do not reproduce categorical medical assertions as present-day consensus. The autobiographical account supplies research beginning in 1967 and the 1971 book date. Family reports about chemotherapy symptoms are explicitly anecdotal. The NORML tribute is identified as a colleague's assessment from a reform organisation.

## Primary legal records

Retrieved the San Francisco Proposition P PDF from American Legal's municipal archive using ordinary shell download. Its scanned page has no extractable text, so rendered and read the full page visually. Heading: **Text of proposed initiative declaration of policy**. It recommends restoration of hemp medical preparations and physician protection; it does not independently repeal state prohibition. The municipal code's initiative appendix confirms **5 November 1991**. Some secondary sources overstate its legal effect; the entries use the primary text.

Read California Secretary of State's original Proposition 215 text. Distinguish its patient/caregiver possession and cultivation exemption, physician-recommendation protection and distribution goal from a comprehensive commercial supply licence. Existing 1996 medical and federal-response history entries supply the surrounding timeline.

Added three checked, unscored context events: the 1971 publication, Rathbun's 1981 arrest and Proposition P in 1991. These support precise links from the biographies and leave score timelines unchanged. No separate long history articles requested in this batch.
