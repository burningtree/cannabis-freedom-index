# US Virgin Islands: the 1937–2014 gap — 26 October 2026

Nine new entries and two articles extend the period between the existing 1937 tax
restriction and the 2014 reforms. The largest interval between dated entries falls
from 77 years to 19 years (1971–1990). The territory now has 23 entries.

## Records actually read

- **Truman, Executive Order 10250, 5 June 1951**, original order reproduced by the
  American Presidency Project. Read section 1(f), together with its delegation
  introduction. It gives Interior the President's existing territorial registration
  and tax powers, to be exercised in consultation with Treasury. This is an
  administrative delegation, not the creation of a new tax or possession ban.
  https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/executive-order-10250-providing-for-the-performance-certain-functions-the-president-the
- **Government v. Rijos, 6 June 1968, 6 V.I. 475**, judicial opinion reproduced by
  Legal Calculators. Read pp. 479–489 and final order, especially pp. 483–489.
  The warrant sought cannabis following a named informant's purchases, but the
  inventory and prosecution concerned heroin and other items. The judge upheld
  probable cause for the warrant, suppressed evidence because of the unannounced
  passkey entry and unlawful personal search, and separately rejected the challenge
  to prosecution by information. No cannabis acquittal is inferred.
  https://calculators.law/caselaw/decisions/Zb18xEPYW3gd/government-of-the-virgin-islands-v-rijos
- **McIntosh v. People, 29 November 2012, 57 V.I. 669**, judicial opinion reproduced
  by OpenJurist. Read facts, pp. 684–692, conclusion and notes on the schedules.
  The court expressly dates the prison law to 13 February 1970 and commencement
  of the replacement drug law to 23 March 1971. Cannabis was non-narcotic within
  the latter but expressly included in the former's own definition. Three years
  for introducing cannabis into prison was upheld; the case was remanded to stay
  or dismiss one conviction because two convictions punished the same act. The
  event is not a release or an acquittal. Its Schedule I discussion reports the
  evidence before that court, not a new independent audit of all ministerial orders.
  https://openjurist.org/57/vi/669/mcintosh-v-people
- **Bigby v. Government, 20 December 2000, 125 F. Supp. 2d 709**, judicial opinion
  reproduced by Justia. Read pp. 710–716, conclusion and notes. Agreement plus an
  overt act had to be alleged; conviction was vacated and a new trial ordered.
  The omitted charge element is distinct from a finding that no act occurred.
  https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp2/125/709/2424929/
- **People v. Rohn, 20 April 2011**, official Superior Court PDF, SX-03-CR-113.
  Read relevant facts and statutory discussion, printed pp. 2–10, 12–14, 16–20.
  It gives the 1971 purpose, possession/distribution maxima, first-offender
  procedure, 1990 licence legislation, and its holding that revocation applied
  even to first-offender probation. This interpretation was **reversed**; both
  public entries and source locators state that subsequent outcome.
  https://cdnsm5-hosted.civiclive.com/UserFiles/Servers/Server_12810747/File/Opinions/Archive/2011_04_26_16_33_23.pdf
- **Rohn v. People, 21 November 2012**, official Supreme Court PDF, S. Ct. Crim.
  No. 2011-0087, all ten pages. Header distinguishes consideration on 9 October
  from filing on 21 November. The court reversed both the April interpretation
  and the licence portion of the December 2011 order. Rohn had received six
  months' probation without adjudication and the underlying case was dismissed
  after completion. The first-offender provision had been enacted in 1971 and
  the licence provision in 1990. Neither a plea nor probation is silently called
  a conviction. No blanket repeal of licence penalties is claimed.
  https://cdnsm5-hosted.civiclive.com/UserFiles/Servers/Server_12810860/File/Opinions/Published/2012/File80.pdf
- **20 V.I.C. § 378, historical 2019 text**, statutory reproduction at Justia.
  Read (a)–(d). Confirms two-year possession and five-year other-drug penalties,
  and the separate exception for the later civil-possession regime. The new
  article uses this for the statute's structure, with the courts supplying its
  historical interpretation and application.
  https://law.justia.com/codes/virgin-islands/2019/title-20/part-ii/chapter-35/378/

## Scoring and follow-up

No new whole-segment change is manufactured from the number of entries. The 1951
administrative delegation, case-specific suppression, charging requirements and
interpretations leave the ordinary adult prohibition categories unchanged.
The 1970 offence concerns prison contraband. The 1990 penalty and 2012 relief
concern driving privileges; other defendants still faced criminal possession
penalties. These directional entries have explicit unscored explanations.

The 1971 replacement has an explicit provisional unscored explanation: recover
the preceding territorial drug chapter and original 1971 Act before assigning a
supported change across the six dimensions. First-offender relief is real, but
its existence does not by itself establish routine implementation or a newly
changed whole enforcement band. Original 1990 Act 5649 and its commencement
remain retrieval targets; the new entry records the verified adoption year.
The 1970 entry records adoption as described by the Supreme Court, without
claiming that a separate commencement notice was examined.

The inherited 1937 aggregate score step has not been re-certified by this pass.
A zero-flag chain check proves structural continuity, not that every earlier
segment was already at its later level. Original territorial restrictions,
1937 administration, pre-1971 law, the 1980s and the later retail/medical
implementation questions in the 24 October memo remain open.
