Reviewed 26 October 2026. Read DATA-MAINTENANCE.md's source, commencement,

## Sources actually read

- Read the original 1985 code reproduced by [Luat Minh Khue](https://luatminhkhue.vn/van-ban/bo-luat-hinh-su-nam-1985.aspx),
  especially Article 203 and the commencement metadata, which gives 1 January
  1986. Organising narcotics use carries six months–five years, or three–ten
  years for serious consequences/dangerous recidivism. The article is generic
  to narcotics and does not itself name cannabis. It supplies context for the
  later drug chapter, without establishing the first cannabis prohibition.
  Article 97's highest original smuggling band reaches life, not death.
- Read the [1989 amendment's reproduced statutory text](https://vanbanphapluat.co/bo-luat-hinh-su-1989-sua-doi-30-lct-hdnn8),
  Article 2(3), introducing Article 96a. The three bands are two–seven,
  five–fifteen, and twelve–twenty years/life/death. The statute itself gives no
  cannabis weight thresholds. The [national database page](https://vbpl.vn/botaichinh/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=2082)
  was opened earlier in this research pass and its legislative metadata read:
  adoption 28 December 1989, commencement 2 January 1990. Later requests returned
  403/502. Retain the established commencement; cite the independently read
  reproduction for the substantive drug clause. The existing Caselaw address
  also failed on later requests.
- Downloaded the actual [government 1997 RTF](https://datafiles.chinhphu.vn/cpp/files/vbpq/2006/07/3420_l57qh.rtf).
  Converted it with textutil and decoded the legacy .VnTime/TCVN characters,
  preserving ASCII. Read Articles 185a, 185b–185e, 185l and the replacement
  clauses. The cultivating offence requires repeated education, livelihood
  assistance and an administrative sanction followed by continued offending.
  The use offence requires repeated education and administrative placement in
  compulsory treatment followed by continued illegal use. Neither text supports
  the old blanket description of every act of use becoming a crime.
- Read the [Supreme People's Court's Letter 59/KHXX](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/cong-van/linh-vuc-khac/cong-van-59-khxx-ap-dung-quy-dinh-cua-luat-bo-sung-bo-luat-hinh-su-46845.aspx),
  dated 6 June 1997, especially Part I(1)–(4). It explicitly gives **22 May
  1997** as commencement following adoption on 10 May and distinguishes new or
  harsher offences from retrospective application of favourable amendments.
  The government catalogue's apparent January date is not used. An English
  reproduction of the amendment shifts Vietnamese letter labels; the original
  Vietnamese Article **185l** is the use offence.
- Read [the 1999 code's English statutory reproduction](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/penal-code-no-15-1999-qh10-dated-december-21-1999-of-the-national-assembly-10166-doc1.html),
  Articles 192–194 and 199. The public page supplies the body; no login or
  access-control workaround was used. Article 194 combines possession,
  transport, trade and appropriation, while retaining the five-kilogram resin
  and seventy-five-kilogram leaves/flowers/fruit thresholds. Its highest band
  adds twenty years as an alternative to life/death. The [national database
  metadata](https://vbpl.vn/TW/Pages/vbpq-thuoctinh.aspx?ItemID=6157) was opened
  and read: adoption 21 December 1999, commencement **1 July 2000**, replacement
  **1 January 2018**. Provincial/database mirrors were intermittently inaccessible.
- Read [the 2015 code's statutory reproduction](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/criminal-code-no-100-2015-qh13-dated-november-27-2015-of-the-national-assembly-101324-doc1.html),
  Article 251, as a continuity check: the sale offence retains twenty years,
  life or death at five kilograms of resin or seventy-five kilograms of the
  then-listed plant parts. This is the older wording, not the current rule.
  The official 2017 consolidated code is split across Gazette issues; the
  accessible 779–780 PDF starts at Article 295 and does not verify Article
  251. The relevant 777–778 download failed and is not claimed as read.
- Read [the government's current Article 251 text](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/muc-phat-toi-mua-ban-trai-phep-chat-ma-tuy-119250722173411884.htm):
  twenty years/life at five to under thirty kilograms of resin or seventy-five
  to under 150 kilograms of listed plant parts; life/death at thirty/150.
  This source already exists as `a251` in the profile. The contemporary plant
  list includes roots, stem and branches; those are not backdated into the
  1997 and 1999 lists of leaves, flowers and fruit.

The previous profile had recreational status `death`, correctly described
capital cannabis sale penalties, but enforcement **1**, whose rubric is custodial
penalties. Enforcement **0** expressly covers death, corporal punishment or long
sentences. Correct the profile to 0 and explain the current capital sale bands
with its existing primary source. This corrects the scoring application; it does
not claim a newly enacted restriction or that personal use itself carries death.

therefore also change only enforcement, from 1 to 0. Preserve the 1990 event's

in 2025, ending at **2.5, displayed as 3** today. The earlier 1968 100→8 step and
pre-reunification territorial scope remain open as documented in the previous
