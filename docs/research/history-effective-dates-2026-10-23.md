## Uganda

section 1: the Act comes into force on a date the internal affairs minister
appoints. The same page records commencement on 14 April 2025 by the
Commencement Instrument, 2025. ULII's page for that instrument is Statutory
Instrument 42 of 2025, made 9 April 2025, with 14 April 2025 as its date.

The scored drop (possession 1 to 0, sharing 1 to 0, total 9 to 6) is

no score. The 2023 court entry already said the annulment did not legalise

own reconstruction.

## Portugal

Law 55/2023 was read in the Diário da República, series I, no. 175, 8 September
2023. Article 5 brings it into force on the first day of the next month, 1
October 2023. Article 40(2) of Decree-Law 15/93 makes acquisition and
possession for personal use an administrative offence. Article 40(3), and
article 2(2) of Law 30/2000, make a quantity above the average ten-day amount
a sign that the purpose may not be personal use. Article 40(4) and article
2(3) say that where exclusive personal use is shown, the judicial authority
closes the case, withholds indictment or acquits, and refers the person to a
dissuasion commission. Article 40(1) still makes cultivation for one's own use
a crime of up to three months or a fine of up to 30 days.

The profile now says 25 g of flower and 5 g of hashish are those reference
amounts. Possession stays at 3 and enforcement at 5. A larger holding can
still open a criminal inquiry until personal use is shown, so no whole segment
moves. The event is `portugal-personal-use-quantity-2023`, linked to the 2008
ruling it supersedes for proven personal use.
