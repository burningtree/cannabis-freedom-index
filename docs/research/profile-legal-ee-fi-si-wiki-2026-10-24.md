# Estonia, Finland and Slovenia, Wikipedia narrowed, 24 October 2026

Scores are unchanged. Euro amounts that Wikipedia gives for Slovenia were not carried forward: the consolidated Act still states the fine in tolars.

## Estonia

- State Agency of Medicines, cannabis, THC and CBD, updated 10 July 2025. Growing cannabis in order to make a narcotic substance, including for medical use, is prohibited. https://www.ravimiamet.ee/en/node/83
- Penal Register Act, current text. Section 6(2): a final decision imposing a penalty in a misdemeanour case is entered, except a decision in a warning procedure. Section 24(1)(1): data on a fine or detention for a misdemeanour are deleted and archived one year after the fine is paid or the detention is served. https://www.riigiteataja.ee/akt/KarRS
- The small-quantity fine, the large-quantity sentence and the small-quantity supply sentence were already stated from the Narcotic Drugs Act and the Penal Code. Those sections now carry the enforcement paragraph. Wikipedia is no longer cited.

## Finland

- Prosecutor General, VKS:2018:2, in force from 1 January 2019. It quotes Criminal Code chapter 50 section 2a: a person who unlawfully uses a narcotic, or for their own use possesses or attempts to acquire a small amount, is sentenced to a fine or imprisonment for at most six months. The small-amount limit applies to possession and attempted acquisition, not to use itself. https://www.syyttajalaitos.fi/vks20182/
- Police Board instruction of 31 May 2024, POL-2023-114107, with the summary-fine table. Hash and marijuana are listed at up to 10 g. The ordinary fine is 10 day-fines, the range is 10 to 15, and continuous use is 15 to 20. https://www.syyttajalaitos.fi/app/uploads/sites/18/2026/05/VKS_2018_2_Menettely-huumausaineen-kayttorikosta-koskevissa-asioissa_poliisihallituksen-saatekirje_ohje-ja-liitteet.pdf
- The earlier "5 to 20 day-fines for under 15 g" and "a positive test can ground the offence" were not in these texts and were not kept. "No licensed production" is still cited to Wikipedia. The current Finlex consolidation did not return the statute text.

## Slovenia

- Production and Trafficking of Illicit Drugs Act (ZPPPD), PISRS consolidation, read 24 October 2026. Article 33: possessing a smaller quantity for one-time personal use is a misdemeanour, with a fine of 10,000 to 50,000 tolars or detention of up to five days. No gram amount is stated. A milder penalty may follow voluntary entry into an approved treatment or social-protection programme. Article 28: authorised officers seize the drugs and propose misdemeanour proceedings. Article 34: the drugs are taken without compensation. The article punishes possession. It does not create a separate use offence. https://pisrs.si/pregledPredpisa?id=ZAKO1388
- Article 186 of the Criminal Code remains the source for the manufacturing and selling offence, as already used on the enforcement paragraph.
