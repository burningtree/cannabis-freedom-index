## Sources actually read

Downloads, extracted text, rendered statutory pages, baseline copies and scripts are retained in `tmp/history-guatemala-gap-2026-10-22/`. A saved web-tool reading records the reproduced bill text when direct downloads were blocked.

- [Penal Code, judicial compilation hosted by the National Office for the Prevention of Torture](https://mnp-opt.gob.gt/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Codigo-Penal-DECRETO-NUMERO-17-73.pdf): relevant drug offences and final clauses read; printed/PDF pages **49 and 83 visually inspected**. Article 306: unauthorised cultivation of drug-producing plants, and trading/possessing/supplying their seeds or plants, three to five years **and** Q500–Q5,000. Article 307: same range and fine for unauthorised import, sale, delivery, transport, supply and keeping drugs. Article 309: providing premises, including free of charge, for trafficking or consumption, two months to one year and a fine. Commencement expressly **15 September 1973**, distinct from legislative adoption on 5 July and executive approval on 27 July. Article 308 is marked as amended in 1977; its later aggravations are not backdated into the 1973 article. An additional [Culture Ministry code PDF](https://mcd.gob.gt/wp-content/uploads/2013/07/Actualizaci%C3%B3n_del_Codigo_Penal_Decreto_17-73.pdf) was downloaded and text extracted as a cross-check, without treating all its amended contents as the original 1973 law.
- [Interior Ministry's judicial compilation of Decree 48-92](https://mingob.gob.gt/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/Decreto-48-92-Ley-contra-la-Narcoactividad-y-sus-Reformas.pdf): relevant definitions, lawful-use rule, offences and closing clauses read. Printed/PDF pages **7, 13 and 14 visually inspected**. Article 36: cultivation five to twenty years **and** Q10,000–Q100,000. Article 37: manufacture/extraction eight to twenty years and Q50,000–Q1,000,000. Article 38: trafficking twelve to twenty years **and** Q50,000–Q1,000,000, broadly including acquisition, transfers on any basis, storage, transport, distribution and supply. Article 39: acquiring/possessing for own consumption four months to two years **and** Q200–Q10,000, requiring a reasonable quantity for immediate consumption plus circumstances supporting personal use. No fixed cannabis grams are specified there. Article 3 permits strictly necessary quantities for medical treatment, analysis, research and medicine manufacture by legally authorised people, with prescriptions for sales to private customers; it is explicitly marked as amended in **1999**, and its current wording is not presented as necessarily the original 1992 text.
- [WTO-notified consolidated copy of the same drug law](https://www.importlicensing.wto.org/sites/default/files/members/59/Decreto%20No.48-92%20-%20Narcotr%C3%A1fico_24.09.1992.pdf): extracted relevant offences and closing formula cross-checked. **24 September 1992 is executive approval**, the date now used for the existing event. Legislative adoption was 23 September. Article 80 specifies operation fifteen days after official publication; approval is not asserted to be commencement. A congressional search result reports publication on 6 October 1992, but the actual catalogue page was blocked and not counted as read; no exact commencement date was inferred from it.
- [Adult and medical cannabis bill 5053, reproduced transcript](https://aprenderly.com/doc/1381191/5053---congreso-de-la-rep%C3%BAblica-de-guatemala): cover, operative proposed articles 1–32 and relevant background read through the web tool. The original [Congress PDF](https://www.congreso.gob.gt/assets/uploads/info_legislativo/iniciativas/Registro5053.pdf) was blocked; source titles expressly identify the transcript host. Cover records plenary presentation **26 April 2016**, and the accompanying letter is 7 April. Proposed private adult consumption, up to five plants at home for personal/family consumption, licences, variety/THC identification, quality controls, tax and drug-law exceptions were read in the operative text. Article 17: 10% sales tax included in price; article 18: half for police cannabis-education programmes and half for the national hospital system; domestic growing exempt. Articles 21–24 propose cannabis exceptions to cultivation/manufacture/supply/personal-possession offences; article 31 proposes repealing older code drug sections. All are described conditionally. The five-plant rule is not represented as five per household member, and related-topic “club” links generated by the transcript website are not treated as operative club rules.
- [Prensa Libre, Carlos Álvarez, 1 November 2016](https://www.prensalibre.com/guatemala/comunitario/comision-rechaza-uso-medicinal-de-la-marihuana-en-le-pais/): full substantive report read and downloaded. Identifies the **legislation and constitutional committee**, led by Oliverio García Rodas, its health/constitutional/treaty objections and sponsor Álvaro Velásquez's response. Existing event now describes committee opposition, replacing the broader claim that Congress itself rejected regulation. Its **year-only date is retained**: the report does not establish the exact date on which the adverse opinion was adopted. A cached congressional record and thesis references point to an opinion in September; that original opinion was not recovered. Do not turn 1 November, the news date, into a verified opinion date.

Its shorthand statement about which committee stopped the earlier bill conflicts with the more specific November report, so the earlier committee is identified from the November account. Its reported club design was not added to the current profile or treated as a verified operative bill clause.
- [Prensa Libre, Carlos Álvarez, 16 October 2017](https://www.prensalibre.com/guatemala/politica/nuevo-reves-a-intento-de-legalizar-la-marihuana/): full substantive report read and downloaded.
