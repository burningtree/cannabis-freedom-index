# Italy's 1981 cannabis referendum ruling — 20 October 2026

Added an article to the existing judgment event and one unscored event for the
central referendum office's preliminary validation on 2 December 1980. Added
the accessible original gazette reproduction and a related-event link to the
existing 1981 record.

## Evidence and limits

- Read the complete Judgment 30/1981 in the official gazette's HTML reproduction,
  including the petition text, factual account, promoters' arguments, legal
  reasoning and operative order. The Court PDF endpoint initially returned
  metadata but later timed out and returned HTTP 500 on download; the article
  relies on the full gazette text rather than a PDF described as inspected.
- The new 1980 event is explicitly attributed to the Constitutional Court's
  subsequent account. The original Cassation office order was not separately
  recovered. The judgment reports its date, procedural checks and more than
  500,000 valid signatures; it does not give a precise count used here.
- Distinguished preliminary legitimacy from constitutional admissibility.
  Neither validation nor rejection was presented as a popular vote. The Court
  found the question coherent but rejected it on treaty-related grounds.
- The article attributes interpretation of treaty obligations to the Court
  in 1981, rather than treating it as a complete account of all later treaty
  developments or possible reform choices. It does not claim that every
  treaty-related provision is immune to referendum or parliamentary amendment.
- Preserved the event's decision date, 12 February. Filing was 13 February and
  publication 17 February. Added no historical score, enforcement estimate or
  claim about public opinion. Profiles, scores and score baselines are unchanged.
