  snippet API (the official session-law volume, Equity de Puerto Rico):
  Act No. 51 of 13 May 1934 (pp. 158–159, English and Spanish) recites 1932
  Act 12 by its full title — "AN ACT TO PUNISH THE PLANTING, IMPORTATION,
  PURCHASE AND SALE OF MARIJUANA" — and approval date 19 April 1932, and
  confers exclusive jurisdiction on the district courts; 1935 Act No. 26
  (p. 159, Spanish section 2) extends the rule to manufacture,
  transportation, possession, use, purchase, sale, transfer and gift of any
  marijuana cigarette or cigar; index pp. xvi, 625. This also settles a
  numbering conflict: the act is No. 51 (as the DPR opinion and our 1934
  entry say), not No. 61 (Wikipedia's number).
- Wikipedia Cannabis in Puerto Rico (read today): Act 12 title, one-month to
  one-year jail range, 1934 alignment with the Uniform State Narcotic Drug
  Act, 2013 decriminalisation failure with El Nuevo Día polling (26/70/4),
  governor's medical executive order.
- Puerto Rico Narcotics Act 48-1959 (official OGP PDF, read via pdftotext):
  prison of no less than five nor more than twenty years plus a fine of up
  to $20,000, with the same range for a second offence — verifying the 1959
  entry's figures independently.
