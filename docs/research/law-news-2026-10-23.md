## Why the score stays

Spain's access score is already 3 because of tolerated clubs ("tolerated shops
or clubs, or easy medical access"). A last-line hospital solution is a hard
medical route. It does not make lawful supply easier than the clubs, so neither
the decree nor the October timetable takes a point. `medical` stays `limited`.
