## #181 — Survey deletion follows relay identities and keeps retries

Read the survey maintenance notes, browser client, take/me handlers and installed nostr-tools pool/relay implementation. The pool's querySync resolves on close and its pool-level EOSE also covers failures; neither is sufficient evidence of a complete empty snapshot. Queries now use individual relay subscriptions with an outer deadline earlier than the library's synthetic EOSE timeout, reporting completion separately from failure.

Publishing returns accepted and attempted relay URLs. Before transmission, local storage records possible destinations; timeouts and reloads cannot erase them. Edits retain old holders and unknown identities from legacy count-only records. Deletion saves and reuses one strictly newer empty replacement, requires that replacement's ACK from every known/possible destination, accumulates per-relay progress across retries, and never accepts a NIP-09 request's ACK as replacement delivery. Retired relay URLs remain deletion targets. Count-only legacy records cannot be fully confirmed and remain pending with an explanation.

Sync uses the tested mergeMine helper in the actual me-page handler: an offline, partial or complete empty query cannot remove pending records. A tombstone on one relay cannot hide a visible old response on another; a newer remote edit invalidates older deletion ACKs. The form's removed-report path uses the same retry state. Same-page mutations are serialised. Existing caveats about relays retaining historical encrypted copies remain; this confirms delivery rather than physical erasure. Tests execute the actual page's sync branch and exercise swapped ACKs, identity migration, persistence, partial retries, query failures, stale copies and strict replacement timestamps.
