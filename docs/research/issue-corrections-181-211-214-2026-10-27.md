## #211 — Arizona product-specific possession and gifts

Read [§36-2852(A)(1)–(3)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/36/02852.htm), [§36-2853(A)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/36/02853.htm), [§13-3405(A), (B)(1)](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/13/03405.htm) and [medical immunity §36-2811](https://www.azleg.gov/ars/36/02811.htm). Ordinary adult carry is 1 oz total including at most 5 g concentrate; petty protection ends above 2.5 oz total OR 12.5 g concentrate. Outside applicable protections the class 6 possession provision below two pounds can apply. Gifts require adults 21+, no remuneration and no public advertisement/promotion, with the same 5 g sub-limit. Preserve lawful on-site home harvest and separate medical permissions. Corrected summary, possession, enforcement, sharing and carry/caught answers. Scores stay 5/4/6/5/3/6: the existing capped adult-use bands already describe these permissions; this corrects omitted product conditions.

## #213 — Delaware personal-use quantity by form

Read [§4701's personal-use and leaf-marijuana definitions](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c047/sc01/) and [§§4750, 4751C(3)(c), 4756, 4764(b), 4764A](https://delcode.delaware.gov/title16/c047/sc04/). The code supplies two versions of §4764A: use the version effective 21 October 2026, which expressly includes marijuana products and infused beverages. Adult possession and unpaid sharing use the personal-use quantity: 1 oz leaf, 12 g concentrate or products with 750 mg delta-9-THC. Purchase-linked reciprocal gifts are excluded. Do not turn an ounce into concentrate permission.

Leaf excess above 1 oz but below 175 g can carry three months/$575/both under §4764(b); 175 g inclusive is Tier 1 and can trigger §4756's class G felony. The profile names leaf when giving that example and requires applicable classification/quantity rules for other products instead of copying its physical-weight shortcut. Medical/control-act exemptions remain. Corrected summary, possession, enforcement, sharing and carry answer. Scores remain 5/1/6/5/3/6; no new score event follows from correcting existing caps.
