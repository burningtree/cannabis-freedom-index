## Earlier court history added

Read the full original Commonwealth v. Atalig decision of 13 February 1987, https://www.cnmilaw.gov/pdf/cr/vol2/2-CR-1006.pdf , printed pp. 1006–1015; visually inspected PDF pages 4, 7 and 9. The case concerns a 12 September 1981 Rota–Saipan flight, boxes of deer meat and five pounds of cannabis, a fruit-fly quarantine and a no-contest possession conviction. Sentence one year suspended except thirty days and $1,000. A 1983 jury-rights reversal was itself reversed by the Ninth Circuit in 1984; the 1987 judgment addresses the separate search question. Do not call the 1983 outcome final or assume five pounds were for personal use. The search holding depends on a valid administrative quarantine, indiscriminate inspection of affected arrivals and no criminal-investigation pretext, not a general power to search any domestic cannabis traveller. Case category enforcement, with court/police-powers/sentencing tags; no numerical step. Article adds the named person and full outcome rather than a generic prohibition claim. Original Trust Territory drug law remains a retrieval lead.
