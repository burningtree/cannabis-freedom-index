Added an article to the existing 1923 law event, corrected its date and scope,

## Evidence and limits

- Read the complete original Law 396 in the 5 March 1923 official gazette,
  printed pp. 1530–1531, and visually checked both pages. Article 1 covers
  holding for sale or administration, not general possession for personal use.
  Articles 8 and 10 separately punish specified drug-taking gatherings. Article
  12 provides for a ministerial list. The law itself does not name cannabis.
- Read the implementing regulation in the 8 December 1923 official gazette,
  printed pp. 7097–7101. Visually checked Table A: entries 13–14 identify Indian
  hemp and its hydroalcoholic extract. Articles 1–2 establish scheduling and
  authorisation, including the public-pharmacy exception; Article 39 concerns
  transitional declarations. Article 31's cultivation rule concerns poppies;
  it was not treated as evidence of a cannabis cultivation prohibition.
- Read Normattiva's metadata for both instruments: law commencement 20 March,
  decree commencement 23 December. These are distinct from instrument dates
  and publication dates. The existing law event now uses its instrument date.

  `tmp/pdfs/italy-2026-10-20/`. Sources were read, not merely summarised from
  search results. No empirical enforcement claims were added.
