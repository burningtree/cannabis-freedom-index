### What the enacted 2023 clauses actually say

- 14.89 possession, 14.90 cultivation: Schedule I for traffic first-degree felony; otherwise second-degree. First-degree non-bailable, second-degree cash twice bond. Same paragraph says 10–20 years OR Penal Law 50.5/50.6. Do not silently choose 10–20 for every personal case or erase the overlapping five-year ordinary second-degree maximum. No judicial resolution retrieved.
- 14.91(a): Schedule I first use 4 months–1 year, first-degree misdemeanor. Second apprehension second-degree felony, 1–5 years or more / Penal Law references. Cash bail. Three–eighteen months was a reported draft proposal, absent from the enacted clause.
- Express rehabilitation alternative is placed in 14.91(b), Schedules II–IV; cannot confidently extend it to Schedule I cannabis. 2014 had a separate general alternative paragraph.
- 14.100: mandatory immediate deportation of any non-Liberian convicted under subchapter after serving sentence/sanction. 2014 only possible deportation proceedings with rights qualification. Thus the previous profile's claim of no special visitor rule was false.
- 14.101: drug clearance for public institutional admission/enrolment/employment; private institutions directed to institute measures. It does not specify urine/blood testing or a THC cutoff; no such test is invented.
- Gift covered by 14.85 and traffic definition. No payment requirement.
- Signed date used for event, not certified commencement. Outer cover and presidency release agree; inner cover has an uncompleted field. Senate attestation dates final passage 29 June; the old June-only event was relabelled to presidential approval rather than adding a redundant stage.
