## Source checks and remaining limits

- **Kenya:** the judgment's exhaustion finding is in paragraphs 35–47; its
  alternative rights/proportionality reasoning follows in 49–74. Paragraphs 75–84
  contain the policy discussion and final disposition. Its account of religious
  indispensability is attributed to the court, not presented as a definition of
  Rastafari belief. The challenge's dismissal leaves existing prohibitions in place.
- **DSBD:** the official indexed record was read in full. The old Drupal address
  now redirects to a missing page on the replacement site; the attached terms of
  reference were not recovered. Scope and advertising/closing dates are supported
  by the record itself. Do not infer an award, completed framework or enacted bill.
- **Landraces:** the project report does not supply an independent accession audit,
  permit copy or evidence of completed community returns. Its historical collection
  provenance and proposed agreements remain claims requiring independent checking
  before a longer Culture feature. No claims of genetic purity or proven medicinal
  efficacy were carried into the profile or history.
- **Dutch evaluation:** the report is labelled first follow-up measurement 2025,
  covers the period through early 2026 and was released in September 2026. Distinguish
  measurement, release and the April 2025 experimental-phase start. A finding of no
  clear difference is not evidence that the intervention can have no effect.
- **Basel:** use PDAG's 14 September release date. The repository lists a November
  journal-issue date; do not backdate that issue or confuse it with first public
  reporting. Read the repository abstract, not the inaccessible ScienceDirect full
  text. Small mean changes coexist with no significant change in the proportion
  above clinical thresholds. The uncontrolled year-one design cannot identify cause.
- **Zimbabwe:** ZimLII's source is the entire 3 July gazette, not a small stand-alone
  SI. Standard retrieval was blocked; its browser PDF viewer loaded the supplement.
  Read printed pages 1651–1668, including the amended producer-licence duration,
  MCAZ functions, cultivation/security/testing rules, export destruction and appeals.
  Section numbers in the amending text are irregular; locators identify the amended
  provisions. The printed definition's low-THC exclusion wording is anomalous; it
  is not used to rewrite the separate criminal-code industrial-hemp definition.
  Fees are not promoted as current charges because later fee reforms need their
  own verification. The event uses gazette publication date.
- **Lesotho:** a future addition needs the report tabled in the National Assembly
  around 19–23 October, its adoption status and the relevant Ministry response.
  An article quoting a committee is insufficient to describe its allegations as
  audited losses. Editorial exclusion disposes of this conditional candidate;
  it does not settle the underlying governance allegations.
