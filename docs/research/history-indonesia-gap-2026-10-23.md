# Indonesia, 1927 to 1958

The dated gap is `indonesia-colonial-ban-1927` to
`indonesia-opium-convention-1958` (3 April 1958).

## Staatsblad 1944 No. 14, read and not added

Staatsblad van Nederlandsch-Indië 1944 No. 14, signed at Brisbane on
21 December 1944 by Lieutenant-Governor-General H. J. van Mook and issued on
5 January 1945. In force the day after promulgation. The scan is at
https://jdih.majalengkakab.go.id/common/dokumen/staatsbladno.14tahun1944.pdf
and was read as page images.

The preamble says the ordinance is meant to abolish, throughout the
Netherlands Indies, the use of narcotics for purposes that are not medical or
scientific. Article 1 amends the Verdoovende-middelen-ordonnantie (Staatsblad
1927 No. 278 jo. 536): in article 1 under t, "afkomstig van de Opiumregie"
replaces "zooals ze door de Opiumregie wordt verkocht", and the provision under
b of article 28 lapses. Article 2 withdraws the Regie-opium-ordonnantie
(Staatsblad 1927 No. 279). The text does not name Indian hemp, ganja or hashish.
It was not added.
