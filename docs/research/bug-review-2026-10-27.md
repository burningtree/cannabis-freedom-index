## Source limits

- Reviews cover the relevant provisions, not whole legal systems or every possible permit. Legal permission does not prove a current company licence, commercial harvest, product registration or stocked pharmacy.
- Romania’s official portal repeatedly returned 502 errors. The sentencing amendment was read in the LegisRO reproduction; Article 17 was read in the February 2025 LexApp reproduction and indexed official passages. The 0.3% subsidy condition is not a general permission to grow any cannabis plant. The sentencing history uses the verified 2 March publication date rather than claiming that publication itself was commencement.
- Belize’s consolidated government PDF could not be downloaded. Its regulation 3 amendment to 0.3% was read in the indexed official PDF; the 2019 gazette reproduction was opened and read for registration, field size, movement and research rules. The original 1% figure was superseded.
- Latvia’s live statute and ministry page timed out repeatedly. The current indexed official Articles 6–7, with the March 2026 annotation, were read; agricultural THC/seed conditions were checked against the ministry’s indexed agriculture report. No new 2026 entitlement is inferred merely from a procedural amendment.
- Kazakhstan’s Resolution 797, including the 2026 amendments, was opened and read. Current Article 22 was retrieved in the official indexed bilingual text. Article 18’s full exception procedure was not recovered; the text preserves that cross-reference rather than expanding its scope.
- Jordan’s JFDA facsimile timed out. Instructions 1/2023 Articles 2 and 5–7 were read in the Hasana statutory reproduction, which identifies the Legislation and Opinion Bureau as its source. No specific product registration or pharmacy stock was established.
- The Gibraltar official listing says NOT IN FORCE. Fiji’s enacted 2026 PDF was downloaded and relevant sections extracted, including section 1(2) and section 79; official/indexed Gazette and parliamentary searches found no activating notice. This is a verification limit, not proof that no notice can exist.
- Malaysia’s current drug statute was read from the ministry PDF. The adult caning cap was checked in the indexed official procedural-code text and the statutory reproduction, alongside the quoted 2024 appellate discussion; the newly structured ranges do not remove statutory exemptions.
- Guernsey’s 2026 official letter supports a present licensing route and limited commercial interest; the 2021 first-company licence claim was not used as evidence of a current operating grower.
