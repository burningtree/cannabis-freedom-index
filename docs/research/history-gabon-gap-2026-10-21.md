## Sources actually read

### Older code, amendments dated 27 August 1993

https://www.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/code-penal_html/Gabon_Code_Penal.pdf

Complete consolidated Penal Code, original enactment 31 May 1963, Articles 208–209 expressly
marked modified by Law 19/93 of **27 August 1993**. Read complete text and visually inspected
PDF pp. 29–30 (printed pp. 28–29).

- Article 208(1): unauthorised growing, holding, offering, transfer, selling, acquiring, buying
  or using scheduled plants/substances: **two–ten years AND 250,000–50 million CFA**.
  Illicit import, production, manufacture or export: criminal imprisonment (`réclusion
  criminelle à temps`) AND 500,000–100 million. Do not invent its exact range from this clause.
- 208(3): illicit use **two months–one year AND 100,000–500,000 CFA**. Court may order medical
  supervision/detox and withhold this use penalty. Evading treatment six months–two years AND
  250,000–50 million.
- 208(4): facilitation two–five years AND fine; stated minor cases five–ten.
- 208(5): direct personal-consumption supply one–five years AND fine; stated minors/education/
  administrative premises two–ten. Do not conflate the shorter supply tier with the general
  possession offence.
- 209: additional restrictions, confiscation/destruction, and a personal-use suspended-sentence
  exception. Article 41 mitigation excluded generally; do not silently infer a fine-only option.

Pre-1993 wording not recovered; category `other`, no invented direction from its predecessor.
Cannabis is not individually named in the old clause's non-exhaustive list. The original
scheduling instrument still needs recovery; the article describes the general drug rules and
does not pretend to have read that schedule. No new 1963 drug event from the later consolidated
text, since that would backdate the amended provisions.

### 2019 original gazette

https://faolex.fao.org/docs/pdf/Gab189449.pdf

Gazette **17 July 2019**, no. 27 Bis Spécial. Code is **Law 042/2018 of 5 July 2019**.
Header and final signature at printed p. 114 both say 5 July; do not use 2018 (law number) as
year of enactment or the website record's 4 July typo. Entry date is signing, not a separately
verified commencement date. Read and rendered PDF pp. 36 and 74, printed pp. 71 and 109;
read associated continuation pages as text.

- Article 303 expressly names **cannabis and its derivatives**. Illicit use up to one year AND
  up to 1.5 million CFA. Supervision/detox can replace use punishment; evasion up to three
  years AND up to 2 million.
- Facilitation up to five years AND up to 10 million; specified minors up to ten years.
- Direct offer/transfer for another person's consumption up to five years AND up to 10 million;
  specified minors/education/administrative premises up to seven years.
- General transport/possession/offer/transfer/acquisition outside specified trafficking cases:
  wording **ten years** imprisonment AND fine **up to** 100 million. The clause does not put
  `au plus` immediately after the ten-year prison term. Do not call it a two-to-ten range or
  invent an explicit fine-only alternative. General sentencing discretion is a separate issue;
  this batch does not label the term a universal mandatory minimum.
- Articles 597–603: directing a drug group thirty years AND five times seized value;
  organising production/manufacture twenty years AND up to 50 million; organised group
  production thirty years AND five times seized value. Organising import/export twenty
  years AND five times seized value; organised cases thirty years AND specified higher fine.
  `Organising production` must not automatically become a claim that every single home plant
  brings twenty years. The code does not supply a separate small-personal-growing tier here.
- Article 305 confiscation and destruction, additional restrictions and closure of premises.

Category `other`, mixed rewrite: changes in minima/maxima and fines go in different directions.
The 1993 use maximum remains a year, fine ceiling rises; possession wording changes; direct
supply fine ceiling falls. No unsupported single-direction index movement claimed.

### 2020 replacement and 2021 amendment

Official complete HTML was opened and relevant full clauses read:
https://journal-officiel.ga/4704-006-2020-/

Law 006/2020 signed/published **30 June 2020**, gazette 72 Bis Spécial. Article 459 expressly
replaces the 2019 code. The same core drug rules appear renumbered as **208–208-3** and
**408–414**. Read all relevant clauses, including the general possession sentence, use and
its treatment alternative, direct supply, production/trafficking organisation and confiscation.
Some cross-references point to adjacent pharmaceutical sections; don't silently repair numbers
or infer additional exemptions from them.

The National Assembly PDF download hung and was stopped; direct HTML download timed out.
The official text was available through the web reader. Do not claim the 2020 PDF was rendered.

2021 amendment opened/read: https://journal-officiel.ga/17697-005-2021/.
It modifies/inserts provisions on torture, sexual offences, harassment, discrimination and
associated penalties; it does not rewrite the cited core drug offences. No generic cannabis
milestone is invented for that amendment. Wider consolidation remains an ongoing research task.

prescription for authorised narcotics; ordinary prescription for authorised psychotropics.

### Seized drugs destroyed in May 2026

Justice Ministry original report:
https://justice.gouv.ga/le-parquet-de-la-republique-procede-a-lincineration-des-scelles-de-stupefiants-dune-valeur-de-5-milliards/

Its complete readable article body was returned by the web search index, although direct
reader/shell requests timed out. Read it: **Sunday 10 May 2026**, Libreville prosecutor
**Dick Fabrice Boungou Mickolo**, Justice Minister **Augustin Emane** present. Three
truckloads of mixed seized cocaine, cannabis, heroin, Skint and tramadol, estimated combined
value nearly **5 billion CFA**. The ministry's stated aim was to prevent re-entry into sale;
it described security support and destruction followed by burial. The number/value is **not
cannabis-only**. No cannabis weight, arrests or convictions inferred. `enforcement` only,
not `eradication` (no crop destruction).
