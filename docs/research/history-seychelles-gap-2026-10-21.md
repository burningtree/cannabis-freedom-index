## What the 2020 rules actually allow

Made **28 February**, effective **1 July 2020**. CBD products only within
its definition; **THC and the other specified substances together ≤2% of CBD
content**, not 2% of total product weight. Products must not produce a
psychoactive effect. Edibles, ointments and tinctures are examples; smoking
and cultivation of all forms remain prohibited (regulation 19), arrest
without warrant allowed. This did not newly establish a hemp-growing route.

Specialist assesses condition, earlier prescribed treatments' failure,
likely benefits versus risks, continuing care/examination or relevant
referral, then applies. Authority consults Council, may investigate evidence,
approves/rejects; approval leads to confidential register and permit. Patient
and caregiver permissions differ. Prescription and dispensing **≤30 days**;
permit normally **≤one year or another period the Authority determines**.
Schedule 1 allows case consideration even without a condition on a gazetted
list. No invented fixed list of eligible diagnoses is published.

Agency can import for its/authorised pharmacies; others require licences.
A **visiting qualifying patient** with an equivalent foreign medical document
still needs **prior written Authority approval to enter or leave carrying
medicine**. This recognition does not automatically recognise all foreign
prescriptions for local dispensing.

Unauthorised use of medicine: **≤SCR 10,000 OR ≤6 months OR both**. Doctor's
false certification: **fine ≥SCR 20,000 OR ≤2 years OR both**. Article keeps
only the user-facing penalty. No claim that the regulations prove first
supply, registered patients or routine practical availability in July 2020.
