# DR Congo: no post-1917 cannabis statute opened

23 October 2026. Gap from the 10 March 1917 consumption offence to accession
to the Single Convention on 19 November 1973.

Leganet publishes the ordinance-law of 22 January 1903, as approved on 1 March
1903, with the consumption sentence that the 1917 amendment added. That text
is the law already recorded. It is not a new event.

A student thesis cites “Ordonnance-loi n° 69-006 du 10 février 1969 portant
Code des stupéfiants”. FAOLEX shows ordinance-law 69-006 of 10 February 1969
as the land-contribution (property tax) law. It was not used. Leganet’s
criminal-law index lists only the 1903 hemp ordinance under stupéfiants.

A 1920 botanical note (De Wildeman) says hemp smoking was already forbidden
and asks for a ban on niando (Alchornea floribunda). Niando is not cannabis.
No entry.
