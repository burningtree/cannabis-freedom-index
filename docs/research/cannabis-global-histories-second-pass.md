## Corrections and article edits

The existing ID `uk-lords-therapeutic-cannabis-inquiry-1997` retains its page address,
but its date is now **11 November 1998**, the report's publication date. The parliamentary
catalogue records an order to print on 4 November 1998; the publication announcement
says the inquiry began in April. Taylor's p. 353 reference to Geoffrey Guy giving
Lords evidence in 1997 conflicts with those records. We do not infer an additional
1997 Lords hearing from it. The original entry also included the 1997 conference,
which now has its own event.

A new article explains the correction and separates prescription recommendations
from enacted law. The existing UK Alliance article now links to the delegation,
conference and CAMS events, and replaces the claim that nobody else had a lawful
option with a distinction between herbal cannabis and exceptional nabilone prescribing.
The East German 1973 article now links to the 1990 police working group.
