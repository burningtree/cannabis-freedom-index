# China, 1934–1979

23 October 2026. The hole runs from the end of the legal Xinjiang hashish
trade in 1934 to the Criminal Law of 1 July 1979.

## Read, and not added

The Government Administration Council order of 24 February 1950 on strictly
prohibiting opium (政务院《关于严禁鸦片烟毒的通令》, 政秘字第一七八号), read on
Wikisource. Zhou Enlai signed it the day the 21st council meeting passed it.
It orders an end to planting 烟, bans trafficking in 烟土毒品, and requires
registration of people who smoke 烟毒. It also says 其他毒品, "other drugs",
without naming 大麻. Planting 烟 in this order is the opium crop. It was not
entered as a cannabis event.

The Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region Regulations on Prohibiting Cannabis
Drugs, passed 2 November 1991 and amended 11 December 1997, were read on
Wikisource. They prohibit smuggling, selling, transporting, making, smoking
and holding cannabis drugs, and planting the cannabis plant used for them.
A small planting can draw up to 15 days' detention and a fine of up to 3,000
yuan. They take effect on publication. They sit after the 1979 Criminal Law
and after the 1990 narcotics decision, so they do not fill this hole. They
were not added in this pass.

A 2008 survey of drug legislation from 1949 to 1998, read earlier the same
day, lists the 1950, 1952, 1963 and 1973 instruments as opium, morphine and
poppy measures and does not name 大麻.
