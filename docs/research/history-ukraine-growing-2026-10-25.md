  Followed the original-edition link from the later 2024 reproduction. Read

  Ordinary cultivation had no plant-count minimum; fine up to 50 tax-free

  for the final old-law position, not the original 1960 position.
- **Original Supreme Court Plenum Resolution 4, 26 April 2002**:
  <https://zakononline.ua/documents/show/220363___220428>.

## What changed in the historical account

**2009 was a tightening**, not the start of an administrative exception after
continuous criminalisation. The missing **2008** amendment set the ordinary
criminal threshold above 100 plants. The 2009 amendment then began the cannabis
criminal tier at **ten** and reduced its aggravated threshold from **500 to 50**.
It kept an administrative band below ten plants. The text's administrative
"up to ten" wording is read alongside the explicit criminal "from ten" wording;
exactly ten is not presented as a safe administrative allowance. Separate prior-
crime and group-for-sale grounds remain relevant independently of plant count.

The 2001 code's ordinary cultivation offence removed the 1995 repetition-after-
administrative-penalty requirement. Its small personal possession position also
changed: the separate old-code repeat offence was absent, and the 2002 court
guidance directs small quantities into administrative article 44. The code's
ordinary article 309 penalty does not mean every trace quantity became criminal.

The 1995 scored event now represents its **mixed** reform, so it is `other`:
cultivation eased while public use became a crime. Its title, description and
tags cover both. The companion release order remains a context entry; its
`unscored` reason now points to the scored underlying law instead of deferring
a grow-law reconstruction already carried out here. Its adoption date remains
an order date, not evidence of completed releases.

## Scores and limits of reconstruction

Current **possession 3→2** corrects the fine-only classification. `segments.yaml`
defines 3 as at most an administrative fine; article 44 also permits custody.
Score 2 places this administrative-but-custodial regime between a crime and the
fine-only administrative tier. It does not label the offence criminal or change
the `decriminalised` status. Other current scores are preserved. Exact total is
now **20**, previously **22.5 (display 23)**.

The possession component is **1 before the 2001 replacement** (criminal exposure
under the old personal-possession/repeat provisions), then **2** under small-
quantity administrative liability with arrest. This is a statutory classification,
not a claim that most users were imprisoned. Earlier pre-1974 possession law is
still not reconstructed, so the first 1934 aggregate remains provisional.

Cultivation is **0 before the 1995 overhaul**, reflecting direct criminal
exposure for the prohibited varieties in the 1974/1987 texts; **2 after the 1995
administrative first tier**; **1 under the 2001 ordinary criminal rule**; and
**2 after the 2008 threshold**. The 2009 threshold tightening remains unscored
because the existing segment scale still classifies a few plants administratively;
it does not award an extra point for the much larger 2008 cap. Likewise, the 2024
probation amendment changes a sentencing choice without establishing a whole
point of changed practice in enforcement; fines/restriction remain possible.

Existing 1987, 1991 and 1995 snapshots are rebased for possession/cultivation;
the 2024 medical snapshot is rebased only for the corrected possession score.
The 1934 FREE `before` vector is unchanged. The 2001 mixed event is **flat in
its total** because equal-weight possession and cultivation changes offset;
its component scores do change. Never drop it merely because its aggregate
movement is zero.
