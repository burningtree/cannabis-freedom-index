## Ghana

## Argentina

The agency's hemp page says applications open during October. Argentine administrative holidays were not counted, so the history date is October 2026 and not a calculated calendar day, and not 3 September.

Resolution 41/2026 was read on the official text. It was adopted on 26 June and published on 30 June. Article 16 puts it into force on the tenth administrative working day after publication. That calendar day was not calculated. The two resolutions are separate entries.
