# Brazil, 1886 to 1915

The dated gap is `brazil-sao-luis-directive-1886` to `brazil-doria-paper-1915`
(December 1915).

## Decree 11.481 of 10 February 1915, read and not added

https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/decreto/1910-1929/d11481.html
promulgates the International Opium Convention signed at The Hague on
23 January 1912 and its closing protocol. The decree names opium, morphine
and its derivatives, and cocaine. It does not name cannabis, Indian hemp or
maconha. It was not added.

Decree 14.969 of 3 September 1921, read at
https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/decreto/1910-1929/d14969.htm, regulates
entry of poisonous substances and names opium and its derivatives and cocaine
and its congeners. It is after this gap and does not name cannabis. It was
not added. Decree-Law 891 of 25 November 1938 already exists as
`brazil-decree-law-891-1938`.
