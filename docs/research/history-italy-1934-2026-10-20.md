# Italy's 1934 narcotics decree — 20 October 2026

Added one unscored event and an article on Royal Decree-Law 151, filling the
gap between the 1923 and 1954 entries.

## Evidence and limits

- Read the complete decree in the 19 February 1934 official gazette, printed
  pp. 838–841 (PDF pp. 22–25). Visually inspected printed pp. 839–841 against
  extracted text, including the Indian hemp clause, penalties, confinement,
  repeal and commencement provisions. OCR errors in headings and dates were
  resolved from the images.
- Article 1's possession clause concerns clandestine or fraudulent commerce.
  Article 2 covers specified gatherings; Article 7 has setting and condition
  requirements. None was recast as a general personal-possession prohibition.
  Article 12's confinement power is discretionary, not automatic for all users.
- Article 21 expressly sets commencement on publication, 19 February. The
  instrument's 15 January date is separately retained in the description.
- Read the complete sole article and signature of Law 1145, printed p. 3394
  of the 25 July gazette (PDF p. 2), and visually checked that page. It converts
  the decree into law; 7 June is its instrument date, not the decree's original
  commencement. No redundant conversion event was added.
- Read the official 15 March corrigendum's text. Its corrections concern the
  poppy wording in Article 1 and a cross-reference in Article 11; neither was
  used to alter the cannabis or confinement account.
- Article 19's colonial application and local implementing powers need separate
  research before adding country-specific events. No colonial score is inferred.
- Original PDFs and page renders are in ignored `tmp/pdfs/italy-2026-10-20/`.
  Sources support the legal text, not estimates of enforcement or confinement
  frequency. No profiles, scores or score baselines were changed.
