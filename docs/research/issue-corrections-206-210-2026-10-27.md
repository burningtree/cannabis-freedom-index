## #206 — Saint Kitts and Nevis: private use and statutory tickets

Read the operative analysis and orders in [Maccabbee, 3 May 2019](https://www.worldcourts.com/ecsc/eng/decisions/2019.05.03_Maccabbee_v_Commissioner_Police.htm), paragraphs 102–107. Orders (6)–(7) cover adult private own-use possession and cultivation of any amount; paragraph 105 expressly says Parliament can and must legislate quantities. The declarations were suspended for ninety days. This does not establish unconditional unlimited permission after later legislation.

Read all four pages of final [Act 12/2023](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2023/ACTs/Act-12-of-2023-Drugs-Prevention-Abatement-of-the-Misuse-and-Abuse-of-Drugs-Amendment-Act.pdf), particularly sections 4–5, together with sections 3–4 of final [Act 14/2023](https://lawcommission.gov.kn/wp-content/documents/Annual-Laws/2023/ACTs/Act-14-of-2023-Freedom-of-Conscience-Cannabis-Act.pdf). The former provides secured unlicensed 56 g cannabis/15 g resin and five-home-plant EC$50 routes, retention, community service on payment default and imprisonment on service default; public smoking has an EC$500 route. The latter supplies adult licence exceptions and temporary seven-day licences. These final Acts replace reliance on drafts.

The [government's February 2023 recap](https://sknis.gov.kn/2023/02/08/st-kitts-and-nevis-government-amends-criminal-records-act-to-address-shortfalls-in-the-legislation/) predates these June laws. Searches did not locate a later appellate reversal of Maccabbee. The visible appellate headnotes in [Percival and Simmonds, 10 November 2022](https://kn.vlex.com/vid/tenielle-percival-v-the-919042075) distinguish the private-use ruling from intent to supply/importation; the paywalled full reasons were not independently read. No claim that all later litigation has been excluded is made.

Corrected profile, visitor guidance, carry/grow/public/caught answers, 2019 entry and its article. Higher private amounts require a case-specific assessment under the ruling and later law. Scores remain 4/3/5/1/3/1: the existing possession/cultivation bands recognise private protection alongside public limits and ticket/licence routes; the sources do not justify unrestricted 10s or a new dated score step.

## #207 — South Carolina: evidence of intent is not automatic felony classification

Read [Code §44-53-370](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t44c053.php), (a), (b)(2), (c), (d)(2), (d)(5), (e)(1), and the simple-possession rows of the [Attorney General's official 2024 chart](https://myag.scag.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/SC_Criminal_Offenses_Penalties_2024_Edition.pdf), codes 0179/0180. Above one ounce is prima facie evidence of intent under (a); the separate simple-possession misdemeanour remains available. First small possession is jail OR a $100–$200 fine. Explain the larger simple-possession first/repeat penalties and felony charging risk, with ten-pound trafficking separate. Corrected summary, possession, enforcement and caught answer. Scores remain 1/0/2/0/1/0: small possession still exposes users to custody; this corrects classification rather than a change in law.
