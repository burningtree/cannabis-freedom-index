### West Virginia: the report missed §401(c)

[§407](https://code.wvlegislature.gov/60A-4-407/) contains the general discretionary
deferral procedure. However, [§401(c)](https://law.justia.com/codes/west-virginia/chapter-60a/article-4/section-60a-4-401/)
mandates that procedure for first possession below 15 g, as well as
[§402(c)](https://law.justia.com/codes/west-virginia/chapter-60a/article-4/section-60a-4-402/)
for first unpaid distribution below 15 g. The report's proposed possession correction
would lose an existing protection. The profile instead distinguishes mandatory routing,
discretionary routing and dismissal after probation. Official WV URLs intermittently
redirected to an unrelated FBI page; the reproduced full texts supplied the missing sections.

### Saint Lucia: read §8A(b), not just §8A(a)

[§8A](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/drugs-prevention-of-misuse-act/section-8a)
also disapplies prosecution and punishment under §37 for small cannabis quantities.
[§12](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/drugs-prevention-of-misuse-act//section-12-02)
retains school-location offences and its rebuttable supply presumption. Reading both
reveals an unresolved interaction; no reviewed judgment establishes the report's
categorical criminal-exposure example. The profile names both rules and withholds a
specific sentence. [§25](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/drugs-prevention-of-misuse-act/section-25)
and [§37](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/drugs-prevention-of-misuse-act/section-37)
were also reviewed. The cultivation restriction is explicit in
[regulation 8](https://attorneygeneralchambers.com/laws-of-saint-lucia/drugs-prevention-of-misuse-act/section-8-01).
