## Remaining searches

Twelve entries still need a suitable image: Anasha, Diamba, Matekoane, Mbanje, Zatla,
The Bena Riamba, Cannabis in the kitchen, The Ethiopian Zion Coptic Church,
Hemp in the Taoist censer, Kumina, Siemieniotka and The elder’s morning pipe.

- Commons searches for Lesotho, Zimbabwe and the Chuy valley did not produce
  usable still photographs of the relevant cannabis crop or custom.
- Siemieniotka searches mainly returned photographs uploaded by a user with that
  name, rather than the soup. Takrouri searches returned an unrelated Nubian soldier.
- Cannabis Cup searches often returned the separate Emerald Cup. Used a photograph
  explicitly identified as the High Times event instead.
- [Ritual del Kumina](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ritual_del_Kumina.jpg)
  claims to be the uploader’s own work from August 2024, but looks like a historical
  black-and-white photograph. Its provenance is not reliable enough to reuse.
- The [Smithsonian record of two Zulu men smoking dagga](https://www.si.edu/object/archives/components/sova-naa-photolot-97-ref14515)
  is a promising lead for The elder’s morning pipe; file access and reuse terms need
  verification before adding it.

None of the remaining entries received a generic substitute or an invented depiction.

## User-selected Diamba specimen

Added the exact [historical specimen image supplied by the user](https://www.alchimiaweb.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/mazorca_1-910x970.jpg).
Its visible label reads “Hemp for smoking (diamba)”, lists the Songo people,
Angola, H. Chatelain as collector and 25 July 1890 as the specimen date. The label
also says it was wrapped in banana leaves. These details describe the specimen
record; the date is not claimed as the date the photograph was taken.
Credited the historical photograph as reproduced by Alchimia, linking its
[source article](https://www.alchimiaweb.com/blogen/malawi-cob-fermented-cannabis/);
no photographer or free licence is invented. The original local image is unchanged.
An optional image fit field preserves the full specimen and label in the culture
article’s height-limited lead figure. Other lead images keep their existing fit.
Diamba now has a lead image; eleven of the original twenty gaps remain.
