# Haggai Ram, *Intoxicating Zion* (2020): prohibition events

Book verified from its title and copyright pages: Stanford University Press, 2020, ebook ISBN
9781503613928, LCCN 2020025589. Read selected sections of chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 6 and the
conclusion for dated legal and prohibition events. Locators are chapter and section; the EPUB has no
verified print-page mapping.

## Score timeline re-based: Israel and Palestine

The site dated the first ban in Mandatory Palestine to the 1936 Dangerous Drugs Ordinance. Ram
(chapter 2) states that the Mandatory authorities promulgated the Dangerous Drugs Ordinance of
1925, based on Britain's Dangerous Drugs Acts, which "categorically outlawed the manufacture,
importation, exportation and possession" of dangerous drugs including Indian hemp, and that it was
amended in 1928, 1932 and 1936. Chapter 3 adds that article 14 of the 1936 ordinance, absent from the
earlier versions, punished premises used for preparing or smoking hashish and the possession of
pipes and utensils.

- New scored entries `israel-mandate-ordinance-1925` and `palestine-mandate-ordinance-1925` carry
  the first drop from the unregulated state.
- `israel-dangerous-drugs-1936` and `palestine-dangerous-drugs-1936` became `tightening` entries,
  one point on consumption for the new smoking-premises and paraphernalia offences.
- Current scores unchanged. Chains: il 98→12 (1925), 12→11 (1936); ps 98→9 (1925), 9→8 (1936).
