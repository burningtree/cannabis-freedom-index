# França, *História da maconha no Brasil* — prohibition pass (15 October 2026)

Edition: São Paulo, Editora Jandaíra, 2022 (© 2015, 2022), ISBN 978-65-5094-013-3, EPUB. The EPUB has no
printed page numbers, so locators give the chapter number and title.

## Read

- Chapter 2, "O vício dos pretos?": whole chapter and notes. Colonial and nineteenth-century use, municipal
  bans from 1830, early twentieth-century medical writing.
- Chapter 3, "Diambismo: um flagelo social": whole chapter and notes. The doctors' campaign; the national
  laws of 1921, 1932, 1938, 1940, 1941, 1964, 1968 and 1976; Geneva 1925.
- Chapter 4, "O canabismo dos hippies e dos excluídos": selected passages on the 1976 law, the Gilberto Gil
  and Rita Lee cases (1976), Judge Mayrink da Costa (1979) and the USP debate (1980).

## Corrections to existing entries

- brazil-rio-pango-ban-1830: users' penalty changed from "up to three days" to eight days in jail. França
  quotes the 1830 code (his note 15: *Código de posturas da Ilustríssima Câmara Municipal*, p. 9). Secondary
  accounts giving three days are noted.
- brazil-penal-code-1940: the 1940 fine was two to ten contos de réis. The fine of ten to fifty times the
  highest minimum wage came with the amendments of 1964 and 1968, so the entry was wrong to give it for 1940.
- brazil-federal-ban-1932: added decree 20.930's listing of Cannabis indica and its penalty of three to nine
  months and a fine.
- brazil-users-as-traffickers-1968: added the penalty from decree-law 385.
- brazil-law-6368-1976: França added as a source; the text already agreed with him.
