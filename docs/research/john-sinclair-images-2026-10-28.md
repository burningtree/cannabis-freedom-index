# John Sinclair: image selection, provenance and reuse terms

Three images were added to `culture/people/john-sinclair.md`: the 1969 arrest photograph as the
lead portrait, the 1971 Freedom Rally poster in the rally section, and the 1971 prison-diary
newspaper page in the "Ten years for two joints" section. All three are local files in
`src/assets/culture/`; nothing is hotlinked. The article uses three images, within the
three-to-four limit.

## Lead portrait: John Sinclair after an arrest in 1969

- File: `src/assets/culture/john-sinclair-arrest-1969.jpg`, 5819 × 3630 pixels, 2.0 MB.
- Source record: Michigan Memories, "Sinclair, John", <https://michmemories.org/records/sinclair-john-9>.
- Holding: Labadie Photograph Collection, University of Michigan Library, item LPF.1577.
- Description on the record: "John Sinclair waiving peace sign after his arrest in 1969. Photo by
  Leni Sinclair. Fifth Estate photo."
- Full-resolution file: downloaded from the University of Michigan Library's image API at
  <https://quod.lib.umich.edu/cgi/i/image/api/image/scl/LPF.1577/LPF1577/full/full/0/default.jpg>.
  This is the 5819 × 3630 original, not the 250-pixel thumbnail shown on the Michigan Memories
  page. The file was inspected: it shows Sinclair in the back of a sheriff's car marked
  "Oakland Patrol County Sheriff", raising two fingers in a peace sign. It is not cropped in the
  article; `fit: contain` preserves the full frame.
- Date and subject care: the record says the photograph was taken after an arrest in 1969. The
  cannabis case discussed in the article began with an offence on 22 December 1966 and an arrest
  on 24 January 1967, in Detroit. The caption therefore says "after an arrest in 1969" and does
  not describe the picture as the two-joint arrest. The car's Oakland County marking also points
  away from the Detroit case. The image is a portrait of Sinclair in custody, not a documentary
  photograph of the 1966 offence.

## Rally poster: John Sinclair Freedom Rally, 10 December 1971

- File: `src/assets/culture/john-sinclair-freedom-rally-poster-1971.jpg`, 1200 × 1800 pixels.
- Source record: The Henry Ford, "Advertising Poster, 'John Sinclair Freedom Rally,' December 10,
  1971", object 2009.193.1,
  <https://www.thehenryford.org/collections/explore/artifact/362531>.
- Object details: designed by Gary Grimshaw, Detroit; lithograph on paper; 22 × 17 inches;
  Poster Collection; credit "From the Collections of The Henry Ford." The printed text includes
  "FREE JOHN SINCLAIR AND ALL POLITICAL PRISONERS", the date, the speakers and musicians, and
  "CRISLER ARENA / ANN ARBOR".
- Image: the museum's public `_detail` file,
  <https://collections-media.thehenryford.org/CollectionImages/_detail/photos/thf152656.jpg>.
  The image server's Deep Zoom descriptor gives a 3744 × 5616 source, but tile requests returned
  403, so the 1200 × 1800 detail image is the highest-resolution public file available without
  special access. The whole poster is shown; no typography, names or event details are cropped.

### Reuse terms

The Henry Ford's copyright policy distinguishes public-domain content, content owned by The Henry
Ford, and content owned by third parties. It states that content "may be subject to copyright
ownership by third parties", that posting it "is not an automatic grant of permission for visitors
to the Site to copy, adapt, distribute, or otherwise use Content", and that, unless permission is
expressly stated, the policy grants no permission to use third-party content. Its personal-use
allowance for The Henry Ford's own copyright does not cover public display or digital transmission
(<https://www.thehenryford.org/copyright-policy>).

The object record does not state a licence, and the museum's custody of the poster does not by
itself establish copyright ownership or permission to reproduce it. The poster is used with the
designer, object number, collection and museum named, and with a link to the object record. No
licence field is set and no permission is claimed. Permission for commercial reuse or
redistribution should be sought from The Henry Ford and the Gary Grimshaw estate or other rights
holder.

## Prison diary: Ann Arbor Sun, 30 April 1971

- File: `src/assets/culture/john-sinclair-prison-diary-1971.jpg`, 2862 × 4726 pixels.
- Source record: Ann Arbor District Library, "Excerpts From The Prison Diary Of John Sinclair",
  <https://aadl.org/node/193130>.
- Publication: *Ann Arbor Sun*, 30 April 1971. The page combines diary entries dated
  1 May to 5 December with a photograph credited "PHOTO - CRAIG CARRELL". The scan was inspected:
  it is the complete newspaper page, including the headline, the diary text and the photograph of
  Sinclair at a typewriter in front of bars.
- The article quotes two lines from the 17 May entry for commentary on Sinclair as a writer in
  prison. The quotation is short and attributed; the diary is not reproduced in full.

### Reuse terms

The Ann Arbor District Library's site footer says "All content CC-by-NC 2.0 unless otherwise
specified" (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/2.0/>). That blanket statement is not a
clearance of the underlying 1971 newspaper text or the Craig Carrell photograph, whose rights are
not established on the page. No item-specific licence or permission was found. The scan is used
with the newspaper, date, photographer and library named, and with a link to the digitised page.
No licence field is set and no permission is claimed. Before commercial reuse or redistribution,
the library and the rights holders should be consulted.
