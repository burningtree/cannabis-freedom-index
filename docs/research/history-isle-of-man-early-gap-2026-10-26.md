## Sources actually examined

- Downloaded both complete original Tynwald circulars: GC1256 (five PDF pages)
  and GC1258 (one page). Read the extracted text, including prescriptions,
  record-keeping, definitions, schedules and commencement. Visually inspected
  GC1256 PDF p.2 (possession, professional authority and locked storage) and
  GC1258 in full. Both explicitly commence on 1 January 1930. GC1256's heading
  appears to say 27 December, while its cover and signature say 17 December;
  this discrepancy does not affect the explicit operative date.
- GC1256 covers extracts/tinctures of Indian hemp alongside other dangerous
  drugs. Regulations 1–3 require authority for manufacture, supply and possession;
  3(2) gives possession authority for lawful prescriptions/dispensing; 5–6 provide
  professional/shop authority; 8–9 prescribe form and dispensing safeguards;
  11 requires registers; 14 sets two-year document retention; 18 commences and
  repeals the 1923 and 1926 regulations. Legal authority for a medicine is not
  evidence of particular products stocked, patients treated or efficacy.
- GC1258 extends the Raw Opium Regulations 1923 to Indian hemp, resin and
  resin-based preparations, except extracts/tinctures; separate registers;
  repeals the 1928 Coca Leaves and Indian Hemp Regulations. The underlying
  1923 raw-opium rules were not recovered: do not invent their detailed offences
  or penalties. Neither 1930 order is claimed as the first cannabis restriction.
- Downloaded Peter W. Edge, Manx Public Law (Isle of Man Law Society, 1997),
  electronic edition prepared 2011, from the Oxford Brookes author repository.
  Examined title/copyright/contents; read printed pp.242–243, PDF250–251.
  Visually verified p.242's year, one-month sentence and Mandryk footnote 7.
  The 1978 entry cites Edge's account of the judgments, not original cases we
  have read. Ward (1978) is a corroborating lead, not a separate dated event.
  Barlow's report span [1981–3] and Buchanan's [1990–2] are not exact decision
  years. No events are invented from those ranges. Chapter/catalogue coverage

## Open leads and dating limits

The original 1923 and 1926 Acts, 1928 hemp regulations and Raw Opium Regulations
1923 remain targets. A later repealer proves earlier regulations existed but
cannot establish the island's first cannabis restriction or its commencement.
Keep the 1925 estimate pending that research. A newspaper account found in search
confuses the 1912/1925 treaty history and is not used as legal evidence.
