## Measurement and classification

Blood serum and whole blood are different sample matrices. Do not directly compare
Germany's serum value with the British whole-blood value or infer that one rule is
more permissive simply because its number is larger. A roadside screening result is
not itself a quantified evidential blood result.

The `driving.status` field summarises the legal trigger, not road safety or a waiting
time after consumption. `limit` denotes a quantified threshold; `zero` denotes a
presence-based prohibition; `impairment` denotes an ability/intoxication standard in
the provision reviewed. A country can have additional offences alongside that
headline rule. Medical prescribing does not create a general permission to drive
while impaired.

No fixed “safe after X hours” advice was added. The two books supplied earlier are
historical sources and do not establish present-day traffic penalties. The profile
citations link to the official sources actually read.

Remaining gaps include medical/novice rules in the new profiles, fuller testing
procedures and a primary-source review of the Dutch penalties. Ukraine's official
code was inaccessible in this session, so no Ukrainian driving section was added.

### Findings retained for further classification review

These findings were researched but **not** added to the profiles. The schema's
`zero` category means detectable THC and `impairment` requires demonstrated impairment.
A prohibition on driving after consumption alone does not establish either evidential
standard. Assigning a category without examining regulations or case law would
claim more than the statute supports.

- **Bangladesh:** [Road Transport Act 2018](https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/act-print-1262.html),
  section 49(1), first part (a), prohibits driving after drinking alcohol or consuming
  intoxicants. Section 92(1) provides up to three months' imprisonment, up to Tk10,000,
  or both, and deduction of one driver point for violating that first part. The
  Tk25,000 figure in section 89 concerns section 46 and must not be substituted for
  the intoxication provision. The current official Bengali text was read in full;
  an evidential THC threshold and fixed driving-ban duration were not established.
- **Sri Lanka:** Motor Traffic Act section 151 prohibits driving on a road after
  alcohol or drug consumption. Section 151(1C) provides medical examination for
  suspected drug use; section 151(1D) permits regulations about drug concentrations.
  No operative THC regulation was verified. The [official Motor Traffic (Amendment)
  Act No. 10 of 2019](https://www.parliament.lk/uploads/acts/gbills/english/6132.pdf),
  sections 7–8, updates the ordinary offence to LKR25,000–30,000 or up to three months'
  imprisonment or both, plus suspension up to twelve months. Public passenger
  carriage has up to six months and licence cancellation. The underlying trigger
  and testing provisions were read in a [third-party reproduction of the 2024
  consolidation](https://lankalaw.net/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Motor-Traffic-Consolidated-2024.pdf);
  a primary-source consolidation and implementing regulations remain to be checked.

Follow-up research should resolve these consumption-based rules before classifying
them, and establish Nigeria's testing and disqualification rules. No waiting time
or general medical exemption was inferred from silence in these sources.

- **Jamaica:** [Road Traffic Act 2018](https://www.japarliament.gov.jm/attachments/article/339/the%20road%20traffic%20act%202018.pdf),

  and the separate smoking-ganja offence must not be merged. The

  numerical THC value was established in the Act or the
  [2022 Regulations](https://jis.gov.jm/media/2023/02/Road-Traffic-Regulations-May-20-2022-complete.pdf).
  The headline classification describes paragraph 1's verified impairment rule.
  [Official commencement guidance](https://jis.gov.jm/radio_programs/road-traffic-act-now-in-effect/)
  confirms that the new Act and regulations came into effect on 1 February 2023.

  sections 26, 26A and 26G. Its disqualification commencement and judicial exception
  are more precise than the [transport department summary](https://www.jpd.gov.bn/acts-and-regulations/).
  The blood-testing provision concerns specified crash circumstances and treatment
  samples; it should not be described as unrestricted random THC testing.
- **Trinidad and Tobago:** Read the [Transport Ministry's statutory extract](https://mowt.gov.tt/News-Room/Media-Releases/Driving-While-Impaired-by-Alcohol-or-Other-Drugs)

  section 10(f). The recent increase applies to section 70A's alcohol offence; it
  must not be transplanted into section 70. A current consolidated Act and operational
  drug-testing arrangements remain follow-up targets. The Ministry's 2019 statement
  about planned drugalizer devices does not prove they are in operation in 2026.

**Ghana deferred:** The [Transport Ministry](https://www.mot.gov.gh/10/16/1/365/transport-minister-launches-nationwide-sensitisation-on-the-new-road-traffic-laws-and-regulations)
