Times of Malta captions the image Dgħajsa tal-Latini, from Edward Alfred

author's collections. The entry follows the requested early-1920s dating.

The text explicitly treats the photograph as maritime context. There is no

Verified Article 25 in the official Parliament PDF's text, PDF page 17
(zero-based page 16), printed A 1579:
https://www.parlament.mt/media/115003/act-lxvi-cannabis.pdf#page=17
It directs the general replacement of qanneb indjan by Cannabis in the
Dangerous Drugs Ordinance. Article 24 changes the definition separately.
The entry distinguishes that literal capitalised legal word from kannabis,
which appears elsewhere in the Maltese Act. A short original-language excerpt
with an English explanation links directly to the official page. A secondary
facsimile was considered but omitted: direct PDF download returned HTTP 403,
so no reconstructed document image or unverified crop was substituted.
