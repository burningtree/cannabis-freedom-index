## What was verified (all sources actually read)

### Executive Order 7715 (Federal Register, 30 September 1937; via govinfo)

- Roosevelt's order, signed 26 September and filed 29 September 1937:
  section 15 of the Marihuana Tax Act (approved 2 August 1937, Public
  No. 238, 75th Congress) directed presidential implementation in the
  Virgin Islands. Mainland-mirror administration (finance commissioner for
  internal revenue collectors; governor for the narcotics commissioner).
  From 1 October 1937, importing, manufacturing, producing, compounding,
  selling, dealing, dispensing, prescribing, administering, giving away or
  acquiring marihuana without compliance was unlawful. Registration with the
  finance commissioner; annual special taxes each July; 2-cent order forms;
  Treasury-supplied transfer-tax stamps; mainland exemptions carried over;
  governor's regulations to follow Treasury's as far as applicable;
  inspection powers.
- New `articles/us-virgin-islands-marihuana-tax-act-1937.md` (357 words,
  3 sections, both sources cited; links /country/vi/). Entry rewritten
  with the signed date and mirror-administration detail, locator added.

### Executive Order 10250 (American Presidency Project, read)

- Truman, 5 June 1951, section (f): the president's Virgin Islands
  marihuana registration/tax powers (26 U.S.C. 2554(b), 2603(b)) delegated
  to the Secretary of the Interior in consultation with the Treasury.
  Used only for the article's closing paragraph; the 1951 entry itself was
  left untouched.
