# Current-law corrections for Bosnia, Kazakhstan and Mexico

23 October 2026. Issues 15, 16 and 17. Read `docs/DATA-MAINTENANCE.md` on
current law and on comparing a profile with the statute. Scores were checked
against `segments.yaml` and left in place.

## Bosnia and Herzegovina

The Paragraf consolidation of the Federation criminal code was read. The
article immediately before article 240 is “Posjedovanje i omogućavanje
uživanja opojnih droga”. Paragraph 3: unauthorized possession of a narcotic
drug is punished with up to one year in prison. The paragraph sets no smaller
personal-use tier.

The Republika Srpska criminal code, consolidated through Official Gazette
73/2023, was read at articles 207 and 208. Article 207 punishes unauthorized
production and trade, and holding a drug in order to sell it is inside that
trade offence. Article 208 punishes enabling another person to use a drug.
That consolidation has no standalone possession offence matching Federation
article 239(3). Brčko District’s possession penalty was not read, so the
profile does not state it.

Possession stays at 1 and enforcement at 2. The scale’s 0 for possession is
“prison is a realistic outcome for a small amount”. One indictment shows that
prosecution is available. It does not show how often courts impose the year.
No new history event was added.

## Kazakhstan

Article 296 was read on kodeksy-kz.com, marked current with changes and
checked 22 October 2026. Part 2 punishes unauthorized manufacture, processing,
acquisition, storage or transport without intent to sell with a fine of up to
160 monthly calculation indices, corrective work, up to 160 hours of community
service, or arrest of up to 40 days. Part 4 punishes an especially large
amount with three to seven years. The detailed possession paragraph already
said this. The summary had called small possession an administrative offence.
That sentence is replaced. A ministry proposal to change the treatment is
named as a proposal. Possession stays at 2. An older Supreme Court plenum
about the previous code’s article 259 was not used.

## Mexico

The Official Gazette text of General Declaration of Unconstitutionality 1/2018
was read at paragraphs 91–93. COFEPRIS may authorize a named adult to acquire
seed, sow, cultivate, harvest, prepare, possess and transport cannabis for
personal recreational use. The authorization excludes import, trade, supply
and distribution. The paragraphs set no universal gram limit and no plant
count. COFEPRIS’s press release of 7 July 2026 says the same about personal
self-consumption and about commerce and supply. The 28 g and six/eight-plant
sentences are removed. The separate rule that up to 5 g is not prosecuted
stays. Possession stays at 3 and cultivation at 3.
