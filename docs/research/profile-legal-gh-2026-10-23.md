# Ghana personal purchase, 23 October 2026

Issue #20. The profile described the Narcotics Control Commission Act as a fine for personal use and left out section 45.

Read from the Act 1019 PDF (assent and Gazette notification both printed as 11 May 2020):

- Sections 37(2)(a) and 41(2)(a): possession for use is a fine. Second Schedule row: not less than 200 and not more than 500 penalty units. The custodial column is N/A. The default column is not more than fifteen months if the fine is not paid.
- Section 45(2): purchasing a narcotic drug or plant for personal use is an offence. Section 45(1) is purchase with intent to sell or supply. Subsection (3) sends both to the same Second Schedule row: not less than 2,000 and not more than 10,000 penalty units, OR not less than four and not more than ten years. The default column is N/A.
- Section 45(5): a court may order treatment for a subsection (2) conviction, without prejudice to the penalty.
- Section 46 mitigation applies to section 44, administering a drug to another person.

GhaLII’s 2 August 2023 consolidation still contains section 45. Its amendment note is attached to section 43 (Act 1100 of 2023). Parliament’s bitstream PDF returned 403, so the section text was read from the copy at tbhivcare.org and checked against the GhaLII HTML. The GhaLII schedule row for possession rendered “not less than five hundred”; the PDF reads “not more than five hundred”.

No sentencing-frequency source was read, so possession stays at 2 and enforcement stays at 3. No new history event: section 45 is in the 2020 Act. The scored date moves from 20 March 2020, the day the existing article gives for passage, to 11 May 2020, the assent and Gazette date printed on the Act. The 2020 year-end score is unchanged.
