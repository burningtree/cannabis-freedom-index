# Argentina possession and cannabis controls — 20 October 2026

Added a 1926 article and two unscored events for the 1968 regulatory law and
1977 cannabis cultivation prohibition. Clarified the existing 1926 event's
scope and added the official register and historical research to its sources.

## Evidence and limits

- Read the official Law 11,331 register, which reproduces the added clause in
  its summary and gives sanction on 16 July and publication on 13 August 1926.
  A complete original gazette scan was not inspected; the clause is attributed
  to the official reproduction rather than described as a recovered scan.
- Read Corda's 2018 book, Chapter 1, printed pp. 12–13, and its publication
  metadata. It distinguishes early administrative references to Indian hemp
  from the disputed cannabis coverage of criminal-law terminology. The article
  does not claim that no cannabis controls existed before 1968 or 1977.
- Read the official Law 17,818 text at Articles 1–3 and its annexed cannabis
  entry, and the adoption/publication metadata. Although labelled original by
  the portal, the displayed text contains later monetary provisions. These were
  not used to infer 1968 fine amounts or historical penalties. Nor were later
  additions to other parts of the list backdated. The event records adoption,
  not an inferred commencement day.
- Read the complete three-article Law 21,671 on the official portal. Adoption
  and publication are separate, and no prison penalty is invented from this
  instrument alone. The portal labels its issuing institution as Congress,
  but the actual text says the president sanctioned and promulgated it under
  the military government's statute; the entry attributes it to the government.
- Left the 1924 entry unchanged: the different sentencing bands in that law
  warrant original-text verification before revising its existing summary.
- The existing 1926 score vector is preserved without fresh validation. Its
  cannabis-specific scope remains uncertain; new events omit `before` rather
  than reconstruct historical enforcement or score dimensions from these texts.
