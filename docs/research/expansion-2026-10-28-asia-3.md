Thailand validation: `profile-audit th` zero errors/warnings; `country-gaps th` has no missing sections, tables or citations. The profile now dates the controlled-herb status to November 2022 and the prescription rule to 26 June 2025, corrects home growing to DTAM's no-permit guidance, lowers public smoking to one month/2,000 baht while keeping shop smoking at one year/20,000, and states the 2026 qualifying-premises and trained-staff test. Nine entries cover 1959–2026; the 1935, 2019, 2022, 2025 and 2026 entries were corrected.

Vietnam validation: `profile-audit vn` zero errors/warnings; `country-gaps vn` has no missing sections, tables or citations. The profile now limits production’s capital tier to 30 kg of cannabis resin with no plant-parts tier, adds the Law 86/2025 transitional commutations, gives the top possession step a 20-year maximum, removes the unsupported deportation-for-use claim, and records the 2024 MPS fibre-hemp guidance. Eleven entries cover 1991–2026, including the 401 kg Quảng Ninh death sentences and the October 2026 death-penalty debate; the 1986 entry now says possession and use were not crimes under that code.

Philippines validation: `profile-audit ph` zero errors/warnings; `country-gaps ph` has no missing sections, tables or citations. It adds the ICC and drug-war death-toll context, the 2025–2026 death-penalty bills, and 15 history entries from 1907 to 2026. The 1972 title, 2014 filing date and 2024 HB 10439 title were corrected.

Timor-Leste validation: `profile-audit tl` zero errors/warnings; `country-gaps tl` has no missing sections, tables or citations. The profile now adds recreational, hemp, driving, black-market and arrest sections, folds away the old products block, states the 5 g herb / 2.5 g resin / 1.25 g oil threshold explicitly, adds possession and cultivation tables, and says plainly that no medical programme exists. The 2017 conviction is now “suspected” cannabis, the 2022 parcel is live seedlings, and the 2017 law is described as superseding the Indonesian law in practice. Ten entries cover 1997–2026.
