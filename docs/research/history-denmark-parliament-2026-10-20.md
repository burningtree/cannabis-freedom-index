# Danish parliamentary history, 1969–1971

Added three checked, unscored events and an article for the committee's cannabis
recommendations. This continues the broader history-coverage objective.

## Sources actually examined

Downloaded original parliamentary PDFs with curl after plain urllib requests
returned 403 and the web reader failed to decode or retrieve some PDFs.
Extracts and page renders remain in ignored `tmp/pdfs/denmark-1969/`.

- **29 May 1969 committee report:** read the report proper, printed columns
  2641–2648 (PDF pp. 1–4), including cannabis discussions at 2646–2647 and the
  distinction between the new supply offence and ordinary drug-law cases.
  Visually inspected PDF pp. 3–4 for the cannabis paragraphs. Read the attached
  draft prosecutorial circular selectively for context, without representing
  the draft as the independently verified final July circular.
- **L 133 as adopted:** read the complete one-page final bill, columns
  1353–1354, and visually inspected it. It supplies the original section 191
  elements, six-year maximum, review clause, Faroese exclusion and annotation
  identifying Law 276 of 18 June 1969. It says commencement occurs upon
  publication, rather than supplying a calendar day on its face.
- **L 72 as introduced, 1 December 1970:** read the opening remarks at columns
  1617–1619 and the relevant discussion at 1625–1626. Visually inspected PDF p. 5,
  where column 1626 explicitly confirms the 1969 supply law took effect on
  **1 July 1969**. Not all medical annexes were read, and the dated medical
  opinions were not treated as current scientific findings.
- **L 72 as adopted:** read and visually inspected the complete one-page text,
  columns 1117–1118. The two repeal provisions remove review clauses, not drug
  offences. Its annotation identifies Law 296 of **9 June 1971**. This is the
  law date used by the entry; separate commencement was not established.
- Opened the official parliamentary summaries and chronology for L 133, L 121
  and L 72 as supporting metadata. Original PDFs supply the operative claims.

## Applied

- `denmark-cannabis-research-recommendation-1969`, **29 May 1969**: committee
  calls for research into cannabis and legal controls, and inquiry into
  exempting personal-use conduct. This is neither legalisation nor an enacted
  decriminalisation measure. Added a 350–500-word article grounded in the report.
- `denmark-serious-drug-supply-1969`, **1 July 1969**: commencement of section 191.
  Transfer circumstances and intent requirements are retained; the six-year
  maximum is not applied to every possession case. Committee guidance about
  serious commercial cannabis activity is distinguished from the statute's
  offence wording. No fixed cannabis threshold was invented from the guidance.
- `denmark-drug-review-clauses-repealed-1971`, **9 June 1971 law date**: removal
  of mandatory review requirements, preserving the distinction between the
  government's stated policy and the narrow statutory repeal.

New events are connected to each other and to the existing 1955 and July 1969
entries. All earlier records, before vectors and current country profiles were
preserved. These additions improve legal and debate coverage without asserting
a newly quantified change in typical enforcement.
