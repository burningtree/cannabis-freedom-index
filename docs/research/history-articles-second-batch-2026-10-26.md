## Articles added

- `articles/suriname-opium-law-1908.md` — the 17 February 1908 ordinance
  (G.B. no. 13): cultivation ban for poppy and Cannabis sativa, licensed
  opium import/sale, pharmacists' exception, and the 1 March 1918 deadline.
  Links the 1918 import-ban, 1921 petition, 1922 convictions, 1955 order and
  1998 act entries.
- `articles/belgium-drug-law-1921.md` — the 24 February 1921 act as the

  1 September 1939, Part III cannabis offences and the later mandatory growing
  sentence. Distinguishes the 1939 wording from the 1980/2021 substitutions in
  the consolidation.

## Sources actually read

- [Encyclopaedie van Nederlandsch West-Indië, Ganja entry](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01_0010.php):
  Dutch original read on 26 October 2026. Resin-bound female tops; much used
  by British-Indian immigrants; ordinance of 17 Feb 1908 (G.B. no. 13), no more
  ganja imports after 1 March 1918; 1907–1913 imports (726, 432, 717, 791, 761,
  718, 689 kg); ƒ40/kg duty.
- [Same work, Opium entry](https://www.dbnl.org/tekst/benj004ency01_01/benj004ency01_01_0018.php):
  Dutch original read the same day. 1908 ordinance bans cultivation of
  P. somniferum and Cannabis sativa and regulates import/sale; licensed
  wholesale/retail traders; permits only for Paramaribo, Nieuw Nickerie and
  Albina (decision of 14 Dec 1908, G.B. no 89); 0.05 kg opium holding ceiling;
  pharmacists' medical exception with governor-set stocks (decision of 17 Feb
  1908, G.B. no 14); ƒ1,000/ƒ120 trader fees; from 1 March 1918 no
  cultivation, import or possession of either plant.
- [United Nations E/NL.1952/68](https://documents.un.org/api/symbol/access?s=E/NL.1952/68&l=en&t=pdf):
  PDF fetched and read via `pdftotext` on 26 October 2026. Belgian government
  communication of 4 August 1952; Royal Order of 31 December 1930 as amended
  20 September 1951; article 1(19) Indian hemp catalogue with external-use
  exception; article 2 enforcement under the Act of 24 February 1921; article 28
  penalties under that Act; article 29 repeals the 6 September 1921 decree.
- [Legislation Malta, Dangerous Drugs Ordinance (Chapter 101)](https://legislation.mt/getpdf/68526f314e900a33f42353cf):
  consolidated PDF fetched and read via `pdftotext` the same day. Ordinance
  XXXI of 1939, in force 1 September 1939; Part III (articles 7–8) resin and
  plant offences with medicinal and transit exceptions; articles 7–8
  substituted 1980/2021; article 8(c) cultivation minimums (six months before
  the magistrates unless for exclusive personal use; four years to life at
  trafficking tier).
