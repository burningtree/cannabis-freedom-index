# Ecuador 1970 drug law (new entry)

- New `ecuador-drug-law-1970` (other, 1970, checked): first drug law —
  Ley de Control y Fiscalización del Tráfico de Estupefacientes, 1970
  (reforms 1972/1974, DS 909). Public-health orientation: users found under
  the influence to hospital, dependency assessment, detention in medical
  facility through rehabilitation. Enforcement: growing plants for
  processing + precursor chemicals highest; pharmacies supervised; little
  attention to informal trafficking.
- Source read: Sandra G. Edwards, "A Short History of Ecuador's Drug
  Legislation and the Impact on its Prison Population" (Systems Overload,
  full PDF: evolution section + footnote law citations Titulo 11 Arts
  24-28, DS 909 Sept 1974). Contrast noted: 1987 law starts harsh
  penalties (12-16 years), 1991 Law 108 fully punitive.
