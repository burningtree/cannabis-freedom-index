## Sources read and material corrections

### Czechia

- [Addictive Substances Act 167/1998](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/1998-167), especially
  §§ 2, 5(12)–(13), 24c and 39. The permission covers adults aged 21+, 100 g in their own
  dwelling and 25 g elsewhere, and growing/harvesting/processing three plants for personal use.
  The growing location is property owned or otherwise legally used, not only a primary
  residence. The next possession/growing bands are administrative offences, with a maximum
  CZK50,000 fine. The statutory cannabis definition concerns flowering/fruiting tops and
  above-ground material containing a top; seed and young-plant wording is limited to that
  definition and does not invent a retail rule for cuttings.
- [Criminal Code 40/2009](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2009-40), §§ 283a and 285. Restored the
  omitted criminal threshold of more than 50 g outside one's own dwelling, alongside more than
  200 g at home. More than five plants is criminal. Supply, including gifts, and import remain
  offences. Basic personal-possession/growing maximum: six months; basic supply maximum: one year.
- [Smoking restrictions, Act 65/2017](https://www.zakonyprolidi.cz/cs/2017-65), § 8, read for
  examples of places where smoking is prohibited. Removed the unsupported blanket historical
  claim that use had never been an offence.
- [SÚKL medical cannabis guidance](https://sukl.gov.cz/zdravotnicti-pracovnici/leciva/konopi-pro-lecebne-pouziti-druhy-indikace-a-specializace-lekare/),
  prescriber table and prescribing/dispensing/reimbursement sections. Read via the web cache
  after direct requests timed out. Supports medical flower/extracts, electronic prescriptions,
  inclusion of general practitioners, and 90% reimbursement up to 30 g per month, with insurer
  approval needed for higher reimbursement quantities. Does not support a claim that every
  doctor can prescribe; that claim was not used.
- [Advokátní deník, 4 April 2026](https://advokatnidenik.cz/2026/04/04/drogove-trestne-ciny-ve-svetle-aktualni-novely-trestniho-zakoniku/),
  read the discussion of processing/manufacture and joint cultivation. Attributes its
  interpretation of chemical extraction and shared growing explicitly. Removed the blanket
  claim that all personal concentrates/edibles are forbidden, because the statute expressly
  permits personal processing and the commentary distinguishes manufacture from processing.
- [Czech Police, Kanabidiol](https://archiv.policie.gov.cz/clanek/kanabidiol.aspx), read the
  road-law paragraph giving 2 ng/ml delta-9-THC in blood under Regulation 41/2014.
- [Supreme Court, Tpjn 300/2020](https://sbirka.nsoud.cz/sbirka/5718/), read the headnote: 10 ng/ml
  delta-9-THC in blood serum supports criminal unfitness to drive. Replaced the old secondary
  2016 account with the Court's 2020 statement.
- [BESIP penalty table](https://besip.gov.cz/Ucastnici-silnicniho-provozu/Ridici-automobilu/Zasady-bezpecne-jizdy-v-aute/Novy-bodovy-system/Tabulka-prestupku),
  read the current columns for driving under the influence and refusal: CZK7,000–25,000,
  six points, 6–18 months; refusal CZK25,000–75,000, six points, 18–36 months.
- [National monitoring centre, summary of the 2025 illegal-drugs report](https://vlada.gov.cz/assets/ppov/zavislosti/media/Priloha-01-k-TZ-2026-01-23_Souhrn-Zpravy-o-nelegalnich-drogach_fin.pdf),
  pp. 9–10 read in extracted text. All retained arrest, conviction, seizure and potency numbers
  match these passages. The government's mirror replaces the original drogy-info URL, which
  returned 403 to the local fetch. The report describes small grow rooms as up to 50 plants;
  wording now reflects that. Online distribution is attributed to the drug market generally.

Broad claims that sub-1%-THC products can be sold freely to anyone were removed: a narcotics-law
threshold does not by itself establish all product, sales-channel or age rules. Medical and
personal-growing routes now have their own sources.

### Slovakia

- [Current Criminal Code, effective 18 August 2026](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2005/300/20260818.html),
  §§ 40a, 135b–135e, 171–173 and 289, annexes 1–2. Downloaded and read the relevant text.
  The lowest cannabis possession band carries up to one year. Section 135c first uses material
  weight (2 g); above it, active-ingredient weight can still place cannabis in the lowest band
  (200 mg THC). These are not simply interchangeable dose allowances. Two plants is the lowest
  personal-growing band; three to five plants is the next. Replaced the old usual-single-dose
  visitor advice with the current quantity system and old newspaper anecdotes with the current
  conditional waiver/treatment rule. Section 135b includes acquisition for another person's
  personal consumption under its quantity, no-distribution and no-profit conditions; the profile
  does not label every such acquisition trafficking. Section 40a(5) supplies the consequence
  after successful completion of treatment/probation.
- [Act 139/1998, text from 1 January 2026](https://www.zakonypreludi.sk/print/zz/1998-139/znenie-20260101.htm),
  § 15. Industrial growing means fibre and seed. Research requires permission, but § 15(4)
  exempts EU-common-catalogue varieties, including those listed at sowing, from the cultivation
  permit. The earlier profile omitted that exemption.
- [Road Traffic Act, effective 1 October 2026](https://static.slov-lex.sk/static/SK/ZZ/2009/8/20261001.html),
  § 4(2)(c)–(d), read the prohibition on driving while the drug can still be in the body.
  Section 289 of the Criminal Code supports up to one year for the basic unfitness/refusal
  offences; it is not limited to repeat offenders. Removed unverified present-day roadside
  testing procedure and administrative fine details.
- [CMS legal guide](https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-a-legal-roadmap-to-cannabis/slovakia),
  dated 11 July 2024, read the medical, industrial-hemp and CBD sections. Retained the account
  that medical cultivation/import/sale is prohibited. The CBD internal-use claim is explicitly
  dated to the guide, rather than treating removal from the drug list as permission for every
  CBD product. This pass did not independently re-audit every medicines or food authorisation.

### Austria

- [Current Suchtmittelgesetz](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011040),
  §§ 6a, 13(2b), 27, 35 and 38, downloaded and read the relevant passages. Possession and
  cultivation to obtain the drug remain offences. The basic exclusively personal-use form has
  a maximum six months or 360 day-fines. Conditional discontinuance is not confined to first
  offenders and is subject to the statute's requirements. Police notify the health authority
  and report to the prosecutor; removed the inaccurate statement that referral always replaces
  a prosecutor's involvement. Probation and needed health measures are explained without
  assuming everyone must undergo treatment. Section 38 supports resumption/final discontinuance.
  Section 6a supports the new production section: AGES or its at-least-75%-owned subsidiary,
  with restricted recipients of the harvest.
- [Current Suchtgiftverordnung](https://www.ris.bka.gv.at/GeltendeFassung.wxe?Abfrage=Bundesnormen&Gesetzesnummer=10011053),
  § 14 and annex I.1.a, downloaded and read. Supports the new product section, the exceptions
  for approved cannabis-extract medicines and isolated THC above 95% purity for pharmacy
  preparations, and exclusion of seeds unmixed with flowering/fruiting tops. Cultivation intent,
  rather than flowering alone, is the relevant offence test under the SMG.
- [Parliamentary report, 22 January 2019](https://www.parlament.gv.at/aktuelles/pk/jahr_2019/pk0039),
  read the account naming Sativex, Canemes, dronabinol drops/capsules and reimbursement with
  medical justification/insurer approval. Attributed those details to the report; current
  prescription restrictions are separately supported by the regulation above.
- [Government driving-penalty table](https://www.oesterreich.gv.at/.syndication?pageId=00f693f1-1729-43a3-a76f-647560445b09),
  read the drug-impaired first-offence row: €800–3,700, one-month licence withdrawal and traffic
  coaching. Replaced the old four-week wording and removed the absolute assertion that no
  imprisonment could arise. The existing EUDA driving source is retained for impairment/no
  numerical threshold; roadside-testing procedural claims were removed.
