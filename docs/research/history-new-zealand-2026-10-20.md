- The browser search tool returned substantive original statutory text for the
  1927 Act (assent, commencement, section 9 and Schedule) and the complete short
  1963 amendment. Direct opens of the legislation portal failed or returned 403;
  a PDF download was empty. The metadata links identify the official originals,
  and the assertions rely on the retrieved statutory text, not search headlines.
- The Law Commission PDF was read at chapter 3, printed pp. 84–85, and Te Ara's
  historical account was read directly. These support the context and licensed
  medical routes. No unsupported count of early users or first prosecutions is
  retained from the old description.
- The 1963 entry records assent explicitly; it does not invent a separate
  commencement provision. The 1965 entry uses the review's enactment year and
  makes no claim about an exact commencement day.
- The 1960 amendment was investigated but not added: the retrieved text did not
  expose all relevant operative provisions and commencement details reliably.
- The old `before` vector is retained without validation. Its representation of
  historical cultivation, consumption and enforcement is not established by
  these sources; the separately documented 1928 commencement also highlights
  the difference between enactment-based scoring and operational restrictions.
  No new score step is assigned to commencement or later amendments.
