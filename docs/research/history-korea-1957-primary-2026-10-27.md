## Sources read

- National Law Information Center, original Narcotics Act, Act 440: https://www.law.go.kr/LSW/lsInfoP.do?lsiSeq=7835 . Web fetch failed; the in-app browser loaded the historical version. Read articles 1–7, 60–73 and licensing definitions. Promulgated 23 April 1957, effective 23 June. Article 2(3) names `인도대마초 “칸나비스사티바ㆍ엘”`, seeds, resin and products; excludes mature stalks, their products and non-germinating seeds. Article 4 prohibits unlicensed handling with exceptions. Article 6(1) prohibits unauthorised use; (3) covers cultivation/materials with ministerial approval exceptions; (4) prohibits making and administering Indian-hemp medicines. Article 60 gives up to ten years for article 4 and article 6(2)–(5) offences; article 62 gives up to five years for article 6(1). Penalties attach to the listed category; actual application to local hemp requires historical interpretation.
- Matt VanVolkenburg, Korea Times, 20 March 2018, full article: https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/southkorea/globalcommunity/20180320/korea-encounters-how-us-style-drug-laws-helped-crush-1970s-korean-youth-culture . Local hemp reportedly ignored, base-town sales, US requests from 1966, July 1970 passage. Historical newspaper reconstruction, not a binding interpretation of article 2.
- Constitutional Court, 2002Hun-Ba24, 27 November 2003: https://law.go.kr/LSW/detcInfoP.do?detcSeq=134634 . Read legislative-history section 3(a)(1): colonial opium/Korean War background, military-government rules replaced in 1957, cannabis control under the 1970 and separate 1976 laws. The case concerns opium; used only for its explicit legislative history, not as a cannabis-definition ruling.
