## Score change (scored chain `jm`)

The 1913 entry said the Ganja Law banned "growing and importing it, and later possession and smoking", and
its after-state treated possession and use as crimes from 1913. The book shows that the 1913 ordinance
covered only cultivation and importation (fine up to £100 or twelve months). Possession, sale and smoking
became offences under the Dangerous Drugs Law of 1924.
