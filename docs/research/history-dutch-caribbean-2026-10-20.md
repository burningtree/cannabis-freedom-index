## Curaçao and Sint Maarten: 1 December 2018

Downloaded **Richtlijn voor strafvordering 2018/01, Opium delicten**. Read its
jurisdiction/commencement metadata and relevant general principles, the cannabis
table (**p. 5**), and cultivation/transitional passages (**p. 6**). Visually
inspected pp. 1 and 5, including the footnote to the table.

The document expressly names both countries and the Caribbean Netherlands. It
provides seizure and potentially conditional dismissal in the 0–25 g band,
a fine demand up to NAF 500/USD 280 in the 25–50 g band, and reference to half
the cocaine/heroin demand from 50 g. The printed table's adjoining bands share
boundary labels; no extra exact-boundary interpretation is invented here.
The footnote lists exceptions and p. 6 separately addresses prohibited cultivation.
The July 2015 predecessor is expressly displaced; its substantive rules were
not independently examined.

Added `curacao-prosecution-guideline-2018` and
`sint-maarten-prosecution-guideline-2018`, linked as the same regional measure.
One 388-word article, under the Curaçao event, explains the shared policy and
links to the Sint Maarten record. No duplicate article was created.
