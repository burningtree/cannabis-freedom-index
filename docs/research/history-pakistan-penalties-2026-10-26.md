# Pakistan: prohibition, whipping and narcotics penalty gaps

Research pass, 26 October 2026. Read alongside the [earlier Pakistan gap note](history-pakistan-gap-2026-10-23.md), [maintenance guide](../DATA-MAINTENANCE.md), [writing guide](../WRITING.md) and [score-scope note](history-scope-scores-2026-10-23.md).

## What this batch changes

Four new records cover the 1995 narcotics ordinance, a reported October 1995 hashish flogging, the April 1996 abolition of non-hadd whipping and the 2022 federal cannabis penalty tables. Three existing records are corrected: the 1979 prohibition, the 1997 Act and the 2023 federal death-penalty repeal. The 1979 and 1997 articles are rewritten against historical texts rather than later consolidated penalties; the 1996 repeal gets a new article. Pakistan moves from 14 to 18 entries and from two to three articles.

## Sources actually read

- [Government reprint of the 1979 prohibition order](https://www.kpexcise.gov.pk/app/wp-content/uploads/2020/08/THE-PROHIBITION-ENFORCEMENT-OF-HADD-ORDER-1979..pdf): commencement, definitions, articles 4–12 and 17, schedule and amendment footnotes. The schedule expressly includes cannabis leaves, stalks, tops, resin and preparations. The two-year-or-thirty-lashes possession provision is distinct from the eighty-lash hadd alcoholic-liquor offence. Article 3's five-year wording is a later 1983 substitution; it must not be presented as the original 1979 manufacturing penalty.
- [1995 government communication to the UN](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/209615/files/e-nl-1995-28-29-e.pdf): E/NL.1995/29, Ordinance XLVII, printed pages 9–16, especially commencement and sections 2, 4–9. Cannabis above one kilogram falls in the death-or-life band, with a fine of at least one million rupees. The lowest band stops at ten grams, and the middle band requires three to seven years. This is the promulgated text communicated in November 1995; section 1 requires a separate commencement notification. That notification was not retrieved. The entry uses the year, records the ordinance, and does not assign an operative score date.
- [UN torture rapporteur's Pakistan visit report](https://humanrts.umn.edu/commission/thematic53/97TORPAK.htm), E/CN.4/1997/7/Add.2, paragraphs 63–69: the flogging account is attributed through Amnesty International to Pakistani media, not presented as eyewitness verification. The abolition proposal dates to November 1995.
- [Original 1996 Gazette](https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1329726978_141.pdf), both scanned pages visually inspected: assent and immediate commencement on 3 April; publication 15 April. Section 3 abolishes non-hadd whipping under existing laws, rules and regulations and remits unexecuted sentences. Section 4 repeals the 1909 Whipping Act. It preserves hadd punishment.
- [Original 1997 Gazette](https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/Control-of-Narcotic-Substances-Act-XXV.pdf), title page visually inspected, together with the [historical text reproduced by UNODC](https://sherloc.unodc.org/cld/uploads/res/document/pak/control-of-narcotic-substances-act-xxv_html/CONTROL_OF_NARCOTIC_SUBSTANCES_ACT_pakistan.pdf), relevant sections read: assent 7 July, immediate commencement, publication 11 July. The original lowest band is 100 grams, not the modern drug-specific table. More than one kilogram permits death, life or up to fourteen years; above ten kilograms requires at least life. Sections 52–53 concern registration and treatment. Sections 73 and 78 preserve additional provincial/special restrictions and identify the repealed enactments, including the preceding 1997 ordinance. Do not describe this as a wholesale repeal of the 1979 order.
- [Ministry of Law and Justice federal consolidation updated to 4 August 2023](https://pakistancode.gov.pk/pdffiles/administrator739c7aa745c5afab5decf2e100caf1c5.pdf): sections 2, 4–9 and amendment footnotes read. Act XX of 2022 substituted section 9; cannabis rows 1, 3 and 4 already top out at life, while small quantities have prison minima. Bhang up to 999 grams carries six months to three years; charas up to 499 grams ten months to five years; hashish oil up to 499 grams fourteen months to seven years, plus fines. The original 2022 Gazette download repeatedly failed; the entry relies on the official consolidation and keeps year precision.
- [Original 2023 Gazette](https://na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/64d4d874b52c0_506.pdf), both scanned pages visually inspected: assent 2 August and immediate commencement, publication 5 August. The amendment deletes death wording from remaining federal rows. Comparing the 2022 substitution footnotes and drug-specific rows shows why this is later than cannabis's federal change.

## Rejected shortcuts and remaining work

The [1977 Prohibition Act](https://www.na.gov.pk/uploads/documents/1491535829_697.pdf), first two scanned pages inspected, defines liquor through alcohol. Its repeal by the 1979 order alone does not establish that it had already prohibited cannabis. The four 1978 provincial prohibition ordinances remain a separate research task.

The Sindh Judicial Academy consolidation mixes federal amendments with a Sindh-specific 2021 section 9A. Its cannabis tables must not be dated to 1997 or assumed to represent every jurisdiction. The clean federal Ministry consolidation resolves the federal 2022/2023 distinction.

Provincial chronology remains open. The official Sindh 2024 consolidation was downloaded and its cannabis rows read, but that does not complete a national repeal audit. Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's original 2019 law, the full 2025 replacement penalty schedule, Punjab's 2025 law and amendments, Balochistan's 2026 law and AJK's adapted legislation need to be assembled together. A partial reading of the KP 2025 Gazette and search-result leads are not marked as full verification of those laws. Secondary/case-law leads for the 1995 commencement date also require retrieval of the underlying notification or a readable primary judicial passage.

The profile and all inherited `before` vectors are preserved. The existing 1979 step assigns enforcement 1 continuously, although documented lashes and later capital exposure require a more careful reconstruction. The new legal records have explicit `unscored` reasons where commencement or the joint federal/provincial chain remains unresolved. This batch improves the documented history; it does not claim to repair that chart. A future pass must locate the 1995 operative date, establish the intervening ordinance sequence, and date the national end of cannabis capital exposure before rebasing enforcement steps coherently.
