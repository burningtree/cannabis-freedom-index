## Uganda 1902 (pop 51M): entry upgraded, article still blocked

`uganda-ban-1902` gains a second source and a context sentence, both cited
per-sentence ([@1] ban, [@2] use). Description now records what the ban
landed on: a 1902 ethnological survey describing home-grown bhang smoked in
gourd water-pipes by men and unmarried women, with married women and young
fighting men excluded.

Source actually read:

- Charles William Hobley, "Eastern Uganda, an Ethnological Survey",
  Anthropological Institute of Great Britain and Ireland, 1902, via
  Internet Archive full text (`easternugandaan00hoblgoog`): tobacco and
  bhang grown for home consumption; bhang always smoked in a hubble-bubble
  or water-pipe made from a gourd; men and unmarried women smoke, married
  women excluded; in some groups only men, with young fighting men barred.

Still missing: the ban instrument itself. The quoted definition (opium to
include bhang, ganja, churus/churns, chandoo natron) is cited on Wikipedia
to *British and Foreign State Papers*, HMSO 1905, p. 928 (vol. 95/96, not
on archive.org — only vols 94/98/100 are). Blocked routes: NZLII-style
hosts 403 (OUP article, NZLII, HathiTrust, Google Books API quota),
KenyaLaw 202 bot-wall, Nkumba thesis server timeout, LSE working-paper PDF
served corrupt (identical broken bytes twice), Sensi Seeds Tunisia page 404.
The parallel 1 April 1902 East Africa Gazette (kenyalaw) carries near-identical
wording but is the wrong protectorate, so it was not used.
