Checked Partridge's full chapter against the locally supplied Cannabis and
Culture (1975), printed pp. 147–172. Printed p. 147 dates the study's start to
July 1972; p. 160 describes small vendors' homes as caletas. Pages 162–164
cover nonkin male work groups, changing membership, workday smoking, user
beliefs, individual cigarettes and nonsmoker acceptance. Pages 167–168 discuss
mobile wage labour, learning to smoke during migration and users/nonusers'
common employment, family and community patterns.

Important qualification: p. 162 describes a shared cigarette in a work-group
scene; p. 163 says individual cigarettes are not passed around. The entry
reports the individual pattern with this exception rather than silently
removing the source's inconsistency. Claims about strength, reduced fatigue
and motivation remain attributed to participants, not measured work effects.
The ethnographic comparison is preliminary and not a statistical causal test.
