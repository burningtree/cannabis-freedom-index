Correction: us-boggs-act-1951 (entry and article) said a first possession offence carried a *mandatory*
two-year minimum and a $20,000 fine. Per pp. 210–211, the act set 2–5 years for a first offence, 5–10 for a
second and 10–20 for a third, with a fine of up to $2,000. Probation, suspension and parole were barred only
from the second offence. The $20,000 fine belongs to the 1956 act (p. 218). The score step (11 → 9) is kept:
prison remained the expected outcome. The article was rewritten from the book.
