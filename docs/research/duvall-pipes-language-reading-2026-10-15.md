## Applied findings

1. New `culture/traditions/the-calabash-water-pipe.md`: identified Angolan mutopa
   engraving; materials, portability, communal use in the Kasai, and the air-control
   opening. Avoided an assertion that every calabash pipe was for cannabis or that
   the pictured vessel documents a particular ceremony.
2. New `culture/language/cachimbo.md`: Portuguese general pipe noun; Duvall's
   proposed lower-Zambezi connection, onward borrowing, dry/water-pipe distinctions.
   Independently read Priberam, which explicitly labels the etymology doubtful:
   <https://dicionario.priberam.org/cachimbo>. Preserve the disagreement rather than
   treating Duvall's reconstruction as a settled dictionary etymology.
3. New history event/article `us-water-pipe-patent-1980`: independently checked
   United States Patent 4,216,785, issued 12 August 1980, filed 25 August 1977,
   naming William V. Erickson, Paul K. Jarvie and Fred L. Miller. Read the primary
   patent's background, abstract and technical description via
   <https://patents.google.com/patent/US4216785A/en>. Its air-admission tube is a
   specific design, not an invention of water-pipe smoking. Duvall's comparison
   does not establish copying or determine legal validity. No scores changed.
4. `culture/language/bangi.md`: immediate Hindi/Persian/Arabic intermediary remains
   unresolved; lexical replacement is distinct from plant introduction. Removed
   the unsupported Zanzibar-to-Bena-Riamba arrival claim. Distinguished porters
   carrying goods from merchants employing them. Removed a repeated hero image.
5. `culture/language/diamba.md`: variable transcription within a wider language
   geography, observation versus arrival, and small household gardens in Angola.
   Qualified Duvall's proposed marihuana etymology rather than presenting it as settled.
6. `culture/language/mbanje.md`: thirteenth-century pipe bowls from archaeological
   context do not establish their contents. Qualified the Kingdom of Zimbabwe
   reconstruction. Added soróma/suruma connections without an invented migration date.
7. `culture/language/dagga.md`: Duvall's note 93 explicitly corrects his earlier
   “Making Dagga” caption: the image showed adobe preparation, not cannabis storage.
   Do not reuse that photograph as cannabis evidence. Existing current-law sections
   and score data were not revised in this pass.

## Boundaries and remaining leads

- Duvall's language database aggregates published sources with uneven spelling and
  definitions. Similar words suggest connections; they do not prove a date or a
  particular person's journey. His collection is historical, not a current census.
- Avoid repeating his strongest universal claim that all water pipes ultimately
  derive from Africa as an independently established fact. The regional designs
  and their early descriptions are more specific evidence.
- The botanical and chemical argument about southern African seed selection for
  THCV and hunger suppression was read but not applied. Current primary evidence
  is needed before turning it into a pharmacological explanation or advice.
- Some chapter 4 notes contain source/title or classification issues; do not copy
  their metadata into new archaeology claims without checking the original.
- Next: chapter 5 for horticulture/preparation, then chapter 7 for Atlantic routes.
  Original dictionaries and pipe descriptions remain follow-up work.
