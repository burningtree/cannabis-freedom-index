## Sources and limits

- Downloaded and reread Uragoda’s 1983 paper, especially pp. 71–72; visually
  checked p. 71. His history identifies the 1867 law, cannabis terminology,
  R. F. Morgan and the committee’s stated purpose. The 13 November date is
  referral to the committee, not verified assent or commencement. Year-only
  dating remains. The full original 1867 law was not recovered.
- Uragoda’s shop counts, possession thresholds and medical discussion mainly
  concern opium. None was transferred to cannabis. The article uses a later
  notice explicitly covering both opium and bhang to explain cannabis sales.
- Reread the locally retained original Colombo notice, p. 2066, in the
  15 September 1893 Gazette, and the municipal ordinance, pp. 2807–2808, in
  the 17 November Gazette. These show actual advertised conditions and legal
  licence ceilings respectively. Neither proves how many licences were sold,
  shops opened or customers served. The prose distinguishes later conditions
  from what is known about the 1867 law.
- Visually reread p. 483 of the original 1897 Gazette for import prohibition,
  possession/sale offences, first-conviction penalties and enforcement. The
  earlier source pass verified the repeal schedule and remains documented in
  `pre-1900-ceylon-medical-and-consolidation-2026-10-16.md`. Later rules are
  linked and included as the outcome of the licensed-trade story.
- A LawNet search located a 1913 opium-partnership case quoting the 1867 law,
  but a full download failed and its cannabis relevance is indirect. It was
  not used for new legal claims. A 1908 report search lead at GIPE was access
  denied; its snippet was not used as evidence. Fresh requests for the Colombo
  PDF timed out; the actual Gazette PDF and text retained from the earlier
  primary-source pass were read locally.
- No separate nationwide possession rule, medical exception or cannabis-use
  statistics is inferred for 1867. Existing historical scores remain unchanged
  without revalidation. Public caveats and committee-date procedure were
  removed from the event description and documented here.
