# Uganda, 1902 to 1988

The dated gap is `uganda-ban-1902` to `uganda-single-convention-1988`
(15 April 1988).

## Pharmacy and Drugs Act, 1970, read as a later consolidation

ULII's text of Act 39 of 1970, Chapter 280, as at 31 December 2000
(https://media.ulii.org/media/legislation/18219/source_file/8c085527ea806337/1970-39.pdf)
says amendments have been applied to the text. A search of that PDF found no
hemp, cannabis, bhang or ganja. Another ULII copy, Chapter 309, as at the same
date, says the act commenced on 15 June 1971 and was amended by the National
Drug Policy and Authority Act on 3 December 1993. The 1993 statute, already
entered, repeals Parts III to VIII of the 1970 act except sections 47 and 48.
The original 1970 hemp provisions were not in the consolidation that was read,
and the 1970 gazette was not opened. No entry was added.
