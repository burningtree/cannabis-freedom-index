# Non-US book pass (15 October 2026)

## Rubin (ed.), *Cannabis and Culture* — Partridge, "Cannabis and Cultural Groups in a Colombian Municipio"

Read pp. 147–163.

- colombia-first-marijuana-law-1928 (context `ban`, no `before`): Partridge cites Patiño, Ardila and Torres
  for Colombian marijuana laws dating from 1928, and Costa Rican ones from 1927. The site's first scored
  Colombian ban stays at 1939 until the 1928 statute is found. The Costa Rica decree stays at 24 October
  1928, because the site has a dated decree and Partridge's 1927 is second-hand.
- colombia-santa-marta-cannabis-economy-1973, plus an article: fieldwork from July 1972 below the Sierra
  Nevada de Santa Marta. It covers growers displaced by la Violencia, the price comparison with coffee, the
  two marketing systems and export to the United States.

## Rubin & Comitas, *Ganja in Jamaica*

- jamaica-indian-labourers-arrive-1845 (pp. 16–17): first indentured labourers in 1845, about 36,000 by
  1917, and no evidence of cannabis use before 1845. The existing jamaica-ganja-arrives-1855 (imports in the
  1850s and 1860s) is consistent with it and is kept.
