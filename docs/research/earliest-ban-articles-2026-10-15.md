# Early prohibitions: article and source expansion, 15 October 2026

Follow-up to [the initial intake](earliest-cannabis-bans-2026-10-15.md).
Read the maintenance and book-research instructions. Five new articles and three
revised articles cover all eight records discussed in the research: Cafour,
Baybars, Cairo 1324, Sudun, Merina, Menou, Rio and Mauritius. Article bodies are
390–429 words. No new event IDs, country profiles or scores were introduced.

## Sources actually examined

- **Al-Maqrizi through de Sacy, 1826**, *Chrestomathie arabe*, second edition,
  tome II, pp. 138–143, especially p. 141. Read the complete relevant translated
  passage and its poems in the Internet Archive full-text transcription; title
  page identifies tome II despite the catalogue's misleading “Tome 1.3.” label.
  https://archive.org/stream/bub_gb_auocmKTblb0C/bub_gb_auocmKTblb0C_djvu.txt
  The burning precedes a poem recited in Rabi I 642 AH. This is a latest bound
  in 1244, not an exact enactment or burning date. No Arabic manuscript examined.
- **Al-Maqrizi through de Sacy, 1806**, *Chrestomathie arabe*, tome II,
  première partie de la traduction, pp. 132–134, and notes pp. 154–155.
  Read the full relevant translated account and reproduced French decree:
  https://archive.org/stream/bub_gb_OFZAWHyFQf0C/bub_gb_OFZAWHyFQf0C_djvu.txt
  The medieval account says approximately 780 AH; the geography is Cairo/Bulaq.
  The French order's introduction says 17 Vendémiaire, conflicting with Guba's
  16 Vendémiaire/8 October citation. Preserve that discrepancy explicitly.
- **Hatim Muhammad Mahamid, 2023**, *Religions* 14(11), 1384,
  “Religious Policy of the Mamluk Sultan Baybars (1260–1277 AC),” p. 5,
  section 2.3. Complete relevant page read and visually checked. The ordinary
  MDPI page was blocked, but the publisher's PDF delivery endpoint worked:
  https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/religions/religions-14-01384/article_deploy/religions-14-01384.pdf
  It puts the broad prohibition under 664 AH/1266. The quoted hanging threat
  concerns wine: it is not imported as a cannabis penalty.
- **Hakan Özkan, 2013**, *Mamlūk Studies Review* 17, pp. 213–215,
  especially p. 214 and note 15, “The Drug Zajals in Ibrāhīm al-Miʿmār’s Dīwān.”
  Relevant pages and notes read from the downloaded PDF. Read the repository's
  bibliographic record and CC BY 4.0 statement:
  https://knowledge.uchicago.edu/records/qg6h1-8m303
  Download: `/records/qg6h1-8m303/files/MSR_XVII_2013_Ozkan_pp212-248.pdf?download=1`.
  The old `/record/1219/files/` path returns 404 and the older Mamluk-domain

## Corrections made alongside the articles

All eight existing history records gained better citations or qualified descriptions.
Two medieval dates changed: Cafour **1253 → by 1244** and Baybars **1265 → 1266**.
Both IDs remain unchanged. Rio's year-only date became **1830-10-04** from Ribeiro.
Sudun's date is explicitly approximate **1378–1379**; Menou retains **1800-10-08**
with a one-day source discrepancy. Merina retains the conventional **1787** date
with a clear statement that the reviewed passages do not independently establish it.

The pre-existing Madagascar score drops from 98 straight to today's score in 1787.
That chain's arithmetic passes, but its substantive reconstruction remains weak:
fibre cultivation and later changes need separate research. This article pass does
not certify that historical score or invent a replacement centuries-long regime.
No score timeline was re-based.

## Unsuccessful leads and limits

Rosenthal's *The Herb* catalogue/preview records were found; no substantive reading
of that book is claimed. The public-domain *Relation de l'Égypte* (1810) was
downloaded and searched but was the wrong work for the relevant cannabis extracts;
it is not cited. The correct *Chrestomathie* volumes were then read as above.

The UFBA Jorge Luz thesis endpoint returned HTTP 468/502; search snippets suggest
useful archival research, but these are not read or checked citations. Mauritius's
1840 ordinance compilation was identified but could not be inspected through the
Google Books endpoint. No precise day, fine or custodial tariff was invented.

Book scans and full extractions remain under ignored `tmp/` or `books/`. No images
or complete source texts were added to public assets. Existing staged changes and
the independently modified source archive are preserved.
