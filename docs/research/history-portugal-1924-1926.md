# Portugal 1924/1926 — decree numbers recovered, texts still wanted

## What changed

`portugal-first-drug-law-1926`: dated to 1926-08-27, retitled (a 1924 law
predates it), description now names Decree-Law 12,210 and the 40-year gap;
EMCDDA profile added as a read second source.

## Sources read

- Wikipedia "Drug policy of Portugal" (fetched): first major drug laws 1924
  (Law 1687, Congress of the Republic, 9 Dec 1924) and 1926 (Decree-Law
  12,210, Government of Portugal, 27 Aug 1926), both incorporating the
  International Opium Convention. DRE reference URLs:
  https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/1687-1924-562193
  https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto/12210-1926-162653
- EMCDDA, Drug policy profiles: Portugal (2011), pp.9-10 (mirror PDF read in
  full): legislating since the 1920s adapting to the 1912 Convention (citing
  Dias 2007; IDT 2011b), nothing for almost 40 years after, until the 1963
  mental-health mention; 1970 Decree-Law 420/70 detail (use criminalised, up
  to 2 years or PTE 5,000-50,000 fine; trafficking 2-8 years).
