JPEG, 1200 × 600. Article credits its photographs to Association Chanvre Réunion. Caption identifies the local landscape and publication year, rather than applying the herbarium caption to this modern photograph. No open licence confirmed. Retained botanical record as supporting evidence; no firm 1771 collection date or claim that the specimen's label says zamal. Existing Benoist discussion remains qualified concerning the plant's introduction and the word's probable Malagasy origin.

## South-East Asian cooking

Read [Austin Bush's Gastro Obscura report](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/cannabis-marijuana-in-thai-food), 8 April 2021. Lead caption identifies minced pork, holy basil and dried cannabis leaves at Ban Lao Reung, Prachinburi; all photographs credited to Bush. Retrieved the original displayed 2880 × 1917 JPEG through browser page assets. Visually confirmed the white bowl, minced dish and fresh cannabis-leaf garnish. The source distinguishes dried leaves in the recipe from the garnish.

Added the 1908 Mae Khrua Hua Pa eel-curry reference and the modern revival context. Photograph is not described as evidence of a historical meal. Did not repeat the report's blanket safety claims concerning raw cannabis drinks. No open licence confirmed; credited Austin Bush / Gastro Obscura with original reporting link.
