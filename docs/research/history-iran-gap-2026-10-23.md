# Iran: the 1949 public-use amendment inside 1926–1959

23 October 2026. The scored gap ran from the 1926 penal law (unscored in the
chain) to the 1959 cannabis ban, the first scored step (100 to 8).

## Read

Nezamat reprint of the law amending article 275 of the General Penal Code,
approved 7 Tir 1328 (28 June 1949) by the justice commission of the National
Consultative Assembly, published 26 Mehr 1328 (18 October 1949). Paragraph 1:
public use of opium, shireh, morphine, heroin, cocaine, bang or charas, or
open use of alcohol, three to six months. Paragraph 2: opening a shireh house,
a bar, or a place for bang and charas, six months to three years. Paragraph 3:
equipment in public places seized and destroyed, and confiscated where
appropriate. Paragraph 4: staff and helpers are accomplices. Paragraph 5:
officials who do not report, or who file a false report, three months to one
year. The closing clause says the commission approval makes the law
executable, under the 20 Ordibehesht 1328 authorisation. Reza Hekmat signed as
speaker.

The page says a later single-article amendment of the Islamic Penal Code
implicitly repealed this law. That repealing text was not opened and is not
an entry.

Ghiabi and others, already the source of the 1926 and 1959 entries, were
re-read only for the surrounding dates. The 1949 penalties come from the
statute reprint, not from that article.
