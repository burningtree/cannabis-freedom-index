# Croatia misdemeanour custody and West Bank personal use, 23 October 2026

Issues #25 and #26. Scores were left unchanged. No history events were added.

## Croatia

Narodne novine 39/2019, published 17 April 2019, article 27 replaces article 54 of the drug-abuse act. Zakon.hr's consolidation, marked NN 80/13 and 39/19, was read. Article 54(1)(1) is possessing a drug contrary to article 3(1). Article 54(3) punishes a natural person for that offence, among others, with a fine of 5,000 to 20,000 kuna or detention of up to 90 days. The consolidated page still prints kuna. The rough euro range already on the profile was left as a conversion of those figures. How often courts choose detention was not read, so enforcement stays at 3 and possession stays at 3.

## Palestine

The Official Gazette bureau's consolidated Decree-Law 18 of 2015 was read. Article 16 punishes possession, purchase, transfer, manufacture or cultivation that is neither for trafficking nor for personal use: four months to one year, or 1,000 to 3,000 Jordanian dinars, or both. Article 17(1), noted on the page as amended by Decree-Law 26 of 2018, article 5, punishes use and possession, import, production, manufacture, cultivation or purchase for personal use: six months to two years, or 1,000 to 5,000 dinars, or both. Article 17(2) withholds prosecution when the person seeks treatment, and when the prosecutor finds a first offence and sends the person to treatment. Article 17(4) raises the minimum to two years, or a fine of 2,000 to 5,000 dinars, for a repeat offence or for driving under the influence. Article 18 lets the court suspend an article 17 sentence for treatment. Article 21 punishes the same acts done in order to traffic, including cultivation, with life imprisonment of at least fifteen years and a fine of at least 15,000 dinars. Article 23 punishes supplying a drug for payment with at least ten years, and without payment with at least three years.

Those articles are stated for the West Bank. Gaza's penalty range was not read. Scores stay at possession 1 and cultivation 0.
