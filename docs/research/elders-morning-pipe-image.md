## History verification

- [Waetjen’s own 2018 historical essay](https://www.pointshistory.org/post/how-cannabis-became-a-drug-in-south-africa)
  documents objections to criminalising older men and fears about enforceability,
  as well as the Health Department’s assurances about restrained rural enforcement.
- [Seganoe and Waetjen’s 2023 journal article](https://scielo.org.za/scielo.php?pid=S0018-229X2023000100006&script=sci_arttext)
  explicitly dates the tacit Native Affairs Department/South African Police agreement
  to as early as 1923 and describes the subsequent urban focus of enforcement.
- The existing December 1922 history entry and the chapter already cited by this
  article record the initial assurance. The 1923 account supplements that chronology;
  it does not shift the ban date or turn informal practice into a statutory exemption.
