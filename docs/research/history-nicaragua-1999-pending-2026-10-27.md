# Nicaragua 1999: penalty comparison still blocked on two texts

Research checked on 27 October 2026. Read `DATA-MAINTENANCE.md`, the
Nicaragua timeline (1964, 1999, 2010) and the ni profile scores, the Leybook
page for Law 1034 (full text read), and the UN-filed English translation
E/NL.1973/55 (read). No entries, scores or profiles changed: scoring 1999
without the two missing pieces would be fabrication.

## What was established

- Law 1034 (18 Nov 1964, La Gaceta No. 286): Art. 1 punishes
  introducing/transporting/growing/keeping/distributing marihuana or any
  Cannabis species without authorisation as a crime against public security
  with "prisión en quinto grado"; Art. 4 gives "prisión en tercer grado"
  for being found with the drugs in cigarettes or any form; Art. 5 gives
  60-180 days non-commutable arrest for being under the effects or proven
  addiction (served apart, with medical treatment). Full text confirmed on
  Leybook and in the UN translation.
- E/NL.1973/55 (Ministry of Public Health communication, filed 1973,
  published May 1974) is the same 1964 law, still in force in 1973. No
  separate 1970s cannabis statute surfaced.

## What is still missing for the 1964/1999/2010 comparison

1. The grado-to-years scale of the era's penal code (what quinto/tercer
   grado meant in years). Without it, 1964 severity cannot be compared to
   1999's stated 5-25 years.
2. The text of Law 285 (1999, reforming Law 177): the OAS MLA PDF
   (Nica_ajm_leg_esp_1.pdf) is dead (redirect loop), WHO MiNDbank item 2852
   serves no file to unauthenticated fetch, and Leybook search does not
   surface it. The drugs-law blog already cited is criminal theory, not a
   penalty history.
