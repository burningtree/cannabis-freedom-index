# Bug reports #160–179 — 26 October 2026

Reviewed README, DATA-MAINTENANCE, WRITING and BOOKS guidance. This pass addresses 19 substantiated reports and documents the contrary primary evidence for #166. It changes legal descriptions, source locators and quick answers without changing current scores or existing historical before-vectors.

## Findings and evidence

- **#160, Paraguay:** Resolution 488/2025, signed 19 December 2025, establishes the two-year THC/CBD pilot. Specialist/patient registration, prescription and dispensing conditions are explicit; current stock is not established. Added an unscored medical history entry. [source](https://dinavisa.gov.py/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/RESOLUCION-DINAVISA-N%C2%B0488.pdf)

- **#161, Hawaii:** HRS 712-1240, 1244–1246.5 distinguish marijuana from harmful-drug concentrates. The 3 g violation does not cover concentrates; felony preparation thresholds are ⅛ oz and 1 oz, with separate dosage-unit rules. [statute](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0712/HRS_0712-1240.htm), [statute](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0712/HRS_0712-1245.htm), [statute](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0712/HRS_0712-1244.htm), [statute](https://data.capitol.hawaii.gov/hrscurrent/Vol14_Ch0701-0853/HRS0712/HRS_0712-1246_0005.htm)

- **#162, Wyoming:** Section 35-7-1031(c): the first-offence ceilings depend on form: 3 oz plant, 0.3 g liquid, 3 g powder/crystalline/pill/capsule. Weights include carriers but exclude packaging. [statute](https://www.wyoleg.gov/statutes/compress/title35.pdf)

- **#163, North Carolina:** G.S. 90-95(d)(4) and 90-94: hashish has separate 1/20 and 3/20 oz boundaries; prohibited isolated or synthetic THC can be a felony at any weight. Exempt hemp/medicines remain separate. [statute](https://www.ncleg.gov/EnactedLegislation/Statutes/HTML/BySection/Chapter_90/GS_90-95.html), [statute](https://www.ncleg.gov/enactedlegislation/statutes/html/bysection/chapter_90/gs_90-94.html)

- **#164, Philippines:** RA 9165 section 13 requires maximum section 11 penalties at a party/gathering or near at least two other people, irrespective of weight. The implementing rules say the others need not possess drugs. [source](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/1435)

- **#165, Philippines:** Section 4 importation is a distinct offence irrespective of weight; RA 9346 substitutes life imprisonment for the abolished death penalty here. Physician/institution compassionate imports require the applicable permit. [source](https://elibrary.judiciary.gov.ph/thebookshelf/showdocs/2/1435)

- **#166, Samoa:** Report not substantiated. In the Attorney General consolidation the Second Schedule starts on printed p.24; cannabis plant material is item 40 on p.25, processed preparations item 1, resin item 41. The Third Schedule starts p.30; its item 40 is lorazepam. Section 18 therefore retains the 14-year Class B maximum, subject to its proviso. Added direct citations to profile/article; no false seven-year correction. [source](https://www.ag.gov.ws/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Narcotics-Act-1967.pdf)

- **#167, Russia:** Decree 1002 lists plant 6/100 g, hashish 2/25 g, oil 0.4/5 g for significant/large grading. The table heading says grams above, so the text preserves strict boundaries. Article 228 and administrative article 6.8 provide the different sanctions. Classification of a preparation must first be established. [source](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_136206/6598ecb6e30274e2fbab0c605a6ebf21250a8eb6/)

- **#168, Vietnam:** Read the State Audit Office explanation quoting Article 256a. Ordinary one-year monitoring alone does not establish the use crime. The post-management category requires both a two-year window and current illicit-user management. Corrected profile, history and article. [source](https://sav.gov.vn/vi/bai-viet/tin-db8b5ce4-c580-4d97-59b3-08de30a94834)

- **#169, France:** Service-Public confirms the separate five-year/€75,000 work-duty aggravation. It already appears in L3421-1 in the official code reproduced in a November 2015 PDF, p.597 (PDF index 596); it was not newly created by the 2026 fine change. Corrected profile and history scope. [source](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F33341)

- **#170, Dominican Republic:** Read the OAS-hosted Law 17-95 amendment, adding Article 6(c): distribution/sale intent overrides the personal-possession classification at any quantity. Existing penalty reference supplies the distributor/seller term. [source](https://www.oas.org/ext/Portals/33/Files/MLA/Dom_multla_leg_eng_2.pdf)

- **#171, Mexico:** Read the Interior Ministry federal code reproduction, Articles 193–194: unauthorised import/export, including temporary entry/transit, has a distinct 10–25-year range. Domestic personal-use authorisations do not authorise importation. [source](https://www.ordenjuridico.gob.mx/Documentos/Federal/html/wo83048.html)

- **#172, New Zealand:** Schedule 5 differentiates 28 g Class C plant, 5 g Class B preparations and 250 mg separately scheduled THC; the presumption is rebuttable. Police corroborates 14/8-year Class B/C supply maxima and the lower personal-possession penalty. Full statute retrieval failed; see limitation below. [source](https://www.legislation.govt.nz/act/public/1975/116/en/latest/)

- **#173, Croatia:** Read official NN 107/2015, Article 7/new 30.b(3): 7.5 g THC per 30 days, not 0.75 g or a flower-weight limit. Corrected profile and 2015 history entry. [source](https://narodne-novine.nn.hr/clanci/sluzbeni/full/2015_10_107_2092.html)

- **#174, Switzerland:** Read VRV Article 2: prescribed use proved by the driver removes the automatic detection-based unfitness finding. Actual unfitness still prohibits driving. [source](https://justement.ch/de/doc/act/ch/741_11/part_1/chap_1/art_2)

- **#175, Peru:** Read Decree 1592, amended Penal Code Article 299: registered patient/qualifying care or guardianship possession is measured against treatment needs separately from ordinary 8 g plant/2 g derivative limits. [source](https://busquedas.elperuano.pe/dispositivo/NL/2244234-2)

- **#176, Norway:** Read the ministerial prescription exception and the driving-licence guide. Numeric-limit exceptions do not grant general licence fitness: Sativex/Epidyolex group 1 treatment has conditions; other cannabinoid products need a formal exemption, and groups 2/3 do not qualify. [source](https://www.stortinget.no/no/Saker-og-publikasjoner/Sporsmal/Skriftlige-sporsmal-og-svar/Skriftlig-sporsmal/?qnid=95447)

- **#177, Morocco:** Read the UN republication of the 1974 law: Article 8 use has 500–5,000 dirhams and two months–one year, or either penalty. Article 2 holding has five–ten years and 5,000–50,000. Article 3 facilitation has a separate range. Avoid presenting the use maximum as a possession ceiling. [source](https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/458193/files/S_2001_1288-FR.pdf)

- **#178, Malta:** Read ARUC’s May 2025 permission and January 2026 Directive 1 v4, Standard IX. Specifically authorised solid solventless resin is possible; oils/tinctures/distillates are excluded. THC bands weight resin against daily/monthly flower limits. Added an unscored easing history entry. [source](https://aruc.mt/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Directive-1-v4.0-16.01.2026.pdf)

- **#179, Latvia:** The official indexed Annex 2 table distinguishes dried 1 g, fresh 5 g, hashish 0.1 g, other resin/oil/extract/tincture 0.05 g, and dried plant mixtures 0.5 g. Corrected ordinary and visitor/quick-answer scope; full retrieval failed, see below. [source](https://likumi.lv/ta/en/en/id/50539-on-the-procedures-for-the-coming-into-force-and-application-of-the-criminal-law)
