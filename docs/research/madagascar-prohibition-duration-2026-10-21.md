# Madagascar: what the chart's 167 means

Research and correction: 21 October 2026, following the user's screenshot.
Re-read docs/WRITING.md, maintenance rules and category/tag definitions.

## Finding

The chart used `S0 = 1860` and counted every year-end score below 15 through
`DATA_SNAPSHOT = 2026`, inclusive. That gives 167 annual observations, not
167 elapsed years and not a verified prohibition beginning in 1860. Its
`longest` variable selected a country with the maximum count; the code did
not establish a historically longest national prohibition.

Madagascar's inherited single scored royal event drops all six components
from 10 to the present vector in 1787. Subsequent 1915, 1958, 1960 and 1997
entries carry no score step. This projects a regional restriction on smoking
into a continuous modern national baseline, including possession, growing,
sharing, access and enforcement. Arithmetic consistency does not validate
those six historical component values. Neither the chart nor the sources
establish 167 uninterrupted years of uniform national prohibition.

No replacement historical vector was invented. The modern profile and the
inherited numerical series remain unchanged pending reconstruction. The
chart now explicitly identifies this Madagascar limitation, removes the
longest-duration comparison, calls the numbers annual snapshots and gives
the endpoints and below-15 threshold. Its hover label uses the same definition.

## Sources examined

Re-read Molet's complete relevant passage on printed p. 214 in retained
`tmp/early-bans-merina.txt` (the previously visually checked combined PDF
p. 655). It quotes the Tantara, p. 674: cultivation for cloth and rope
allowed, smoking forbidden in the heart of Imerina. The passage does not
supply an enactment year. Molet's later reports of widespread cultivation
and consumption are not proof of legal repeal.

Molet: https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/divers14-04/09821.pdf
Condominas, p. 51, previously read: https://horizon.documentation.ird.fr/exl-doc/pleins_textes/divers11-03/22732.pdf

New evidence: read the complete four-entry rongony proverb page and followed
its 2.415 bibliography link. Proverb 5371 attributes a death-penalty rule to
Andrianampoinimerina and says it was applied only briefly. The bibliography
identifies *Le Livre de la Sagesse Malgache*, Paul de Veyrières and Guy de
Méritens, Éditions Maritimes et d'Outre-Mer, 1967, 664 pages. The site is a
transcription of a historical proverb collection, not a contemporary decree
or a newly read original book scan. It corroborates a tradition of a severe
royal smoking restriction and introduces an enforcement-duration caveat.
It cannot date a legal repeal or establish a later lawful period. The article
attributes the recollection to the collection.

https://malagasyworld.org/bins/ohabolana/rongony
https://malagasyworld.org/bins/teny2/lelivredelasagessemalgache

Re-read the complete three-article 9 October 1915 decree in the retained
contemporary legal collection, printed p. 13, PDF p. 8 (already visually
checked in the previous Madagascar batch). It covers preparation, peddling,
sale and consumption of smoking cannabis throughout the colony and
its dependencies, with six days to twelve months, a fine, or both. The
1960 official explanatory memorandum separately supplies local promulgation
on 3 December and colonial publication on 11 December 1915. See the earlier
Madagascar research note for the UN document access limitations and the
subsequent 1958/1960/1997 restrictions.
