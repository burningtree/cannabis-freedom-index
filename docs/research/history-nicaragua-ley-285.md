# Nicaragua — Ley 285 text found via OAS MLA

The OAS MLA PDF previously treated as a dead link now downloads:
https://www.oas.org/ext/Portals/33/Files/MLA/Nica_ajm_leg_esp_1.pdf
(28 pp, Ley No. 285, approved 6 April 1999, La Gaceta Nos. 69-70 of 15-16
April 1999). Key penalty articles read in full (local copy /tmp/nica285.txt):

- Art. 50 financing: presidio 10-25 years, fine 5-10 million cordobas.
- Art. 51 internal trafficking (acquiring for distribution/sale): 5-20 years,
  fine 1-5 million.
- Art. 52 international trafficking: 20-30 years.
- Art. 53 industrialisation: 20-25 years.
- Art. 54 illegal transport: 10-15 years.
- Art. 55 production/cultivation (sowing, growing, harvesting): 5-15 years,
  fine 0.5-2 million.
- Art. 56 storage: 6-12 years. Art. 58 inducing consumption: 5-10 years.
- Arts. 71-73: aggravating (up to double), mitigating (half minimum for
  cooperation), exempting circumstances.
- Prevention/treatment/rehabilitation chapters (arts. 14-22 etc.); Ministry
  of Health treatment centres for consumers.

No personal-use quantity threshold found in the text (searched dosis,
consumo personal, tenencia para, eximentes). Entry triaged unscored: a
rewrite of the prohibition with multi-year presidio throughout.

Also added as second source to `nicaragua-marihuana-law-1964`: UN
E/NL.1973/55 English translation of the 1964 provisions (fifth-degree
presidio for cannabis traffic, third degree for possession in cigarettes,
60-180 days detention for influence/addiction).
