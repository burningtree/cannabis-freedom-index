# Malta history pass (15 October 2026)

Prompted by a Grokipedia page the user pasted. It was used only as a list of leads and is not cited:
it is AI-written and several of its claims did not match the sources. For example, it describes the 2018
production act as amending the Drug Dependence Act, and its account of the 2015 thresholds differs.

## Added (country mt, all context, no `before`)

- malta-feuz-sentence-1997: six months for a Swiss 16-year-old. The amount is disputed (under 8 g or
  under 1 g).
- malta-holmes-sentence-2011 and malta-holmes-appeal-2013, with an article. Accounts of the plants differ
  (5; 5 mature and 28 seedlings; 32); the article gives all three. Reports conflict about the 2014
  Constitutional Court ruling, so it is left out. The outcome of the European Court of Human Rights
  application is not known.
- malta-releaf-manifesto-2017, malta-medical-patients-2019, malta-first-licences-2023,
  malta-eurobarometer-2024, malta-odour-enforcement-2025.

## Second pass: older history

- malta-miege-hemp-rope-1841, with a culture entry, language/qanneb. Source: Martin Morana (Kliem u
  Storja), citing Miège (1841), Muscat (2000), Wettinger (2002), Fenech (1993) and Hennen (1830). The
  secondary citations have not been checked against the originals.
- malta-hashish-to-tunisia-1895: Duvall ch. 9 n. 95, which says Tunisia's shira came from Malta.
- egypt-tanti-hashish-cafe-1914 (country eg): Mills p. 193. A British subject running a Cairo hashish café
  served his sentence in Malta's prison. Mills does not say he was Maltese.

  (MaltaToday). The consolidated Cap. 101 lists Act IV of 2020 among its amendments, but the link between
  that Act and these amendments rests only on a search summary and was not stated in the entry.
