## Evidence limits and further leads

The 1970 seizure total is not an East German consumption or prevalence figure.
The 1971/1972 transit agreements followed the initial increase and cannot be
its sole chronological explanation. Police case descriptions do not establish
the motives of every traveller, passenger or foreign worker.

The chapter's broad statement that contract workers came exclusively from the
Eastern Bloc is in tension with its later Angolan-worker example. Do not import
the blanket claim. Likewise, limited cannabis availability does not mean the
GDR lacked intoxicants: the chapter expressly discusses alcohol and medicines.

Gateway-to-heroin narratives, claims about sexual behaviour, conspiracy claims
and the police interview's historical clinical warnings are not imported as
current findings. No present medical recommendation or prevalence estimate.
The chapter's interpretation of propaganda is distinguished from the newspaper
and officials' own claims. Full ND article, konkret source and border archives
remain separate primary-source research tasks.

No new image: no authenticated public image of the actual headline retrieved,
and no generic Berlin Wall or cannabis-leaf substitute used. No local chapter
text or book scan copied into public assets. No country scores changed.
