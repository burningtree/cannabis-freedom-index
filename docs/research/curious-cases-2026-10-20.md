# Curious cases — 20 October 2026

Added five history entries chosen as curious or odd cases not yet in the history, three with
articles, and four new curious-case cards in `src/lib/facts.js`. No scores changed. The two
directional entries (`canada-mine-cannabis-sales-2003`, `us-arizona-proposition-200-1996`) and
`us-dc-initiative-71-2015` carry `unscored` reasons.

## Source handling

- **R. v. J.P. (2003):** read in full from the judgment PDF (text extracted locally). Dates of
  the two lower rulings come from its heading; the article's claims follow paragraphs 1–11 and
  31–34. Nothing is said about how police or other provinces acted in the gap, since the
  judgment does not say.
- **Flin Flon mine:** CMAJ (2001) through PubMed Central for the contract; NORML (August 2003)
  for the start of sales, price and patient numbers; AP (November 2003) for the minister's
  "interim measure" and THC; Red Deer Advocate (2009) for the departure. A widely repeated
  account of patients returning bags as "unsuitable for human consumption" was found only in a
  forum reprint of a wire story and was left out. CMAJ's own pages returned 403.
- **DC Initiative 71:** Wikipedia for dates and the Harris rider; Harris Sliwoski (2022) for the
  "transfer without payment (but not sell)" wording and the failed emergency bill. Plant and
  ounce limits were not given in the sources read and are not stated.
- **Arizona Proposition 200:** NORML (April 1997) for the legislature's FDA-approval condition;
  Wikipedia for the 65% vote, the "prescribe" wording and the 1998 referendum.
- **Robert Mitchum:** HISTORY's This Day in History. The exact arrest date (31 August or
  1 September 1948) and the 1951 set-aside reported elsewhere were not confirmed, so the entry
  uses the year and leaves the set-aside out.
