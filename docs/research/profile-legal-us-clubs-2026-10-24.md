# United States clubs, 24 October 2026

Issues #74 and #75. Texts opened before the national clubs paragraph was rewritten. The six national scores are unchanged. Colorado and Nevada state files do not repeat the two errors, so they were left as they were. No earlier history entry said Nevada licensed lounges in 2017.

## Read

- Colorado Revised Statutes § 44-10-609: a marijuana hospitality business may allow consumption under a local ordinance, and may not sell or exchange marijuana for payment on the premises. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_44-10-609
- Colorado Revised Statutes § 44-10-610: a retail marijuana hospitality and sales licence authorises a premises where marijuana may be sold and consumed, subject to a local ordinance. https://colorado.public.law/statutes/crs_44-10-610
- Colorado Department of Revenue, marijuana sales tax page: hospitality and sales establishments sell retail marijuana to consumers and allow consumption on the premises. https://tax.colorado.gov/marijuana-sales-tax
- Colorado Marijuana Enforcement Division, licence list: the hospitality licence is consumption under § 44-10-609, and the hospitality and sales licence is sale and consumption under § 44-10-610. https://med.colorado.gov/licensee-information-and-lookup-tool/licensed-facilities
- Nevada Cannabis Compliance Board FAQ: Assembly Bill 341 of 2021 authorises the board to license consumption lounges. A retail lounge is attached or immediately adjacent to an adult-use shop. An independent lounge is not. A lounge sells single-use or ready-to-consume cannabis for consumption there. A local government may limit or bar them. https://ccb.nv.gov/faq/
- Board press release of 28 June 2022: the board approved the lounge regulations that day and expected the first application round in the autumn. https://ccb.nv.gov/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/CCB-Approves-Cannabis-Consumption-Lounge-Regulations-6-28-22for-release.pdf
- Las Vegas Sun, 11 September 2017: Senator Segerblom's bill to license lounges missed a legislative deadline. The Legislative Counsel's opinion said a business could let patrons use marijuana if it was not sold. https://lasvegassun.com/news/2017/sep/11/marijuana-lounges-weed-consumption-at-special-even/
