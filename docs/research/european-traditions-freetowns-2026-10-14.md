Added five Traditions entries: Ruigoord, Metelkova, Italian hemp rope-makers, Latvian hemp-seed butter and Scottish Halloween hemp-seed divination. ## Evidence and limits

- **Ruigoord:** Oneindig Noord-Holland documents the village's preservation in 1973. The community's own programme documents recurring gatherings. Soft Secrets' contemporary report establishes the Highlife Cup on 24 June 2011, the third time hosted there. Christiania's cultural association documents sister-community visits and the Free Cultural Spaces network. Its account contains inaccurate summaries of Dutch cannabis law; those were not carried into the atlas. Landjuweel's 2026 five-gram personal-use rule is an organiser's festival policy, not a statutory exemption or permanent village-wide rule.
- **Metelkova:** Culture of Slovenia documents occupation on the night of 10–11 September 1993. Harmony's firsthand travel account appeared in its September 2017 issue and describes cannabis/hashish sales. This is explicitly dated evidence, not confirmation of today's market or a special legal status. The shorter entry reflects limited reliable evidence about continuity of cannabis practices; it avoids padding that gap with assumptions about autonomous sites.
- **Italian cordai:** municipal Carmagnola and Castelponzone museum sources document the tools and local vocabulary. Carmagnola's covered sentè dates from 1905. Castelponzone's sentèer, masoòl and fibre supply history remain attributed to that community, rather than being transferred to Piedmont. The 2016 law is cited as a historical framework, not a comprehensive statement of today's flower rules.
- **Latvian food:** Visit Valmiera and Lauku Ceļotājs document Adzelvieši's seed processing, demonstrations and tasting. The latter's specialisation page independently confirms that its preparation adds no butter, oil or salt. The 2019 culinary-route manual was searchable but too large for the web reader; the equivalent HTML page was read directly. Iecavnieks is used for its own ingredients only; unsupported medical claims and an eighth-century origin story were excluded. Existing LV portāls/LSM history supplies the agricultural revival and mistaken police seizure context.
- **Halloween:** Burns's poem and authorial notes are literary evidence for a courtship charm, not psychoactive cannabis use. Glasgow's Kilmarnock digitisation establishes publication in July 1786, not the custom's origin. The short entry reflects the narrow evidence and makes no claim of widespread modern survival. UK section 37 was read through the official XML endpoint (`/section/37/data.xml`) because the web reader could not parse the normal page; separated seeds are excluded from the definition of cannabis. No general cultivation permission is implied.

Four context events were added to changes.yaml, with no `before` fields and no changes to scores: Ruigoord's preservation, its 2011 Highlife Cup, Metelkova's occupation and Burns's 1786 publication. The other historical dates are supporting context, not separate developments in cannabis policy.
