# Guatemala 1932: Wiki-only decree upgraded with the 1935 police memoir

Research checked on 27 October 2026. Read `DATA-MAINTENANCE.md`, the entry,
the Wikipedia wikitext (used only as a lead to its cited source), and the
cited primary itself through the Google Books volume text search. No scores,
profiles or other timelines changed.

## What changed

- `guatemala-decree-1331-1932`: date refined 1932 to 1932-02; title and
  description rewritten. The decree set penalties (not just classification)
  for cultivating lethal plants, preparing soporific principles, and
  introducing/transporting/trafficking them, naming Indian hemp and marihuana
  with poppy and opium. Added a documented enforcement case from the same
  memoir (Muñoz Salazar). Wikipedia citation dropped; the official memoir is
  now the sole source, with per-sentence citations.

## Source actually read

- Guatemala, Dirección General de la Policía Nacional, *Memoria de los
  trabajos realizados* (Tipografía Nacional, 1935; Google Books
  id J_hWAAAAYAAJ), via the volume's text search, which returns the primary's
  own words: p. 123 decree list ("1331. - Penas por el cultivo de plantas
  letales y preparación de sus principios soporíferos, su introducción,
  transporte o tráfico (cáñamo indiano, marihuana, adormidera, opio,
  etcétera)", February 1932); p. 21 (David Muñoz Salazar grew marihuana and
  prepared its derivatives with two accomplices; Enrique Gándara detained in
  Parque Central).

## Honest limitations
