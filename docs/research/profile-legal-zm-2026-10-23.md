# Zambia profile, 23 October 2026

Issue #65. Texts opened before the profile was rewritten.

## Read

- ZambiaLII, Cannabis Act 2021 (Act 33): banner “This Act has not yet come into force”; section 1 comes into operation on a date the Minister appoints by statutory instrument. Published 20 May 2021, assented 19 May 2021. https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2021/33/eng@2021-05-20
- ZambiaLII, Industrial Hemp Act 2021 (Act 34): commenced 3 January 2025 by the Industrial Hemp Act (Commencement) Order, 2024. Section 1 is the appointment clause. Section 2 defines industrial hemp, including viable seed, at not more than 0.3% delta-9-THC. Section 4 prohibits growing, processing, distributing, buying, exporting or research without a licence. https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2021/34/eng@2021-05-20
- Government Gazette No. 7697, Friday 3 January 2025, supplement: the Industrial Hemp Act (Commencement) Order, 2024, says the Act comes into operation on the date of publication of the Order. The instrument number was not readable in the text extract (the digit printed as a bar). https://archive.gazettes.africa/archive/zm/2025/zm-government-gazette-dated-2025-01-03-no-7697.pdf
- ZambiaLII, Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances Act 2021 (Act 35): commenced 20 May 2021. Section 17 trade, up to twenty-five years. Section 18 possession, up to fifteen years, with an admission-of-guilt fine of up to 1,200 penalty units where the drug is cannabis. Section 19 cultivation, up to one million penalty units or ten years or both. Section 21 supply for cultivation or manufacture, one to fifteen years. Section 22 consumption, up to 200,000 penalty units or two years. https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2021/35/eng@2021-05-20
- ZambiaLII, Controlled Substances Act 2023 (Act 2): commenced 16 January 2026 by the Controlled Substances Act (Commencement) Order, 2026. Section 6 licences manufacture, import, export and dealing. Section 40 possession without authorisation, up to fifteen years. Section 52 repeals the Dangerous Drugs Act 1967 and keeps existing licences. Cannabis and cannabis resin appear in the narcotic-drug lists. The schedule headings themselves were not in the extract, so the profile does not name a schedule number. https://zambialii.org/akn/zm/act/2023/2/eng@2023-04-18
