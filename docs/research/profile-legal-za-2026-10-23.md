# South Africa profile, 23 October 2026

Issues #70 and #71. Texts opened before the profile was rewritten.

## Read

- Constitutional Court, Centre for Child Law v Director of Public Prosecutions, Johannesburg [2022] ZACC 35, 29 September 2022. The order confirms that section 4(b) of the Drugs and Drug Trafficking Act is invalid insofar as it criminalises a child's use or possession of cannabis, suspends that invalidity for 24 months, and during the suspension forbids arrest, prosecution and diversion. A child who is apprehended may be referred to civil processes. Paragraph 98 says use or possession by a child is still not permitted, and that dealing and an adult who uses a child to possess or use cannabis are outside the order. Paragraph 29 records that an adult who uses or possesses cannabis in public can still face a criminal sanction. https://www3.saflii.org/za/cases/ZACC/2022/35.html
- LawLibrary, Cannabis for Private Purposes Act 7 of 2024: banner “This Act has not yet come into force.” Section 2(1)(b) would allow an unpaid gift between adults in private. Section 1 leaves seed and seedling out of the definition of cannabis. Section 8 comes into operation on a date the President proclaims. https://lawlibrary.org.za/akn/za/act/2024/7/eng@2024-06-03
