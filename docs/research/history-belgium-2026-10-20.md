# Belgium's narcotics framework — 20 October 2026

Added an article on the 1930 order as reproduced in the government communication
to the UN in 1952. Clarified both the 1921 and 1930 event descriptions to avoid
backdating the consolidated text's cannabis controls or treating the law's
date alone as proof of its original cannabis coverage.

## Evidence and limits

- Read the complete 1930 order, Articles 1–30, in E/NL.1952/68, including the
  cover, edition heading, amendment footnotes and the following 1946 order and
  repeal list. Visually checked the cover, printed p. 2's cannabis entry and
  external-use exception, and p. 5's Article 11 authorisation provisions. Other
  provisions were read in extracted text. Printed and PDF pages coincide here.
- The document is dated 4 August 1952 and expressly incorporates the Royal
  Order of 20 September 1951. Amendment indications and exemptions are real
  parts of the source, not a basis for presenting every provision as newly
  enacted on 31 December 1930. The event retains the instrument date without
  inventing a commencement date.
- The original 1921 law and original unamended 1930 gazette were not recovered.
  Replaced the existing unsupported categorical assertion that cannabis was
  controlled by the 1921 enactment itself with the verified framework/order
  relationship at Articles 2 and 28 and an explicit uncertainty statement.
- Consulted the Library of Congress 1974 report's Belgium page and its
  bibliography, and the title and beginning of Belgian bill 1186/1 (1997).
  These were leads, not substitutes for the original 1921 statute, and no
  retrospective penalty amount or original cannabis commencement claim was
  taken from them. Secondary search summaries likewise were not treated as
  verified original legislation.
- No new event, enforcement estimate or medical-availability claim was added.
  Country profiles, current scores and score baselines are unchanged. Preserved
  the existing 1921 vector without revalidation; its first-ban interpretation
  still needs original-text research before any score timeline is reconstructed.
