## Edition and actual coverage

Inspected the embedded cover, internal title, rights statement, prologue and NCX/spine. The
cover names Bernardo Parrella, Stampa Alternativa and the subtitle about Italy. The prologue is
signed February 2014 and explains that the earlier book has been revised. The OPF date (1999)
and ISBN 9788872264638 refer to the older work and cannot identify this updated text.
The [2014 print catalogue record](https://www.libreriauniversitaria.it/cannabis-non-solo-fumo-storia/libro/9788862223782)
corroborates the subtitle and publisher, with print ISBN 9788862223782. No EPUB-specific ISBN is
verified. The internal rights statement gives CC BY-NC-ND 2.5; no book illustrations were reused.

Read the complete prologue and these three complete sections of **Il caso italiano**:

- Cannabis Social Club e terapie collaborative (the extracted heading has “GOLLABORATIVE”).
- L’inevitabile disastro della legge Fini-Giovanardi.
- Erba medica e normative locali.

The rest of the Italian chapter and other chapters remain unread. Navigation indexing does not
count as reading. No printed page mapping exists, so public citations use chapter and section.

## Withheld claims and next reading

The book is an advocacy account. Do not import its treatment claims, Amnesia adulteration story,
prison statistics or half-gram trafficking threshold as established facts. Do not count membership
requests as members, or a forecast autumn harvest as proof of a completed or authorised crop.
The 2007 decree date needs checking against the original. The 2013 constitutional referrals and
Montecitorio demonstrations are leads for a later pass. Remaining Italian sections on the wider
movement, industry, earlier history and future proposals have not been credited as read.
