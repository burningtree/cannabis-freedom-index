## Content verification

NLJ History Notes supports Kongo roots, communal rites, drumming, dancing and ancestral
possession, and explicitly acknowledges competing origin histories. The existing
Rastafari source describes Kumina’s ceremonial cannabis use as one possible route
into Rastafari, alongside Hindu practice and wider Afro-Jamaican use. The entry now
keeps the two traditions distinct and treats that influence as a hypothesis.
