# Hasch in the GDR: original 1973 law

Primary illustration is an actual rendered crop of Gesetzblatt der Deutschen
Demokratischen Republik, Teil I, Nr. 58, p. 572. The law is dated 19 December
1973; the issue date in the page header is 21 December 1973. Both are retained.

§ 1(3) prohibits circulation of specified substances; § 3(1) permits ministerial
exceptions in individual cases, and § 3(2) defines circulation broadly. The
text explains these qualifications and distinguishes this East German law
from West Germany's Betäubungsmittelgesetz.

The Stasi report is dated 17 October 1970, Info Nr. 1193/70, BArch, MfS,
ZAIG 1867, Bl. 1–18. Section 7 reports approximately 125 kg hashish detected
in the reporting period and describes attempted imports/transit, including
3 kg bought in Afghanistan. It is not a domestic prevalence survey:
https://www.ddr-im-blick.de/jahrgaenge/jahrgang-1970/report/stoerung-und-missbrauch-des-reise-und-gueterverkehrs-berlin-brd/

Richardson-Little's existing cited chapter, chapter 9 of Cannabis: Global
Histories (2021), was checked in the local EPUB. It documents rare domestic
cannabis availability, high alcohol use and recreational psychopharmaceutical
use, including Faustan with alcohol (note 4). The entry does not invent a
numerical comparison of sedative and cannabis prevalence. Western communal
smoking and socialist suspicion are presented as political interpretations,
not universal claims about youth or evidence of widespread GDR cannabis use.
The 1989 customs photograph was unnecessary and is not used.
