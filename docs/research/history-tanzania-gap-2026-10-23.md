# Tanzania, 1935 to 1996

The dated gap is `tanganyika-dangerous-drugs-ordinance-1935` to
`tanzania-illicit-traffic-act-1995` (1 January 1996).

## Weed Seeds Order, 1976

Read the TanzLII consolidation of Government Notice 31 of 1976, as at 31 July
2002: https://media.tanzlii.org/media/legislation/317099/source_file/1effc5b40dd52c67/1976-31.pdf
The 1976 gazette was not opened. The commencement line is blank. No amendment
mark is printed on the hemp rows.

Class 1 prohibited noxious weed seeds may not appear in a graded seed sample.
Tolerance is one seed per kilogram. Two or more put the lot under stop-sale
until recleaned and retested. Item 4 is hemp, common name marijuana. Item 5 is
Cannabis sativa, true hemp, Swahili bhang or bangi. Added as
`tanzania-weed-seeds-order-1976`, category hemp. The score is unchanged: the
estimated first restriction still runs from 1900 at 13 until 15 September 2015.
