## What changed

- Added `italy-cannabis-light-derivatives-ruling-2019` (2019-05-30,
  tightening, court/hemp, unscored context): the Court of Cassation ruled
  en banc that selling leaves, flowers, oil and resin from industrial
  hemp falls outside Law 242/2016 and is an offence unless devoid of
  narcotic effect. Information note 30 May 2019; judgment 30475 filed
  10 July. Mirrors the unscored treatment of the 2025 hemp-flower ban.
