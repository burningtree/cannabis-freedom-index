# Brick weed

Added `/culture/traditions/brick-weed/` as an entry about the physical form, supply-chain practices and social context. Related terms are searchable through `also`; Mota and Hashish link to it. No separate Language entry duplicates the subject.

Sources read: DEA Museum's description of bulk cannabis bricks; Garat's 2016 TNI briefing on Paraguay's production and labour; Macedo, Kosmann and Pujol-Luz's 2013 forensic study; Castro and Fraga's 2023 observational/interview study of Brazilian home-growers; Wikipedia for regional terminology only. The dated studies support the account rather than constituting new events in cannabis policy. Existing history links supply the legal context.

The article distinguishes plant-material bricks from resin and treats contamination as evidence about particular samples. It does not repeat unsupported assertions that every brick contains glue, faeces or petroleum products, assign a universal potency, or claim every pressed sample originated in Paraguay. The comparison with growers' knowledge is interpretive, based on the documented contrast between bulk supply and cultivation networks.
