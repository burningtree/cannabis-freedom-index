# Glossary review: 29 October 2026

Reviewed the titles, summaries and bodies of 622 Cannabis Enforcement Tracker reports. Added 38 glossary entries in glossary.yaml, plus the existing Hashish atlas entry's alias “cannabis resin” and verb forms for eradication. 561 reports contain at least one newly explained entry. Counts below are report occurrences, with a term counted once per report; they are not a promise to annotate links or headings.

Definitions are brief, original explanations. Legal entries describe concepts rather than exporting a single country's procedures to every jurisdiction. A charge is distinguished from a conviction, seizure from forfeiture, and “remand” acknowledges its two court meanings. The aliases avoid ordinary baggage “charges” and do not equate ambiguous “tons” with metric tonnes.

## Editorial references

- Arrest/court process, defendants, prosecution, conviction and acquittal: [Australian CDPP legal vocabulary](https://www.cdpp.gov.au/legal-speak-explained), [PPS Northern Ireland](https://www.ppsni.gov.uk/legal-terms-explained), [US Department of Justice glossary](https://www.justice.gov/usao/justice-101/glossary).
- Bail and custody: [US Ninth Circuit glossary](https://www.ca9.uscourts.gov/guides-resources/glossary/), the CDPP and PPS glossaries above.
- Remand and the context-dependent use of the word: [GOV.UK remand](https://www.gov.uk/charged-crime/remand), [US Sentencing Commission glossary](https://www.ussc.gov/education/glossary).
- Concurrent/consecutive sentences and parole: [GOV.UK sentence types](https://www.gov.uk/types-of-prison-sentence/concurrent-and-consecutive-sentences), [US Sentencing Commission](https://www.ussc.gov/education/glossary). Life-sentence wording deliberately states that release rules vary.
- Seizure, forfeiture and reporting's broader “confiscation”: [US DOJ forfeiture policy manual](https://www.justice.gov/usdoj-media/criminal-mlars/media/1140236/dl), [US Treasury overview](https://home.treasury.gov/policy-issues/terrorism-and-illicit-finance/asset-forfeiture/forfeiture-overview).
- Extradition: [US DOJ international extradition FAQ](https://www.justice.gov/criminal/criminal-oia/frequently-asked-questions-regarding-extradition).
- Controlled delivery, precursors and enforcement terminology: [1988 UN convention](https://www.unodc.org/pdf/convention_1988_en.pdf), [UNODC drug law enforcement curriculum](https://www.unodc.org/documents/southasia/reports/Curriculum_on_Drug_Law_Enforcement.pdf).
- Street value: [UNODC technical assessment of drug statistics](https://www.unodc.org/documents/data-and-analysis/statistics/Drugs/Background_paper_session_II_ARQ_Technical_assessment.pdf). This is an estimate, not transaction proceeds.
- Psychotropic effects and treaty vocabulary: [WHO psychoactive drugs overview](https://www.who.int/health-topics/drugs-psychoactive).
- Hydroponics: [USDA National Agricultural Library](https://www.nal.usda.gov/farms-and-agricultural-production-systems/hydroponics). Grow-house wording describes a building's use, not its legal status.
- Hectares and metric tonnes: [IPCC conversion appendix](https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2018/03/srl-en-1.pdf).
- Lakh/crore: [Government of India's Office of the Principal Scientific Adviser, hosted report, endnote 1](https://psa.gov.in/CMS/web/sites/default/files/publication/WEF_Transforming_Small_Businesses_2025.pdf).
- Rai: [FAO, The Vegetable Sector in Thailand, conversion table](https://www.fao.org/4/ac145e/ac145e00.htm).
- Wet weight: [California Department of Cannabis Control data glossary](https://testing.cannabis.ca.gov/resources/data-dashboard/data-dashboard-glossary/).
